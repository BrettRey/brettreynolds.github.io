CG-0002 · CGEL Ch. 5 §1, p. 328

Dummy there as an NP

Supported Supported means that the tested distinction survives the stated challenge.

CGEL’s claim

CGEL includes dummy there on distributional grounds.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Finding and limits

Tag behaviour independently supports the pronoun analysis, despite limited distribution.

Evidence

There occupies the subject slot and can itself appear as the subject of a question tag.
