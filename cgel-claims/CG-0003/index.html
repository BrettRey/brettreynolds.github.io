CG-0003 · CGEL Ch. 5 §2, p. 329

Full NPs before the head

Textual mismatch Textual mismatch records incompatible wording or labels across passages.

CGEL’s claim

The overview excludes full NPs as pre-head dependents in its discussion of nominal modifiers.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Finding and limits

Qualify the generalization for genitive attributes. The plain-case institutional examples still distinguish nominal from NP.

Evidence

CGEL gives full NP status to measure genitives and to all girls’ in an all girls’ school.

  • Audit noteChapter 5 systematic audit (working note, not published)
    CGEL passage
    §2, p. 329. The overview excludes full NPs as pre-head dependents in its discussion of nominal modifiers.
    Example, contrast or countermodel
    The Ministry of Defence officials example tests plain-case noun modification. At p. 444 CGEL permits a full measure genitive, and on p. 470 assigns NP status to all girls’ in an all girls’ school.
    What it establishes
    The chapter itself supplies a pre-head NP outside the plain-case pattern. The Ministry of Defence contrast can retain its narrower use.
