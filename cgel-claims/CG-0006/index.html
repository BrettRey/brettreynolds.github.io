CG-0006 · CGEL Ch. 5 §3.2, p. 345

Low cardinals with quasi-count nouns

Open Open means that live alternatives remain.

CGEL’s claim

The chapter excludes low cardinals in the use exemplified in [37–39].

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Finding and limits

The empirical issue is how a low cardinal combines with the head. A count of strings alone cannot settle it.

Evidence

Brett’s countability work distinguishes rare counted-head examples from attributive uses.
