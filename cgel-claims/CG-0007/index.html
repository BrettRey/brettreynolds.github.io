CG-0007 · CGEL Ch. 5 §3.3, pp. 349–350

Head of a pseudopartitive

Open Open means that live alternatives remain.

CGEL’s claim

The first noun heads a number of protesters, with number transparency where appropriate.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Finding and limits

Partitive movement does not distinguish the two proposed structures for the pseudopartitive.

Evidence

Movement of of whom comes from a true partitive. This number of people come also challenges a simple second-noun analysis.
