CG-0009 · CGEL Ch. 5 §3.4, p. 354

Measure respecification

Supported Supported means that the tested distinction survives the stated challenge.

CGEL’s claim

Another can combine with a plural head even where singular respecification is unavailable.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Finding and limits

The lexical distribution of another and singular measure agreement make different predictions.

Evidence

Another three days allows a singular time-span construal. Another three bodies have been discovered retains plural agreement.

  • Audit noteChapter 5 systematic audit (working note, not published)
    CGEL passage
    §3.4, p. 354. Another can combine with a plural head even where singular respecification is unavailable.
    Example, contrast or countermodel
    Compare another three days with another three bodies have been discovered (p. 354 [74]). A time span can be conceived as one interval. Plural agreement is required whether the discoveries were simultaneous or separate.
    What it establishes
    A plural after another cannot always be explained by a singular measure reading. The two diagnostics apply to different phenomena.
