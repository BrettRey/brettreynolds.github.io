CG-0010 · CGEL Ch. 5 §4, p. 357

Weekday as determiner

Open Open means that live alternatives remain.

CGEL’s claim

The weekday is assigned determiner function in Sunday morning.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Finding and limits

Identify a test for the weekday’s function in bare on Sunday morning. Locating the head settles only half the analysis.

Evidence

Plural Sunday mornings establishes the head. Constructed three Sunday mornings admits an internal modifier reading.

  • Audit noteChapter 5 systematic audit (working note, not published)
    CGEL passage
    §4, p. 357. The weekday is assigned determiner function in Sunday morning.
    Example, contrast or countermodel
    Adding plural -s to morning locates the head. In three Sunday mornings, three counts mornings and Sunday occurs within the material it quantifies. Compare the bare temporal use on Sunday morning.
    What it establishes
    There is evidence for a modifier use in the counted phrase. An independent test is needed to assign the weekday the determiner function in the bare phrase.
