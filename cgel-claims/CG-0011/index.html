CG-0011 · CGEL Ch. 5 §4, p. 357

Definiteness of a genitive NP

Open Open means that live alternatives remain.

CGEL’s claim

A Type I genitive determiner marks the whole containing NP as definite.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Finding and limits

Test whole-NP uniqueness and quantification against competing accounts. The article on the possessor is insufficient evidence.

Evidence

An indefinite possessor can introduce a new dog. A listener can also accommodate a salient dog relative to that possessor.
