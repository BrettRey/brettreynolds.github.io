CG-0017 · CGEL Ch. 5 §7.1, p. 373

Presentative this

Qualification Qualification narrows its scope or evidential force.

CGEL’s claim

Demonstrative determiners are introduced as markers of definiteness.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Finding and limits

Cross-reference the later treatment when describing the central demonstrative use.

Evidence

Presentative this can introduce a new discourse entity. CGEL discusses the construction elsewhere.

  • Audit noteChapter 5 systematic audit (working note, not published)
    CGEL passage
    §7.1, p. 373. Demonstrative determiners are introduced as markers of definiteness.
    Example, contrast or countermodel
    In the presentative use exemplified by There was this man …, the speaker can introduce the man for the first time. Compare the chapter’s central demonstratives with the discussions on pp. 1401 and 1510.
    What it establishes
    A cross-reference would reconcile introductory wording with a use CGEL already recognizes. Prior mention and grammatical marking of definiteness are distinct.
