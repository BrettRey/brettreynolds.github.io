CG-0018 · CGEL Ch. 5 §7.10, p. 392

Internal expansion of a few

Qualification Qualification narrows its scope or evidential force.

CGEL’s claim

A short list of internal expansions helps motivate lexical treatment of a few.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Finding and limits

Expand the distribution recorded for few. Further evidence is needed to choose a lexical analysis.

A Wisconsin opinion uses a scant few minutes.
