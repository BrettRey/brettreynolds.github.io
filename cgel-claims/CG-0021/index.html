CG-0021 · CGEL Ch. 5 §8.3, p. 406

Group identity over time

Open Open means that live alternatives remain.

CGEL’s claim

The police are getting younger is explained through different sets of people at different times.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Finding and limits

A theory of what the NP picks out is needed to choose between an institutional and a member-set analysis.

Evidence

A stable force could have a falling mean age as membership changes.

  • Audit noteChapter 5 systematic audit (working note, not published)
    CGEL passage
    §8.3, p. 406. The police are getting younger is explained through different sets of people at different times.
    Example, contrast or countermodel
    Compare a police force whose older members retire and whose younger recruits replace them. The sets of people compared change even if speakers identify the same continuing institution at every date.
    What it establishes
    Both descriptions can be true in the same history. A model of the referent is needed to distinguish readings of The police are getting younger.
    Locator
    The difference between possible variation and actual variation
