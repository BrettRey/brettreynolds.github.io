CG-0022 · CGEL Ch. 5 §8.3, p. 407

Exceptions to generic predication

Qualification Qualification narrows its scope or evidential force.

CGEL’s claim

Generic a lion is glossed using any lion that exists.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Finding and limits

An unrestricted domain does not require that the predicate hold of every member. The book gives generic a restricted technical use.

Evidence

CGEL’s own generic about Italians and pasta tolerates individual exceptions.

  • Audit noteChapter 5 systematic audit (working note, not published)
    CGEL passage
    §8.3, p. 407. Generic a lion is glossed using any lion that exists.
    Example, contrast or countermodel
    Compare the any-lion gloss with CGEL’s own generalization about Italians and pasta. An Italian who dislikes pasta can belong to the intended population without making the generic unusable.
    What it establishes
    Including every individual in the domain leaves open whether the property must hold of every individual. The chapter’s restricted technical sense of generic matters here.
