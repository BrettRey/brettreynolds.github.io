CG-0024 · CGEL Ch. 5 §8.5, p. 409

Definiteness of bare role NPs

Qualification Qualification narrows its scope or evidential force.

CGEL’s claim

CGEL infers from the definiteness of the office that bare role NPs invariably allow substitution of a corresponding NP with the.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Finding and limits

The bare form readily assigns the office to both players. The definite counterpart invites a single-holder reading, though a salient office may sustain a shared-role reading. The attestation establishes shared tenure. Preservation of the reading under insertion is a judgment.

Evidence

ASU reports a shared appointment. Hold team, meeting and date fixed. Compare “Faatulu became co-captain of the team that day, and so did Peck” with “Faatulu became the co-captain of the team that day, and so did Peck”. Both versions are constructed.

  • AttestationArizona State University, Sun Devil Athletics. Jolynn Faatulu Overcomes Injuries to Shine.
    CGEL passage
    §8.5(a), p. 409, discussion of [19i–iii]. The office is “invariably definite”. The inference to substitution is signalled by “therefore invariably replaceable”. The examples concern the office of treasurer.
    Example, contrast or countermodel
    The report says Faatulu “was named co-captain along with fellow junior Jamie Peck”. It never supplies a definite counterpart in this context.
    What it establishes
    Attests a bare role with two officeholders. The two versions with became in the audit are constructed comparative judgments.
