CG-0025 · CGEL Ch. 5 §9, p. 410

Second function of a fused head

Open Open means that live alternatives remain.

CGEL’s claim

CGEL assigns both a dependent function and head function where a phrase occurs alone.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Finding and limits

Brett’s fusion paper tests the additional function independently. This question also arises where the head noun is overt with a genitive.

Evidence

Standalone some retains the modifiers it takes before a noun. Ordinary-head and fusion accounts both predict that continuity.
