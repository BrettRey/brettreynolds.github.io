CG-0027 · CGEL Ch. 5 §9.3, p. 416

Ranking and fused adjectives

Supported Supported means that the tested distinction survives the stated challenge.

CGEL’s claim

CGEL limits which adjectives license the fused construction.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Finding and limits

The inventory needs a lexical restriction alongside the semantic tendency.

Evidence

Main has a ranking interpretation yet fails to license the corresponding fused use.

    Example, contrast or countermodel
    Compare admissible ranking adjectives in §9.3 with main. A speaker who understands which item is principal still cannot freely use the main in the same fused use.

    CGEL explicitly records this lexical restriction. The semantics predicts a tendency and the inventory supplies an independent control.
