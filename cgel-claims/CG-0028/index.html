CG-0028 · CGEL Ch. 5 §9.7, p. 425

An understood partitive PP

Open Open means that live alternatives remain.

CGEL’s claim

Almost all in [47ii] is assigned an unspoken of them.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Finding and limits

Domain recovery alone cannot select ellipsis. A test should distinguish silent syntax from contextual restriction.

Evidence

Context also restricts everyone in A hundred guests came. Everyone enjoyed it. The explicit almost all of them alternant supports CGEL.
