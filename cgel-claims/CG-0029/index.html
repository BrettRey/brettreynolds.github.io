CG-0029 · CGEL Ch. 5 §10.1, p. 427

Wordhood of us both

Open Open means that live alternatives remain.

CGEL’s claim

Adjacency and substitution support the proposed universal personal-pronoun analysis.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Finding and limits

Evidence for one NP leaves wordhood open. First-, second- and third-person forms need separate controls.

Evidence

To us both behaves as one constituent. Compare outside of us two and you two.

  • Audit noteNouns and noun phrases notebook (working note, not published)
    CGEL passage
    §10.1, p. 427. Adjacency and substitution support the proposed universal personal-pronoun analysis.
    Example, contrast or countermodel
    In to us both the two words can form a single complement of to. The personal forms also combine with two in independently documented phrases (pp. 639, 1466). More than one word can occupy that same constituent position.
    What it establishes
    Constituency follows more readily than single-word status. The distributions of us, you and them require separate controls.
