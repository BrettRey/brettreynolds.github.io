CG-0032 · CGEL Ch. 5 §10.2, p. 430

Subjects containing lucky me

Correction Correction proposes changing a particular claim.

CGEL’s claim

CGEL excludes subject uses of personal-pronoun expressions modified by poor or lucky. The empirical target here is the external function of the whole expression.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Finding and limits

Record an integrated subject use in informal writing. Third-person agreement is compatible with a special construction or a nominal reanalysis of me. This undated token establishes neither the form’s distribution across registers nor its availability in 2002.

Evidence

A first-person book review has “lucky me gets to interview Jolene”. The finite predicate gets has no separate overt subject. Reading lucky me as a detached exclamation leaves the subject of gets unexplained.
