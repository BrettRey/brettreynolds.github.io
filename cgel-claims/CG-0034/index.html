CG-0034 · CGEL Ch. 5 §12, p. 434

Indefinite NPs after a multiplier

Correction Correction proposes changing a particular claim.

CGEL’s claim

Multipliers such as twice are restricted to definite NPs.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Proposed wording

Multipliers commonly take definite NPs. They also take indefinite NPs denoting a magnitude, as in twice a number or twice an angle.

Finding and limits

Mathematical English allows the indefinite magnitude in these expressions. A restriction limited to a distinct predeterminer construction would need an independent way to distinguish it from multiplicative twice here.

Evidence

Math textbooks use twice a number and of twice an angle. The latter cannot mean twice per occasion.
