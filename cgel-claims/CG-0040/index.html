CG-0040 · CGEL Ch. 5 §14.2, p. 445

Postpositive possible and available

Correction Correction proposes changing a particular claim.

CGEL’s claim

Postpositive adjectives in this class are said to require a superlative or only.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Finding and limits

Qualify the licensing condition by adjective and construction.

Evidence

CGEL gives the money available elsewhere. The Congressional Record supplies every effort possible.

  • AttestationU.S. Senate, Congressional Record (24 April 1998). S. Res. 216.
    CGEL passage
    §14.2, p. 445, after [12i]. CGEL requires “an attributive superlative or only” for postpositive adjectives of the class exemplified by suitable and possible. Compare the money available on p. 57.
    Example, contrast or countermodel
    The resolution uses every effort possible with neither only nor an attributive superlative.
    What it establishes
    A pre-2002 counterexample for possible after every. Different adjectives and postpositive constructions may have different licensing conditions.
    Locator
    P. S3593, right column, resolved clause (3). PDF p. 33.
