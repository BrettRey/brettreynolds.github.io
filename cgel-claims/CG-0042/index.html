CG-0042 · CGEL Ch. 5 §14.3, p. 448

Heads of marked appositives

Correction Correction proposes changing a particular claim.

CGEL’s claim

Heads before appositive of are limited to month and politico-geographical terms.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Finding and limits

Extend the inventory to festival in this identifying use. A festival of music has a different relation.

Evidence

The Foreign Office names the Hindu festival of Holi. Holi identifies the festival being celebrated.

  • AttestationUK Foreign & Commonwealth Office (13 March 2017). Alok Sharma extends his wishes for a happy Holi festival.
    CGEL passage
    §14.3, p. 448, [21]. CGEL restricts heads in the identifying of construction to month and names of politico-geographical categories. The construction specifies which entity bears the name.
    Example, contrast or countermodel
    The account identifies the occasion celebrated as the Hindu festival of Holi. Compare a festival of music, where music supplies subject matter.
    What it establishes
    Festival admits an identifying use outside CGEL’s stated head inventory.
