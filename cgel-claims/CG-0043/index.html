CG-0043 · CGEL Ch. 5 §14.4, p. 449

Coordination as a diagnostic of wordhood

Argument gap Argument gap identifies an inference the evidence fails to establish.

CGEL’s claim

Passing any one of the coordination or modification tests suffices for a composite nominal.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Finding and limits

Successful coordination alone cannot choose between base coordination and syntactic reanalysis. A compound of another type is a calibration case.

Evidence

CGEL admits coordinated bases inside compounds and allows washing-machine to pass by reanalysis.
