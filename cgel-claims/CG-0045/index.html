CG-0045 · CGEL Ch. 5 §15, p. 452

Adjective ordering preferences

Supported Supported means that the tested distinction survives the stated challenge.

CGEL’s claim

CGEL distinguishes rigid order from labile preferences among modifiers.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Finding and limits

A contextually reversed size/colour sequence fits the stated account.

Evidence

A context that has already established the set of large sofas licenses black large sofa.

  • Audit noteChapter 5 systematic audit (working note, not published)
    CGEL passage
    §15, p. 452. CGEL distinguishes rigid order from labile preferences among modifiers.
    Example, contrast or countermodel
    Suppose the large sofas are already established as a set and colour distinguishes two of them. Within that set, black can precede large in black large sofa. This is the sort of contextual reversal §15 anticipates.
    What it establishes
    A reversal in an appropriate context supports the description of a preference. It could refute only a rigid ordering rule.
