CG-0047 · CGEL Ch. 5 §16.5.1, p. 472

Subject as a second NP function

Open Open means that live alternatives remain.

CGEL’s claim

Every Type I genitive is assigned both determiner and subject function.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Finding and limits

Test whether binding or control tracks genitive position when participant role and discourse perspective are held constant.

Evidence

Semantic parallels survive changes of function. Local reflexive antecedence supplies independent evidence, but a post-head by-phrase can also antecede a reflexive.

Parallel to a criticism of traditional grammar
P. 248 keeps the recipient role distinct from indirect-object function when the recipient is expressed in a PP. A genitive and a clausal subject likewise can share a role across different constructions. A methodological inconsistency would require comparable independent evidence: local binding, constituent position and control in NPs need their own tests.
