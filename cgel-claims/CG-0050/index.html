CG-0050 · CGEL Ch. 5 §16.5.3, p. 477

Inanimate genitives denoting a colour

Correction Correction proposes changing a particular claim.

CGEL’s claim

Gold’s colour is starred as a relation unavailable to the genitive.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Proposed wording

The of construction and the genitive differ in the relations they readily express. Colour is available in both. For the remaining proposed exclusions, distinguish a difference in grammaticality from a preference conditioned by the discourse.

Finding and limits

The genitive can express this specific relation in ordinary explanatory prose. Nothing here decides the other pairs in [60] or whether speakers prefer the of-construction in other contexts.

Evidence

Lorch explains the different appearances of elemental silver and gold using the phrase gold’s colour. The surrounding discussion fixes both the substance and the literal colour relation.
