CG-0052 · CGEL Ch. 5 §18.2, p. 502

Collective agreement with consist of

Correction Correction proposes changing a particular claim.

CGEL’s claim

A whole-group reading with consist of is said to preclude plural agreement.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Finding and limits

Plural agreement occurs with the whole-group predicate CGEL uses to exclude it. Rickman credits a reviewer for the examples. Neither that acknowledgment nor the club webpages supply a frequency estimate.

Evidence

Rickman (2018, p. 42) acknowledges authentic examples of the committee consist of. Swansea’s committee consist of 9 members gives the full composition predicate, whose subject denotes the group taken as a whole.
