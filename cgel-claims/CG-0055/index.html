CG-0055 · CGEL Ch. 5 §20.2.1, p. 517

The article inside a proper name

Qualification Qualification narrows its scope or evidential force.

CGEL’s claim

Definite reference is taken to exclude an indefinite determiner in a proper name.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Finding and limits

Distinguish a fixed article inside a conventional name from productive external determination.

Evidence

A Flock of Seagulls names a single band and occurs as subject in BMG’s prose.

  • AttestationBMG (12 March 2026). BMG Acquires Publishing & Recording Catalog of A Flock of Seagulls.
    CGEL passage
    §20.2.1, p. 517. Definite reference is taken to exclude an indefinite determiner in a proper name.
    Example, contrast or countermodel
    BMG uses the full band name A Flock of Seagulls in subject position before broke through internationally.
    What it establishes
    An indefinite article is fixed inside this conventional name. This leaves open what external determination it allows.
