CG-0060 · CGEL Ch. 14 §5.4, p. 1237

Passive be after perception see

Correction Correction proposes changing a particular claim.

CGEL’s claim

CGEL excludes passive be inside the complement of active perception see. It allows get or a bare past participial.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Proposed wording

Active perception see can take a bare past participial or an embedded passive with get or be. The availability of be can vary by speaker and context.

Finding and limits

Active see licenses embedded verbal be-passives for some speakers and contexts. The evidence leaves relative frequency and the historical distribution in literal perception open.

Evidence

CNN and CBS transcripts attest eventive be-passives with agentive by-phrases. An ABC concordance reports a return to see the car be unearthed. Sheehan accepts I saw him be fired.
