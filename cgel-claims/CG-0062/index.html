CG-0062 · CGEL Ch. 5 §20.3–20.4, p. 520

Indefinite articles with embellished names

Qualification Qualification narrows its scope or evidential force.

CGEL’s claim

CGEL gives bare NPs and NPs with the as the adjective-embellishment patterns for names, and treats selection of a manifestation as a secondary use.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Proposed wording

Nonrestrictive adjectival dependents can occur in bare NPs and with the. With an adjective describing the bearer’s state on an occasion, a/an is also available.

Finding and limits

The article can accompany an adjective that characterizes an already identified bearer. An analysis in terms of manifestations would need an independent reason to posit selection of a stage here.

Evidence

Two reports from 2001 have a visibly shaken Giuliani and a visibly-shaken Yuri Lutsenko. CBS has already named Giuliani and described his emotional state before the indefinite NP.
