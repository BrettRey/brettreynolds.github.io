CG-0063 · CGEL Ch. 5 §2, p. 332

Cross-reference for the hybrid NP

Production error Production error records a misprint, a wrong cross-reference or a misnumbered example that the authors’ errata do not list.

Not yet reviewed by the author. This entry was drafted with AI assistance, and its verdict may change once it has been checked.

CGEL’s claim

Note 3 sends readers to Ch. 14 §1.6 for the hybrid NP in There was no stopping her. Page 333 cites the same section for the argument that gerund-participials are clauses.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Proposed wording

For discussion, see Ch. 14, §1.5.

Finding and limits

Both references should point to §1.5. Section 1.6 concerns the case of the subject. Ch. 16, p. 1394, repeats the same reference.

Evidence

Ch. 14 begins §1.5 on p. 1187 and §1.6 on p. 1191. The hybrid constructions are on p. 1189 [57], and the argument for the clausal analysis runs through pp. 1188–1190.
