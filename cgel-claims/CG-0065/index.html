CG-0065 · CGEL Ch. 5 §3.2.3, p. 346

Hives among singular -s nouns

Open Open means that live alternatives remain.

Not yet reviewed by the author. This entry was drafted with AI assistance, and its verdict may change once it has been checked.

CGEL’s claim

Hives is listed with shingles, mumps and rabies as a singular noun in -s.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Finding and limits

The evidence conflicts. Present-day agreement data (Hives is/are) in edited medical or general prose would decide whether hives belongs with hiccups in [42ii].

Evidence

Wiktionary codes hives as plural with singular hive, and shingles as uncountable. The OED entry hives, n. says “Chiefly with plural agreement”, but it dates from 1898, is not fully revised, carries a Scottish English label, and its one Hives are quotation is from an 1893 Northumberland glossary. An NHS page has hives keeps coming back (seen through a summary only).
