CG-0066 · CGEL Ch. 5 §3.2.3, p. 347

The ounces of sugar example

Production error Production error records a misprint, a wrong cross-reference or a misnumbered example that the authors’ errata do not list.

Not yet reviewed by the author. This entry was drafted with AI assistance, and its verdict may change once it has been checked.

CGEL’s claim

The discussion refers to “our two ounces of sugar example”.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Proposed wording

our three ounces of sugar example

Finding and limits

Change two to three. The reference is unambiguous.

Evidence

The example is [41ii] Three ounces of sugar is rather too much (p. 346). Two pounds of sugar occurs separately at p. 354 [71v].
