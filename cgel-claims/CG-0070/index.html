CG-0070 · CGEL Ch. 5 §7.5, p. 384

Count singular some and the indefinite article

Production error Production error records a misprint, a wrong cross-reference or a misnumbered example that the authors’ errata do not list.

Not yet reviewed by the author. This entry was drafted with AI assistance, and its verdict may change once it has been checked.

CGEL’s claim

Some and anyn occur with count singular heads “as in [30]” and may therefore contrast with the indefinite article.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Proposed wording

as in [29], for example

Finding and limits

Change [30] to [29]. As printed, the reference also conflicts with the paragraph’s second reason, the restriction to quantitative uses.

Evidence

[30] is the exclamatory use (Those are SOME elephants!), non-quantitative and plural in [30a]. [29] is “Vague count singular use”, where some “is in competition with the indefinite article a”.
