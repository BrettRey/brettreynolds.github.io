CG-0072 · CGEL Ch. 5 §8.2, p. 401

Bare role NPs outside predicative complement

Textual mismatch Textual mismatch records incompatible wording or labels across passages.

Not yet reviewed by the author. This entry was drafted with AI assistance, and its verdict may change once it has been checked.

CGEL’s claim

Treasurer in I was elected treasurer is a bare role NP restricted to the function of predicative complement.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Proposed wording

Bare role NPs are restricted to predicative functions, the complement of of after nouns such as role, and supplements.

Finding and limits

Extend the restriction to the of construction and supplements, or explain how they count as predicative. CG-0024 concerns insertion of the in the same NPs.

Evidence

Page 409 lists complement of of after role, part or position, with [19iii] The role of treasurer will fall to Henry. Page 1358 has the supplement Wilson, Secretary to the Cabinet. Ch. 4 p. 255 treats the as phrase as a marked predicative, which covers [19ii] only.
