CG-0074 · CGEL Ch. 5 §9.4, p. 419

Two thirds as a fused predeterminer

Textual mismatch Textual mismatch records incompatible wording or labels across passages.

Not yet reviewed by the author. This entry was drafted with AI assistance, and its verdict may change once it has been checked.

CGEL’s claim

In [31i] (two thirds) the predeterminer is a nominal.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Proposed wording

In [i–ii] the predeterminer is respectively an NP and a noun

Finding and limits

Call two thirds an NP. A nominal cannot contain the determiner two.

Evidence

Page 434: “Predeterminers expressing fractions have the form of NPs”, since half, quarter and third take determiners such as cardinals. Page 331 [10a] also gives an NP in this function.
