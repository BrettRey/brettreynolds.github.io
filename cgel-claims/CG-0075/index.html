CG-0075 · CGEL Ch. 5 §9.5, p. 420

Cross-reference for case in fused relatives

Production error Production error records a misprint, a wrong cross-reference or a misnumbered example that the authors’ errata do not list.

Not yet reviewed by the author. This entry was drafted with AI assistance, and its verdict may change once it has been checked.

CGEL’s claim

The text refers to “the discussion of case in fused relatives, Ch. 12, §5.3”.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Proposed wording

Ch. 12, §6.3

Finding and limits

Change §5.3 to §6.3.

Evidence

Ch. 12 §5 is “Infinitival relative clauses” and has no subsections. Case in fused relatives is under §6.3 (p. 1073). Page 332 cites §6.3 correctly. The authors’ errata correct a similar reference on p. 269 from §5.4 to §6.4.
