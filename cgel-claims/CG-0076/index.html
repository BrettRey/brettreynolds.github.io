CG-0076 · CGEL Ch. 5 §9.5, p. 421

Items in both pronoun and determinative categories

Textual mismatch Textual mismatch records incompatible wording or labels across passages.

Not yet reviewed by the author. This entry was drafted with AI assistance, and its verdict may change once it has been checked.

CGEL’s claim

Just four items belong in both categories: what, which, we and you.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Proposed wording

just five items that belong in both categories: what, whatever, which, we, and you

Finding and limits

Add whatever to the count.

Evidence

Page 422 refers to “pronouns what (and whatever)”. Page 356 lists determinative whatever. Ch. 12, p. 1074, gives pronoun and determinative uses of the ·ever forms.
