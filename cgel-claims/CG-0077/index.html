CG-0077 · CGEL Ch. 5 §9.5, p. 422

Example reference for singular demonstrative fused heads

Production error Production error records a misprint, a wrong cross-reference or a misnumbered example that the authors’ errata do not list.

Not yet reviewed by the author. This entry was drafted with AI assistance, and its verdict may change once it has been checked.

CGEL’s claim

Singular examples like [17] and [9ii–iii] match corresponding plural forms.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Proposed wording

[17] and [9ib–c]

Finding and limits

Change [9ii–iii] to [9ib–c].

Evidence

[9] (p. 412) has rows i (a–c) and ii only. [9ii] is lend me [one]. The intended examples are [9ib–c], as cited on p. 414.
