CG-0078 · CGEL Ch. 5 §9.6, p. 423

Ordering of post-head dependents

Qualification Qualification narrows its scope or evidential force.

Not yet reviewed by the author. This entry was drafted with AI assistance, and its verdict may change once it has been checked.

CGEL’s claim

The restriction on else departs from the general rule that, syntactically, the order of post-head internal dependents is completely free (see §15).

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Proposed wording

departs from the general lability of post-head internal dependents (see §15)

Finding and limits

The §9.6 restriction is one of the rigid constraints §15 recognizes. General rule and from a syntactic point of view partly rescue the sentence. Two readers weighed it differently.

Evidence

§15, p. 454: “Although the order of post-head internal dependents is essentially labile, certain specialised types are subject to rigid ordering constraints”. Its rule [14] includes else before restrictors.
