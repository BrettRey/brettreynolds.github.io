CG-0079 · CGEL Ch. 5 §10.2, p. 430

Predeterminer modifiers with pronoun heads

Textual mismatch Textual mismatch records incompatible wording or labels across passages.

Not yet reviewed by the author. This entry was drafted with AI assistance, and its verdict may change once it has been checked.

CGEL’s claim

Predeterminer modifiers are virtually incompatible with pronouns. The one exception is all with 1st and 2nd person plural pronouns followed by a modifier.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Proposed wording

The exceptions are all with 1st and 2nd person plural pronouns … and fractions or multipliers with fused relative what (§12).

Finding and limits

Add the second case to p. 430 or cross-refer to §12.

Evidence

§12, p. 433, gives “two very restricted cases”: all with we or you, and fractions and multipliers with a fused relative, as in I sold it for half/twice what I had paid.
