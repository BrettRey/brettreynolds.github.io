CG-0081 · CGEL Ch. 5 §14.2, p. 446

Cross-reference for non-finite modifiers

Production error Production error records a misprint, a wrong cross-reference or a misnumbered example that the authors’ errata do not list.

Not yet reviewed by the author. This entry was drafted with AI assistance, and its verdict may change once it has been checked.

CGEL’s claim

For the non-finite modifiers in [15ii–iv], see Ch. 14, §8.2.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Proposed wording

see Ch. 14, §9

Finding and limits

Change §8.2 to §9.

Evidence

Ch. 14 §8.2 (p. 1259) is “Non-finite complements of nouns”. §9 (p. 1264), “Non-finite clauses as modifiers and supplements”, opens with near-copies of [15iii–iv].
