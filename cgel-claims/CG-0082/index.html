CG-0082 · CGEL Ch. 5 §14.2, p. 446

Attributive use of a- adjectives

Qualification Qualification narrows its scope or evidential force.

Not yet reviewed by the author. This entry was drafted with AI assistance, and its verdict may change once it has been checked.

CGEL’s claim

The adjectives in [12iv] (the house currently ablaze, all people now alive, the ones asleep) are altogether excluded from attributive position.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Proposed wording

are excluded from attributive position unless expanded by modification or coordination (Ch. 6, §4.2)

Finding and limits

Chapter 5 omits the caveat its cross-reference supplies. Altogether may contrast with the sense difference in [12ii].

Evidence

Ch. 6, p. 559: modification or coordination “can greatly improve the acceptability of the attributive use”, with their still awake children and attested an aware, amused glance.
