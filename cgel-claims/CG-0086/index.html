CG-0086 · CGEL Ch. 5 §16.2.1, p. 461

Genitive subjects after without

Correction Correction proposes changing a particular claim.

Not yet reviewed by the author. This entry was drafted with AI assistance, and its verdict may change once it has been checked.

CGEL’s claim

In the clausal complement of with or without, a gerund-participial cannot take a genitive subject.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Proposed wording

In the absolute construction with with, a gerund-participial cannot take a genitive subject. After without a genitive subject is possible.

Finding and limits

Genitive subjects occur after without. The restriction may hold for absolute with, as in With me out of the way.

Evidence

CGEL itself treats without as part of the construction (n. 61; [23id]). Edited prose: Owens (2012) without my having communicated; Thomasson (2015) without their being governed by the standard rules of use; Mill without our being aware of it.
