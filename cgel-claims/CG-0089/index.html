CG-0089 · CGEL Ch. 5 §16.6, p. 481

Bare genitive and plural formation

Textual mismatch Textual mismatch records incompatible wording or labels across passages.

Not yet reviewed by the author. This entry was drafted with AI assistance, and its verdict may change once it has been checked.

CGEL’s claim

The bare genitive is obligatory when the plural is formed regularly with ·s, and ’s is required when the plural is not formed with ·s.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Proposed wording

the bare genitive is obligatory when the plural form ends in s, however it is formed, and ’s is required otherwise

Finding and limits

State the rule in terms of plurals ending in s. The argument from geese and mice for inflectional status is unaffected.

Evidence

Ch. 18, pp. 1595–1596 [35i], lists indices’ theses’ species’ (and wives’) as obligatory bare genitives: “regular or irregular, including foreign plurals like indices”.
