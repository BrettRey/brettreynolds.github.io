CG-0090 · CGEL Ch. 5 §17.2.1, p. 486

Cross-reference for anaphoric restrictions

Production error Production error records a misprint, a wrong cross-reference or a misnumbered example that the authors’ errata do not list.

Not yet reviewed by the author. This entry was drafted with AI assistance, and its verdict may change once it has been checked.

CGEL’s claim

Subject to restrictions described in Ch. 17, §2.3, antecedent and pronoun can occupy the full range of NP functions.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Proposed wording

Ch. 17, §§2.4 and 3

Finding and limits

Probably §§2.4 and 3. Confidence is lower than for the other cross-references because §2.3 states an agreement restriction.

Evidence

Ch. 17 §2.3 (pp. 1468–1475) covers 3rd person pronouns and their semantic relations. Restrictions on position are in §2.4 (order of anaphor and antecedent) and §3 (reflexives).
