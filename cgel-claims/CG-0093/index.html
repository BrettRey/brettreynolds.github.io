CG-0093 · CGEL Ch. 5 §18.2, p. 503

Number-transparent singular nouns

Textual mismatch Textual mismatch records incompatible wording or labels across passages.

Not yet reviewed by the author. This entry was drafted with AI assistance, and its verdict may change once it has been checked.

CGEL’s claim

The clear cases of number-transparent singular nouns are lot, number and couple₂.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Finding and limits

The p. 503 list is restricted to nouns taking a + N + of without saying so.

Evidence

Page 350 [57] also lists singular plenty, remainder and rest without hedging. Page 504 adds percent.
