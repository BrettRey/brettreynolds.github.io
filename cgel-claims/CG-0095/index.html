CG-0095 · CGEL Ch. 5 §20.2, p. 517

Titles in the account of proper names

Textual mismatch Textual mismatch records incompatible wording or labels across passages.

Not yet reviewed by the author. This entry was drafted with AI assistance, and its verdict may change once it has been checked.

CGEL’s claim

Titles of written works, films and periodicals are much less constrained than other proper names and are excluded from the following account.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Finding and limits

Narrow the exclusion or move these examples.

Evidence

The account includes them: [3x–xi] the Bible, the Koran, the Talmud; The Economist, The Guardian, The Times, with following prose on newspaper and periodical names. Page 522 calls them newspaper titles.
