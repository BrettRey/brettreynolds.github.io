CG-0097 · CGEL Ch. 5 §20.3, p. 520

Title and appellation

Production error Production error records a misprint, a wrong cross-reference or a misnumbered example that the authors’ errata do not list.

Not yet reviewed by the author. This entry was drafted with AI assistance, and its verdict may change once it has been checked.

CGEL’s claim

Note 80 refers to “such titles as Emperor, Empress, Archduke”.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Proposed wording

such appellations as Emperor, Empress, Archduke

Finding and limits

Change titles to appellations.

Evidence

Note 79, one page earlier, reserves title for the name of a literary work or comparable artefact and uses appellation for Emperor and similar words.
