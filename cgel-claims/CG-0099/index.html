CG-0099 · CGEL Ch. 5 §20.4, p. 521

One country named Zaire

Correction Correction proposes changing a particular claim.

Not yet reviewed by the author. This entry was drafted with AI assistance, and its verdict may change once it has been checked.

CGEL’s claim

“Although there is only one country named Zaire, there are thousands of people named Mary.”

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Proposed wording

Although there was only one country named Zaire, …

Finding and limits

Change the tense or the example. The point about secondary uses is unaffected. Compare the authors’ correction of the Bronx on p. 518.

Evidence

The country was renamed the Democratic Republic of the Congo in 1997, five years before CGEL appeared.
