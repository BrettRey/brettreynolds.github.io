CG-0100 · CGEL Ch. 5 §20.5, p. 522

Personal names in vocatives

Production error Production error records a misprint, a wrong cross-reference or a misnumbered example that the authors’ errata do not list.

Not yet reviewed by the author. This entry was drafted with AI assistance, and its verdict may change once it has been checked.

CGEL’s claim

“Personal nouns are used alone or with certain appellations.”

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Proposed wording

Personal names are used alone or with certain appellations.

Finding and limits

Change Personal nouns to Personal names.

Evidence

The sentence glosses [12i], labelled personal names (Mary, Smith, Mary Smith, Mr/Dr Smith, Sir John). Elsewhere CGEL uses personal noun for a noun denoting persons (pp. 1050, 1691).
