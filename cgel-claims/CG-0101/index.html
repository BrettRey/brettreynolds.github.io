CG-0101 · CGEL Ch. 5 §20.5, p. 523

Officer as an appellation

Correction Correction proposes changing a particular claim.

Not yet reviewed by the author. This entry was drafted with AI assistance, and its verdict may change once it has been checked.

CGEL’s claim

The occupational terms in [12iv] (driver, officer “member of police force”, waiter, vicar) are not used as appellations.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Proposed wording

The occupational terms in [iv] other than officer are not used as appellations

Finding and limits

Officer + surname is an appellation in at least American English. The claim holds for driver and waiter.

Evidence

Gee, Officer Krupke, from West Side Story (1957), addresses a police sergeant with Officer + surname. Page 519 already lists the police rank Inspector, and [4ii] has Nurse Fox.
