CG-0105 · CGEL Ch. 5, various sections, pp. 323–517

Misprints in this printing

Production error Production error records a misprint, a wrong cross-reference or a misnumbered example that the authors’ errata do not list.

Not yet reviewed by the author. This entry was drafted with AI assistance, and its verdict may change once it has been checked.

CGEL’s claim

Fourteen misprints not in the authors’ errata, confirmed on the page images of the corrected printing.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Proposed wording

it is/they are; a(n) additional/further/good; Psychology; arbitrary; delete repeated heading; delete NP; [6i a, b]; custard-pie-in-your-face; disadvantaged; constraint; Himalayas; Colosseum; Bosporus

Candidates for the authors’ errata page. The printing used already incorporates the p. 446 and p. 518 corrections.
