CG-0110 · CGEL Ch. 5 §8.5, p. 410

At dark as a time of day

Correction Correction proposes changing a particular claim.

Not yet reviewed by the author. This entry was drafted with AI assistance, and its verdict may change once it has been checked.

CGEL’s claim

Bare NPs for times of day follow at, by, before, until and after, but morning, daytime, evening and dark take in + the instead of at.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Proposed wording

note, however, that morning, daytime and evening take in + the instead of at: in the / ∗at morning

Finding and limits

Dark patterns with night and dawn in taking bare at. In the dark is a locative or state expression rather than a time of day. The claim holds for morning, daytime and evening.

Evidence

OED quotations with at dark for nightfall: returned at dark with only five men (D. Haig, diary, 1915); We reached Biela at dark (Daily News, 1877); At dark John Henry came to the back door (C. McCullers, The Member of the Wedding, 1946); at daybreak or at dark (Hunting Annual, 1980).
