CG-0116 · CGEL Ch. 8 §2.1, p. 671

∗Modernly

Open Open means that live alternatives remain.

Not yet reviewed by the author. This entry was drafted with AI assistance, and its verdict may change once it has been checked.

CGEL’s claim

[6iii] We’re furnishing the house in a modern style / the Italian fashion “illustrates the case where there is no adverb at all derived from the adjective (∗modernly, ∗Italianly)”.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Finding and limits

A lead, not yet a finding. If edited prose attests manner modernly, the claim needs qualifying: the paragraph speaks of “natural counterparts”, so the point may be that the adverb is unavailable in ordinary use, but the ∗ says more than that.

Evidence

Wiktionary has modernly, ‘in a modern manner or style’, quoting Anthony Byars, Texas, in Fact (2004): The living room was huge and modernly furnished. That is the manner sense, with the verb of [6iii]. The book itself hasn’t been seen. Wiktionary also lists Italianly, ‘in an Italian manner’, with no quotation.
