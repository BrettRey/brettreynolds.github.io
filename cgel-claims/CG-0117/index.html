CG-0117 · CGEL Ch. 8 §2.1, p. 673

Lettering of the cognate object examples

Production error Production error records a misprint, a wrong cross-reference or a misnumbered example that the authors’ errata do not list.

Not yet reviewed by the author. This entry was drafted with AI assistance, and its verdict may change once it has been checked.

CGEL’s claim

The prose cites the cognate object examples as [i] and [ii]. “Thus [i] means essentially the same as She fought heroically”, and [ii] “likewise describes the manner of his dying”.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Proposed wording

Thus [a] means essentially the same as She fought heroically; [b] likewise describes the manner of his dying

Finding and limits

Change [i] and [ii] to [a] and [b].

Evidence

Example [11] is lettered a and b: [11a] She fought a heroic fight., [11b] He died a long and agonising death.
