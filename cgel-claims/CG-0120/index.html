CG-0120 · CGEL Ch. 8 §2.2, p. 674

Example reference for instruments with agentive subjects

Production error Production error records a misprint, a wrong cross-reference or a misnumbered example that the authors’ errata do not list.

Not yet reviewed by the author. This entry was drafted with AI assistance, and its verdict may change once it has been checked.

CGEL’s claim

“Instruments normally occur in clauses where the subject (in the case of active clauses) has an agent role, as in [ia–d].”

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Proposed wording

as in [iia–d]

Finding and limits

Change [ia–d] to [iia–d]. The following paragraph gives the parallel point for means, “as in [12ie]”.

Evidence

[12ia–d] (p. 673) are the means examples: She travels to work by bus, Sometimes you have to translate a noun in one language by a verb in another, He had gained access to the board by highly dubious means, They communicate by means of sign language. The instruments are [12iia–e], beginning She opened my door with the master-key. The next sentence (p. 674) turns to “the instrumental in [iie]”.
