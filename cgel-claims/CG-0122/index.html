CG-0122 · CGEL Ch. 8 §2.2, p. 674

Means adverb pharmaceutically

Open Open means that live alternatives remain.

Not yet reviewed by the author. This entry was drafted with AI assistance, and its verdict may change once it has been checked.

CGEL’s claim

Realisation of means and instrument by adverbs is “extremely limited”. While treat surgically and communicate electronically are possible, “we could not replace the adverbs here with pharmaceutically or postally”.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Finding and limits

A lead, not yet a finding. Preverbal pharmaceutically treated is attested in medical prose, but the postverbal order CGEL tests wasn’t found, so the claim may hold for treat pharmaceutically. A search of a general corpus for both orders would settle whether it needs qualifying.

Evidence

The audit reader found 23 PubMed title or abstract hits for pharmaceutically treated, including a verbal participle with a means reading: 1 200 769 patients pharmaceutically treated for their first occurrence of depression (Reps, Ryan and Rijnbeek 2021). The same search found no hits for the quoted phrases treated pharmaceutically and treat pharmaceutically. Postally wasn’t searched.
