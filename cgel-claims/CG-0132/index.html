CG-0132 · CGEL Ch. 8 §7.2, p. 703

Paraphrase of [3ib] changes the duration

Production error Production error records a misprint, a wrong cross-reference or a misnumbered example that the authors’ errata do not list.

Not yet reviewed by the author. This entry was drafted with AI assistance, and its verdict may change once it has been checked.

CGEL’s claim

Under the remark “There are in many cases approximate paraphrases in which duration is expressed by an NP in object function”, [3ib] I reached the summit in two hours is paired with [4ib] It took me an hour to reach the summit.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Finding and limits

Low to medium confidence: “approximate” may excuse a loose paraphrase. Align the durations; the text doesn’t show which number was intended.

Evidence

The other three pairs keep their measure: six years ([3ia], [4ia]), all year ([3iia], [4iia]) and two days ([3iib], [4iib]). Ch. 3, p. 122, pairs [18iia] It took him an hour to reach the summit / die with [18iiia] He reached the summit in an hour.
