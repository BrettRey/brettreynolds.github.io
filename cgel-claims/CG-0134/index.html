CG-0134 · CGEL Ch. 8 §7.3, p. 706

Scope label for the duration complement of last

Textual mismatch Textual mismatch records incompatible wording or labels across passages.

Not yet reviewed by the author. This entry was drafted with AI assistance, and its verdict may change once it has been checked.

CGEL’s claim

In [13ib], The strike didn’t last two days, the duration phrase is labelled an adjunct: “[negative has scope over adjunct]”.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Proposed wording

[negative has scope over complement]

Finding and limits

The label contradicts §7.1 and the prose beneath it on p. 706, which calls the phrase the “duration dependent”. Page 665’s definition excludes complements, so the label can’t be read as a cover term. Read “complement”, as in p. 703 [2ii].

Evidence

The prose below the display on p. 706 says “In [i] two days is part of the complementation of last”, and calls it the “duration dependent”. Page 703 [2ii] labels The staff meeting lasted (for) five hours “[complement: S orientation]”. Page 665 defines the term: “the term ‘adjunct’ covers modifiers in the VP or clause together with related supplements”.
