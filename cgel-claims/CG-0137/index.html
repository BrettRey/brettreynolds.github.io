CG-0137 · CGEL Ch. 8 §9, p. 715

Never among bounding frequency adjuncts

Textual mismatch Textual mismatch records incompatible wording or labels across passages.

Not yet reviewed by the author. This entry was drafted with AI assistance, and its verdict may change once it has been checked.

CGEL’s claim

The non-bounding expressions in [9iii] “consist of those that function as bounding frequency adjuncts (other than never) together with a postmodifier that cancels the bounding effect”.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Finding and limits

Medium confidence: the parenthesis puts never among the bounding adjuncts, against [9i] and the inventory on the same page. Page 714’s “except in the special case of one or zero quantity” may explain the motive (never as zero times), but doesn’t remove the conflict.

Evidence

The same page lists never in [9i] under “The form of non-bounding frequency adjuncts”, and its inventory of bounding forms leaves it out: “The class of bounding frequency expressions contains the adverbs once and twice (and archaic thrice), NPs with times as head, PPs consisting of on + NP with occasion as head”. It glosses frequency never as universal, “always doesn’t”.
