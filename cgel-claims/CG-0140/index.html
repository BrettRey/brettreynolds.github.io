CG-0140 · CGEL Ch. 8 §11, p. 725

Wide and narrow scope negation with degree adjuncts

Textual mismatch Textual mismatch records incompatible wording or labels across passages.

Not yet reviewed by the author. This entry was drafted with AI assistance, and its verdict may change once it has been checked.

CGEL’s claim

After setting aside metalinguistic negation, the text says “In what follows we ignore cases of this kind and consider below only narrow scope ordinary negation.”

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Proposed wording

consider below only wide scope ordinary negation

Finding and limits

The text means wide scope negation: the discussion that follows concerns negation with scope over the adjunct, which p. 724 and Ch. 9 call wide scope. Reading the phrase as ordinary negation in which the adjunct has narrow scope doesn’t work, since p. 724 uses the same collocation for the opposite reading.

Evidence

The cases discussed next all have the adjunct inside the scope of the negative; the first is [22] (p. 724), labelled “[adjunct in scope of negation]”. Page 724 uses the term for the opposite configuration: “Nevertheless, narrow scope negation is not entirely excluded”, with [24] labelled “[negation in scope of adjunct]”. Ch. 9 p. 793 fixes the convention: “in [23ii] the negative has narrow scope relative to the adjunct, whereas in [23iii] it has wide scope”, and [23iii] is labelled “[negative has scope over adjunct]”.
