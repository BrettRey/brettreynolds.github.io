CG-0148 · CGEL Ch. 8 §14.1.1, p. 742

Example [12] missing in §14.1.1

Production error Production error records a misprint, a wrong cross-reference or a misnumbered example that the authors’ errata do not list.

Not yet reviewed by the author. This entry was drafted with AI assistance, and its verdict may change once it has been checked.

CGEL’s claim

The displays on p. 742 are numbered [10], [11], [11], [13]: [11] If he proposes – and he will – she’ll probably turn him down, then [11ia] #If I am you I will accept the offer, then [13] If that is Princess Anne, I’m a Dutchman.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Proposed wording

[12]

Finding and limits

Renumber the second [11] as [12].

Evidence

Page 740’s “the don’t-know implicature seen in [11]” means the first [11], and p. 749’s “[13] above” is right, so no reference is misdirected. The duplicate makes p. 740’s “[11]” ambiguous on the printed page.
