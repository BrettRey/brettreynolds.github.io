CG-0149 · CGEL Ch. 8 §14.1.1, p. 743

Footnote 24 and the modal category

Production error Production error records a misprint, a wrong cross-reference or a misnumbered example that the authors’ errata do not list.

Not yet reviewed by the author. This entry was drafted with AI assistance, and its verdict may change once it has been checked.

CGEL’s claim

Footnote 24: “It is possible for a conditional adjunct to have scope over a multiple situation, as in She cycled to work if I remember correctly. The adjunct here, however, belongs to the modal category discussed in §18.”

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Proposed wording

belongs to the modal category discussed in §16

Finding and limits

Change §18 to §16. The category word and the section number disagree; if I remember correctly hedges the speaker’s certainty like if I’m not mistaken, so §16 is the likelier target. Three readers found this independently.

Evidence

§18 (p. 773) is “Speech act-related adjuncts”; modal adjuncts are §16 (p. 767). In §16, p. 771 says “conditional adjuncts too can function in this way”, with [14iia] If I’m not mistaken, that’s a kookaburra over there, and p. 666 [2ib] labels He’s going to buy the house if I’m not mistaken “[modal conditional]”. §18’s conditional if you remember/recall (p. 774) “gives the utterance the force of a reminder”.
