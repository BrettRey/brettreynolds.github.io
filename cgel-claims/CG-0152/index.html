CG-0152 · CGEL Ch. 8 §14.2.1, p. 750

Only and even in remote conditionals

Production error Production error records a misprint, a wrong cross-reference or a misnumbered example that the authors’ errata do not list.

Not yet reviewed by the author. This entry was drafted with AI assistance, and its verdict may change once it has been checked.

CGEL’s claim

Under the heading “Only if and even if”: “The remote vs open contrast is the same in combination with only and if as in the examples given in [33].”

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Proposed wording

in combination with only and even

Finding and limits

Read only and even. Every example contains if, and the sentence as printed leaves out even.

Evidence

[41] has only if in [i] and even if in [ii–iii]. Page 751 goes on: “Even likewise has the same interpretation in the remote versions as in the open ones.”
