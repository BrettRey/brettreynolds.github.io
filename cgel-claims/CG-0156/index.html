CG-0156 · CGEL Ch. 8 §14.2.2, p. 754

Irrealis protasis and the open reading

Qualification Qualification narrows its scope or evidential force.

Not yet reviewed by the author. This entry was drafted with AI assistance, and its verdict may change once it has been checked.

CGEL’s claim

Of [49] If we weren’t home by ten o’clock the landlady would lock us out, CGEL says that “an irrealis protasis such as if I weren’t home by ten o’clock permits only the remote reading.”

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Proposed wording

for most speakers an irrealis protasis such as if I weren’t home by ten o’clock permits only the remote reading (cf. Ch. 3, §1.7)

Finding and limits

Medium confidence. For speakers who accept [33iii], an irrealis protasis allows the open past-time reading that p. 754 excludes. The defence that p. 754 describes the majority grammar is true, but the unhedged only needs a hedge such as Ch. 3’s for most speakers.

Evidence

Ch. 3, p. 87 [33iii] is %If he were surprised, he didn’t show it [past time]: “Example [iii] is a conditional, but of the open type, not the remote”. The same page restricts the use to some speakers (“For some speakers, irrealis were is not restricted to the modal remoteness constructions”) and says that was is “for most speakers probably the only possibility”.
