CG-0157 · CGEL Ch. 8 §14.3, p. 756

Status of yet in a positive protasis

Textual mismatch Textual mismatch records incompatible wording or labels across passages.

Not yet reviewed by the author. This entry was drafted with AI assistance, and its verdict may change once it has been checked.

CGEL’s claim

Explaining why unless can’t replace if not in [55iii] I’m cancelling the order if the goods aren’t ready yet, CGEL says that yet “is not compatible with the other component” of an unless conditional, and stars the positive version: “∗If the goods are ready yet I’m not cancelling the order.”

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Finding and limits

Medium-low confidence. Either mark the p. 756 example ? or say why yet is worse here than at all in [24]. The defence that yet is a stronger negative polarity item than at all (Ch. 9, p. 823, “Varying strength of the restrictions”) partly works.

Evidence

Page 745 accepts yet in a positive if protasis without a mark: [23ib] I’ll be surprised if they are here yet. It marks comparable clashes of orientation with ?, not ∗: [24ii] ?If you were at all worried about the project, why didn’t you let me know?, where at all “is here infelicitous”, and [24iii] ?If you improve your performance at all, we give you a bonus. Ch. 9, p. 838: “A clause embedded as complement of if generally allows NPIs”.
