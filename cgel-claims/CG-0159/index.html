CG-0159 · CGEL Ch. 8 §14.4, p. 758

Supposing as verb and preposition

Textual mismatch Textual mismatch records incompatible wording or labels across passages.

Not yet reviewed by the author. This entry was drafted with AI assistance, and its verdict may change once it has been checked.

CGEL’s claim

Of the items in [61] that can replace if: “Assuming and supposing are verbs heading gerund-participial clauses.” The example is [62v], supposing (that) one were to waste money in that way.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Finding and limits

Medium-high confidence, for supposing only: Ch. 11 doesn’t list assuming. Ch. 7’s treatment of subjectless verbal adjuncts whose understood subject is the speaker ([23], p. 611) could support the verb analysis of assuming, but it doesn’t reconcile Ch. 8 with Ch. 11’s listing of supposing. Ch. 7’s list also omits two items that Ch. 11 lists.

Evidence

Ch. 11, p. 971 [58] lists supposing among the “Prepositions governing content clauses of this kind”, the expandable type that allows that, which is the construction of [62v]. Ch. 7, p. 611’s list of deverbal prepositions taking content clause complements (“given, granted, provided, providing, seeing”) omits supposing and considering.
