CG-0162 · CGEL Ch. 8 §14.6.2, p. 762

Polar and alternative questions: Ch. 10, §§4.3–5

Production error Production error records a misprint, a wrong cross-reference or a misnumbered example that the authors’ errata do not list.

Not yet reviewed by the author. This entry was drafted with AI assistance, and its verdict may change once it has been checked.

CGEL’s claim

Closed interrogatives “characteristically express questions that can be distinguished as polar or alternative on the basis of how they define the set of answers (cf. Ch. 10, §§4.3–5).”

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Proposed wording

(cf. Ch. 10, §§4.3–4)

Finding and limits

Low confidence. The reference runs one section too far: change §§4.3–5 to §§4.3–4, as in Ch. 11.

Evidence

Ch. 10, §4.3 (p. 868) is “Polar questions” and §4.4 (p. 868) “Alternative questions”. §4.5 (p. 872) is “Variable questions”, which isn’t about closed interrogatives. Ch. 11, p. 989 makes the same point with “see Ch. 10, §§4.3–4”.
