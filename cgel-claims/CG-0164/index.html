CG-0164 · CGEL Ch. 8 §17, p. 772

∗ and # for presupposed information

Textual mismatch Textual mismatch records incompatible wording or labels across passages.

Not yet reviewed by the author. This entry was drafted with AI assistance, and its verdict may change once it has been checked.

CGEL’s claim

Evaluative adjuncts “do not, therefore, occur in interrogatives, imperatives, or pragmatically presupposed subordinate clauses”, with [5iii] ∗Since Deidre fortunately recovered from her illness, she has lived in California, where since is temporal.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Finding and limits

Low confidence. Both restrictions rest on the difference between asserted and presupposed information, and p. 772 explains its restriction in pragmatic terms, so by CGEL’s own conventions [5iii] fits # better than ∗. The defence that CGEL treats the evaluative restriction as grammaticalised, since it is stated alongside interrogative and imperative clause types, partly works.

Evidence

§19, p. 778 explains a restriction on connective adjuncts in the same terms but marks it #: “The felicitous use of these connectives requires that the similarity be between propositions that are informationally in the foreground, not presupposed”, with [12ii] #Kim has stopped smoking and Pat used to smoke too. On p. 702 a #-marked example “certainly does not violate any rule of grammar”.
