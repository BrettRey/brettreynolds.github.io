CG-0165 · CGEL Ch. 8 §18, p. 775

Modal adjuncts in questions

Textual mismatch Textual mismatch records incompatible wording or labels across passages.

Not yet reviewed by the author. This entry was drafted with AI assistance, and its verdict may change once it has been checked.

CGEL’s claim

“adjuncts relating to felicity conditions occur readily in questions, which modal adjuncts do not.”

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Finding and limits

Medium confidence. The defence that p. 775 means modal adjuncts seldom fall inside a question’s scope (p. 770, “modality is more readily questioned by a predication than a modification construction”) partly works. But the felicity adjuncts of [5ii–iii] are outside the question’s scope too, and p. 771 still says such examples “can readily be questioned”.

Evidence

§16, p. 771: “Will he probably die? seems quite reasonable, and Won’t he probably die? is fully acceptable”, and examples with modal adjuncts like [11] “can readily be questioned”. Page 770 [12iib] Was he perhaps poisoned? is unmarked, with perhaps “likely to be construed as outside the scope of the question”. Ch. 3, p. 208: “The subjectivity of perhaps is reflected in its occurrence in interrogatives outside the scope of the question”.
