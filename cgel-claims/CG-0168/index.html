CG-0168 · CGEL Ch. 8 §20.2, p. 784

Leftward incongruent adjuncts

Open Open means that live alternatives remain.

Not yet reviewed by the author. This entry was drafted with AI assistance, and its verdict may change once it has been checked.

CGEL’s claim

Adjuncts placed to the left of their expected position, as in [14iib] The party will long be remembered, “are found with a very restricted range of adjuncts: compare, for example, The supply will be drastically reduced with ∗The supply will drastically be reduced.”

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Finding and limits

Unresolved. These examples count against the claim only if the adverb belongs semantically with the participle, which is the reader’s analysis, not CGEL’s, and most have a reading with scope over the be clause. Readily and optionally are the likeliest cases, and a corpus search for manner adverbs in this slot (will carefully be + past participle) would decide.

Evidence

CGEL’s own prose puts a range of ·ly adverbs before passive be: some can readily be used (p. 366), bare NPs can optionally be used (p. 410), can usefully be subdivided (p. 425), would traditionally be treated (p. 600), has subsequently been changed (p. 701). Ch. 6’s ?-marked manner adjuncts before an auxiliary (pp. 575, 578) point the same way.
