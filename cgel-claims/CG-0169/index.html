CG-0169 · CGEL Ch. 8, various sections, pp. 665–774

Misprints in Chapter 8

Production error Production error records a misprint, a wrong cross-reference or a misnumbered example that the authors’ errata do not list.

Not yet reviewed by the author. This entry was drafted with AI assistance, and its verdict may change once it has been checked.

CGEL’s claim

Misprints not in the authors’ errata, each confirmed by a reader on the page images of the corrected printing.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Proposed wording

§§4–5; Did he return fortunately or surprisingly?; He wrote it by hand; faecium or [sic]; in(to) the, on(to) the; add full stops on p. 701 and to [26iv]; participial or verbless; concessive; personal TV set; breakdown

Finding and limits

Candidates for the authors’ errata page. Faicium is inside quotation marks in the example and may reproduce its source, so a [sic] may be the better fix there.

Evidence

p. 665 [1vi–viii] “[source: §4–5]” and the like, against [1v] “§§4–5”; p. 667 [3iib], an alternative question, “∗Did he return fortunately or surprisingly.”; p. 674 “In he wrote it by hand”, with the example in lower case; p. 679 [12ii] “Enterococcus faicium”; p. 686 “in(to)the room” and “on(to)the platform”, with no space after the bracket; p. 701 [31i] I left home before my parents divorced with no final stop; p. 701 “see Ch. 17, §10.2” with no final stop; p. 731 [26ii] “activities,]they” with no space after the bracket, and [26iv] with no final stop; p. 736 “a participial and verbless clause”, where or is meant; p. 737 “concessional adjuncts”, the book’s only use of concessional, where it elsewhere has concessive; p. 760 “a personal TV” for [70]’s “a personal TV set”; p. 774 “nervous break-down” for [3iv] breakdown.
