CG-0170 · CGEL Ch. 4 §5.2, p. 258

Ch. 4’s reference to spatial location in Ch. 8

Production error Production error records a misprint, a wrong cross-reference or a misnumbered example that the authors’ errata do not list.

Not yet reviewed by the author. This entry was drafted with AI assistance, and its verdict may change once it has been checked.

CGEL’s claim

“for further discussion of spatial location and extension of the roles to the dimension of time, see Ch. 8, §§5–6”.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Proposed wording

see Ch. 8, §§4–6

Finding and limits

Change §§5–6 to §§4–6. References into Ch. 8, §4 also point one place too high at Ch. 4 p. 305 (CG-0171) and Ch. 8 p. 665 [1ix]. That fits a late renumbering of §4, but doesn’t establish one.

Evidence

Ch. 8, §4 (p. 679) is “Location and change of location in space”, §5 (p. 690) is “Spatial extent and scalar change”, and §6 (p. 694) is “Temporal location”.
