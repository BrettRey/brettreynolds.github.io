CG-0171 · CGEL Ch. 4 §8.1.3, p. 305

The way construction: Ch. 8, §4.4

Production error Production error records a misprint, a wrong cross-reference or a misnumbered example that the authors’ errata do not list.

Not yet reviewed by the author. This entry was drafted with AI assistance, and its verdict may change once it has been checked.

CGEL’s claim

Push, shove and similar verbs “allow a way object before a goal complement”, and the example is followed by “(cf. Ch. 8, §4.4)”.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Proposed wording

(cf. Ch. 8, §4.3)

Finding and limits

Change §4.4 to §4.3. Like Ch. 4 p. 258 (CG-0170) and Ch. 8 p. 665 [1ix], this reference into Ch. 8, §4 points one place too high.

Evidence

The way construction is in §4.3, p. 685, under its own heading: [23] We made our way to the station, Jill had to elbow her way to the exit. §4.4 (p. 689) is “Metaphorical extension of the locative categories to other domains”.
