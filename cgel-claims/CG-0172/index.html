CG-0172 · CGEL Ch. 6 §7.3.2, p. 594

Even though and the reference to Ch. 8, §14.1.3

Production error Production error records a misprint, a wrong cross-reference or a misnumbered example that the authors’ errata do not list.

Not yet reviewed by the author. This entry was drafted with AI assistance, and its verdict may change once it has been checked.

CGEL’s claim

After [64iv], where even modifies the pro-form so: “For discussion of even though and even if, see Ch. 8, §14.1.3.”

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Proposed wording

For discussion of even though and even if, see Ch. 8, §§13.2–13.3 and 14.1.3.

Finding and limits

Low confidence. The reference is right for even if, but even though is treated in Ch. 8, §13, which the reference should add.

Evidence

Ch. 8, §14.1.3 treats even if (p. 747). Even though is treated in §13.2, p. 736 ([8] He knew they were there even though he couldn’t see them), and §13.3, p. 737, which contrasts I’m going out, even if it rains with I’m going out, even though it’s going to rain.
