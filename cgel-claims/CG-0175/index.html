CG-0175 · CGEL Ch. 14 §9, p. 1266

Ch. 14’s reference to exhaustive conditionals

Production error Production error records a misprint, a wrong cross-reference or a misnumbered example that the authors’ errata do not list.

Not yet reviewed by the author. This entry was drafted with AI assistance, and its verdict may change once it has been checked.

CGEL’s claim

Of [4iii] Whether working or relaxing, he always has a scowl on his face: “Example [iii] belongs to the exhaustive conditional construction discussed in Ch. 11, §5.3.5.”

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Proposed wording

discussed in Ch. 11, §5.3.6

Finding and limits

Change §5.3.5 to §5.3.6.

Evidence

Ch. 11, §5.3.5 (p. 985) is “Infinitival interrogatives”. §5.3.6, on the same page, is “Interrogatives as adjunct: the ungoverned exhaustive conditional construction”.
