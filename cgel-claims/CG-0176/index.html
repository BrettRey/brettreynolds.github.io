CG-0176 · CGEL Ch. 11, various sections, pp. 971–1205

Missing brackets in Chapters 11 and 14

Production error Production error records a misprint, a wrong cross-reference or a misnumbered example that the authors’ errata do not list.

Not yet reviewed by the author. This entry was drafted with AI assistance, and its verdict may change once it has been checked.

CGEL’s claim

Three misprints outside Chapter 8 not in the authors’ errata, confirmed on the page images.

Paraphrase of the printed text; quotation marks mark short excerpts.

Proposed wording

I’ll come along, [provided (that) I can leave early].; We’ll invite them both [though I don’t think he’ll come].; [17]

Finding and limits

Candidates for the authors’ errata page.

Evidence

Ch. 11, p. 971 [56iia] “I’ll come along, [provided (that) I can leave early.” has no closing bracket, and [56ib] “We’ll invite them both [though I don’t think he’ll come]” has no full stop. Ch. 14, p. 1205 prints the display number as “[17” with no closing bracket.
