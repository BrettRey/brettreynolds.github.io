# CG-0002: Dummy there as an NP

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 5 §1, p. 328
- Verdict: Supported
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0002/

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

CGEL includes dummy *there* on distributional grounds.

## Finding and limits

Tag behaviour independently supports the pronoun analysis, despite limited distribution.

## Evidence

*There* occupies the subject slot and can itself appear as the subject of a question tag.
