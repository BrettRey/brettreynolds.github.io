# CG-0003: Full NPs before the head

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 5 §2, p. 329
- Verdict: Textual mismatch
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0003/

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

The overview excludes full NPs as pre-head dependents in its discussion of nominal modifiers.

## Finding and limits

Qualify the generalization for genitive attributes. The plain-case institutional examples still distinguish nominal from NP.

## Evidence

CGEL gives full NP status to measure genitives and to *all girls’* in an *all girls’ school*.

- Audit note: Chapter 5 systematic audit (not online)
  - passage: §2, p. 329. The overview excludes full NPs as pre-head dependents in its discussion of nominal modifiers.

  - establishes: The chapter itself supplies a pre-head NP outside the plain-case pattern. The *Ministry of Defence* contrast can retain its narrower use.
