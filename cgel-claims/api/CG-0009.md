# CG-0009: Measure respecification

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 5 §3.4, p. 354
- Verdict: Supported
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0009/

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

*Another* can combine with a plural head even where singular respecification is unavailable.

## Finding and limits

The lexical distribution of *another* and singular measure agreement make different predictions.

## Evidence

*Another three days* allows a singular time-span construal. *Another three bodies have been discovered* retains plural agreement.

- Audit note: Chapter 5 systematic audit (not online)
  - passage: §3.4, p. 354. *Another* can combine with a plural head even where singular respecification is unavailable.
  - example: Compare *another three days* with *another three bodies have been discovered* (p. 354 [74]). A time span can be conceived as one interval. Plural agreement is required whether the discoveries were simultaneous or separate.
  - establishes: A plural after *another* cannot always be explained by a singular measure reading. The two diagnostics apply to different phenomena.
