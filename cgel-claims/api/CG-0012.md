# CG-0012: Noun as head of an ordinary NP

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 5 §4, p. 357
- Verdict: Supported
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0012/

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

The grammar argues for N-headedness from semantic selection and the distribution of bare NPs.

## Finding and limits

The distributional argument survives a challenge to selection as a sufficient head diagnostic.

## Evidence

*Kim*, *money* and *women* can each constitute an NP without an overt determiner.

- Audit note: Chapter 5 systematic audit (not online)
