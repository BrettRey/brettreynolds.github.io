# CG-0014: Exact and lower-bound numerals

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 5 §5.2, p. 363
- Verdict: Open
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0014/

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

For *four children* the lower bound is entailed and the upper bound implicated.

## Finding and limits

Either local strengthening or an exact analysis with pragmatic weakening needs consideration. The dinner example alone cannot choose.

## Evidence

Fourteen chairs can suffice for dinner. A four-child fee band can also exclude a five-child household under negation.
