# CG-0018: Internal expansion of a few

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 5 §7.10, p. 392
- Verdict: Qualification
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0018/

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

A short list of internal expansions helps motivate lexical treatment of *a few*.

## Finding and limits
