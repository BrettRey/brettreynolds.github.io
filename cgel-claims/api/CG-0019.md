# CG-0019: Dependent scope across students

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 5 §8.3, p. 405
- Verdict: Open
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0019/

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

The dependent reading of *two languages* requires at least three candidate languages.

## Finding and limits

Coincidence rebuts a requirement for distinct realized pairs, which CGEL does not impose here. Opening a third course could distinguish two policies. This leaves open whether the original sentence admits both readings in the two-language setting.

## Evidence

Suppose all students independently choose Welsh and German from five languages. That meets CGEL’s candidate-domain condition. If only those two are offered, assigning two to each student and assigning the same two to everyone have identical outcomes.
