# CG-0020: Dependent scope across occasions

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 5 §8.3, p. 406
- Verdict: Correction
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0020/

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

The non-referential lunch-colleague reading requires variation across occasions.

## Proposed wording

A partner can be determined afresh on each occasion even when every assignment selects the same colleague. Variation across lunches makes this reading easier to distinguish from fixed reference.

## Finding and limits

Changing a draw would have changed my partner. A standing appointment with Jo would have survived that change. The procedure establishes dependence despite identical outcomes. Even in that history the speaker could mean Jo specifically.

## Evidence

In a completed twelve-Friday placement I usually had lunch with a colleague. I ate alone twice. On the other ten Fridays an independent draw assigned the partner, I followed the draw, and all ten draws selected Jo. Constructed.
