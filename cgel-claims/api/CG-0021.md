# CG-0021: Group identity over time

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 5 §8.3, p. 406
- Verdict: Open
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0021/

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

*The police are getting younger* is explained through different sets of people at different times.

## Finding and limits

A theory of what the NP picks out is needed to choose between an institutional and a member-set analysis.

## Evidence

A stable force could have a falling mean age as membership changes.

- Audit note: Chapter 5 systematic audit (not online)
  - passage: §8.3, p. 406. *The police are getting younger* is explained through different sets of people at different times.
  - example: Compare a police force whose older members retire and whose younger recruits replace them. The sets of people compared change even if speakers identify the same continuing institution at every date.
  - establishes: Both descriptions can be true in the same history. A model of the referent is needed to distinguish readings of *The police are getting younger*.
  - locator: The difference between possible variation and actual variation
