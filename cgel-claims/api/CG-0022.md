# CG-0022: Exceptions to generic predication

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 5 §8.3, p. 407
- Verdict: Qualification
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0022/

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

Generic *a lion* is glossed using *any lion that exists*.

## Finding and limits

An unrestricted domain does not require that the predicate hold of every member. The book gives generic a restricted technical use.

## Evidence

CGEL’s own generic about Italians and pasta tolerates individual exceptions.

- Audit note: Chapter 5 systematic audit (not online)
  - passage: §8.3, p. 407. Generic *a lion* is glossed using *any lion that exists*.
  - example: Compare the *any-lion* gloss with CGEL’s own generalization about Italians and pasta. An Italian who dislikes pasta can belong to the intended population without making the generic unusable.
  - establishes: Including every individual in the domain leaves open whether the property must hold of every individual. The chapter’s restricted technical sense of generic matters here.
