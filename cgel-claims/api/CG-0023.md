# CG-0023: Weak definite NPs

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 5 §8.4, p. 408
- Verdict: Extension
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0023/

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

The grammar records activity readings with *the bus* and similar expressions.

## Finding and limits

These tests extend the account of when the activity reading is available.

## Evidence

Under VP ellipsis two people may go to different hospitals. Keeping the noun constant and varying its governor affects the weak reading.
