# CG-0024: Definiteness of bare role NPs

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 5 §8.5, p. 409
- Verdict: Qualification
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0024/

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

CGEL infers from the definiteness of the office that bare role NPs invariably allow substitution of a corresponding NP with *the*.

## Finding and limits

The bare form readily assigns the office to both players. The definite counterpart invites a single-holder reading, though a salient office may sustain a shared-role reading. The attestation establishes shared tenure. Preservation of the reading under insertion is a judgment.

## Evidence

ASU reports a shared appointment. Hold team, meeting and date fixed. Compare “*Faatulu became co-captain of the team that day, and so did Peck*” with “*Faatulu became the co-captain of the team that day, and so did Peck*”. Both versions are constructed.

- Attestation: Arizona State University, Sun Devil Athletics. Jolynn Faatulu Overcomes Injuries to Shine. <https://thesundevils.com/jolynn-faatulu-overcomes-injuries-to-shine>
  - passage: §8.5(a), p. 409, discussion of [19i–iii]. The office is “invariably definite”. The inference to substitution is signalled by “therefore invariably replaceable”. The examples concern the office of treasurer.
  - example: The report says Faatulu “*was named co-captain along with fellow junior Jamie Peck*”. It never supplies a definite counterpart in this context.
  - establishes: Attests a bare role with two officeholders. The two versions with *became* in the audit are constructed comparative judgments.
  - locator: Paragraph naming fellow junior Jamie Peck.
