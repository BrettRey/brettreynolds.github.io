# CG-0027: Ranking and fused adjectives

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 5 §9.3, p. 416
- Verdict: Supported
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0027/

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

CGEL limits which adjectives license the fused construction.

## Finding and limits

The inventory needs a lexical restriction alongside the semantic tendency.

## Evidence

*Main* has a ranking interpretation yet fails to license the corresponding fused use.

- Audit note: Chapter 5 systematic audit (not online)
  - passage: §9.3, p. 416. CGEL limits which adjectives license the fused construction.
  - example: Compare admissible ranking adjectives in §9.3 with *main*. A speaker who understands which item is principal still cannot freely use *the main* in the same fused use.
  - establishes: CGEL explicitly records this lexical restriction. The semantics predicts a tendency and the inventory supplies an independent control.
