# CG-0029: Wordhood of us both

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 5 §10.1, p. 427
- Verdict: Open
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0029/

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

Adjacency and substitution support the proposed universal personal-pronoun analysis.

## Finding and limits

Evidence for one NP leaves wordhood open. First-, second- and third-person forms need separate controls.

## Evidence

*To us both* behaves as one constituent. Compare *outside of us two* and *you two*.

- Audit note: Nouns and noun phrases notebook (not online)
  - passage: §10.1, p. 427. Adjacency and substitution support the proposed universal personal-pronoun analysis.
  - example: In *to us both* the two words can form a single complement of *to*. The personal forms also combine with *two* in independently documented phrases (pp. 639, 1466). More than one word can occupy that same constituent position.
  - establishes: Constituency follows more readily than single-word status. The distributions of *us*, *you* and *them* require separate controls.
