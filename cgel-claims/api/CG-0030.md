# CG-0030: Sufficiency of excluding determiners

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 5 §10.1.4, p. 429
- Verdict: Argument gap
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0030/

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

CGEL classifies the four deictic temporal nouns as pronouns on the strength of their inability to take determiners. It then adduces their deictic meaning.

## Finding and limits

Exclusion is shared by noun uses on opposite sides of the proposed boundary. Its presence alone cannot identify a pronoun. Deixis remains positive evidence of resemblance, and the genitive contrast bears on the comparison with prepositions.

## Evidence

Compare *today* with quantificational *plenty* in *plenty of time*. CGEL says this use of *plenty* permits no determiner or modifier (p. 350), yet treats it as nominal outside the pronoun subclass.
