# CG-0031: Necessity of excluding determiners

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 5 §10.2, p. 429
- Verdict: Open
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0031/

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

Excluding determiners is given as a defining property of pronouns.

## Finding and limits

Seek independent evidence for conversion before deciding whether determiner-taking defeats pronoun status.

## Evidence

*The real me* retains person deixis and takes third-person agreement. CGEL treats *a he* or *a she* as common-noun uses.
