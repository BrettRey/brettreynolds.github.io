# CG-0035: Bracketing of all three proposals

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 5 §12, p. 434
- Verdict: Open
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0035/

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

*All* is analysed as determiner because adding it makes the proposals identifiable.

## Finding and limits

Identifiability cannot alone settle attachment. CGEL’s analogy with *all proposals* remains structural evidence.

## Evidence

External *all* is independently available in *all the three proposals* and *all you who …* . Either bracketing can identify the set.
