# CG-0036: Scope and attachment of possibly

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 5 §13, p. 436
- Verdict: Supported
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0036/

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

*Possibly* may attach to the subject NP or to the clause.

## Finding and limits

Constituency evidence supports the interpretation contrast.

## Evidence

Passivisation separates clause-initial *possibly* from *by possibly the best actress*.
