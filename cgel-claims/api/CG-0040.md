# CG-0040: Postpositive possible and available

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 5 §14.2, p. 445
- Verdict: Correction
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0040/

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

Postpositive adjectives in this class are said to require a superlative or *only*.

## Finding and limits

Qualify the licensing condition by adjective and construction.

## Evidence

CGEL gives *the money available elsewhere*. The Congressional Record supplies *every effort possible*.

- Attestation: U.S. Senate, Congressional Record (24 April 1998). S. Res. 216. <https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CREC-1998-04-24/pdf/CREC-1998-04-24.pdf#page=33>
  - passage: §14.2, p. 445, after [12i]. CGEL requires “an attributive superlative or *only*” for postpositive adjectives of the class exemplified by *suitable* and *possible*. Compare *the money available* on p. 57.
  - example: The resolution uses *every effort possible* with neither *only* nor an attributive superlative.
  - establishes: A pre-2002 counterexample for *possible* after *every*. Different adjectives and postpositive constructions may have different licensing conditions.
  - locator: P. S3593, right column, resolved clause (3). PDF p. 33.
