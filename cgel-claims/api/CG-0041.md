# CG-0041: Entailment as a test for apposition

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 5 §14.3, p. 447
- Verdict: Argument gap
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0041/

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

Replacing a containing NP with its appositive is supposed systematically to preserve entailment.

## Finding and limits

Restrict the test to suitable identifying uses. CGEL already admits name-bearing readings with indefinite matrix NPs.

## Evidence

In *We found no city of Manchester in the gazetteer*, replacement by *Manchester* loses the quantifier.

- Audit note: Chapter 5 systematic audit (not online)
  - passage: §14.3, p. 447. Replacing a containing NP with its appositive is supposed systematically to preserve entailment.
  - example: *We found no city of Manchester in the gazetteer* can be true when the gazetteer contains no city bearing that name. Substitution of *Manchester* for the entire object produces *We found Manchester in the gazetteer*, which can then be false.
  - establishes: The loss of *no* breaks the entailment. The test remains available for definite identifying NPs. CGEL explicitly includes an indefinite *city of Manchester* on p. 448. The audit extends that name-bearing use to a negative quantifier.
