# CG-0042: Heads of marked appositives

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 5 §14.3, p. 448
- Verdict: Correction
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0042/

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

Heads before appositive *of* are limited to *month* and politico-geographical terms.

## Finding and limits

Extend the inventory to *festival* in this identifying use. *A festival of music* has a different relation.

## Evidence

The Foreign Office names *the Hindu festival of Holi*. *Holi* identifies the festival being celebrated.

- Attestation: UK Foreign & Commonwealth Office (13 March 2017). Alok Sharma extends his wishes for a happy Holi festival. <https://www.gov.uk/government/news/alok-sharma-extends-his-wishes-for-a-happy-holi-festival>
  - passage: §14.3, p. 448, [21]. CGEL restricts heads in the identifying *of* construction to *month* and names of politico-geographical categories. The construction specifies which entity bears the name.
  - example: The account identifies the occasion celebrated as *the Hindu festival of Holi*. Compare *a festival of music*, where music supplies subject matter.
  - establishes: *Festival* admits an identifying use outside CGEL’s stated head inventory.
  - locator: Second body paragraph after the photograph, first sentence.
