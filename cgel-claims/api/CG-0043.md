# CG-0043: Coordination as a diagnostic of wordhood

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 5 §14.4, p. 449
- Verdict: Argument gap
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0043/

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

Passing any one of the coordination or modification tests suffices for a composite nominal.

## Finding and limits

Successful coordination alone cannot choose between base coordination and syntactic reanalysis. A compound of another type is a calibration case.

## Evidence

CGEL admits coordinated bases inside compounds and allows *washing-machine* to pass by reanalysis.

- Attestation: U.S. Census Bureau (May 2008). Changes in the Relationship Categories Between 2007 & 2008. <https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/technical-documentation/user-notes/2008-05.html>
