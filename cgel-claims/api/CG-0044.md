# CG-0044: Adjacency of a pre-head complement

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 5 §15, p. 452
- Verdict: Qualification
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0044/

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

Alleged pre-head complements are expected immediately adjacent to the head.

## Finding and limits

Reassess the analysis of these attributes. Extending the result beyond the studied heads needs evidence.

## Evidence

Payne’s corpus studies of *student* and *advisor* challenge the ordering prediction for their putative complements.
