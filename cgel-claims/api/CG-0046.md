# CG-0046: Spatial measure genitives

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 5 §16.3, p. 470
- Verdict: Correction
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0046/

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

Measure genitives are restricted to duration and value. *A mile’s walk* is starred.

## Finding and limits

Spatial length is available in precisely the genitive starred on p. 470. This 2024 decision documents usage at that date. The separate proposal about weight in *a pound’s carrots* still needs testing.

## Evidence

A planning inspector evaluates an 800-metre definition of *walkable* and says *a mile’s walk* would be twice that distance. Holding the unit of comparison fixed rules out an interpretation in minutes.
