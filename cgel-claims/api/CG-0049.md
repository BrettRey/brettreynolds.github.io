# CG-0049: Participants introduced by nominal by

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 5 §16.5.3, p. 476
- Verdict: Correction
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0049/

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

CGEL limits nominal *by*-obliques to agents and uses that limit in its argument against voice in NPs.

## Proposed wording

The nominal head constrains which participant can be expressed in a *by*-phrase. Attested possibilities include the agent of a conquest, the recipient in a receipt event, the experiencer of a perception, and the holder of knowledge.

## Finding and limits

The examples defeat the agent-only premise in reason (c). Reasons (a) and (b) still stand: nominal forms lack the active/passive contrast, and a single nominal can express both participants as obliques. These data alone do not decide nominal voice.

## Evidence

Bruening’s receipt example makes Gift Processing the recipient of a form. In the advance-knowledge example the investors are the people who knew about the attacks. The latter state can hold without the investors doing anything. Neither *by*-phrase gives a place, instrument or time.
