# CG-0053: Distributivity of a singular predicative

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 5 §19.2, p. 513
- Verdict: Correction
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0053/

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

In §19.2, with semantically plural predicands in ascriptive constructions lacking explicit quantification, rule [12] assigns non-distributive interpretation to singular predicatives.

## Proposed wording

With a semantically plural predicand, a singular predicative may attribute a property to the group or to each member. In the latter case each member may satisfy the description with a different value.

## Finding and limits

Suitability applies separately to each pot although the predicative NP is singular and their sizes differ. A single shared value is possible but unnecessary. The argument against general grammatical number concord survives.

## Evidence

“*Coin envelopes are a good size for most seeds*” describes the individual envelopes. Give a seedling a 10-cm pot and a tree a 40-cm pot. “*The pots are a suitable size for their respective plants*” can be true. In that context the unmarked “*These pots are a suitable size*” can also be true, then false after swapping the plants.

- Attestation: Mikaela Boley (2020). Collecting Native Seed. University of Maryland Extension. <https://extension.umd.edu/sites/extension.umd.edu/files/2021-02/Native%20Plant%20Seed%20Saving%20Resources.pdf#page=9>
  - passage: §19.2, p. 513, [12], following [11i–ii]. The rule assigns plural forms distributively and “singular forms non-distributively”. Its domain excludes specifying predicatives and explicit quantification. The quantified cases are taken up on p. 515.
  - example: “Coin envelopes are a good size for most seeds.” The advice concerns which individual envelopes can hold the material. The dimensions of a stack would not answer that question.
  - establishes: A singular predicative is applicable to individual bearers. The different-sized pots in the audit test the further suggestion that those bearers have to share one value.
  - locator: Handout p. 9, Taking Care of Collected Seed, first bullet.
