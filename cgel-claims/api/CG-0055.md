# CG-0055: The article inside a proper name

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 5 §20.2.1, p. 517
- Verdict: Qualification
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0055/

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

Definite reference is taken to exclude an indefinite determiner in a proper name.

## Finding and limits

Distinguish a fixed article inside a conventional name from productive external determination.

## Evidence

*A Flock of Seagulls* names a single band and occurs as subject in BMG’s prose.

- Attestation: BMG (12 March 2026). BMG Acquires Publishing & Recording Catalog of A Flock of Seagulls. <https://www.bmg.com/news/bmg-acquires-publishing-and-recording-catalog-of-a-flock-of-seagulls>
  - passage: §20.2.1, p. 517. Definite reference is taken to exclude an indefinite determiner in a proper name.
  - example: BMG uses the full band name *A Flock of Seagulls* in subject position, referring to the one band.
  - establishes: An indefinite article is fixed inside this conventional name. This leaves open what external determination it allows.
