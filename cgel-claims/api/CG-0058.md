# CG-0058: Degree modification and lexical conversion

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 19 §3.3(b), p. 1643
- Verdict: Argument gap
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0058/

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

CGEL treats gradability and compatibility with *very* and *too* as evidence that a noun has acquired adjectival status.

## Proposed wording

Compatibility with *very* or *too* is evidence for a gradable use. Use independent morphological or syntactic evidence to establish whether a lexical adjective has developed.

## Finding and limits

Degree modification can diagnose a gradable use. Independent tests are needed to establish conversion of a lexical item. CGEL’s *a very Oxbridge accent* adds distributional evidence for *Oxbridge*.

## Evidence

Bylinina accepts *very McDonalds* as a predicative but rejects *these very McDonalds french fries*. Gonzálvez-García documents nominal grading with several intensifiers.
