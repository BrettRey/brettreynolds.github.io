# CG-0059: NP modifiers of adjectives

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 19 §4.1, p. 1644
- Verdict: Textual mismatch
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0059/

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

In the discussion of *tax-exempt*, Chapter 19 excludes a contrasting syntactic construction in which a noun or NP modifies an adjective.

## Proposed wording

*Tax-exempt* is a compound adjective. A limited range of NPs can also modify adjectives in phrases such as *two inches long* and *a bit lax*.

## Finding and limits

The general wording in §4.1 conflicts with CGEL’s own phrasal cases. *Tax-exempt* can retain its compound analysis.

## Evidence

Chapter 6 identifies the NPs in *two inches long*, *a foot wide* and *a bit lax* as modifiers of adjectives. Chapter 19 later acknowledges the measure phrase and contrasts it with compound forms.
