# CG-0061: Modified personal pronouns and person agreement

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 5 §10.2, p. 429
- Verdict: Qualification
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0061/

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

CGEL defines pronouns by their exclusion of determiners and treats *a he* and *a she* as common-noun uses.

## Proposed wording

Ordinary personal pronoun NPs exclude determiners. Restricted modified patterns such as *the younger me* allow an article and can preserve the pronoun’s person features for reflexives.

## Finding and limits

An article-marked NP can show third-person verbal agreement and retain pronoun-specific person information in coreference. A converted-noun analysis needs to explain that split. The distribution in 2002 is open.

## Evidence

Halm finds *a younger me* with *myself* as the object of *ask*. The *five-year-old you* governs *was*. He also reports first-person plural *ourselves* with the *younger us*.
