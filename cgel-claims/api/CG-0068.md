# CG-0068: Body-part the with a passive subject

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 5 §6.1, p. 370
- Verdict: Qualification
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0068/
- Review: not yet reviewed by the author (drafted with AI assistance)

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

In *She grabbed me by the arm*, the body-part NP and the NP associated with it must be oblique and direct complements of a single verb.

## Proposed wording

the body-part NP is oblique complement and the associated NP is direct complement (or the corresponding passive subject) of a single verb

## Finding and limits

Extend the condition to a passive subject corresponding to the active object, or state that the condition holds at that level.

## Evidence

Chapter 8, p. 681 [9iiia], gives *He was wounded in the foot* as a contact-with-body-part example. The associated NP is the passive subject.
