# CG-0069: Approximators with any

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 5 §7.5, p. 383
- Verdict: Textual mismatch
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0069/
- Review: not yet reviewed by the author (drafted with AI assistance)

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

Free-choice *any*, unlike non-affirmative *any*, permits modification, for example by *almost*.

## Proposed wording

*Anyf*, unlike *anyn*, permits modification by *almost*, *nearly* or *practically*. *Anyn* takes only the approximate negators *hardly*, *barely* and *scarcely* (§11).

## Finding and limits

The contrast holds for *almost*-type approximators. Non-affirmative *any* takes the approximate negators. Brett’s determinatives paper relies on the p. 431 description.

## Evidence

§11(b), p. 431: “The approximate negators *hardly*, *barely*, *scarcely* can modify *anyn*”. Page 424 [45iia] has *They did [hardly any] work.* Page 816 makes the same point.
