# CG-0070: Count singular some and the indefinite article

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 5 §7.5, p. 384
- Verdict: Production error
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0070/
- Review: not yet reviewed by the author (drafted with AI assistance)

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

*Some* and *anyn* occur with count singular heads “as in [30]” and may therefore contrast with the indefinite article.

## Proposed wording

as in [29], for example

## Finding and limits

Change [30] to [29]. As printed, the reference also conflicts with the paragraph’s second reason, the restriction to quantitative uses.

## Evidence

[30] is the exclamatory use (*Those are SOME elephants!*), non-quantitative and plural in [30a]. [29] is “Vague count singular use”, where *some* “is in competition with the indefinite article *a*”.
