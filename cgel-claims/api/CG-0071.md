## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

“The degree determiners can also function directly as predicative complement: *Their enemies were many.*”

## Proposed wording

The degree determinatives can also function directly as predicative complement

## Finding and limits

Replace determiners with determinatives. This is the only occurrence of degree determiners in the book.

## Evidence

Determiner is CGEL’s name for a function. The sentence concerns items outside that function. The section title and the book’s other uses say degree determinatives.
