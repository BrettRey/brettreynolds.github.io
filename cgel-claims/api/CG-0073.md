# CG-0073: Personal those with non-clausal modifiers

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 5 §9.2, p. 414
- Verdict: Correction
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0073/
- Review: not yet reviewed by the author (drafted with AI assistance)

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

The personal fused-head construction with *those* requires a clausal modifier.

## Proposed wording

the construction requires a post-head dependent (a relative or non-finite clause, an AdjP such as *present*, or a PP such as *with a bus to catch*) and the plural form *those*

## Finding and limits

An AdjP or PP can also license the people reading. Non-finite clauses such as *those arrested* are consistent with the stated requirement.

## Evidence

CGEL’s own prose has about *those present in the area affected by the storm* (p. 1466) and to *those present at the time* (p. 1468), each with no antecedent set. Page 928 [13iia] has *Those with a bus to catch don’t hesitate to leave.*
