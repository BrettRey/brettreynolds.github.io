# CG-0076: Items in both pronoun and determinative categories

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

Just four items belong in both categories: *what*, *which*, *we* and *you*.

## Proposed wording

just five items that belong in both categories: *what*, *whatever*, *which*, *we*, and *you*

## Finding and limits

Add *whatever* to the count.

## Evidence

Page 422 refers to “pronouns *what* (and *whatever*)”. Page 356 lists determinative *whatever*. Ch. 12, p. 1074, gives pronoun and determinative uses of the *·ever* forms.
