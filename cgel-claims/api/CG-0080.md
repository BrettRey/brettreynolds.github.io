# CG-0080: Position of so-licensed content clauses

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

In *[so great] a loss that we’re likely to go bankrupt* the content clause is an indirect complement within the NP. It can be “further delayed so that it occurs later in the clause rather than within the NP”.

## Proposed wording

Reconcile with Ch. 11, §4.6, where a content clause licensed by *so* occurs at the end of the matrix clause.

## Finding and limits

The two chapters place the *so* clause in different constituents. The Ch. 5 generalization holds for comparatives and for *too* (Ch. 14, pp. 1262–1263). Which analysis is correct is a separate question.

## Evidence

Ch. 11, p. 967: the content clause is delayed to the end of the matrix clause, and “This position is obligatory”. It stars *So many people that we had to move to a larger room enrolled for the course* and analyses the clause as a complement in clause structure.
