# CG-0083: NP modifiers denoting time and place

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

Modifiers with NP form are limited to those denoting age, size and similar properties, or non-referential distributive indefinites.

## Proposed wording

limited to those denoting age, size, location in time or space and similar properties, or non-referential distributive indefinites

## Finding and limits

Extend the list to temporal and spatial location, or say how similar properties covers them. CX-20 treats the same examples as a construction family.

## Evidence

The same display [13i] includes *the results last year* and *houses this side of the lake*, which locate the referent in time or space.
