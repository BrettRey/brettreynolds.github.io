Subject to restrictions described in Ch. 17, §2.3, antecedent and pronoun can occupy the full range of NP functions.

## Proposed wording

Ch. 17, §§2.4 and 3

## Finding and limits

Probably §§2.4 and 3. Confidence is lower than for the other cross-references because §2.3 states an agreement restriction.

## Evidence

Ch. 17 §2.3 (pp. 1468–1475) covers 3rd person pronouns and their semantic relations. Restrictions on position are in §2.4 (order of anaphor and antecedent) and §3 (reflexives).
