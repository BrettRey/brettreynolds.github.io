## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

The contrast between *who* with *man* and *woman* and “*which* with *roof*” applies in the plural examples too.

## Proposed wording

*which* with *house*

## Finding and limits

Change *roof* to *house*.

## Evidence

In [45iii] (p. 497), *the house which lost its roof*, the antecedent of *which* is *house*.
