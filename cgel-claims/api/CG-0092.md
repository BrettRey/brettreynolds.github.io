# CG-0092: Which with human antecedents

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 5 §17.3, p. 498
- Verdict: Qualification
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0092/
- Review: not yet reviewed by the author (drafted with AI assistance)

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

With human nouns, *which* is possible only with nouns denoting infants without specification of sex.

## Proposed wording

With referential human antecedents, *which* is possible only with nouns denoting infants … (but see Ch. 12, §3.5.1)

## Finding and limits

Restrict the claim to referential antecedents or cross-refer to Ch. 12, §3.5.1. Brett’s personhood paper discusses infants and the *who*/*which* split but not these cases.

## Evidence

Ch. 12, p. 1048, “Two special cases of *which* used with human antecedents”: *They accused him of being a traitor, which he undoubtedly was* [44i], and complements of *have (got)* [45]. That passage refers back to §17.3.
