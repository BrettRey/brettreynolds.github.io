# CG-0098: This with an integrated relative after a name

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 5 §20.3, p. 520
- Verdict: Textual mismatch
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0098/
- Review: not yet reviewed by the author (drafted with AI assistance)

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

In *Who’s [this Penelope who’s been sending you emails]?*, *this* is an embellishment of the proper name.

## Finding and limits

Replace the example (*That Senator Fox* in the same display has no relative) or reclassify it as a secondary use.

## Evidence

Ch. 12, p. 1062: with an integrated relative the name is “a nominal, not an NP”, and the larger nominal “is not a proper name and hence … it requires a determiner”. §20.4(a) [6i] treats *The Mary that you met yesterday* as a secondary use.
