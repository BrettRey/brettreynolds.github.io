# CG-0102: Vocatives as interpolations

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 5 §20.5, p. 523
- Verdict: Textual mismatch
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0102/
- Review: not yet reviewed by the author (drafted with AI assistance)

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

A vocative is best regarded as a kind of interpolation that can appear in front, central or end position.

## Proposed wording

It is best regarded as a kind of supplement, an interpolation in central position and an appendage in front or end position (cf. Ch. 8, §20).

## Finding and limits

Call the vocative a supplement, or say that it is an interpolation in central position and an appendage elsewhere.

## Evidence

Ch. 15, p. 1350: an interpolation “is located at a position between the beginning and end of a main clause”, and “An appendage is attached loosely at the beginning or end of a clause.” Page 67 draws the same distinction.
