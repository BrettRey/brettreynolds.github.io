## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

Determiner *both* and *either* are described in Ch. 5, §§7.2, 7.7.

## Proposed wording

as described in Ch. 5, §§7.3, 7.7

## Finding and limits

## Evidence

Ch. 5 §7.2 treats *we* and *you*. *Both* is in §7.3, “The universal determinatives *all* and *both*”.
