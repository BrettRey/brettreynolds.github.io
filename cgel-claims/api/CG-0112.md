## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

The overview example [1ix] *I crawled towards the door* is labelled “[direction: §4.4]”.

## Proposed wording

[direction: §4.3]

## Finding and limits

Change §4.4 to §4.3. Medium confidence: §4.4 does include direction examples, but only static ones, and [1ix] is literal motion. Ch. 4, p. 305, cites §4.4 for the *way* construction, which is also in §4.3 (CG-0171).

## Evidence

The contents (p. 663) title §4.3 “Change of location: goal, source, path, and direction” and §4.4 “Metaphorical extension of the locative categories to other domains”. Direction of motion is treated in §4.3 under “Realisation of direction” (p. 688): “While *to* alone marks goal, *towards* marks direction”. §4.4 has direction only in static uses, as in [35i] *The house faces towards the forest* (p. 689).
