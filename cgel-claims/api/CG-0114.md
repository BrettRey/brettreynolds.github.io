# CG-0114: Cross-reference for manner as and like

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 8 §2.1, p. 671
- Verdict: Production error
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0114/
- Review: not yet reviewed by the author (drafted with AI assistance)

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

A manner PP with comparative *as* or *like* as head is “discussed in more detail in Ch. 13, §§5–6”.

## Proposed wording

Ch. 13, §§5.5–5.6

## Finding and limits

§6 is wrong. The intended reference is probably §§5.5–5.6, but that’s the reader’s inference. The authors’ erratum for p. 733, which corrects Ch. 15, §§10–11 to §§2.10–2.11, is the same kind of slip.

## Evidence

The Ch. 13 contents (p. 1097) list “5.5 As”, “5.6 Like” and “6 Scalar set comparison”. Manner *as* is treated on p. 1149 (“Here the *as* phrase functions as manner element in the matrix”), and manner *like* on pp. 1155–1156 (“(b) Manner complement”; “Likeness of manner”). §6, on plain, comparative and superlative grade, has nothing on manner *as* or *like*.
