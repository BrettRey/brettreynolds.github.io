## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

The prose cites the cognate object examples as [i] and [ii]. “Thus [i] means essentially the same as *She fought heroically*”, and [ii] “likewise describes the manner of his dying”.

## Proposed wording

Thus [a] means essentially the same as *She fought heroically*; [b] likewise describes the manner of his dying

## Finding and limits

Change [i] and [ii] to [a] and [b].

## Evidence

Example [11] is lettered a and b: [11a] *She fought a heroic fight.*, [11b] *He died a long and agonising death.*
