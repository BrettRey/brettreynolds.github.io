## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

Of [13a] *The master-key opened the door* and [13b] *The door opened with the master-key*, “for further discussion of these constructions see Ch. 4, §8.3.3”.

## Proposed wording

see Ch. 4, §8.3.4

## Finding and limits

Change §8.3.3 to §8.3.4. Both subsections begin on p. 319.

## Evidence

Ch. 4, §8.3.3 (p. 319), is “Type III: *I wiped the marks off the wall* vs *I wiped the wall*”, with *wash* in [74] and *brush*, *dust* and others in [75]. The master-key pair is [76i] in §8.3.4, “Type IV: *We filled the bucket with water* vs *Water filled the bucket*”: *We opened the door with the master-key* / *The master-key opened the door*. Page 320 covers [13b]: “Only [i] and [iv] allow a third construction”.
