# CG-0123: David for Donald

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 8 §3, p. 679
- Verdict: Production error
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0123/
- Review: not yet reviewed by the author (drafted with AI assistance)

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

“The two acts are both explicitly expressed in structures like *Dick wisely got himself taught the tricks of the trade by David* or *Ed reluctantly allowed his stepfather to send him to boarding-school*.”

## Proposed wording

*Dick wisely got himself taught the tricks of the trade by Donald*

## Finding and limits

Change *David* to *Donald*.

## Evidence

[10iii] (p. 678) is *Dick was wisely taught the tricks of the trade by Donald.* Footnote 3 (p. 678) and the preceding sentence on p. 679 (“Donald’s teaching him the tricks of the trade”) keep *Donald*. The companion paraphrase keeps the participants of [10iv], *Ed* and *his stepfather*.
