## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

Of the contact-with-body-part examples in [9] (*She poked him in the ribs* / *She poked his ribs*): “see Ch. 4, §7.4, for further discussion of the alternation in [9]”.

## Proposed wording

see Ch. 4, §8.4

## Finding and limits

Change §7.4 to §8.4. Ch. 4 has no §7.4. CG-0068 concerns the passive pair [9iii].

## Evidence

Ch. 4, §7, “Light verbs”, has only §§7.1–7.2 (contents, p. 214). The alternation is treated in §8.4, “Contrasts between separate complements and a single combined complement” (p. 320): [78i] *She kissed him on the cheek* / *She kissed his cheek*, with *poke* and *hit* in the verb list [79i] (p. 321).
