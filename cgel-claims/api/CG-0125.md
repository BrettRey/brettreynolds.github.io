# CG-0125: Stop-start movement in the way construction

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 8 §4.3, p. 685
- Verdict: Open
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0125/
- Review: not yet reviewed by the author (drafted with AI assistance)

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

“The movement denoted by the *way* construction has to be one that consists of a series of stops and starts, rather than being continuous movement.”

## Finding and limits

A lead, not yet a finding. Attestations of continuous movement (*weaving their way along*, *snaked its way*, *glided its way*) in edited prose would show whether “has to be” overstates the constraint.

## Evidence

Adele E. Goldberg (1995: 204) states the constraint as motion along a self-created path, either against some external difficulty or deliberate, careful, methodical or winding. Her example (19), from the *Wall Street Journal*, describes continuous jogging: *weaving their way along a street of fractured and fallen houses*. CGEL’s own [35v] (p. 689), *The track winds its way along the banks of the river*, describes continuous, static extension.
