# CG-0129: Extended the grounds by 5 acres under spatial extent

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 8 §5.2, p. 691
- Verdict: Textual mismatch
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0129/

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

[5vi] *They extended the grounds by 5 acres / to a total of 55 acres* is among the examples that “illustrate various dimensions of spatial extent. The differences are purely lexical, the range of grammatical constructions being the same throughout.”

## Finding and limits

Medium-high confidence. By p. 692’s criterion, *extended the grounds by 5 acres* is scalar change, like *widened the road by two metres*, and its *by* phrase contradicts “the same throughout”. Move [5vi] to the scalar change examples in [6], or qualify the remark.

## Evidence

Page 692: “the preposition for scalar change is *by*, while that for spatial movement is *for*”. Spatial measures with *by* (*They have widened the road by two metres*) “clearly belong in the scalar change construction”. The overall extent phrases within [5] differ too: *(for) half a mile* in [iv], *by 5 acres* in [vi].
