# CG-0136: # and ∗ for already over a negative

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 8 §8, pp. 711–712
- Verdict: Textual mismatch
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0136/

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

Page 711 marks [4ib] #*She already isn’t here* as anomalous: “In the primary sense we are concerned with here, *already* can’t have scope over a negative, as seen in [ib].”

## Finding and limits

One constraint gets # on p. 711 and ∗ on p. 712, and the p. 712 prose presents it as the point already made. Either one mark or the explanation needs to change.

## Evidence

Page 712 [8iib] stars ∗*He has already not read the report* and explains it by the same constraint: “But [iib] is again excluded: as noted above, *already* in its primary sense does not take scope over a negative.” The notational conventions (p. xii) use ∗ for ungrammatical and # for semantically or pragmatically anomalous, and p. 702 says a #-marked example “certainly does not violate any rule of grammar”.
