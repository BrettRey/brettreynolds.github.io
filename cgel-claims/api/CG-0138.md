# CG-0138: Scope readings of for the third time

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 8 §10, pp. 719–720
- Verdict: Textual mismatch
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0138/
- Review: not yet reviewed by the author (drafted with AI assistance)

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

For [2i] *Mary Smith performed the sonata for the third time last year* (p. 719), p. 720 says that with the subject inside the scope of the adjunct “it was Mary Smith herself who performed on all three occasions, whereas when the subject is outside the scope of the adjunct there is no indication of who performed before”.

## Finding and limits

Medium confidence: [2i] maps the subject’s wide scope onto the reading where the performer may differ, while [2iii] and §9 map it onto the reading with the same individual. Because *Mary Smith* is referential, the inside reading can be defended, but not the outside one; and if relative scope makes no truth-conditional difference for a referential subject, the [2i] ambiguity isn’t one of scope in the sense used for [2iii]. Either way, the two mappings conflict.

## Evidence

For [2iii] *People are dying of TB again*, p. 720 says “the adjunct has wider scope than the subject: we can assume that people do not die twice”, which makes adjunct over subject the reading with different individuals. §9 maps scope the same way. In [16i] (p. 717) the same person is sacked repeatedly, and p. 718 says “the quantified NP has scope over the adjunct”; under [17], where the adjunct has wider scope, “it is not the same person who is sacked”.
