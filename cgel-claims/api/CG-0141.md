## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

“In [ii] the airline’s overbooking is treated as subordinate and expressed within an adjunct of cause (more specifically, of reason), whereas in [ii] it is the other situation, our not being able to get on the plane, that is expressed as a subordinate clause, this time within an adjunct of result.”

## Proposed wording

whereas in [iii] it is the other situation

## Finding and limits

Change the second [ii] to [iii].

## Evidence

[1ii] (p. 725) is the reason example. [1iii] is the result example: *The airline had overbooked, so that two of us couldn’t get on the plane*, labelled “[result]”.
