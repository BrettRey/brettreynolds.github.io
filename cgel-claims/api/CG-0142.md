# CG-0142: So as as a preposition

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 8 §12.2, p. 727
- Verdict: Textual mismatch
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0142/
- Review: not yet reviewed by the author (drafted with AI assistance)

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

Page 727 lists *so as* among the prepositions heading purpose PPs: “The prepositions found here are *in order* (finite or infinitival), *so* (finite), *so as* (infinitival)”. Page 728 has “In subjectless clauses governed by *in order* or *so as*”.

## Finding and limits

Medium confidence: Ch. 8 lists *so as* as a preposition taking an infinitival, which Ch. 14 p. 1262 rules out, while Chs. 14 and 17 make *so* the head and *as* + infinitival its complement. Page 728’s “Infinitivals governed by *so* + *as*” recognizes two words, but it still makes the infinitival a dependent of the pair.

## Evidence

Ch. 14 p. 1262: “The only prepositional expressions that take *to*-infinitivals other than interrogatives as direct complement are purposive *in order*, and *as if*/*though*”. Ch. 14 p. 1264: “The *as* here is itself licensed by *so* or *such*.” Ch. 17 p. 1540 makes *so* the preposition and says its complement “can have the form of *as* + infinitival”, citing Ch. 8, §12.2.
