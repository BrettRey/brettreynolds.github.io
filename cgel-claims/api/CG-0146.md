# CG-0146: Concessive for and for all that

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 8 §13.2, p. 736
- Verdict: Qualification
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0146/
- Review: not yet reviewed by the author (drafted with AI assistance)

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

“In its concessive use, *for* takes an NP complement beginning with *all*”, as in [9iv] *For all our good intentions, the meeting soon broke up in acrimony*.

## Finding and limits

Medium confidence: §13.2 allows concessive *for* only an NP complement and makes *for* the head, while Ch. 7 makes *for all* the head of a content clause. These are different constructions, so neither statement is strictly false, but §13.2 needs widening and a cross-reference to Ch. 7, §3.2.

## Evidence

Ch. 7 p. 624 [18i] has a content clause instead: *For all that I’m not guilty, I’ll still be a suspect in the eyes of history*. There “the content clause is clearly licensed by *for all*”, which is analysed as head, like *for fear* and *on condition*.
