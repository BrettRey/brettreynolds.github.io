# CG-0154: Modal forms in remote apodoses: the reference to Ch. 3

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 8 §14.2.2, p. 751
- Verdict: Production error
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0154/
- Review: not yet reviewed by the author (drafted with AI assistance)

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

“The most common modal auxiliaries occurring in the apodosis are *would*, *should*, *could*, and *might*”, and “for other modal forms, see Ch. 3, §§5, 9.8.4”.

## Finding and limits

Ch. 3 §5 is the wrong target (high confidence). §2.5 is the likely intended one, but that is an inference (medium confidence).

## Evidence

Ch. 3 §5 (p. 139) is “Perfect tense”, and a search of pp. 139–147 for remote, conditional, apodosis and each modal auxiliary finds no discussion of modals in remote apodoses. §9.8.4 is a fitting target: on p. 202 *must* and *ought* “occur readily in construction [60ib], if only marginally in [iib]”, where [60ib] is a backshifted report and [60iib] the remote conditional. §2.5 (p. 108), “The auxiliaries considered in turn”, tests each auxiliary for “[L] Can occur in remote apodosis”; p. 109 has %*If he had stayed in the army he must surely have become a colonel*.
