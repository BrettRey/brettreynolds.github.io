# CG-0160: Disappointed paraphrased as mistaken

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 8 §14.4, p. 759
- Verdict: Production error
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0160/
- Review: not yet reviewed by the author (drafted with AI assistance)

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

[65ii] is *Anyone who thought they could take advantage of us would be disappointed*, which “corresponds to the remote conditional *If anyone thought they could take advantage of us they would be mistaken*.”

## Proposed wording

*If anyone thought they could take advantage of us they would be disappointed*

## Finding and limits

Change *mistaken* to *disappointed*. The paraphrase is meant to differ from [65ii] only in form.

## Evidence

On the same page the open paraphrase of [65i] keeps the predicate: “*If anyone thinks they can take advantage of us, they will be disappointed*”.
