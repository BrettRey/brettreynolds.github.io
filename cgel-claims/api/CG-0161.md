# CG-0161: Footnote 29’s pointer to footnote 20

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 8 §14.5, p. 760
- Verdict: Production error
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0161/
- Review: not yet reviewed by the author (drafted with AI assistance)

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

Footnote 29: “This then is one of the cases alluded to in footnote 20, where the grammatical apodosis doesn’t match the logical consequent”.

## Proposed wording

This then is one of the cases alluded to in footnote 23

## Finding and limits

Change footnote 20 to footnote 23.

## Evidence

Footnote 20 (p. 723) concerns scalar implicature: “The implicature is of the scalar type discussed in Ch. 5, §5.2.” Footnote 23 (p. 738) introduces the logical terms antecedent and consequent for protasis and apodosis, and adds “but we will see that the correspondence is not complete”.
