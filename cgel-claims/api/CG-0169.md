# CG-0169: Misprints in Chapter 8

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 8, various sections, pp. 665–774
- Verdict: Production error
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0169/
- Review: not yet reviewed by the author (drafted with AI assistance)

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

Misprints not in the authors’ errata, each confirmed by a reader on the page images of the corrected printing.

## Proposed wording

§§4–5; *Did he return fortunately or surprisingly?*; *He wrote it by hand*; *faecium* or [sic]; *in(to) the*, *on(to) the*; add full stops on p. 701 and to [26iv]; *participial or verbless*; *concessive*; *personal TV set*; *breakdown*

## Finding and limits

Candidates for the authors’ errata page. *Faicium* is inside quotation marks in the example and may reproduce its source, so a [sic] may be the better fix there.

## Evidence

p. 665 [1vi–viii] “[source: §4–5]” and the like, against [1v] “§§4–5”; p. 667 [3iib], an alternative question, “∗*Did he return fortunately or surprisingly.*”; p. 674 “In *he wrote it by hand*”, with the example in lower case; p. 679 [12ii] “*Enterococcus faicium*”; p. 686 “*in(to)the room*” and “*on(to)the platform*”, with no space after the bracket; p. 701 [31i] *I left home before my parents divorced* with no final stop; p. 701 “see Ch. 17, §10.2” with no final stop; p. 731 [26iv] with no final stop; p. 736 “a participial and verbless clause”, where *or* is meant; p. 737 “concessional adjuncts”, the book’s only use of *concessional*, where it elsewhere has *concessive*; p. 760 “*a personal TV*” for [70]’s “*a personal TV set*”; p. 774 “nervous break-down” for [3iv] *breakdown*.
