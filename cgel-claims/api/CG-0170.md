# CG-0170: Ch. 4’s reference to spatial location in Ch. 8

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 4 §5.2, p. 258
- Verdict: Production error
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0170/
- Review: not yet reviewed by the author (drafted with AI assistance)

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

“for further discussion of spatial location and extension of the roles to the dimension of time, see Ch. 8, §§5–6”.

## Proposed wording

see Ch. 8, §§4–6

## Finding and limits

Change §§5–6 to §§4–6. References into Ch. 8, §4 also point one place too high at Ch. 4 p. 305 (CG-0171) and Ch. 8 p. 665 [1ix]. That fits a late renumbering of §4, but doesn’t establish one.

## Evidence

Ch. 8, §4 (p. 679) is “Location and change of location in space”, §5 (p. 690) is “Spatial extent and scalar change”, and §6 (p. 694) is “Temporal location”.
