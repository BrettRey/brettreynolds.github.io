# CG-0175: Ch. 14’s reference to exhaustive conditionals

- Verdict: Production error
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0175/

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

Of [4iii] *Whether working or relaxing, he always has a scowl on his face*: “Example [iii] belongs to the exhaustive conditional construction discussed in Ch. 11, §5.3.5.”

## Proposed wording

discussed in Ch. 11, §5.3.6

## Finding and limits

Change §5.3.5 to §5.3.6.

## Evidence

Ch. 11, §5.3.5 (p. 985) is “Infinitival interrogatives”. §5.3.6, on the same page, is “Interrogatives as adjunct: the ungoverned exhaustive conditional construction”.
