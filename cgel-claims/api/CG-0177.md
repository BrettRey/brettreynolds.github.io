# CG-0177: Example reference for correlative both and either

- CGEL: CGEL Ch. 15 §2.3, p. 1305
- Verdict: Author erratum
- URL: https://brettreynolds.ca/cgel-claims/CG-0177/
- Review: not yet reviewed by the author (drafted with AI assistance)

## CGEL’s claim (paraphrase)

After [38], “In the coordinations [iia/iib] they function as marker of the first coordinate in correlative” coordination.

## Proposed wording

In the coordinations [ib/iib]

## Finding and limits

The authors’ errata already correct [iia/iib] to [ib/iib]. The entry isn’t marked as fixed in a later printing, and the 9th printing (2016) still has [iia/iib]. The audit’s image check found it independently, beside the separate reference error in CG-0104.

## Evidence

[38] has [ia] *both players*, [ib] *both Kim and Pat*, [iia] *either player* and [iib] *either Kim or Pat*. The preceding sentence cites the NPs as “[ia/iia]”. [iia] is an NP, not a coordination; the coordinations are [ib] and [iib].
