Negative words that stand in for a noun phrase

Words like nobody, nothing and none can make a whole sentence negative without a separate not.

In Nobody knew where Kim was, the word nobody does two jobs. It fills the place of the person who knew, and it makes the whole sentence negative. The same goes for nothing in Kim had done nothing about it, and for none in We liked none of them.

This dictionary treats nobody, nothing, nowhere, no one and no place as compounds of the word no, and none as another form of no. They aren't pronouns here. A sentence with one of them can be rephrased with a verb negated and a word like any, as in Kim didn't do anything about it.

Examples

  • Nobody knew where Kim was.
  • Kim had done nothing about it.
  • We liked none of them.

Try it

Take a sentence with nothing in it and say it again with didn't and anything. Do the two versions give the same message?
