Admitting a drawback with although

An although phrase acknowledges something that might have counted against the main claim.

In Although the steps were icy, we reached the door, both things are presented as true. The steps were icy, and we reached the door. The contrast comes from an expectation about what the ice might have stopped.

Even if works differently. In Even if the steps are icy, we'll reach the door, the ice is uncertain, so the sentence concedes a possibility rather than a fact.

Examples

  • Although the steps were icy, we reached the door.
  • Even if the steps are icy, we’ll reach the door.
