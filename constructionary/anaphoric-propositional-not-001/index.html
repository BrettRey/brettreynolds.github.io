I hope not

After a word like hope, not can stand in for a whole statement, the negative of something just raised in the conversation.

Early sketch. The full entry for this pattern has one example so far and its evidence isn’t written up yet, so treat this page as a starting point.

Asked Is the screen broken?, someone can answer I hope not. The word not here stands in for the content the screen is not broken. So the answer means I hope the screen is not broken.

This isn't the same as I don't hope so, which can instead deny that the speaker hopes. Not every attitude word allows the short answer, and which ones do depends on the word.

Examples

  • “Is the screen broken?” “I hope not.”

Try it

Answer a yes-or-no question with I hope not. Then say what the full sentence would be.
