# Absolute Supplement

## Meaning 1

Adds a circumstance with a subject of its own.

In Her hands shaking, Mia set the glass down, her hands is the subject of shaking. The supplement can instead be verbless, as in Her keys in her coat pocket, Mia left the house. The relation to the main situation can be simultaneous, explanatory, or sequential, as context permits.

- *[Her hands shaking], Mia set the glass down.*: Constructed non-finite supplement with an overt subject.
- *[Her keys in her coat pocket], Mia left the house.*: Constructed verbless supplement.

## Projection

Removing the overt subject of the non-finite supplement changes who is understood to be shaking in the ordinary reading. The same frame can host a verbless predicate.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: A circumstance with its own subject

A phrase added to a sentence can have a subject of its own and describe the circumstances of the main action.

In Her hands shaking, Mia set the glass down, the added phrase has its own subject, her hands, and its own predicate, shaking. It tells us the circumstances in which Mia set the glass down.

The added phrase doesn't need a verb. Her keys in her coat pocket, Mia left the house works the same way. How the circumstance relates to the main action depends on context: it can happen at the same time, explain why, or be part of a sequence of events. On this dictionary's analysis, taking the subject away changes the ordinary reading: we would no longer understand it to be her hands that are shaking.

### Teacher

For advanced classes, a supplement with its own subject, as in Her hands shaking, Mia set the glass down.

An absolute supplement is added to a clause and has a subject of its own, followed by a non-finite VP or a verbless predicate. In Her hands shaking, her hands is the subject of shaking. In Her keys in her coat pocket the predicate is verbless. Context decides whether the circumstance is simultaneous, explanatory or sequential. Removing the overt subject changes who is understood to be shaking in the ordinary reading, so an absolute supplement should be kept apart from a supplement with no subject of its own. This dictionary marks the analysis as provisional.
