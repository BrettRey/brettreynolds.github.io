# *all* Modifier Restricting a Role

- id: `all-class-role-modifier-001`
- pattern: Nom[all + class-denoting nominal or adjective + N]
- status: provisional analysis; evidence level: unassessed
- linguist edition: https://brettreynolds.ca/constructionary/linguist/all-class-role-modifier-001/

## Meaning 1

Restricts the occupants of a salient role associated with the head noun to one class.

With *an all girls’ school* the relevant role is eligibility to attend. The school can educate only some of the town’s girls and have men on staff. With *an all-women team* the team supplies the members. Genitive and plain-case nominal forms and adjectives support the interpretation. CGEL treats *all girls’* as a genitive NP with peripheral *all*. The related patterns leave that token’s internal bracketing open.

- *an [all girls’ school]*: The restriction concerns who can attend the school.
- *[all-female police unit]*: Adjectival class specification.
- *[all-women team]*: Plain plural form. The same contingent is described by both published expressions.

## Related constructions

- A kind of: Nominal Construction (`nom-001`): The role-restricting expression combines with a nominal head.
- Compare: Genitive *'s* (`genitive-s-001`): CGEL analyzes the spelling *girls’* in its example as genitive.

## Projection

The head helps select the set to which *all* applies. The restriction applies to students in the school case and members in the team case.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: *All* as a restriction, as in *an all girls' school*

Putting *all* before a class word can say that only members of that class fill a role connected to the noun.

In *an all girls' school*, the role at issue is who can attend. The school educates only girls, but it can serve only some of the town's girls and it can have men on staff. In *an all-women team*, the role is team membership, and the team supplies the members.

The pattern works with several forms. There's a plural noun with an apostrophe (*all girls'*), a plain plural (*all-women*), and an adjective (*all-female*). The head noun helps decide which role *all* restricts.
