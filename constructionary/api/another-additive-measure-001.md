# Additive Another before a Measure or Cardinal

- id: `another-additive-measure-001`

## Meaning 1

Adds another amount of the stated size to the relevant course or quantity.

- *We stayed [another three days].*: Constructed illustration.

## Related constructions

- A kind of: Indefinite NP Construction (`np-indef-001`)

## Projection

The added quantity can be plural, even though ordinary another with no intervening quantifier selects a singular count nominal. The example adds days rather than replacing the prior period.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: Another three days

Another before a number or measure adds an amount of that size to what has gone before.

In We stayed another three days, another adds three days to the stay. It doesn't replace the first period. It continues it.

Ordinary another goes with a singular count noun, as in another day. Here, though, another comes before a plural, three days.

### Child

We say "another day". Can we also say "another three days"?

Another means one more. With a number it means that much more. We stayed another three days means three more days on top of the ones before.
