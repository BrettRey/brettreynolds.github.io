## Meaning 1

Recovers a predicate from the preceding discourse while pronouncing the auxiliary.

- *Nora could repair it, and [Kai could too].*: Constructed illustration.
- *Nora repaired it, but [Kai didn’t].*: Constructed comparison. The antecedent is positive and has a lexical preterite. The ellipsis host supplies negative do.

## Projection

The stranded auxiliary can differ in tense or polarity from the antecedent, as in Nora repaired it, but Kai didn't. A lexical verb does not freely strand in the same pattern.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: Leaving out the rest after a helper verb

After a helper verb, the rest of the predicate can be left unsaid because we can recover it from earlier.

In Nora could repair it, and Kai could too, the second part says only Kai could too. We understand it to mean Kai could repair it, because the earlier part tells us what the missing part is.

The helper verb that remains doesn't have to match what came before. In Nora repaired it, but Kai didn't, the first part is positive and in the past, while the second supplies a negative did. A main verb doesn't freely get left alone like this.

### Teacher

Teaches how a pronounced auxiliary can be followed by an unexpressed predicate recovered from the preceding discourse.

In this pattern a clause has a subject and a finite auxiliary, with the complement left unexpressed and recovered from the preceding discourse. The auxiliary is pronounced. It can differ in tense or polarity from the antecedent: Nora repaired it, but Kai didn't has a positive antecedent with a lexical preterite, and the ellipsis host supplies negative do. A lexical verb does not freely strand in the same pattern. This dictionary relates it to gapping, to stranding of infinitival to (Jo tried to) and to sluicing.

### Child

In "Kai could too", what is Kai able to do?

You don't have to say the whole thing again. Nora could repair it, and Kai could too. We know Kai could repair it, because Nora's part told us.
