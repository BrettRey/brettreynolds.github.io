# Bare Role Construction

- id: `bare-role-001`
- pattern: NP[Det:absent + Nom[Head:singular count role noun]], in a licensed role slot
- status: provisional analysis; evidence level: unassessed
- linguist edition: https://brettreynolds.ca/constructionary/linguist/bare-role-001/
- licence: CC BY 4.0; snapshot 2026-11-19 (5c11b28)

## Meaning 1

Ascribes an office or institutional role, or gives the content of an office expression.

The same nominal can follow be, become, remain, appoint, or elect when the verb licenses a predicative complement. It can also occur with as and in role of or position of, in a matrix-licensed except phrase, or as an ascriptive supplement. In an appointment construction the direct object identifies the appointee and the bare NP gives the office.

- *They appointed Lee [treasurer of the club].*: Constructed object-oriented predicative. In They questioned the treasurer of the club, the article-marked NP instead identifies the person questioned.
- *Lee served [as treasurer].*: Constructed prepositional frame. Compare Lee applied for the position [of treasurer]. Both retain a bare role nominal.
- *Faatulu was named [co-captain] along with fellow junior Jamie Peck.*: The sports report explicitly names two holders of the role.
- *Lee is [secretary of the club].*: Constructed after CGEL’s comparison. Lee is the secretary of the club can also specify the incumbent in answer to Who is the secretary?
- *I’ve been everything except [president].*: The except phrase picks out a role from the alternatives made available by the matrix clause.
- *Wilson, [Secretary to the Cabinet], had informed the Prime Minister immediately.*: Ascriptive supplement. The bare NP cannot replace Wilson as the referential subject.

## Meaning 2

Ascribes a professional assignment construed as a post.

A workplace can make a particular assignment available for the construction. The availability of a bare form is sensitive to lexical choice and how a job title is used in the institution.

- *They appointed Lee [lead counsel] for the appeal.*: Constructed. Lead counsel specifies the assignment.
- *Lee became [a lawyer].*: Constructed comparison. In ordinary standard usage, became lawyer is degraded on the professional-class reading.

## Meaning 3

Ascribes a relational or ceremonial role in a licensed frame.

A following relational dependent can specify whom the role is held in relation to. This use is limited to independently available role expressions. It is distinct from using a kin term as a form of address or as a standing name. Holding a kin role doesn't confer the kin term as a designation: Schneider and Homans report that when a child is reared from infancy by an aunt playing a maternal role, she “is still not called ‘mother’” (1955: 1203).

- *They asked Lee to be [godfather to their son].*: Constructed relational example. The to-phrase supplies the other participant.
- *There can likewise be only one person who is [father of the student I have in mind].*: CGEL’s own prose, discussing the identifiability of the father of the student; the sentence's opening In [2ii] is omitted. A kin noun heads the bare predicative, and of the student gives the relatum.
- *She was [mother to three boys].*: Constructed. A single kin noun, with the to-phrase giving the relatum. Kin nouns under this meaning are an adjudication (Brett, 2026-11-19): CGEL lists kin or similar relations separately from offices or roles (Ch. 4 §5.5.1(e)).

## Constraints

- (syntactic) A bare role NP can describe the subject or object. It does not ordinarily occupy the subject or object position as a referential expression for the roleholder.
- (semantic) The choice of noun and context must support a role reading. Predicative function alone does not license removal of the article from become a miser or become a teacher.
- (semantic) The role can have multiple holders. A counterpart with an overt determiner can also ascribe a role, as in became a co-captain or remained our captain. With be, a counterpart with the can additionally identify the contextually relevant incumbent.

## Projection

In ordinary standard English, with a reading that ascribes an assignment or gives its content, a nominal such as treasurer can remain bare in She stayed treasurer, They appointed her treasurer, and As treasurer, she signed. Reuse as a stand-alone referential object, as in They questioned the treasurer, normally calls for a determiner. The same bare office ascription can apply to more than one holder.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: Jobs and roles without "the" or "a"

Words like treasurer or captain can describe the role someone holds without a word like the or a in front.

Normally a word like treasurer needs company: we say the treasurer or a treasurer. But in some sentences it stands alone. They appointed Lee treasurer. Lee served as treasurer. Here the word doesn't point to a particular person. It says what role Lee has.

Compare They questioned the treasurer. That sentence is about a person, so it normally needs the. The bare version works where a role is being given or described. The role can also have more than one holder, as when two people are named co-captain.

### Teacher

Explains why role nouns such as treasurer can lack a determiner in predicative and some prepositional frames.

A singular count noun that names an office or post can appear with no determiner when the sentence ascribes the role to someone: after be, become, remain, appoint or elect, after as, after role of or position of, in an except phrase licensed by the main clause, or as an ascriptive supplement (Wilson, Secretary to the Cabinet, ...). The bare NP describes the subject or object. It doesn't normally stand as a referential subject or object for the role-holder, where a determiner is needed (They questioned the treasurer). The noun and context must support a role reading: the article stays in become a teacher. A version with the or a can also ascribe a role (became a co-captain), and with be, the secretary of the club can identify the current holder, answering Who is the secretary?
