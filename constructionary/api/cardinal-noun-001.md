## Meaning 1

Cardinal numerative used as a noun to denote a number entity or quantity concept

- *[Ten] is even.*: Proper-noun use (abstract numeral)
- *a ten*: Common-noun use (denominator/token)
- *Room 101*: Proper-noun use (numerical label)
- *hundreds of people*: Common-noun use (magnitude quantifier)

## Constraints

- (semantic) Denotes a number entity or quantity concept, not a determiner function.
- (syntactic) Proper-noun uses head bare NPs; common-noun uses take determiners and plural.

## Projection

A quantifying or token use can take number inflection and another determiner, as in *two tens*. The naming use *ten* in *Ten is even* and the determinative use in *ten cards* lack that count-noun environment.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: Number words that act as nouns

A number word can name a number, label one thing or stand for a quantity, behaving like an ordinary noun.

A number word can come before a noun, as in *ten cards*. But it can also be a noun in its own right. In *Ten is even*, *ten* names a number and doesn't count anything. In *Room 101*, the number labels one particular room.

Other uses count or measure. You can say *a ten*, and in *hundreds of people* the number gives a large quantity. In these uses the number word can take a plural ending and another word before it, as in *two tens*. The naming use and the use before a noun, as in *ten cards*, don't work that way.

### Teacher

Helps explain why a number word sometimes behaves like a noun, with its own plural and determiner, and sometimes doesn't.

A cardinal noun is a number word used as a noun. Proper-noun uses head bare noun phrases: *Ten is even* (the number itself) and *Room 101* (a numerical label). Common-noun uses take determiners and plural forms: *a ten*, *two tens*, *hundreds of people*. They denote number entities, quantities, labels or numerical tokens, and they are not determiners. Contrast *ten cards*, where *ten* is a determinative and the noun is *cards*.
