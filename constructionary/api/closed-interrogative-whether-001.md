## Meaning 1

Introduces an embedded closed question or choice between alternatives.

- *[Whether to reschedule] still needs discussion.*: Constructed illustration.

## Projection

Whether can introduce an infinitival, where interrogative if is unavailable. Certain finite prepositional and subject environments likewise favour or require whether.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: Questions inside sentences with whether

Whether can introduce a question or a choice between alternatives inside a larger sentence.

In Whether to reschedule still needs discussion, whether introduces a choice between alternatives, and the whole phrase acts as the subject of the sentence. Whether can be followed by a full clause or by to and a verb.

It also works in places where the question word if isn't available, such as before to and a verb. Some other positions, including some where the phrase is the subject, favour whether or require it.

### Teacher

Useful for explaining when whether, and not if, introduces an embedded closed question.

Whether introduces an embedded closed question or choice between alternatives, followed either by a clause in declarative order or by a to-infinitival. It can introduce an infinitival where interrogative if is unavailable. Certain finite prepositional and subject environments likewise favour or require whether. When teaching embedded questions, show whether alongside if, so students see where whether is the form to use.
