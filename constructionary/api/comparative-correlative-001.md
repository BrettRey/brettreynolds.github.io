# Comparative Correlative

- id: `comparative-correlative-001`
- pattern: the + fronted comparative in first unit, the + fronted comparative in second unit; also matrix comparative + final the-comparative unit
- status: provisional analysis; evidence level: unassessed
- linguist edition: https://brettreynolds.ca/constructionary/linguist/comparative-correlative-001/
- licence: CC BY 4.0; snapshot 2026-11-19 (5c11b28)

## Meaning 1

Makes variation on one scale contingent on the value on another.

The more we pay them, the harder they work makes effort vary with pay. Reversing the order can make pay vary with effort, which licenses different inferences. The second comparative can stay in its ordinary position when the dependent unit comes last. Fronted pairs also allow reductions such as the sooner, the better. The comparative marker the also occurs before comparative AdjPs and AdvPs in this pattern.

- *[The more we pay them, the harder they work.]*: Pay supplies the scale from which variation in effort is projected.
- *[He becomes more cynical the older he gets.]*: The dependent comparative comes last.
- *[The sooner, the better.]*: Both units are reduced.

## Related constructions

- Compare: Comparative -er (`comparative-er-001`): An inflected comparative is one possible realization of each degree phrase.
- Overlaps with: Clause-Analogue Construction (`clause-analogue-001`): Fully reduced pairs can express the relation with no overt VP.

## Projection

Matched comparatives cue linked variation rather than two unconnected comparisons. The fronted pairing permits reduced units, and reversing which scale sets the condition can change truth conditions.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: Two comparisons that change together

Two matching the-parts show that one thing varies in step with another.

In The more we pay them, the harder they work, both parts start with the and contain a comparison. Together they tell us that effort varies with pay.

Order matters. Swapping the parts can make pay vary with effort instead, which supports different conclusions. The parts can be cut down, as in The sooner, the better. The dependent part can also come last, as in He becomes more cynical the older he gets.
