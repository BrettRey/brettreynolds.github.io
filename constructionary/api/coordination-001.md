## Meaning 1

A non-headed constituent made up of coordinates; not a phrase

Coordinations contrast with phrases, which are headed (see phrase-001). No coordinate is the head; the coordination's distribution follows from the coordinates jointly, and a difference of category is generally tolerated where there is likeness of function (e.g., the DP + NP numerative coordinations in two thousand and twenty-seven).

- *[bread and butter]*: NP coordinates with coordinator and
- *[slowly but surely]*: AdvP coordinates with contrast marker
- *[two thousand and twenty-seven]*: Numerative coordination; coordinates of different categories under likeness of function

## Constraints

- (syntactic) No head; each coordinate could typically stand in the coordination's position alone.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: Joining things with and, or and but

Words like and, or and but can link two or more equal parts, and none of the parts is in charge.

In bread and butter, two nouns sit side by side, linked by and. In slowly but surely, two adverbs are linked by but. Neither part is the boss of the other. A group like this is called a coordination, and it adds up the contributions of its parts: joining, choosing, contrasting or adding.

A coordination can do whatever its parts do together in the sentence. Its parts needn't be exactly the same kind of word, as long as they do a similar job. Two thousand and twenty-seven joins two number expressions of different kinds this way. This dictionary treats the analysis as provisional.

### Teacher

Helps explain coordinations as headless groups of equal parts joined by and, or, but or nor.

A coordination is a non-headed constituent made up of two or more coordinates, optionally linked by a coordinator (and, or, but, nor). It isn't a phrase, because phrases are headed. No coordinate is the head, so the group's distribution follows from the coordinates jointly. Each coordinate could typically stand alone in the coordination's position. Coordinates can differ in category where their functions are alike, as in two thousand and twenty-seven. The meaning is the joint contribution of the coordinates: conjunction, disjunction, contrast or addition.

### Child

What do the words and, or and but do when they join two things?

Small words like and, or and but can join two things. In bread and butter, both words matter equally. Neither one is the boss.
