## Meaning 1

Forms a verb for causing or undergoing a change connected to the adjectival base.

- *The update should [stabilize] the connection.*: Constructed illustration.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: Verbs ending in *-ize*

Adding *-ize* to an adjective can make a verb meaning to cause or undergo a change tied to that adjective.

*Stable* is an adjective, and *stabilize* is a verb. In *The update should stabilize the connection*, the verb means to make the connection stable. The adjective names the result of the change.

The *-ize* ending is also added to nouns, and some *-ize* verbs have meanings of their own that go beyond this pattern. This dictionary's pattern is the one built on an adjective, where the verb means causing or undergoing a change connected to that adjective. The analysis is provisional. The pattern is also written with the spelling *-ise*.
