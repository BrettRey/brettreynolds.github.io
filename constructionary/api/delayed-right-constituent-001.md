## Meaning 1

Expresses shared right-edge material once while contrasting what precedes it.

In She knew of but never mentioned the proposal, the proposal completes both knew of and mentioned. Its functions differ across the two coordinates. Delay can foreground the contrast between the predicates.

- *She [knew of but never mentioned the proposal].*: Constructed after the CGEL model with a PP complement in one coordinate and a direct object in the other.
- *I am [interested in but worried about the new plan].*: Constructed example where different prepositions precede the delayed NP.

## Projection

The single pronounced NP can satisfy two distinct local frames. The preposition inside the first coordinate can select that NP even though the second frame takes a direct object.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: One ending shared by two halves

In a linked pair of phrases, the shared last part can be said once, at the very end.

In She knew of but never mentioned the proposal, the phrase the proposal finishes both parts of the sentence. She knew of the proposal, and she never mentioned the proposal. Saying it only once, at the end, lets the speaker set the two verbs against each other.

The shared ending does a different job in each half. After knew of it is the object of a preposition, and after mentioned it is a direct object. This dictionary treats the analysis as provisional.
