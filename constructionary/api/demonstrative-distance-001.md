## Meaning 1

Directs attention to a referent and presents it as proximal or distal in a relevant deictic dimension.

- *Could you pass me [that blue folder] over there?*: Constructed illustration.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: This one or that one

This, that, these and those direct attention to something and can present it as near or far.

In Could you pass me that blue folder over there?, that points to one folder among others, and over there adds where it is. The demonstrative words are this and that, with these and those for more than one. They present the thing as near or far in some relevant dimension.

Distance in space is one such dimension, but it isn't required. This and that can also pick out different candidates without any earlier noun phrase to point back to, and they can refer to things said earlier. This dictionary treats the analysis as provisional.

### Teacher

Helps explain this, that, these and those as words that direct attention and mark proximal or distal position.

The pattern is an NP with a demonstrative determiner: this or that with a singular, these or those with a plural. It directs attention to a referent and presents it as proximal or distal in a relevant deictic dimension. This and that can select different candidates without a prior noun-phrase antecedent. Spatial distance is one dimension and isn't obligatory in discourse-deictic uses. The determiner function is realized by determinatives: this, that, these and those. The analysis is provisional.

### Child

When do you say this one and when do you say that one?

Words like this and that help you point out one thing. You might use this for something close and that for something farther away. For more than one, say these or those.
