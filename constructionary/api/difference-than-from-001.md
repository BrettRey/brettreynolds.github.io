## Meaning 1

Specifies the referent or situation from which something differs.

- *The result was [different from what we expected].*: Constructed illustration.

## Projection

The choice of marker depends on variety and complement form. Than can introduce a finite comparison in uses where from requires a nominal or other licensed complement.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: What something is different from

The word different can be followed by a phrase that names what something differs from.

In The result was different from what we expected, the phrase after different says what the result is compared with. This dictionary's pattern lists three small words that can introduce that phrase: from, to and than.

The phrase can name a thing or a whole situation. Here it is what we expected, a situation the result is measured against.

### Teacher

Gives the pattern for the standard of comparison after different.

Different heads an AdjP whose complement specifies the referent or situation from which something differs. The pattern in this dictionary lists from, to and than as introducers of that standard. The standard can be a plain NP or a clause-like expression, as in different from what we expected. This dictionary gives only one illustration and relates the pattern to same-as comparison and to than-reduced clauses.
