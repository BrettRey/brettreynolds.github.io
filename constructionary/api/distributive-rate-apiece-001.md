## Meaning 1

Assigns the stated amount to each unit or participant.

- *The tickets cost [six dollars apiece].*: Constructed illustration.

## Projection

The six dollars is the price of an individual ticket. With six dollars altogether, the same amount instead applies to the whole set.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: Prices and amounts per item

Words like apiece and each after an amount say the amount applies to every item or person in a group.

In The tickets cost six dollars apiece, six dollars is the price of one ticket, not the price of all of them together. Apiece gives the amount to each ticket in the group.

The group isn't named in the amount phrase itself. The context supplies it, here the tickets. Each can do the same job.

### Teacher

Helps explain apiece and each after an amount as a rate assigned to each member of a group.

A measure or counted NP followed by each or apiece assigns the stated amount to each unit or participant, with the group supplied by context. In The tickets cost six dollars apiece, six dollars is the rate per ticket. The pattern is related to indefinite rates and to floating distributives.

### Child

If pens are two dollars each, how much is one pen?

Each and apiece tell the price of one thing. The tickets cost six dollars apiece means one ticket costs six dollars. The talk around it tells you which things.
