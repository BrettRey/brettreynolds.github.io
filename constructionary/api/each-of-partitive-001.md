# *each* in a Partitive NP

## Meaning 1

Distributes a predication over every member of an established group.

- *[Each of the six drawers] has a lock.*: Constructed illustration.

## Related constructions

- A kind of: True Partitive with *of* (`true-partitive-001`): *Each of the six drawers* uses a partitive domain and distributes the predication over every drawer in it.
- Compare: Grammatical Number (`grammatical-number-001`)
- Compare: Floating *each* (`floating-distributive-001`)
- Compare: Predeterminer *all* before a Determined NP (`prenominal-all-the-001`)

## Projection

The *of*-phrase has a plural complement, while *each* favours singular verbal agreement. *Every* without a following *one* does not normally form the same construction.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: *Each of* the group

*Each* followed by *of* and a known group says something about every member of that group.

In *Each of the six drawers has a lock*, the six drawers are a group already in view. The sentence says that every one of them has a lock, one by one. The word *each* singles out the members, and the phrase after *of* names the group they belong to.

The group is expressed with a plural and a definite word such as *the*.

### Teacher

Helps explain *each of* plus a plural definite NP as a way to apply a statement to every member of a known group.

In *each* + PP[*of* + plural definite NP], *each* distributes the predication over every member of an established group, as in *Each of the six drawers has a lock*. This dictionary treats it as a specialization of the true partitive: the *of*-phrase gives a partitive domain, and the predication is distributed over every drawer in it. This dictionary also relates the pattern to grammatical number, since the group is plural and the distribution works member by member.

### Child

Can one word talk about every drawer in turn?

*Each of the six drawers has a lock* means every drawer has one. You count them one at a time. The group is one you already know about.
