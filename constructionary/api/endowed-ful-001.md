## Meaning 1

Characterizes something as having or displaying the substance or property associated with the base.

- *The chair gave a [thoughtful] reply.*: Constructed illustration.

## Projection

The output is adjectival and can modify reply. Noun formations like cupful have a separate measure interpretation and can take plural morphology.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: Adjectives ending in -ful

Adding -ful to a noun gives an adjective saying something has or displays what the noun names.

A thoughtful reply is one that shows thought. The -ful adjective describes something as having or displaying the substance or property associated with the base word.

The base is typically a noun. Colour gives colourful, a word for something that has colour or displays it. A related ending, -less, works in the opposite direction.
