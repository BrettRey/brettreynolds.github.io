## Meaning 1

Emphasizes that someone exemplifies a role or type in the relevant respects.

Every inch marks completeness in the characterization. CGEL identifies this use as idiomatic and contrasts the predicative with an unacceptable use as a by-agent. The relation to every bit as good remains open.

- *She is [every inch a philosopher].*: The relevant respects come from the description philosopher.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: Being a philosopher in every way

Every inch a philosopher says that someone fully fits a role or type, in the respects that matter for it.

In She is every inch a philosopher, the words every inch add emphasis. They suggest nothing is missing from the description: she is a philosopher completely.

The respects that count come from the description itself. Here the word philosopher tells us what to look at, so the phrase is about her qualities as a philosopher, not about her height. The phrase is an idiom, and in this example it describes the person after a form of be.

### Teacher

Helps with the idiom every inch a ..., which emphasizes that someone fully fits a role or type.

Every inch plus a or an plus a nominal works as a predicative complement and marks completeness in the characterization: the subject exemplifies the role or type in the relevant respects. The respects come from the noun (philosopher), not from any measurement. CGEL identifies this use as idiomatic and contrasts the predicative use with an unacceptable use as a by-agent.
