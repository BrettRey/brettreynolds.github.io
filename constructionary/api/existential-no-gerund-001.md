# Existential No with a Gerund-Participial

- id: `existential-no-gerund-001`
- pattern: there + BE + no + VP[gerund-participial]
- status: provisional analysis; evidence level: unassessed

## Meaning 1

Presents an action as unavailable in the circumstances.

There was no moving it normally suggests that moving it was impossible or impracticable. In a constructed context where moving the crate was easy and nobody chose to do it, nobody moved it fits directly and there was no moving it calls for further explanation. A rule can also be the obstacle, as in a prohibitive use. The availability and source of this modal force are open.

- *[There was no stopping her.]*: Chapter 5 mentions the pattern in its survey of what can head an NP.
- *[There was no telling what he might do next.]*: The action at issue is telling what he might do.
- *[There’ll be no stopping her.]*: A future action is presented as unavailable.
- *[There was no moving it.]*: Constructed test. Compare a crate bolted to the floor with one that people simply chose to leave.

## Projection

On the impossibility reading, the form invites an explanation of what blocks the action even when its nonoccurrence is already given.
