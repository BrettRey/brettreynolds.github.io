## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: Sentences that start with there

There plus be can introduce something, or say that it's present, available or going on.

In There are two spare seats, the word there doesn't point to a place. It opens the sentence so that the thing being introduced, two spare seats, comes after the verb. Sentences like this introduce something new into the conversation, or assert that it's present, available or happening.

The word there moves around like a subject. It switches places with the verb in a question (Is there a seat?) and can turn up in a tag at the end. The thing introduced favours information that's new to the listener, though some definite things are allowed in the right context.
