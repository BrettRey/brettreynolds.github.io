## Meaning 1

Distributes the predication over individual members associated with a nearby argument.

- *The three hikers [each carried a radio].*: Constructed illustration.

## Projection

Each can follow the subject across a finite auxiliary in The hikers had each carried a radio. The linked NP can have its own determiner and is not contained in each.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: Each, placed away from its group

The word each can sit after a group of people or things and say the action applies to every member separately.

In The three hikers each carried a radio, each isn't stuck to a noun. It sits later and says the carrying applies to the hikers one by one: every hiker carried a radio.

The word can follow the subject even across a helper verb, as in The hikers had each carried a radio. The group it's linked to can have its own the, and the group isn't contained inside each.

### Teacher

Helps explain floating each, which distributes an action over the members of a nearby plural noun phrase.

Floating each is separately positioned from the plural participant NP it is associated with, and it distributes the predication over the individual members: The three hikers each carried a radio. Each can follow the subject across a finite auxiliary, as in The hikers had each carried a radio. The linked NP can have its own determiner and isn't contained in each. This dictionary relates the pattern to the each-of partitive and to each in subject determiner function, and marks it provisional, with medium confidence.
