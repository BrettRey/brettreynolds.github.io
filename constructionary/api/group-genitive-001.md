## Meaning 1

Places genitive marking at the right edge of a possessor expression.

- *It was [the person next door’s ladder].*: Constructed illustration.

## Projection

Door is the rightmost host of the marker, though the ladder is associated with the person. The possessor reading is determined by the whole genitive NP.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: The 's at the end of a longer phrase

When the owner is described by a long phrase, the 's goes at the very end of that phrase, not on the main word.

In It was the person next door's ladder, the owner is the person next door. The 's doesn't go on person, the main word, but at the end of the whole phrase, on door.

So the word right next to the 's isn't the one the ladder belongs to. The ladder belongs to the person, and the reading comes from the whole phrase the 's closes off.

### Teacher

For advanced classes, where the genitive 's goes when the possessor is a long phrase.

The genitive marker attaches at the right edge of an extended possessor: the person next door's ladder. Door is the rightmost host of the 's, though the ladder is associated with the person. The possessor reading is determined by the whole genitive NP, not by the word that hosts the marker. This dictionary treats this as a specialization of the genitive 's pattern, with a genitive NP in determiner function, and marks the analysis as provisional.
