# Proposal with How About

## Meaning 1

Offers an item or course of action for consideration.

- *[How about a short break?]*: Constructed illustration.

## Related constructions

- Compare: Question (`question-001`)
- Compare: Topic Proposal with What About (`what-about-topic-001`): How about a short break? and What about a short break? can both propose a break. What about the budget? can also take up an outstanding topic.
- Compare: Deliberative Why Not with a Bare Infinitival (`why-not-proposal-001`): How about taking a break? proposes an option with a gerund-participial. Why not take a break? puts forward a proposal using a plain verbal predicate.

## Projection

The response can accept or reject the proposal. The phrase need not ask how a break would be carried out.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: How about a short break?

How about followed by a thing or an activity puts it forward as an option for the listener to consider.

How about a short break? doesn't have to be a question about how a break would happen. It's a proposal: here's an option, what do you think? The listener can accept or reject it.

How about can be followed by a noun phrase, or by an activity ending in -ing, as in How about taking a break? What about a short break? can propose the same thing, though What about the budget? can also bring up a topic that's still outstanding. Why not take a break? also proposes a break, using a plain verb.

### Teacher

Gives learners a way to make a suggestion, with variants using a noun phrase or an -ing form.

How about plus an NP, a gerund-participial or a licensed clause offers an item or course of action for consideration: How about a short break? The response can accept or reject the proposal. The phrase need not ask how a break would be carried out, so the phrase works as a proposal. What about a short break? can propose the same thing, but What about the budget? can also take up an outstanding topic. Why not take a break? proposes with a plain verbal predicate, while How about taking a break? uses a gerund-participial. This dictionary marks this analysis as provisional.

### Child

How do you suggest something to a friend?

How about can offer an idea. How about a short break? means maybe we could take one. Your friend can say yes or no.
