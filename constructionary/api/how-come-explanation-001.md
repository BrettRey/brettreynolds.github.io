## Meaning 1

Asks for an explanation of an actual or apparently actual state of affairs.

- *[How come the gate is open?]*: Constructed illustration.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: How come

How come asks for an explanation of something that seems to be the case.

How come the gate is open? asks why the gate is open. It treats the open gate as a fact that needs explaining.

The pattern differs from an ordinary why question. Why is the gate open? puts the verb before the subject. How come keeps the subject first: how come the gate is open.

### Child

What is another way to ask why?

How come can ask why. How come the gate is open? means the same as asking why it is open. How come starts the question, then the gate comes next.
