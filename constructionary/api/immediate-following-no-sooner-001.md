## Meaning 1

Presents one event as occurring almost immediately after another.

- *[No sooner had we parked than the rain started].*: Constructed illustration.

## Projection

The comparative-shaped than-clause supplies the subsequent event. Fronted no sooner licenses inversion in the initial clause.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: No sooner ... than

No sooner ... than presents one event as following almost at once after another.

In No sooner had we parked than the rain started, the rain starts almost as soon as the parking is done. The sentence starts with no sooner, and the helper word had comes before we.

The second part begins with than, which brings in the later event. Placing no sooner first is what brings in the changed word order in the first part.

### Teacher

For advanced classes, a fronted negative expression with inverted order, and a than-clause for the later event.

The pattern is No sooner plus an inverted primary clause, then than plus a second clause: No sooner had we parked than the rain started.
