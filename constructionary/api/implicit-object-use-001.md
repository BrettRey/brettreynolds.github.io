## Meaning 1

Leaves a usual participant nonspecific while asserting the activity or event.

- *Have you [eaten] yet?*: Constructed illustration.

## Related constructions

- Compare: Simple Intransitive VP (`simple-intransitive-vp-001`)
- Compare: Monotransitive VP (`monotransitive-vp-001`): Compare Jo ate lunch with Jo ate. The second leaves the food unspecified. Availability depends on the verb and its use.

## Sources

## Projection

The implied food need not have been mentioned. Leaving the relevant object unexpressed is natural for eat, even when plausible near-synonyms such as consume strongly favour a stated object.

### Curious reader: Leaving out what was eaten

Some verbs that can take an object can also be used without one, with the object left unspecified.

Have you eaten yet? doesn't say what was eaten. The food is left open. Compare Jo ate lunch with Jo ate, where the second leaves the food unspecified.
