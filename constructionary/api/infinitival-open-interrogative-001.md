## Meaning 1

Presents an unresolved choice about an envisaged action.

- *They discussed [which route to take].*: Constructed illustration.

## Related constructions

- A kind of: Open Interrogative Clause (`open-interrogative-clause-001`)
- Compare: *for*-Infinitival with an Overt Subject (`for-infinitival-001`)

## Sources

## Projection

*Which route* supplies the missing object and the action is prospective or modal. The corresponding finite question can ask instead what was actually done.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: *Which route to take*

A question word followed by *to* and a verb, as in *which route to take*, presents an open choice about something that could be done.

In *They discussed which route to take*, the part after *discussed* is a question with *to* and a verb instead of a full clause. *Which route* fills in what's missing after *take*.

The question is about an action that's being considered, not one that has been carried out. A full finite question can ask instead what was actually done, so the two forms can ask different things.

### Teacher

Covers embedded questions such as *which route to take*, which have a question phrase and a *to*-infinitival.

The pattern is an interrogative phrase followed by a *to*-infinitival with a linked gap. It presents an unresolved choice about an envisaged action. In *They discussed which route to take*, *which route* supplies the missing object of *take*, and the action is prospective or modal. The corresponding finite question can ask instead what was actually done. This dictionary treats the pattern as a specialization of the open interrogative clause and relates it to *for*-infinitivals. It is provisional.
