## Meaning 1

Realizes an infinitival complement with overt to and a contextually recoverable predicate.

- *I didn’t go, but I [wanted to].*: Constructed illustration.

## Projection

To can remain in the position where the full infinitival would occur. The same speaker can recover go without substituting lexical do so.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: Ending a sentence with to

After words like wanted, the word to can stand without a verb after it when the listener can work out what verb is meant.

In I didn't go, but I wanted to, the sentence stops at to. You know the missing verb is go, because it was just mentioned. The to stays where a full phrase such as to go would have been.

This lets a speaker avoid repeating a verb. It's a different way of recovering the meaning from using do so in its place.
