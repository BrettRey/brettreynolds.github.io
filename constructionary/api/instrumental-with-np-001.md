## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: Doing something with a tool

A phrase with with and a thing can name what was used to do something.

In Lee opened the case with a small screwdriver, the phrase with a small screwdriver names what Lee used. The screwdriver helped the action happen.

The same words with can introduce someone who came along, as in with the watchman. That's a different meaning, so the sentence around it matters.

### Teacher

Covers with plus a noun phrase naming the instrument used in an action.

The pattern is a prepositional phrase of with plus an instrument noun phrase, used as an adjunct. It identifies an object that mediates or enables an action: in Lee opened the case with a small screwdriver, the screwdriver has a means role. With the watchman can instead identify an accompanying participant, which is a distinct interpretation. This dictionary treats the phrase as a specialization of the prepositional phrase and relates it to by plus gerund means phrases. It is provisional.
