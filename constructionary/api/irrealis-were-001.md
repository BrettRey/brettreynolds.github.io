## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: I wish I were taller

In some wishing and imagining sentences, a form of be, were, appears with I or with he, she or it.

In I wish I were taller, were is the form of be used with I. The sentence is about something that isn't the case. This distinct form is called irrealis, and it shows up in the first and third person singular.

With remote if, was may also be used by many speakers. But in Were I taller, where were comes before the subject, was fails in standard usage.
