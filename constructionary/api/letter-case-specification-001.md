## Meaning 1

Specifies the capitalization of a letter in a label.

Capitalization can distinguish institutional and descriptive uses. Name conventions and ordinary spelling supply other uses. The capital-letter format can also emphatically present an example as fully deserving its label.

- *Catholic [with a small c].*: Non-institutional, descriptive sense.
- *She is a [Big-D] Democrat.*: Institutional or party-affiliated sense.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: Saying which letter case you mean

A phrase like with a small c tells the reader how a label is spelled and, with it, what it means.

Sometimes the letter at the start of a word changes what the word means. Catholic with a small c points to a non-institutional, descriptive sense. A big-D Democrat points to the party. The phrase naming the letter is a quick instruction about how to read the label.

Another use presents something as fully deserving a label. Saying a person is generous with a capital G suggests that the person fully deserves the label. The case of the letter doesn't decide by itself what kind of word the label is.
