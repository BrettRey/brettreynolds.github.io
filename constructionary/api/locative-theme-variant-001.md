# Locative Theme-Object Frame

- id: `locative-theme-variant-001`
- pattern: VP[verb + material/theme Od + goal PP]
- status: provisional analysis; evidence level: unassessed; early sketch (one example, evidence not yet written up)

## Meaning 1

Presents a material or moved entity as direct object and a location as goal.

- *They [sprayed paint onto the panel].*: Constructed illustration.

## Related constructions

- A kind of: Caused-Motion Construction (`caused-motion-001`)
- Alternates with: Locative Location-Object Frame (`locative-location-variant-001`): In sprayed paint onto the panel the material is the object. In sprayed the panel with paint the location is the object. The alternation depends on the verb and sense.

## Projection

What was sprayed? can ask about the material. The goal need not be understood as comprehensively affected, unlike the customary implication of the corresponding location-object variant.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: Putting the material right after the verb

With some verbs, the material or moved thing comes right after the verb and the place comes later.

In They sprayed paint onto the panel, the paint comes straight after the verb and the panel follows. The paint is the thing that was moved, and the panel is where it ended up.

You can ask about the paint with What was sprayed? There's a second way of saying it, in which the panel comes first: They sprayed the panel with paint. Which verbs allow both depends on the verb and its sense. When the panel is placed later, it needn't be understood as affected throughout, whereas the panel-first version customarily suggests more of the surface was affected.

### Teacher

Helps explain the frame where the material is the object and the location is a goal PP.

In this frame the material or moved entity is the direct object and the location is a goal PP: sprayed paint onto the panel. A question can target the material (What was sprayed?). The goal needn't be understood as comprehensively affected, unlike the customary implication of the corresponding location-object variant. It alternates with sprayed the panel with paint, but whether a verb takes both depends on the verb and sense. This dictionary treats it as a specialization of caused motion.
