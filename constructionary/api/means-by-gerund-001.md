## Meaning 1

Presents an activity as the means by which another outcome is obtained.

- *Jo reopened the file [by restoring a backup].*: Constructed illustration.
- *Jo reopened the file [after restoring a backup].*: Constructed comparison. After supplies temporal order and leaves the means relation to context.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: Saying how with by and an action word

By followed by an action that ends in ing says how something was done.

In Jo reopened the file by restoring a backup, the restoring is the method. It's how Jo got the file back. The sentence presents the activity as the means by which the outcome was reached.

Compare Jo reopened the file after restoring a backup. After gives the order of events but leaves the connection to context. By says more: the restoring is part of the method, not merely something that happened around the same time.

### Teacher

Helps explain by plus an ing phrase as the means by which something was done, and how it differs from after.

A PP with by and a gerund-participial clause presents an activity as the means of obtaining another outcome: Jo reopened the file by restoring a backup. The restoring is interpreted as part of the method, not merely a coincidental event in the same time period. Compare after restoring a backup, which supplies temporal order and leaves the means relation to context. This dictionary treats the by-phrase as a specialization of the prepositional phrase and relates it to infinitival purpose adjuncts.

### Child

How did you get your shoes on this morning?

You can say how you did something with by and an ing word. I opened the box by cutting the tape. The cutting is how the box got opened.
