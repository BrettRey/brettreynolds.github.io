## Meaning 1

Rejects the wording, implication, or strength of a description as inadequate.

- *It isn’t [warm]. It’s scorching.*: Constructed illustration.

## Projection

An ordinary scalar reading of scorching would imply warmth. The emphatic correction licenses negation of the weaker label while preserving the stronger characterization.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: Saying not that word, but a stronger one

A speaker rejects a description as too weak, then corrects it with a stronger one.

In It isn't warm. It's scorching, the speaker doesn't mean that the weather is cold. They mean that warm is too weak a word. The correction, scorching, is the stronger description.

This works because scorching on its own would imply warmth. The correction rejects the weaker label while keeping the stronger characterization. So the negation targets the wording, its implication or its strength, not the plain facts about the temperature.
