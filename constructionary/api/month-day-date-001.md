# Month-First Calendar Date

- id: `month-day-date-001`
- pattern: date expression[month name + day number]
- status: provisional analysis; evidence level: unassessed

## Meaning 1

Identifies a day by its ordinal position within the named month.

In June fourteenth, the final numeral identifies the day of the month. The same convention can be written June 14. The month name and day number can do this without an article or a preposition inside the date. A separately formed expression such as the fourteenth of June identifies the same date by a different order and marking.

- *[June 14th]*: Published example with month followed by an ordinal.
- *They set the deadline for [November 18].*: Constructed example in the articleless written format.

## Projection

A day numeral after a month name is interpreted as an index within that month, with the same interpretation available to ordinal forms and written digits.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: Dates written month first

A month name followed by a number picks out a day, as in June 14 or November 18.

In June 14th, the number after the month tells you which day of that month is meant. It can be written as a digit, June 14, or as an ordinal, June fourteenth. The date needs no the or of inside it.

The same date can also be said in another order, with extra words: the fourteenth of June. That pattern uses different order and marking. This dictionary describes only the month-first pattern here and leaves other date formats to separate accounts.

### Teacher

Helps with month-first dates and how the day number relates to the month name.

A month name followed by a day number identifies a day by its ordinal position within that month. The number can be written as a digit or an ordinal (June 14, June 14th, June fourteenth). No article or preposition appears inside the date. The fourteenth of June identifies the same date with a different order and marking, so teach it as a separate pattern. This dictionary's high-confidence account covers the month-first pattern only. The number works as an index in a conventional system, like the numeral in a numerical label.
