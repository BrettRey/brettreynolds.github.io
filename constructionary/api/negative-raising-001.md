# Inferred Embedded Negation under a Matrix Negative

- id: `negative-raising-001`
- pattern: matrix negation + opinion/expectation predicate + content clause
- status: provisional analysis; evidence level: unassessed

## Meaning 1

Often invites a negative interpretation in the subordinate content while expressing negation in the matrix.

- *I [don’t think the shop is open].*: Constructed illustration.
- *I [don’t believe the gate is open], but I have no view on whether it is closed.*: Constructed comparison. The continuation can cancel the ordinary interpretation that the speaker believes it closed.

## Projection

The natural interpretation often approximates I think the shop isn’t open. The two differ if the speaker lacks an opinion either way. The availability depends on the matrix predicate and context.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: Negatives that apply to the clause after them

With verbs like think, a negative in the main clause can invite a negative meaning in the clause after it.

I don't think the shop is open often invites a negative meaning in the clause after think: roughly, I think the shop isn't open. The negation sits in the first clause, but the meaning is about the second.

The two sentences can differ if the speaker has no opinion either way. I don't believe the gate is open, but I have no view on whether it is closed cancels the reading that the speaker believes it closed. This dictionary adds that how available the reading is depends on the main verb and the context.

### Teacher

For advanced classes, a main-clause negative that is interpreted as negating the content clause.

With a matrix negation, an opinion or expectation predicate and a content clause, the interpretation often shifts to a negative in the subordinate content. I don't think the shop is open is naturally read as approximating I think the shop isn't open. The two differ if the speaker lacks an opinion either way, as the comparison with I don't believe the gate is open shows. The availability of the reading depends on the matrix predicate and context. This dictionary treats the effect as an interpretive inference, not a change in the grammar of the clause.
