## Meaning 1

Negates both conjuncts in a coordination

- *He [neither] knew [nor] cared where his children were.*: VP coordination
- *[Neither] Kim [nor] Pat replied.*: NP coordination

## Constraints

- (syntactic) Correlative markers neither and nor introduce paired coordinates.
- (semantic) Both coordinated alternatives fall under negation.

## Projection

The coordinator can link two phrases as in neither Jo nor Kai or two verb phrases as in neither called nor wrote. The construction differs from additive neither did Jo, which depends on a preceding negative assertion.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: Neither this nor that

Neither ... nor links two things and says that neither of them applies.

In Neither Kim nor Pat replied, the pair neither ... nor joins Kim and Pat, and the sentence denies the reply for both. It also joins actions: He neither knew nor cared where his children were says that he did not know and did not care.

Both items fall under the negation. The pair can link two phrases, as in neither Jo nor Kai, or two verb phrases, as in neither called nor wrote. It is different from the neither in neither did Jo, which depends on a negative statement that came before it.

### Teacher

Helps teach correlative neither ... nor, which negates both of two coordinated items.

Neither ... nor is a correlative coordination: the markers neither and nor introduce paired coordinates, and both coordinated alternatives fall under negation. It can coordinate noun phrases (Neither Kim nor Pat replied) or verb phrases (He neither knew nor cared where his children were). It differs from additive neither, as in neither did Jo, which depends on a preceding negative assertion. This dictionary treats neither ... nor as a specialization of coordination and lists absolute negator determinatives and so-neither inversion as related.

### Child

How can you say that two people both didn't do something?

Neither and nor go together. Neither Kim nor Pat replied means Kim didn't reply and Pat didn't either.
