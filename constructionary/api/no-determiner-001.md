# Negative determinative *no* in determiner function

- id: `no-determiner-001`
- pattern: no + N

## Meaning 1

Negatively quantifies a nominal by asserting zero instances

- *no books*: Zero instances of books
- *no evidence*: Absence of evidence

## Constraints

- (syntactic) A DP headed by the determinative *no* functions as determiner within the NP.
- (semantic) Construes the NP as having zero members or instances.

## Projection

*No* can determine a singular count, plural count, or non-count nominal. A clause with *We saw no damage* normally licenses a positive confirmation tag, linking the local determiner to wider clausal polarity.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: *No* before a noun

Put before a noun, *no* says that there were zero instances of whatever the noun names.

In *no books* and *no evidence*, the word *no* tells you that there were zero instances. *No books* means no books at all, and *no evidence* means an absence of evidence.

*No* can go before a singular count noun, a plural count noun or a non-count noun. A clause with *We saw no damage* also normally takes a positive confirmation tag, such as *did we?* That ties the word before the noun to the sentence as a whole: it makes the whole sentence negative.

### Teacher

Teaches *no* as a determinative that quantifies a noun phrase down to zero, with clause polarity as a test.

*No* is a determinative in determiner function: a determinative phrase headed by *no* is the determiner in the noun phrase. It construes the noun phrase as having zero members or instances. It can determine a singular count, plural count or non-count nominal. *We saw no damage* normally licenses a positive confirmation tag, which links the local determiner to wider clausal polarity. This dictionary separates those polarity diagnostics from the claim that the relevant quantity is zero. This dictionary treats *no* as interfacing with primary-auxiliary negation.

### Child

What does *no* mean in *no books*?
