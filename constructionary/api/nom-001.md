## Meaning 1

Specifies/restricts feature bundles

Modifies/narrows the feature bundle denoted by the head

- *wooden chair*: Restricts material features
- *helpful neighbors*: Specifies relational features

## Meaning 2

Elaborates relational features

Extends relational aspects of feature bundle through complements

- *mother of three*: Elaborates parental relation
- *knowledge that it is true*: Elaborates contents of the knowledge

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: A noun with its describing words

The part of a noun phrase made of a noun and its modifiers, not counting the word like the in front.

In wooden chair, the noun chair is described by wooden. In mother of three, of three adds more about the relation. In knowledge that it is true, the clause after knowledge tells you what the knowledge is about.

This dictionary calls such a unit a nominal: a head noun with optional modifiers before it and optional modifiers or complements after it. It supplies the core description in a noun phrase. A nominal such as old chair fits in a frame like the ___ without adding another determiner.

### Teacher

Explains the nominal as the head noun plus its modifiers and complements, apart from the determiner.

A nominal has a required head noun, optional pre-head modifiers and optional post-head modifiers or complements. Its descriptive content restricts or elaborates the head's denotation: wooden chair restricts material features and mother of three elaborates a relation. It is immediately dominated by the noun phrase and can be bare in certain contexts. An expansion of chair to old chair can fit inside the frame the ___ without adding another determiner, but the resulting old chair normally still requires a determiner as an independent singular count argument.
