## Meaning 1

Property attribution to nominal head

- *old men*: Direct property attribution; Attribution of age property to men
- *other men*: Special case where property is relative to context; Complement set relation

## Constraints

- (structural) Mod must be AdjP with head Adj
- (position) AdjP occurs in pre-head position in Nom

## Projection

In a former president, former modifies the relation expressed by president. The president is former does not give the same reading. A pre-head adjective need not have a matching predicative use.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: Describing words in front of a noun

An adjective phrase placed before a noun says something about it, as in old men.

In old men, the word old says something about the men: they are old. The describing word comes before the noun it describes.

Not every case is that direct. In other men, the property depends on the context. And in a former president, former doesn't describe the person in the way old describes men. It modifies the relation that president expresses. This dictionary notes that an adjective placed before a noun doesn't have to work in the same way after the verb be.

### Teacher

Teaches pre-head adjective phrases in the nominal, and why attributive adjectives need not have predicative uses.

A pre-head adjective phrase attributes a property to the head noun: in old men, old attributes age to the men. Other men is a special case where the property is relative to context, with a complement-set relation. The modifier must be an adjective phrase headed by an adjective, in pre-head position in the nominal. A pre-head adjective needn't have a matching predicative use: in a former president, former modifies the relation expressed by president, and the president is former doesn't give the same reading. This dictionary treats this as a specific type of pre-head modification.

### Child

Which word tells you more about the men in old men?

A describing word can come before a noun. In old men, the word old tells us about the men. The describing word comes first.
