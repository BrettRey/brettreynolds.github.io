## Meaning 1

Independent personal genitive pronoun functioning as an NP

- *The red one is [mine].*: Independent personal genitive
- *Those seats are [theirs].*: Independent personal genitive

## Projection

A form such as mine can occupy an NP slot without an overt noun, as in mine fell. The dependent counterpart my requires a nominal. His is formally shared across the two slots.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: Mine, yours, hers and the rest

The words mine, yours, his, hers, ours and theirs can stand alone as a full phrase, with no noun after them.

In The red one is mine, the word mine says whose the red one is, and nothing follows it. Those seats are theirs works the same way. Each of these words fills a whole phrase's spot on its own.

Compare my. It goes before a noun, as in my book. The word his is the same in both uses: his book and The book is his.
