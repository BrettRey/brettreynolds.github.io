## Meaning 1

Allows the number of an embedded complement to determine agreement in a licensed nominal frame.

- *A number of applicants [were waiting].*: Constructed illustration.

## Related constructions

- A kind of: Verb Number Agreement (`verb-number-agreement-001`)
- Compare: Measure Pseudopartitive with Of (`measure-pseudopartitive-001`): The plural in A number of applicants were waiting follows the of-complement. Compare an amount reading with singular agreement in Three litres of milk was enough.

## Sources

- Huddleston, Rodney & Geoffrey K. Pullum (2002), The Cambridge Grammar of the English Language. Cambridge: CUP, Ch. 5 §18.2 (discusses; bears on projection)

## Projection

The plural is licensed despite singular number. With the number of applicants, the nominal normally denotes the numerical quantity and takes singular agreement.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: When the verb follows the noun after of

In a number of applicants, the verb agrees with applicants, not with the singular word number.

In A number of applicants were waiting, the word number is singular, yet the verb is plural. The verb follows the plural applicants after of.

Compare The number of applicants. There, number normally names a quantity, and the verb goes with it: singular. Three litres of milk was enough works the same way, with a singular verb for an amount.
