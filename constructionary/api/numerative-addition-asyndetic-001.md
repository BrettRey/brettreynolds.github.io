## Meaning 1

Additive numerative formed by juxtaposing magnitude and addition without and

- *two hundred five*: 200 + 5 (asyndetic addition)
- *two thousand seven*: 2000 + 7 (asyndetic addition)

## Constraints

- (structural) Coordination targets numerative constituents; the coordinator and is omitted.
- (structural) Recursive: the addition coordinate can itself be a basic numerative or a magnitude phrase; its category may be DP or NP (category difference is tolerated under likeness of function).
- (pragmatic) Strongly preferred in AmE and in faster or less formal registers.

## Projection

In two hundred five, five is added to a complete magnitude phrase. It contributes the same remainder as five in two hundred and five even though there is no spoken coordinator. This differs from a factor selecting a magnitude, as in five hundred.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: Big numbers said without and

Some numbers are built by putting a small number straight after a large one, with no and between them.

In two hundred five, the five is added to two hundred, even though nothing joins them. It means the same as two hundred and five. Two thousand seven works the same way.

This dictionary says the version without and is strongly preferred in American English and in faster or less formal speech. The five here is an addition to a complete amount. That differs from five hundred, where five says how many hundreds.
