## Meaning 1

Specifies a cardinality formed by additive composition of tens and ones

- *twenty-one*: 20 + 1
- *forty-two*: 40 + 2
- *ninety-nine*: 90 + 9

## Constraints

- (structural) Requires a decade base with a single-digit addition; excludes *twenty-ten.

## Projection

Forty-three expresses one lexicalized sum and supplies a base for forty-third. A coordinator inside forty and three produces a separate structure and is not the ordinary lexical compound.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: Numbers like twenty-one and ninety-nine

Numbers such as twenty-one, forty-two and ninety-nine join a tens word and a ones word.

Twenty-one is twenty plus one. Forty-two is forty plus two, and ninety-nine is ninety plus nine. Each is typically written with a hyphen and counts as one word.

A number like forty-three also supplies the base for forty-third. A version with and inside, like forty and three, would be a different structure, not the ordinary word.

### Teacher

Explains two-part numbers from 21 to 99 as single lexical units formed from a decade and a single digit.

A compound basic numerative is a decade word followed by a single-digit word, typically hyphenated: twenty-one is 20 + 1. It's a lexical compound below the magnitude boundary, with the same distribution as simple basic numeratives (determiner, factor, addition). Forty-three expresses one lexicalized sum and supplies a base for forty-third. It requires a decade base with a single-digit addition. A coordinator inside, as in forty and three, produces a separate structure and isn't the ordinary lexical compound. This dictionary marks this account as provisional.
