## Meaning 1

Specifies a cardinality in tens (20, 30, …, 90)

- *twenty*: Cardinality 20
- *seventy*: Cardinality 70

## Projection

Sixty can stand by itself and can supply the first component of sixty-four. The relation to six is transparent, but a new decade form cannot automatically be generated from any numerical expression by adding -ty.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: Number words for whole tens

The words twenty to ninety each name a number of tens.

Twenty means 20, and seventy means 70. These words name whole tens, from twenty up to ninety, and each is built from a single-digit base with the ending -ty.

Sixty stands by itself as a word, and it also supplies the first part of sixty-four. Its link to six is easy to see. But you can't make a new tens word by adding -ty to just any number expression.

### Teacher

Covers the tens words twenty to ninety, built with -ty, as the base for numbers like sixty-four.

A decade numerative specifies a cardinality in tens (20, 30, ..., 90), formed from a single-digit base and the suffix -ty. It's a basic numerative, so it can serve as determiner or factor, and it combines with single digits to form compounds such as twenty-one. Sixty can stand alone and can supply the first component of sixty-four. The relation to six is transparent, but a new decade form can't automatically be generated from any numerical expression by adding -ty. The dictionary treats the decade endings as lexical word formation, and marks this account as provisional.
