## Meaning 1

Numerative used as a standalone DP/NP without an overt head noun

- *She bought twenty books, but I bought [twenty-two].*: Fused determiner-head use
- *How many thousand? [Two].*: Factor substitution (fragment answering the factor constituent, not a fused head)

## Constraints

- (structural) Functions as a fused determiner-head in NP structure; may license partitive *of*-complements.

## Projection

In *Lee bought thirty books and I bought forty*, the second numerative fills the object NP and can recover the kind from context. In *Forty is even*, the bare form can instead name the number. Stand-alone shape does not fix the analysis.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: Number words that stand in for a whole phrase

A number word can stand alone when the thing counted is clear from the situation.

In *She bought twenty books, but I bought twenty-two*, the second number does the work of a whole phrase. It means *twenty-two books*, and we recover books from the first half of the sentence.

Not every bare number works this way. In *Forty is even*, the number names the number itself. And an answer like *Two* to the question *How many thousand?* fills in only the multiplier part of a number such as *two thousand*. This dictionary treats these as different readings of a similar-looking form.

### Teacher

Shows number words standing alone for a whole noun phrase, and separates that use from bare numbers that name numbers.

A numerative can fill a whole NP by itself, with the noun recovered from context. This dictionary calls this a fused determiner-head: one word has both the determiner function and the head function. A partitive *of*-complement may follow. Stand-alone shape doesn't settle the analysis: in *Lee bought thirty books and I bought forty*, *forty* fills the object NP and recovers books, but in *Forty is even* the bare form can name the number. The fragment *Two*, answering *How many thousand?*, substitutes for the factor in *two thousand* and isn't a fused head. This dictionary marks the analysis as provisional.
