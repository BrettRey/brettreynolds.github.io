## Meaning 1

Specifies rank or order position within a sequence

- *first*: Rank 1
- *twenty-second*: Rank 22

## Constraints

- (structural) Ordinal adjectives typically function as modifiers in NPs and can co-occur with determiners.

### Curious reader: Words for position in a line

Words like first and twenty-second say where something comes in an ordered sequence.

Words like first, second and twenty-second give rank or position. They're built from a number word by adding -th, or they're special forms, as with first, second and third.

They work like describing words. In the third chapter, third sits before the noun alongside the word the. This dictionary separates that use from three thirds, where the same-looking word is a noun naming a fraction, with a plural ending and a number in front.

### Teacher

Helps explain ordinal words as adjectives, and how they differ from fraction nouns that look the same.

Ordinal adjectives specify rank or position. They're formed from a cardinal base plus -th or are suppletive (first, second, third). They head AdjPs and function as attributive modifiers or predicative complements, and they can co-occur with determiners: the third chapter. Twenty-second is a further example, at rank 22. The same form can be a fractional noun: in three thirds, third is a count noun with plural marking and a preceding numeral. This dictionary, which marks the analysis as provisional, uses distribution in the NP to tell the two apart.
