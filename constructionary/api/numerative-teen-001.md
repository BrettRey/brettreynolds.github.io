## Projection

The -teen word form gives a value ten greater than the base related to it. The same base element can give a different value with -ty, as thirteen versus thirty shows. The two endings do not have the same lexical or prosodic distribution.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: Thirteen to nineteen

The numbers from thirteen to nineteen are built from a single-digit word plus -teen.

Thirteen through nineteen are formed by adding -teen to a single-digit word. The -teen word has a value ten greater than the base it comes from, so thirteen is ten more than three.

The ending -ty works differently: the same base gives a different value, as thirteen and thirty show. The two endings also don't have the same distribution as words. Once built, a -teen word acts as one unit and can do the same jobs as a single-digit number.
