## Meaning 1

Suppletive teen numeratives for 11 and 12

- *eleven*: Cardinality 11
- *twelve*: Cardinality 12

## Constraints

## Projection

Eleven and twelve occupy the first two positions after ten but do not use productive -teen suffixation. Their related ordinals eleventh and twelfth can be compared with thirteenth without analyzing eleven and twelve as -teen stems.

### Curious reader: Eleven and twelve

Eleven and twelve sit right after ten, but they aren't built with -teen.

Eleven and twelve come right after ten in the counting order, but they don't use the -teen ending that thirteen and the rest use. They're their own forms, not made by adding anything to a single-digit word.

This dictionary treats them as separate words with the same jobs as the -teen numbers: in front of a noun, as a multiplier, or added to another number. Their position-words, eleventh and twelfth, can be compared with thirteenth without treating eleven and twelve as -teen words.

### Child

Do eleven and twelve end in teen like thirteen does?

Thirteen ends in teen. Eleven and twelve don't. They're special words that don't follow the teen pattern. You use them just like the other numbers.
