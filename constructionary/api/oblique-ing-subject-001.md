## Meaning 1

Expresses the subject of an *-ing* clause without genitive marking where the environment allows it.

- *We objected to [them blocking the exit].*: Constructed illustration.

## Projection

The prepositional context can license *them* as the embedded subject, and *blocking* still takes a direct object. Alternatives with a genitive vary with syntactic environment and style.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: *Them blocking the exit*

An *-ing* phrase can have its own subject, and a pronoun there can take its object form, as in *them blocking the exit*.

In *We objected to them blocking the exit*, the *-ing* phrase *them blocking the exit* has its own subject, *them*. The pronoun has the form used for objects, not a genitive form such as *their*.

The word *them* is the doer of the blocking, not the thing objected to. And *blocking* still has its own object, the exit. This dictionary says that where a genitive form is an alternative, the choice varies with the surrounding grammar and with style, and that this pattern is the one with no genitive marking.
