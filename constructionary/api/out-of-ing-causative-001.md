# Out-of Causative with an -ing Clause

- id: `out-of-ing-causative-001`
- pattern: VP[Head:V + Od:NPᵢ + Comp:PP[out of + gerund-participial Clause with understood subjectᵢ]]
- status: provisional analysis; evidence level: unassessed
- linguist edition: https://brettreynolds.ca/constructionary/linguist/out-of-ing-causative-001/
- licence: CC BY 4.0; snapshot 2026-11-19 (fa37529)

## Meaning 1

Presents the object referent as caused to abandon an action or prevented from carrying it out.

Talk someone out of leaving can prevent a prospective departure. Other uses involve stopping an ongoing course of action or losing an opportunity through deception. The object supplies the understood subject of the gerund-participial. A report of trying, intending, or planning to bring this about does not assert that the prevention occurred.

- *They [talked Lee out of leaving].*: Constructed prospective action. Lee is understood as the person who would leave.
- *I [cheated you all out of being in my life].*: Published corpus example. The opportunity is lost rather than supplied.

## Related constructions

- Compare: Into-Causative (`into-causative-001`): Both link the matrix object to the understood subject of a gerund-participial and contrast in the result.
- Compare: Caused-Motion Construction (`caused-motion-001`): The literal counterpart can have a spatial source after out of.

## Projection

The affected object is understood as the participant in the action that is prevented, abandoned, or made unavailable. The paired into-frame can express the opposite outcome.

### Curious reader: Talked out of doing something

In They talked Lee out of leaving, the person after the verb is steered away from an action.

In They talked Lee out of leaving, Lee is the person who would have left, and the talking is aimed at stopping that. The person after the verb is understood as the doer of the action in the -ing phrase.

This dictionary describes the meaning as causing someone to abandon an action or preventing them from carrying it out. Other uses involve stopping an ongoing course of action, or losing an opportunity through deception, as in I cheated you all out of being in my life. A report of trying to talk someone out of something doesn't say that it worked.
