## Meaning 1

Presents something directly perceived as an occurrence.

- *We [heard Kai close the latch].*: Constructed illustration.
- *We [heard Kai closing the latch].*: Constructed comparison. Progressive-shaped comparison presents a phase of the perceptible event.

## Related constructions

- A kind of: Verb Phrase (`vp-001`)
- Alternates with: Perception with a Gerund-Participial (`perception-gerund-participial-001`): Heard Kai close the latch presents the occurrence as a whole. Heard Kai closing the latch presents it in progress.

## Projection

The plain form follows the perceived participant. The construction can present the closing as a whole, while heard Kai closing the latch can target an ongoing phase.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: Hearing or seeing something happen

A verb of perceiving, a person or thing, and a plain verb form together present what was perceived as an event.

In We heard Kai close the latch, the closing is something we perceived directly, and the plain verb form close names it. Here the closing can be presented as a whole event.

Compare We heard Kai closing the latch. The -ing form presents the closing as it was going on, a stretch of the event rather than the whole of it. Both sentences report hearing, and the choice between the plain form and the -ing form changes how the event is framed.

### Teacher

Useful for showing how a plain verb form after see or hear frames an event as a whole, compared with an -ing form.

In this pattern a perception verb is followed by an NP and a bare infinitival: heard Kai close the latch. The NP is the perceived participant and the plain form follows it. The construction can present the event as a whole, while the gerund-participial alternative (heard Kai closing the latch) can target an ongoing phase. This dictionary marks this contrast as provisional, with medium confidence, and cites CGEL on perception complements.

### Child

What is the difference between hearing someone close a door and hearing someone closing it?

Both sentences tell what you heard. We heard Kai close the latch can mean the whole closing. We heard Kai closing the latch can mean it was happening as we listened.
