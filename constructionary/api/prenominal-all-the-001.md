## Meaning 1

Applies universal quantification outside a following determiner phrase or constituent.

- *They catalogued [all those shells].*: Constructed illustration.

## Related constructions

- A kind of: Determined Noun Phrase (`np-det-001`)
- Compare: Each in a Partitive NP (`each-of-partitive-001`)

## Sources

- Huddleston, Rodney & Geoffrey K. Pullum (2002), The Cambridge Grammar of the English Language. Cambridge: CUP, Ch. 5 §§7.3, 12 (discusses; bears on projection)

## Projection

All can precede those and the in the same NP. Each and every do not generally occupy that same position before an existing central determiner.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: All before this, that or the

All can stand before a determiner such as those or the, applying to the whole group named.

In They catalogued all those shells, all comes before those and takes in every shell in the group that those picks out. The two words sit one after the other in the same phrase.

All can also come before the, as in all the shells. This dictionary notes that each and every don't generally take that same place before a determiner that is already there.
