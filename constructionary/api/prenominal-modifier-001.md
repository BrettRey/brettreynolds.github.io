## Meaning 1

Category/kind modification with property attribution

- *old men*: Prototypical property-denoting usage; Direct property modification (age attribute)
- *other men*: Requires contextual reference set; Complementary set relation

## Constraints

- (syntactic) The modifier function must be realized by an AdjP; the lexical head is the plural noun
- (morphological) Head must be plural

## Projection

In a clause such as Old men were waiting, the modified bare plural can be an ordinary subject without an article. In the same episodic use an old man needs the article. The difference in bare licensing follows the head’s count and number profile and survives expansion to very old.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: Plural nouns with an adjective in front

A plural noun with an adjective before it, such as old men, can stand as a subject without a word like the or a.

In old men, the adjective old tells us a property of the men, here their age. In other men, the adjective needs a surrounding set of men to make sense, because other points to the rest of a set.

A plural noun like this can be a sentence subject without a small word in front: Old men were waiting. A single man isn't the same: in the same kind of sentence, an old man needs the article a. Adding words such as very to the adjective, as in very old men, doesn't change that.

### Teacher

Covers plural nouns modified by a prenominal adjective, which can serve as subjects with no article.

The pattern is a plural noun as lexical head of a nominal, with an AdjP modifier before it: old men. It expresses category or kind modification with property attribution. Old men is direct property modification (an age attribute); other men requires a contextual reference set and expresses a complementary-set relation. The modifier must be an AdjP and the head must be plural. In an episodic clause such as Old men were waiting, the modified bare plural can be an ordinary subject without an article, while an old man needs the article. The licensing follows the head's count and number profile and survives expansion to very old. This dictionary marks this as provisional, with medium confidence.
