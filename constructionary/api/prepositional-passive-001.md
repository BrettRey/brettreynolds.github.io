## Meaning 1

Allows the complement of a preposition to correspond to the subject of a passive.

- *This proposal [was referred to] repeatedly.*: Constructed illustration.

## Related constructions

## Projection

To remains part of the passive VP. Selection by the verb strongly supports the construction, and some independently licensed adjunct PPs can also occur under restricted conditions.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: Passives that leave a small word behind

The thing that follows a preposition can become the subject of a passive sentence, leaving the preposition behind after the verb.

In This proposal was referred to repeatedly, the subject, this proposal, would follow to in an ordinary active sentence. In the passive it becomes the subject, and to stays behind after the verb.

Selected prepositions, the ones a verb takes, are a major source of this pattern. Jo was spoken to works the same way. This dictionary also notes that some other prepositional phrases can take part under restricted conditions.

### Teacher

Covers passives whose subject corresponds to the complement of a preposition, with the preposition stranded.

In a prepositional passive the complement of a preposition corresponds to the subject of the passive: This proposal was referred to repeatedly. The preposition is stranded after the verb and remains part of the passive VP. Selection by the verb strongly supports the construction, and some independently licensed adjunct PPs can also occur under restricted conditions. This dictionary treats the pattern as a specialization of the be-passive, and marks it provisional, with medium confidence.
