# Progressive -ing

## Meaning 1

Marks an ongoing or unfolding situation at a reference time (imperfective viewpoint; endpoints are not asserted)

- *She is reading*: Situation in progress
- *They were walking home*: Ongoing event in past time frame
- *She was building a house*: Ongoing process; does not entail completion
- *She built a house*: Perfective contrast with completion entailment

## Meaning 2

Marks current-phase or contingent construal (often contrasted with a default habitual/simple-present reading)

- *He is living in Chicago*: Current-phase framing of residence
- *The server is running slow today*: Contingent framing of a state
- *I'm living in Mississauga*: Current/contingent residence framing

## Projection

Be plus a gerund-participle typically presents an activity in progress or a current phase. An -ing complement in Jo avoided staying does not itself have progressive aspect. A future adjunct can instead give the progressive an arrangement reading.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: Being plus -ing for things in progress

A form of be plus a verb ending in -ing presents something as ongoing or unfolding at a particular time.

In She is reading, the action is in progress. In They were walking home, it was going on at some time in the past. In She was building a house, the house building is ongoing, and the sentence doesn't say it was ever finished. Compare She built a house, which does tell us it was completed.

The same form can also frame something as current or temporary, as in He is living in Chicago or The server is running slow today. This dictionary marks the account as provisional.

### Teacher

Core explanation of the be plus -ing progressive, with its two readings and a contrast with the simple past.

The progressive is be plus a gerund-participle. Its first reading marks an ongoing or unfolding situation at a reference time (imperfective viewpoint), where endpoints aren't asserted: She was building a house doesn't entail completion, while She built a house does. Its second reading marks a current-phase or contingent construal, often contrasted with a default habitual or simple-present reading (He is living in Chicago). The progressive auxiliary and its gerund-participial complement form a VP headed by be. This dictionary marks the account provisional. A future adjunct can give the progressive an arrangement reading.

### Child

Is the house finished if she was building it?

Words like is, was and were plus an -ing word can show something going on. She was building a house means she was in the middle of it. She built a house means it got done.
