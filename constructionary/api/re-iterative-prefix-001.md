## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: *Re-* meaning do it again

Putting *re-* on the front of some verbs makes a new verb meaning to do the action again.

In *Please recheck the numbers*, *recheck* means to check them another time. The normal reading takes it for granted that there was an earlier check.

A word that only starts with the same letters, such as *reject*, doesn't necessarily mean again in the same way. This dictionary marks the account as provisional.
