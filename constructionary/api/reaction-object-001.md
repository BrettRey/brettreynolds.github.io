# Reaction Object

- id: `reaction-object-001`
- pattern: VP[Head:V[expression or action] + NP[reaction expressed]]
- status: provisional analysis; evidence level: unassessed
- linguist edition: https://brettreynolds.ca/constructionary/linguist/reaction-object-001/
- licence: CC BY 4.0; snapshot 2026-11-19 (5c11b28)

## Meaning 1

Expresses a reaction through the action named by the verb.

In smiled her thanks, the smile is the means of expressing thanks. The NP can denote an attitude, communicative response, or emotion. The verb need not ordinarily take an object of this type. The sources distinguish the pattern from a cognate object naming the event itself and report restrictions on passive versions.

- *She [nodded satisfaction].*: Published example. The NP denotes the attitude expressed by the nod.
- *He [grunted assent].*: COCA example of a bare response NP.

## Projection

An action verb can combine with an NP describing what the action expresses, even when its ordinary intransitive use has no such NP.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: Nodding satisfaction

An action verb can take a noun naming the reaction it expresses.

In She nodded satisfaction, the nod is how the satisfaction is shown. In He grunted assent, the grunt expresses agreement. The noun can name an attitude, a communicative response or an emotion.

The verb needn't ordinarily take an object of this kind. In smiled her thanks, the smile is the means of expressing thanks. This dictionary marks the account as provisional.
