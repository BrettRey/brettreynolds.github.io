# Recipient Passive with a Retained Object

- id: `recipient-passive-retained-object-001`

## Meaning 1

Makes the recipient the passive subject while keeping the theme in object position.

- *Lee [was sent a replacement key].*: Constructed illustration.
- *[A replacement key was sent to Lee].*: Constructed comparison. Theme subject with an oblique recipient.

## Related constructions

- A kind of: Verbal Be-Passive (`be-passive-001`)
- Interacts with: Ditransitive VP (Double-object Construction) (`ditransitive-vp-001`): The recipient corresponds to the indirect object of the active frame.
- Compare: Monotransitive Transfer with To (`transitive-to-recipient-001`)

## Projection

The replacement key remains an ordinary NP complement of sent. In A replacement key was sent to Lee, the theme instead controls agreement and the recipient is an oblique.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: Being sent something

A passive sentence can make the person who receives something the subject while the thing received stays after the verb.

In Lee was sent a replacement key, Lee is the recipient and the subject of the sentence. The replacement key still sits after the verb, as an ordinary part of it.

Compare A replacement key was sent to Lee, where the key is the subject and Lee comes in a phrase with to. The two sentences describe the same sending but arrange the people and things differently. This dictionary marks the account as provisional.
