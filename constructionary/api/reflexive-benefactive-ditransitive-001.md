## Meaning 1

Presents the subject’s action as securing goods or a resource for the subject.

- *Jo [found herself a quieter room].*: Constructed illustration.

## Related constructions

- Interacts with: Ditransitive VP (Double-object Construction) (`ditransitive-vp-001`): Its two postverbal NPs are indirect and direct object.
- Compare: Direct-Object Reflexive Anaphor (`np-pron-reflexive-001`): Here the reflexive is indirect rather than direct object.

## Projection

Jo is understood as the intended beneficiary and room is the acquired resource. In found herself in a quieter room, herself is instead the direct object and the PP locates Jo.

### Curious reader: Finding yourself a room

A sentence can show someone securing something for themselves by using a reflexive word, like herself, right after the verb.

In Jo found herself a quieter room, Jo is both the finder and the person who benefits. The word herself comes right after found, and the room follows. The sentence presents Jo's action as securing a resource for Jo.

Compare Jo found herself in a quieter room, where herself is the direct object and the phrase in a quieter room says where Jo was. This dictionary marks the account as provisional.
