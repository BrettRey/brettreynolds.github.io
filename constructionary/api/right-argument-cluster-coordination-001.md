## Meaning 1

Coordinates corresponding postverbal bundles that need not form ordinary independent phrases.

- *I took [Jo to the station and Lee home].*: Constructed illustration.

## Projection

Each person pairs with a separate goal under one overt verb. Lee home is the object and goal sequence, and is not an ordinary nominal constituent.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: Joining pairs of things after one verb

One verb can serve two pairs of things joined by and, even though each pair isn't an ordinary phrase.

In I took Jo to the station and Lee home, the verb took appears once but works for two jobs. Jo goes with the station, and Lee goes with home. Each person pairs with a separate destination.

What's joined by and isn't an ordinary phrase: Jo to the station and Lee home are bundles of two things that follow the verb. Lee home, for example, is a person plus a destination. This dictionary marks the analysis as provisional.
