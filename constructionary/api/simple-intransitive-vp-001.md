## Meaning 1

Presents a one-place predication without a core object or predicative complement.

- *The guests [laughed].*: Constructed illustration.

## Projection

An adjunct may be added without changing the verb's core frame. An NP after the verb can instead require a separately licensed construction, as with a cognate event object.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: Verbs that stand alone

Some verbs need nothing after them to make a complete statement about someone or something.

In The guests laughed, the verb laughed is enough. It says one thing about one thing, the guests, and needs no object or other required partner. Extras can still be added, such as when or where it happened, without changing what the verb itself needs.

This dictionary calls this the simple intransitive pattern. A noun phrase after such a verb isn't automatically part of it. Something like a cognate event object, where the object repeats the verb's meaning, is treated as a separate pattern. The analysis is marked provisional.

In a simple intransitive VP, the head verb has no core complement slot in the use at issue: it presents a one-place predication without a core object or predicative complement. An NP after the verb can instead require a separately licensed construction, as with a cognate event object.
