# Some N or Other

- id: `some-n-or-other-001`
- pattern: NP[some + singular count Nom + or other]
- status: provisional analysis; evidence level: unassessed

## Meaning 1

Introduces an individual while leaving a more specific identification open.

The whole pattern licenses bare other. An immediate identifying continuation with namely is degraded in Abenina-Adar’s comparison with an ordinary indefinite. CGEL suggests a modifier analysis for the tail. A coordinate analysis is also compatible with the pairing. The use by speakers who know the identity but choose to omit it needs a separate test.

- *[Some student or other] was waiting outside the door.*: Introduces a student with their identity left open.
- *[some book or other]*: Published comparison with a book and an immediate identifying continuation.

### Curious reader: Some something or other

Some student or other introduces someone while leaving open who exactly it was.

In Some student or other was waiting outside the door, there was a student, and the sentence doesn't say which. The words or other signal that the identity is left open.

The pattern is an ordinary kind of indefinite phrase with a particular effect. One published comparison found that following it straight away with an identifying remark is degraded, compared with a plain some book. Whether it fits a speaker who knows the identity but leaves it out is something this dictionary says still needs testing. The analysis is marked provisional.
