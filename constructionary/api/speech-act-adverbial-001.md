## Meaning 1

Characterizes the speaker’s manner or purpose in making the utterance.

- *[Frankly], the repair won’t last.*: Constructed illustration.

## Projection

Frankly characterizes the speaker’s statement, not the repair's manner of failing. A paraphrase can mention speaking frankly without changing the content asserted.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: Words about how you're speaking

An adverb like frankly can describe how or why the speaker is making the statement, not what the statement is about.

In Frankly, the repair won't last, the word frankly isn't about the repair. It describes the speaker's way of speaking: the speaker is being frank in saying this.

You can check by paraphrase. Something like I'm speaking frankly captures it, and the content asserted stays the same: the repair won't last. This dictionary marks the analysis as provisional.
