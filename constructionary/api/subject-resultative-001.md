## Meaning 1

Uses a postverbal complement to specify the state the subject reaches.

- *The basin [froze solid].*: Constructed illustration.

## Projection

Solid describes the outcome of freezing, whereas a depictive predicate ordinarily holds during the event. No separate object licenses solid in this use.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: Saying how the subject ends up

A word after the verb can tell you what state the subject reaches, as in froze solid.

In The basin froze solid, the word solid says what state the basin ended up in. It describes the outcome of the freezing, and no other object is needed to make it work.

That makes it different from a word that describes how someone was while an action happened. This dictionary notes that this second kind ordinarily holds during the event, while the result word marks where the event ends up.

This dictionary marks the analysis as provisional and rests on one illustration.

### Teacher

Helps students distinguish a result state that the subject reaches from a description that holds during the action.

The pattern is a verb followed by a predicative complement that specifies the state the subject reaches. In The basin froze solid, solid describes the outcome of freezing. A depictive predicate, by contrast, ordinarily holds during the event, as in Jo arrived hungry. No separate object licenses solid here. This dictionary marks the analysis provisional, rests on one constructed example, and names a related object-oriented resultative as a separate entry.
