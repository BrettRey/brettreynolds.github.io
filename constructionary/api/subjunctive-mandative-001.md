## Meaning 1

Presents the content of a requirement, recommendation, or request.

- *They requested [that the gate remain closed].*: Constructed illustration.
- *They requested [that the gate not be opened].*: Constructed comparison. The subjunctive uses plain be after not, without support from do.

## Projection

Remain does not carry third-person singular marking. With negative not the plain form can occur without finite do, as in that it not remain closed.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: Clauses that state a requirement

After words like requested, a that-clause can use the plain form of the verb to say what is required or recommended.

In They requested that the gate remain closed, the clause after that gives the content of a request. The verb remain is in its plain form: it doesn't take an -s even though the gate is singular.

The plain form also shows up with not. In that the gate not be opened, not comes before plain be, and no do is needed. The grammar books call this the subjunctive.

This dictionary marks the analysis as provisional and gives only constructed examples.
