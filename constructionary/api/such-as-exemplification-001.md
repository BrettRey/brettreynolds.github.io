## Meaning 1

Introduces one or more illustrative members of the class just described.

- *Bring safety gear [such as gloves and goggles].*: Constructed illustration.

## Projection

The examples do not exhaust the class. Other suitable safety gear can meet the instruction without being on the example list.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: Giving examples with *such as*

*Such as* introduces some examples of the kind of thing you've just named.

In *Bring safety gear such as gloves and goggles*, *such as* introduces examples of safety gear. The phrase is an offer of illustration, not a full list.

That means other safety gear can still meet the instruction. Someone who brings a helmet hasn't ignored the request just because a helmet wasn't in the list.

This dictionary marks the analysis as provisional and rests on one constructed illustration.

### Child

When you say *such as*, do you name everything?

*Such as* lets you name a few examples. It doesn't have to name everything. Other things of the same kind can fit too.
