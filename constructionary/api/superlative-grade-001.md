## Meaning 1

Places the chosen instance at an extreme relative to a contextually delimited set.

- *Jo chose the [narrowest of the three aisles].*: Constructed illustration.

## Projection

Of the three restricts the comparison set. The aisle need not count as narrow by an independent contextual standard.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: The most extreme of a group

A word like narrowest picks one item as the extreme within a set the speaker has in mind.

In Jo chose the narrowest of the three aisles, narrowest picks out one aisle at the extreme end of a comparison. The comparison set is the three aisles, and of the three marks it.

The set limits what the word is compared with. So the chosen aisle needn't be narrow by any other standard. It's the narrowest only among those three.

This dictionary marks the analysis as provisional and rests on one constructed illustration.
