## Meaning 1

Builds a compound whose first element supplies a participant associated with the verbal base.

- *They hired a [window-cleaner].*: Constructed illustration.

## Projection

In the agentive reading the eventual cleaner cleans windows. Ordinary nondeverbal compounds can connect their two nouns through a much wider range of contextually inferred relations.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: Nouns like window-cleaner

In its agentive reading, a compound like window-cleaner names a person by the thing they work on and what they do.

In They hired a window-cleaner, the first part, window, names what is acted on, and cleaner names the one who does the action. A window-cleaner is someone who cleans windows.

Other compounds are looser. A compound made of two ordinary nouns can connect them through a much wider range of relations, which the listener works out from context. In the pattern here, the first part matches a participant in the action of the verb.
