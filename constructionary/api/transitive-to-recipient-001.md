# Monotransitive Transfer with To

## Meaning 1

Packages a transferred item as direct object and its intended recipient as a to-phrase.

- *Kim [sent the keys to Lee].*: Constructed illustration.

## Related constructions

- Compare: Heavy Complement Postposing (`heavy-np-shift-001`)

- Alternates with: Ditransitive VP (Double-object Construction) (`ditransitive-vp-001`): Send licenses both frames. Other verbs, including explain in mainstream standard English, select only the to frame in this sense.

## Projection

The Theme can be the ordinary passive subject, and certain verbs such as explain allow a recipient to-phrase while resisting double objects in mainstream standard usage.

## Reader editions (draft, AI-assisted, unreviewed; the linguist entry is the reference)

### Curious reader: Passing something to someone with to

A verb can name the thing passed along, followed by a to-phrase naming who it is meant for.

In Kim sent the keys to Lee, the keys are what is passed along and Lee is the person they are meant for. The thing comes right after the verb, and the receiver comes in a phrase beginning with to.

Some verbs, such as send, also allow a version with the receiver first and no to: Kim sent Lee the keys. Other verbs allow only the to version. According to this dictionary, explain is one of these in mainstream standard English. The dictionary marks this account as provisional.

### Teacher

Helps explain verbs of transfer that take a direct object plus a to-phrase for the recipient.

The pattern is a monotransitive VP: the head verb, a Theme noun phrase as direct object, and a prepositional phrase with to whose noun phrase is the recipient. It is a specialization of the monotransitive VP. Send licenses this frame and the double-object frame, so the two alternate. Other verbs, including explain in mainstream standard English, select only the to frame in this sense. The Theme can be the ordinary passive subject. This dictionary marks its analysis provisional, so present verb-by-verb licensing as something to check, not predict.

### Child

What do you say when you hand the keys to someone?

Some sentences name what is passed first. Then they say who it is for, starting with to. Kim sent the keys to Lee.
