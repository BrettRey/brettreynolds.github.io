## Meaning 1

Presents the condition as an exception to the main claim.

- *The door will stay locked [unless Lee comes back].*: Constructed illustration.

## Related constructions

- Compare: Conditional Provided That (`sufficiency-provided-that-001`)

## Sources

- Huddleston, Rodney & Geoffrey K. Pullum (2002), The Cambridge Grammar of the English Language. Cambridge: CUP, Ch. 8 §14.4, unless (discusses; bears on projection)

## Projection

Outside cases in which Lee comes back, the door remains locked. The sentence need not determine what happens if Lee does return.

### Curious reader: Unless as an exception

A phrase starting with unless presents a condition as an exception to the main claim.

In The door will stay locked unless Lee comes back, the part with unless presents a condition as the exception. Outside the cases in which Lee comes back, the door remains locked.

The sentence doesn't need to say what happens if Lee does return. It only tells us what to expect in the ordinary case, and flags the exception.

The dictionary marks this account as provisional.
