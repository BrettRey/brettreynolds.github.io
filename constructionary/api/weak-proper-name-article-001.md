## Meaning 1

Realizes a conventional proper name that uses an article in ordinary argument position.

- *They crossed [the Thames].*: Constructed illustration.

## Projection

The article forms part of the ordinary naming pattern, while a label or compound can omit it in a suitable form. Arbitrary replacement with a does not preserve the primary name use.

### Teacher

Helps explain proper names that conventionally take an article.

A weak proper name is a noun phrase in which a lexically conventional article combines with a name expression, as in the Thames. It realizes a conventional proper name in ordinary argument position. The article forms part of the ordinary naming pattern, while a label or compound can omit it in a suitable form. Arbitrary replacement with a does not preserve the primary name use. This dictionary contrasts this with strong proper names, which have no such article, and marks its analysis provisional.
