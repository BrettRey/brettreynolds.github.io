## Projection

In the literal reading, with a small c specifies lowercase c without committing the speaker to an account of word category. A nomenclatural convention or a descriptive contrast supplies the interpretation in context.

### Curious reader: With a small letter

Adding with a small c (or another letter) says a word is written in lowercase, which can point to an everyday meaning.

Catholic with a small c points to the common descriptive sense, not the institution. Romantic with a small r points to the ordinary sense of the word.
