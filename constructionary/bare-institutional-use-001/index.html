At school, with no the

Some everyday nouns, such as school, can appear with no article when they refer to an activity or status.

Early sketch. The full entry for this pattern has one example so far and its evidence isn’t written up yet, so treat this page as a starting point.

In The children are at school, the word school has no the or a before it. It can describe school as a setting for an activity, without picking out one particular building in the same way as at the school.

At the school can instead point to a particular building. Not every noun works this way, and whether it does depends on the noun, the context and the variety of English. Went to school keeps school bare, while took the bus keeps the.

Examples

  • The children are at school.
