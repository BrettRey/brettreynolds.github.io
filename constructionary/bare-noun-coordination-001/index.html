Two bare nouns joined by and

Two singular nouns with no article can be joined by and to name a pair, as in husband and wife.

In between husband and wife, neither noun has a the or a before it, yet together they name a pair. The same happens in He had pad and pencil, where the two items are introduced as new.

Context can make the pair work as the subject or object of a sentence. In Bride and dog can be customized!, the bride and the dog were already described earlier, so the pair is easy to identify. The pair can be a familiar one or a new one, as long as the link between the two is recoverable.

Examples

  • between husband and wife
  • Bride and dog can be customized!
  • He had pad and pencil.
