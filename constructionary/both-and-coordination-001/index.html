Both a and b

Both before the first item and and before the second say outright that two things are included.

Early sketch. The full entry for this pattern has one example so far and its evidence isn’t written up yet, so treat this page as a starting point.

In We need both a permit and an inspection, both comes before the first item and and comes before the second. Together they show clearly that we need the permit and the inspection.

Both marks the first item, and and marks the second. Both can also appear alone before a noun phrase, but that's a different use, with a different relation to the words that follow.

Examples

  • We need both a permit and an inspection.
  • I like both cats and dogs.
