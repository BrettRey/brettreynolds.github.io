Next Tuesday, with no on

Words like this, last and next before a day or calendar period say when something happens, measured from now.

Early sketch. The full entry for this pattern has one example so far and its evidence isn’t written up yet, so treat this page as a starting point.

In I'll call next Tuesday, the phrase next Tuesday tells us when, and it needs no on in front. The word next picks out a Tuesday by counting from the present moment. This, last and next work this way.

Which Tuesday counts as next can vary between speakers, since it depends on how next is resolved. The context of the present is what fixes the period.

Examples

  • I’ll call next Tuesday.
  • We met last Friday.

Try it

Say I'll call next Tuesday. Which Tuesday do you mean? Ask a friend which one they would pick.
