How can a sentence say no without the word not?

Words like nobody, nothing and none make a sentence mean no all by themselves. You don't need not. You can also say the idea another way, with didn't and anything.

Examples

  • Nobody ate the last cookie.
  • I found nothing under the bed.
  • I didn't find anything under the bed.

Play

Say a sentence with nobody or nothing. Then say it again with didn't and anybody or anything. Take turns.
