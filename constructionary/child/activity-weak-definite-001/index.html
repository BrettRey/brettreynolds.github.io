For young readers

When you say "We took the bus", do you mean one special bus?

We took the bus can be about travelling, without one special bus in mind. Lee parked the bus is about one particular bus. The verb matters, and so does the noun.

Examples

  • We took the bus.
  • Lee parked the bus.

Play

Say each sentence and ask a partner, "Which bus?" Do you need to know which bus in each one? Try it with other sentences of your own.
