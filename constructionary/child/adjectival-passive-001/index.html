For young readers

Is "locked" something a person does, or how the door is?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

In The door remained locked, locked tells us how the door is. It doesn't say who locked it. In The door was locked by security at noon, we hear about something that happened.

Examples

  • The door remained locked.
  • The window remained closed.

Play

Think of things that stay in a state, like closed or broken. Say a sentence with remained for each one, and tell a partner how the thing is.
