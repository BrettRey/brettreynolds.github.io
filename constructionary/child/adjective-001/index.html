For young readers

Can you say "an angry neighbour" and also "the neighbour is angry"?

Adjectives are words that tell us what something is like. Tall, wooden and happy are adjectives. They can go before a noun, and they can go after is.

Examples

  • a tall tree
  • The tree is tall.
  • a wooden spoon

Play

Pick a describing word like tall, happy or wooden. Say it before a noun, then after is. Find three that work both ways.
