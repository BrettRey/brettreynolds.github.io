For young readers

Three hours is more than one hour. So why can we say "three hours is enough"?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

Three hours is a plural-looking phrase, but we can treat it as one amount of time. Then we can say is. Three litres is enough works the same way.

Examples

  • Three hours is enough.
  • Three litres is enough.

Play

Think of amounts like two hours, five kilometres or four litres. Say each one with is enough. Do they sound like one amount?
