When someone says "I hope not", what do they hope?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

Someone asks, "Is the screen broken?" You answer, "I hope not." Not stands for the whole idea that the screen is not broken. It saves you from saying the whole sentence.

Examples

  • “Is the screen broken?” “I hope not.”
  • “Is it raining?” “I hope not.”

Play

Ask a partner a yes-or-no question. They answer with I hope not. Then they say the whole sentence that those words stand for.
