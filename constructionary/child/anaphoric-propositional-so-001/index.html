When someone says "I think so", what does "so" stand for?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

Someone asks, "Are the keys inside?" You answer, "I think so." The word so stands for the whole idea that the keys are inside. It saves you saying it all again.

Examples

  • “Are the keys inside?” “I think so.”
  • “Is it Friday?” “I think so.”

Play

Ask a partner a yes-or-no question. They answer with I think so. Then they say the whole sentence that those words stand for.
