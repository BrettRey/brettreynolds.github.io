For young readers

In "Kai could too", what is Kai able to do?

You don't have to say the whole thing again. Nora could repair it, and Kai could too. We know Kai could repair it, because Nora's part told us.

Examples

  • Nora could repair it, and Kai could too.
  • Mia can swim, and Ben can too.

Play

Say something you can do, like skip. Your partner says and I can too. Then say what the second part left out.
