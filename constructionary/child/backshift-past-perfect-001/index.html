Which happened first, Jo leaving or the speaker arriving?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

When I arrived, Jo had already left. Here, Jo's leaving came first. Had plus a verb like left tells us that something happened earlier than another past moment.

Examples

  • When I arrived, Jo had already left.
  • By noon, she had finished her homework.

Play

Draw a timeline with two past events. Say which came first, using had for the earlier one. Swap with a partner.
