What's the difference between "at school" and "at the school"?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

At school can be about taking part in school. At the school can point to the building. Some words let us leave out the, and some don't.

Examples

  • The children are at school.
  • The children are at the school.

Play

Say a sentence with at school, then one with at the school. Ask a partner which one talks about the building.
