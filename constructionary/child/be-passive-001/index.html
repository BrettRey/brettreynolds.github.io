For young readers

In "My surfboard was stolen", who is missing from the sentence?

You can start a sentence with the thing something happened to. My surfboard was stolen by Pat. If we don't need to say who did it, we can leave out by Pat.

Examples

  • My surfboard was stolen by Pat.
  • My surfboard was stolen.

Play

Make up a sentence like Pat stole my surfboard. Turn it around so the thing comes first. Then try it without the doer.
