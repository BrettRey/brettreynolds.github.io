What does "both" tell us in "both a permit and an inspection"?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

Both and and work together. Both comes first and and comes before the second thing. They tell us that we need the two things.

Examples

  • We need both a permit and an inspection.
  • I like both cats and dogs.

Play

Say what you like using both and and, like both apples and pears. Take turns with a partner and name two things each time.
