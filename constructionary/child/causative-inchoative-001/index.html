Can a window crack without anyone saying who cracked it?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

Sometimes a sentence tells who made a change. The heat cracked the window. Sometimes it just tells what changed. The window cracked.

Examples

  • The heat cracked the window, and the window cracked.
  • Mia melted the ice, and the ice melted.

Play

Think of a change, like melting or cracking. Say one sentence with a cause and one with only the thing that changed.
