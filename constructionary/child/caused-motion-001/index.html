For young readers

Can a sneeze send a tooth across town?

Some sentences tell who made something move. Pat shoveled the hay into the wagon. Pat made the hay move, and the words into the wagon tell where the hay went.

Examples

  • He sneezed his tooth right across town.
  • Pat shoveled the hay into the wagon.
  • I kicked the ball over the fence.

Play

Pick an action like kick, push or blow. Say what it moved and where it went. Can your friends guess the action?
