Which questions can you answer with yes or no?

Some questions have a short set of answers, like yes or no. In Are you generous? the helping word are comes before the person. In Did Kim finish the report? the helping word did is added to make the question.

Examples

  • Are you generous?
  • Did Kim finish the report?
  • Are you hungry?

Play

Turn You are hungry into a question. Which word moves in front of the person? Then turn Kim finished the report into a question. Which helping word gets added?
