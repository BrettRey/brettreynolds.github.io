Can you count by fives?

A number can tell how big each group is. In twos means two at a time. Count by fives means you jump five each time.

Examples

  • The children lined up in twos.
  • We count by fives.

Play

Line up your friends in twos, then in threes. Say what you did with in twos and in threes.
