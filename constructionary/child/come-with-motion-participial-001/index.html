What do you notice about the word after came in Sally came running into the room?

You can add a word ending in -ing after came. It can tell how someone moved. Sally came running into the room.

Examples

  • Sally came running excitedly into the room.
  • The dog came running into the kitchen.

Play

Act out came running, came hopping and came skipping. Which word tells you how the person moved?
