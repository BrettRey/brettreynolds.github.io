What comes after good, then better?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

Some words change into a different word when you compare. Good turns into better and best. Bad turns into worse and worst.

Examples

  • This plan is better than the first.
  • This soup is worse than that one.

Play

Finish the chain out loud. Good, better, and then what? Bad, worse, and then what? Make a sentence with each word.
