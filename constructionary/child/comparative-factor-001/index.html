For young readers

If one rope is twice as long as another, how long is it?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

Twice as long as means two times the length. It compares two things and tells how many times as long one is.

Examples

  • This cabinet is twice as deep as that one.
  • This rope is twice as long as that one.

Play

Find two pencils or two strings. Can you find a pair where one is twice as long as the other? Say it as a sentence.
