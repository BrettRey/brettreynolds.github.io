Is kicking at a ball the same as kicking a ball?

Kicked the ball means your foot hit it. Kicked at the ball means you aimed a kick at it. You might have missed.

Examples

  • The horse kicked at me.
  • Mia kicked at the ball and missed.
  • Mia kicked the ball to Sam.

Play

Kick a soft ball or pretend to. Say what happened with kicked the ball or kicked at the ball.
