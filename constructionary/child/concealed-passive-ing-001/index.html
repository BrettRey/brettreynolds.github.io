Who does the oiling in The hinge needs oiling?

Sometimes a word ending in -ing has a hidden meaning. The hinge needs oiling means someone should oil the hinge. The hinge doesn't oil itself.

Examples

  • The hinge needs oiling.
  • My bike needs washing.

Play

Say each one the longer way. The hinge needs to be oiled. My bike needs to be washed. Make one for something in your room.
