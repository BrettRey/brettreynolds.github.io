What does away mean in "She worked away at her homework"?

Sometimes away doesn't mean going anywhere. It means you keep doing something for a while. Working away at homework means working on it for a good while.

Examples

  • They worked away at the problem all afternoon.
  • Dad was chipping away at the ice.

Play

Pick a job, like scrubbing or colouring. Say it with away at, then act it out for ten seconds. Does it feel like one quick action or like something that keeps going?
