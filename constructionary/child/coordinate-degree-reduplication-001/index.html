How would you say a sound is getting quieter and quieter?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

When we say a word like fainter or warmer twice with and, it can mean something keeps changing. The sound grew fainter and fainter. It means it kept getting fainter.

Examples

  • The sound grew fainter and fainter.
  • The days grew warmer and warmer.

Play

Pick a changing thing, like a balloon or the sun. Describe it with one word twice, like bigger and bigger. Can a friend guess what is happening?
