For young readers

Can you say two things in one sentence without saying the verb twice?

Sometimes the second half of a sentence leaves out a word we already heard. Sam ordered tea and Lee coffee. We still know Lee ordered coffee.

Examples

  • Sam ordered tea and Lee coffee.
  • Kim lives in Perth, Pat in Melbourne.

Play

Pick two friends and two foods they like. Say a sentence about the first. Then add the second friend and their food, but don't say the verb again.
