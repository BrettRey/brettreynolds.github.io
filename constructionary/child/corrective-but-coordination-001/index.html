For young readers

How do you tell someone they guessed wrong and give the right answer?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

You can say what something isn't and then what it is. I ordered not tea but coffee. The first thing is turned down. The second is the right one.

Examples

  • I ordered not tea but coffee.
  • We went to not the park but the beach.

Play

Make up two guesses about what's in a box. Correct each with not and but. Who can come up with the silliest right answer?
