For young readers

How can you ask a question without changing any words?

You can say a sentence in a questioning voice. They've finished? You think they have, and you want to check. The other person can answer, "That's right."

Examples

  • They've finished?
  • They haven't finished?

Play

Say "You're hungry" two ways, as a plain statement and then as a question. Ask a friend to listen. Can they hear which one you want an answer to?
