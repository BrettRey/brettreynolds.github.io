For young readers

What can you call a group of friends and say how many without naming each one?

You can name a group by saying the, a number and of us. The four of us shared a tent. It points to a group of four that you can pick out from the situation.

Examples

  • The four of us shared a tent.
  • The two of us walked home.

Play

Pick a group you were in, like a team or a table at lunch. Say how many were in it using the number and of us. Then say what some of the group did.
