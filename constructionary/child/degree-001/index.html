How do you say one thing is more than another?

We compare things with words like taller, tallest and as tall as. Taller than compares two things. The tallest in the class means no one in the class is taller. As tall as says they match.

Examples

  • Mia is taller than Sam.
  • Mia is as tall as Sam.
  • Sam is the tallest in the class.

Play

Compare three objects, like pencils or shoes. Make a sentence with taller, one with the tallest and one with as tall as. Can a friend tell which object you mean?
