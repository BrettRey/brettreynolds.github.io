For young readers

Can a number word have a plural, like tens?

A number can name a score or a card. I rolled a ten. You can even add an s, as in three tens. That means three of them, each one a ten.

Examples

  • I rolled a ten.
  • She drew three tens.

Play

Roll two dice or draw cards a few times. Describe each result with a number word, like a six. If you get the same number twice, give it an s.
