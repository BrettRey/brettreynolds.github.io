How do you tell someone exactly what a friend said?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

You can say a person's exact words and then say who spoke. Or you can say who spoke first. Jo said, "I'm ready" repeats her words. Jo said that she was ready tells what she meant.

Examples

  • “I’ll call tomorrow,” Jo said.
  • Max said, “I’m hungry”.

Play

Ask a friend to say one sentence. Write it down in quotation marks and add who said it. Then tell it again without the exact words.
