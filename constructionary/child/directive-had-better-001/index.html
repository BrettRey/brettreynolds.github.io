For young readers

What do you say when you really need a friend to hurry?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

Had better is how you tell someone what is a good idea to do. It can hint that something bad might happen if they don't.

Examples

  • You had better bring your coat.
  • We had better leave now.

Play

Make a had better sentence for each picture in a story. Then say what might go wrong.
