What do you shout when you see the bus leaving?

Sometimes a few words are all you need to make someone look. You say the name of the thing that is there or about to happen. You aren't talking about the person you shout to.

Examples

  • The bus!
  • A snake!
  • Your keys!

Play

Pick a scene, such as a bus, a snake or lost keys. Say just a few words to make a friend look. Can they guess what you mean?
