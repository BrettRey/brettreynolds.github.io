For young readers

Can you give a friend a pencil without saying "to"?

Some verbs can be followed by who something is for and then the thing itself. Dad baked Kim a cake. The cake is meant for Kim. The words come in that order.

Examples

  • Dad baked Kim a cake.
  • I sent Sue a copy.
  • She told him the truth.

Play

Make up sentences with who gets something, then what they get. Try sent, told and baked. Then say each one again with to or for and a name at the end.
