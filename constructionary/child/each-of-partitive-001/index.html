For young readers

Can one word talk about every drawer in turn?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

Each of the six drawers has a lock means every drawer has one. You count them one at a time. The group is one you already know about.

Examples

  • Each of the six drawers has a lock.
  • Each of the girls has a bike.

Play

Look at a group of things in the room. Say one true thing about each one, starting with each of the.
