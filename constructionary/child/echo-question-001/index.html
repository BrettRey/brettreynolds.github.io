What do you say when you didn't hear a word?

You can say back what you heard as a question. You can swap the missing word for who or what. Someone says Give it to Angela. You say Give it to who?

Examples

  • A: Give it to Angela. B: Give it to who?
  • A: I found a snake. B: You found a what?

Play

Whisper a sentence to a friend and mumble one word. They say it back and swap the mumbled word for who or what.
