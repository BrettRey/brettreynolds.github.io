How do you offer someone two choices at once?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

Either goes before the first choice. Or goes before the second. You can say Pick either the blue folder or the red one.

Examples

  • Pick either the blue folder or the red one.
  • You can have either tea or juice.

Play

Offer a friend two snacks using either and or. They pick one and say which they chose.
