How tall must a wall be to hide the bins?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

Enough after a describing word means there is as much of it as you need. Then to and a verb say what it lets you do. The wall is high enough to hide the bins.

Examples

  • The wall is high enough to hide the bins.
  • The box is big enough to hold the toys.

Play

Look around the room. Make sentences like The table is big enough to ... Then say what is not big enough.
