For young readers

You see wet grass. What can you say about the night?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

You can use must have to say what you work out from a clue about something earlier. It must have rained last night.

Examples

  • It must have rained last night.
  • The cake must have burned.

Play

Look at a picture clue, like muddy boots. Say what must have happened. Can a friend think of another idea?
