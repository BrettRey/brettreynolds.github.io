How do you say two things match in size?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

Put as before a describing word and as before the thing you're comparing with. The new shelf is as wide as the old one. It doesn't say the new shelf is wider.

Examples

  • The new shelf is as wide as the old one.
  • Max is as tall as his sister.

Play

Pick two things you can see. Say how they compare using as ... as, such as as long as or as tall as.

Related constructions

Kinds of this
