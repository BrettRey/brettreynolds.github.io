How do you thank a friend for something kind?

Some sentences tell what you think of a person for something they did. It was kind of you to share. The words after of tell who did it. The words after to tell what they did.

Examples

  • It was kind of you to share.
  • It is good of you to help.

Play

Think of something kind or careless someone did. Say what you think of them with of you or of and a name.
