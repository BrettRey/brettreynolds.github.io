What does the speaker mean by Even if it rains, we'll go?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

Even if tells you the plan won't change. Rain might come, and that's the bad news. The people will go anyway.

Examples

  • Even if it rains, we'll go.
  • Even if you're late, we'll wait for you.

Play

Make a plan, like going to the park. Then think of something that could get in the way. Say your plan with Even if at the start.

Compare

  • Only if as a requirement
