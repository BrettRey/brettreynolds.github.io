Why does the speaker think Jo is home?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

Sometimes because tells why something happened. Sometimes it tells why you think something. The keys are a clue that Jo is home. They help the speaker guess it.

Examples

  • Jo must be home, because her keys are on the table.
  • She must be asleep, because the lights are off.

Play

Be a detective. Spot a clue, like wet boots by the door. Say what it makes you think, then add because and the clue.
