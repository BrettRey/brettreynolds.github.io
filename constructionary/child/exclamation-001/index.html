How do you say That's amazing when you are really amazed?

An exclamation shows a big feeling. How you say it matters. Ouch! and A snake! are exclamations. So is That's amazing! if you say it with feeling.

Examples

  • Ouch!
  • What a lovely day!
  • A snake!

Play

Say That's amazing like a sleepy robot. Now say it like you just saw a rocket. Only one is an exclamation. Which?
