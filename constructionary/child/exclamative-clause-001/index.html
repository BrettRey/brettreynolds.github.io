For young readers

Which sentence shows surprise, What a fine player she is or What did she play?

Some sentences start with What a or How to show something is remarkable. What a fine player she is!

Examples

  • What a fine player she is!
  • How generous you are!
  • What a big dog he is!

Play

Look at a picture. Say something amazing about it that starts with What a or How.
