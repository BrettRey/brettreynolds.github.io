What does Whether Lee comes or not tell you about the meeting?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

Whether Lee comes or not names two things that could happen. The meeting goes ahead either way. The sentence isn't asking a question.

Examples

  • Whether Lee comes or not, the meeting goes ahead.
  • Whether it snows or rains, the match will be played.

Play

Name two things that could happen, like sun or rain. Join them with whether and or. Then say what happens either way.
