How do you ask if someone has done something at least once?

Have you ever driven a bus? asks if you did it at some time before now. It doesn't ask when. If you ask about yesterday, you say Did you drive the bus yesterday?

Examples

  • Have you ever driven a bus?
  • Did you drive the bus yesterday?
  • Have you ever ridden a horse?

Play

Ask three friends Have you ever ... about animals, places or foods. Then ask one of them about a single day with Did you ... yesterday?
