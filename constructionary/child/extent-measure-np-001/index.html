For young readers

How could you answer How far did we walk?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

You can say a distance right after the verb. We walked three kilometres. The words three kilometres tell how far.

Examples

  • We walked three kilometres before lunch.
  • They ran two kilometres along the river.

Play

Pick a way to move, like walking, running or swimming. Say a sentence that tells how far you went. Then say how long it took, using for.
