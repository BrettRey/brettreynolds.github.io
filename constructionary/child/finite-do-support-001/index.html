For young readers

Which little word helps you ask Did you check the hinge?

In Did you check the hinge?, the question begins with did, and check stays plain. English adds did when the question needs a helper word and there isn't one. With can, you don't need did.

Examples

  • Did you check the hinge?
  • Can you check the hinge?
  • Did Jo leave?

Play

Take a statement like Jo left and ask it as a question starting with did. What happens to left? Then ask Jo can swim as a question. Do you need did?
