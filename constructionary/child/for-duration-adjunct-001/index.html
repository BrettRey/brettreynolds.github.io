How do you say how long Jo stayed outside?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

For and a length of time tell how long something lasted. Jo stayed outside for fifteen minutes. The time is how long she stayed.

Examples

  • Jo stayed outside for fifteen minutes.
  • Mia waited for an hour.

Play

Tell a partner something you did today. Say how long it lasted using for. Your partner guesses what you did.
