What is the difference between Jo was picked and Jo got picked?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

Got can show that something happened to someone. Jo got promoted means somebody promoted Jo. The sentence is about Jo and what happened to her.

Examples

  • Jo got promoted last week.
  • Sam got picked for the team.

Play

Tell a partner something that happened to you using got, like I got invited. Then say the same thing with was and see whether it sounds different.
