What does it mean to have had it up to here?

This means you can't put up with something any more. A hand held up high can show where your limit is. I've had it up to here with the noise.

Examples

  • I've had it up to here with the noise.

Play

Hold your hand up high and say you've had it up to here with something silly, like broken pencils.
