How do you suggest something to a friend?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

How about can offer an idea. How about a short break? means maybe we could take one. Your friend can say yes or no.

Examples

  • How about a short break?
  • How about taking a break?

Play

Take turns suggesting things with How about. Your partner says yes or no. Try one with a thing and one with an -ing word.
