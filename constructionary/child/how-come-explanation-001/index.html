What is another way to ask why?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

How come can ask why. How come the gate is open? means the same as asking why it is open. How come starts the question, then the gate comes next.

Examples

  • How come the gate is open?
  • Why is the gate open?

Play

Ask a friend about something odd you see with How come. Then ask with Why and notice which words move.
