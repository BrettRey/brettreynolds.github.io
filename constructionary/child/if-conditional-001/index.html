What does if add to a sentence?

An if-part names a possibility. The rest says what follows from it. If he takes the later plane tonight, he won't have to rush.

Examples

  • If he takes the later plane tonight he won't have to rush.
  • If he took the later plane tonight he wouldn't have to rush.

Play

Say the two example sentences aloud. Which words change? Then make up your own if sentence about tonight, saying the possibility first and then what follows.
