Does Touch that wire and the alarm will ring tell you to touch it?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

It sounds like a command, but it works as a warning. It tells you what will happen if you touch the wire.

Examples

  • Touch that wire and the alarm will ring.

Play

Make up warnings that start with a command and say what will happen next. Then say each one again with if.
