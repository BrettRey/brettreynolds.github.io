Can you say Have you eaten yet? without saying what?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

Some verbs can leave out what the action was done to. Have you eaten yet? doesn't say what. It only asks whether you ate.

Examples

  • Have you eaten yet?
  • Jo ate.

Play

Say Jo ate lunch. Then say it again without the word lunch, like Jo ate. What did the second one leave out? Try it with your own sentences about eating.
