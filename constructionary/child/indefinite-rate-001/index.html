How fast does a car go in "fifty miles an hour"?

Sometimes a means "for each." Fifty miles an hour means fifty miles for each hour. A yard of ribbon at $20 a yard costs $20 for each yard.

Examples

  • The car went 50 miles an hour.
  • It costs $20 a yard.
  • The nurse checked it twice a day.

Play

Find prices and speeds on signs or packages. Say each one with a or an, such as three dollars a bag. Then say what it means with for each.
