For young readers

What is the difference between "my book" and "the book is mine"?

My needs a word after it, like my book. Mine can stand alone. You can say the book is mine.

Examples

  • That car is mine.
  • The red bike is hers.
  • Whose is this?

Play

Point to things in the room and say whose they are. Try it two ways, such as "my pencil" and "this pencil is mine."
