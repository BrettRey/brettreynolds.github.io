Why did Jo borrow the van?

To plus a verb can tell why someone did something. Jo borrowed the van to move the chairs. That was the plan. It doesn't say the chairs got moved.

Examples

  • Jo borrowed the van to move the chairs.
  • I grabbed a pencil to draw a map.

Play

Ask a partner why they did something today. Answer with "to" and a verb. Add "but it didn't work" to one answer.
