What's a bench to sit on?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

To plus a verb can describe a thing by what you could do with it. A bench to sit on is a bench you could sit on. You sit on the bench.

Examples

  • We need a bench to sit on.
  • I found a rope to climb on.
