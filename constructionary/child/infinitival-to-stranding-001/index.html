Can a sentence end with the word "to"?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

Sometimes a sentence ends with to, and you still know the verb. I didn't go, but I wanted to. You know the missing word is go.

Examples

  • I didn’t go, but I wanted to.
  • Mia asked me to play, and I wanted to.

Play

Tell a partner something you didn't do but wanted to, like I didn't swim, but I wanted to. Stop after to. Can your partner say the missing verb?
