For young readers

What do you use to do things, and how do you say it?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

With can name a tool you used. Lee opened the case with a small screwdriver. The screwdriver is what helped him do it.

Examples

  • Lee opened the case with a small screwdriver.
  • I drew the picture with a blue pen.

Play

Act out a job without saying the tool. A partner says what you did, ending with the tool, such as "with a spoon."
