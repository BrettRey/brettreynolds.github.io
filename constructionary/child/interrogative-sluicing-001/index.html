What does "I don't know who" leave out?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

Sometimes you say only the question word. Someone moved the keys, but I don't know who. You and your listener both know the rest: who moved the keys.

Examples

  • Someone moved the keys, but I don’t know who.
  • Mia left early, but I don't know why.

Play

Say "Someone took my pencil, but I don't know" and stop at the question word. Can a partner say the whole question?
