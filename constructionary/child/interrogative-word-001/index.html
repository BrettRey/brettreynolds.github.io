What do question words ask for?

Question words ask for the missing piece. Who asks about a person. Where asks about a place. The question word normally comes first.

Examples

  • Who came?
  • I wonder where she went.
  • Whose coat did Jo borrow?

Play

Pick a sentence like "Jo borrowed a coat." Ask a question about each part of it, using who, what or whose. What would each answer be?
