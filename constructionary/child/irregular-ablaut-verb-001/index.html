What changes in the word sing when you say yesterday?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

Some words change their middle sound. Today I sing. Yesterday I sang. Today I have sung. Each word has its own pattern.

Examples

  • Jo sang yesterday and has sung twice today.
  • Today I sing, yesterday I sang.

Play

Say sing, sang and sung with a partner. Find other words whose middle sound changes, and test each one.
