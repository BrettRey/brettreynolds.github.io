How do you say more than one tooth?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

One tooth, two teeth. The word doesn't get an s at the end. The middle of the word changes instead. Only some words do this.

Examples

  • One tooth was loose and two teeth were sore.

Play

Say one tooth, then two teeth. Listen for the part that changes. Then think of other words that change in the middle when there's more than one. Ask a grown-up whether you found any.
