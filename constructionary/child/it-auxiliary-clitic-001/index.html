What two words can hide inside it's?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

It's can be short for it is or for it has. Words like been and being can show which. It's been repaired means it has been repaired. It's being repaired means it is being repaired.

Examples

  • It’s been repaired.
  • It’s being repaired.

Play

Say each example, then say it again with the short word spelled out in full. Look at been or being after it's. Does it help you choose between is and has?
