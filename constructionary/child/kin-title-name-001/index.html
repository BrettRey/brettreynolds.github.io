For young readers

Do you say "Grandma" and then a name for one of your relatives?

People can call a relative by a family word plus a name, like Aunt Sarah. The family word tells who she is to you. The name tells which aunt you mean.

Examples

  • We're visiting Aunt Sarah on Sunday.
  • Thanks, Aunt Sarah.
  • Grandpa Dale made pancakes.

Play

List your relatives. For each one, write the family word and the name together. Then try calling the same person by the name alone, or by "my" plus the family word.
