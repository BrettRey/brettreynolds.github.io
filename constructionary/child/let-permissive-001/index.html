What do you call it when a grown-up says you can do something?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

When someone lets you do something, they allow it. Say let, then who gets to do it, then the action. Dad lets Mia use his bike. The action word stays plain.

Examples

  • Dad lets Mia use his bike.
  • They let Jo use the key.
