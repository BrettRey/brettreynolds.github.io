Do you take a look or just look?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

You can say we took a quick look. Here the word look names what happened, and took just goes with it. Because look is a noun here, you can count it: two quick looks.

Examples

  • We took a quick look at the charts.
  • We took two quick looks at the charts.
