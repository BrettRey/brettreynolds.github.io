How long is the board?

You can say the board is six feet long. Before a noun, you can say a six-foot-long board. There, feet has changed to foot.

Examples

  • The board is six feet long.
  • We ordered a six-foot-long board.

Play

Pick something in your room and measure it. Say how long it is in feet. Then put the measure before the thing, like a six-foot-long board.
