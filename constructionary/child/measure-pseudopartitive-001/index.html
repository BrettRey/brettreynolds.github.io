How do you say how much milk you want?

You can name an amount or a container, then say of, then say what it holds. Three litres of milk. The word of links the amount to the stuff.

Examples

  • They bought three litres of milk.
  • She poured two cups of water.

Play

Look in the kitchen. Name a container or a measure, then say of and what is inside. Make three sentences, like a bag of apples or a cup of tea.
