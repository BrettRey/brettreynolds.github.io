For young readers

Which words name a kind of thing, like dog?

Words like dog and city name a kind of thing. They don't name just one special one. Even in the tallest building, the main word is still building.

Examples

  • I saw a dog.
  • We live in a city.

Play

Look around. Say five words for kinds of things, like chair or tree. Then say one special name for each, like a friend's name for their pet.
