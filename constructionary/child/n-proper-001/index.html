Why does Kim get a capital letter but dog doesn't?

A proper noun is a word used as a name, like Einstein or Everest. New Zealand is one name made of two words. A name normally starts with a capital letter.

Examples

  • We visited New Zealand.
  • Everest is a mountain.

Play

Write names of a person, a pet, a town and a mountain. Circle the capital letters. Which names have more than one word?
