For young readers

How can you say that two people both didn't do something?

Neither and nor go together. Neither Kim nor Pat replied means Kim didn't reply and Pat didn't either.

Examples

  • Neither Kim nor Pat replied.
  • Neither Jo nor Kai came.

Play

Say something two friends both didn't do. Then say it again starting with neither and putting nor between their names.
