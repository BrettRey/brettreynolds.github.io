Which word tells you more about the men in old men?

A describing word can come before a noun. In old men, the word old tells us about the men. The describing word comes first.

Examples

  • The old men sat down.
  • old men

Play

Pick a noun, like dog or house. Add a describing word before it. Then try a different one, and see how the picture changes.
