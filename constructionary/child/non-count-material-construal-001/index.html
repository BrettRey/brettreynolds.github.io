Does "apple" have to mean one whole apple?

Sometimes a word for a thing can mean the stuff it's made of. If a toddler gets apple all over the chair, it's a mess, not a whole apple.

Examples

  • The toddler got apple all over the high chair.
  • There's apple on the floor.

Play

Pick a fruit and make two sentences. In one, use a whole fruit. In the other, use the fruit as stuff on a table or a floor.
