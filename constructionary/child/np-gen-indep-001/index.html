For young readers

How can you talk about your things without naming them?

Words like mine and yours can stand on their own. Mine is blue doesn't name the thing, but we know what is meant. A name with 's can do it too, as in Jo's is blue.

Examples

  • Mine is blue.
  • Yours is red.

Play

Put two toys on a table. Point at your own and say Mine is, then its colour. Your friend points at a toy of theirs and does the same.
