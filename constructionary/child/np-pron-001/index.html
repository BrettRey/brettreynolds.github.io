For young readers

In "It is raining", what does "it" stand for?

Words like he can take the place of a name. In John arrived. He looked tired, he means John. In It is raining, it doesn't mean anything. It just fills the spot.

Examples

  • John arrived. He looked tired.
  • It is raining.
  • She is my sister.

Play

Read a short story aloud. Every time you hear he or she, point to the person it means. Does every it point to something?

Related constructions

Kinds of this

  • Each other and one another
  • Words like herself and themselves
  • They for one person
