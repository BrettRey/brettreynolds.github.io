For young readers

Can one little word say who owns the whole thing?

Words like mine, yours and theirs can stand alone. They say who owns something without naming it. The red one is mine.

Examples

  • The red one is mine.
  • Those seats are theirs.

Play

Point to things in the room. Say who owns each one using only mine, yours, his, hers, ours or theirs.
