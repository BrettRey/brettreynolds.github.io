What do two friends do to each other?

Each other says that two people do the same thing to one another. If Kim and Pat defended each other, Kim defended Pat and Pat defended Kim.

Examples

  • Kim and Pat defended each other.
  • The two friends hugged each other.

Play

Pick two friends. Say what they did, using each other. Then say the same thing with one another.
