How do we say numbers like twenty-one?

Some numbers are made of two parts. Twenty-one is twenty and one put together. We typically write a hyphen between the parts.

Examples

  • It's twenty-one steps to the door.
  • She has forty-two cards.
  • Grandpa is ninety-nine years old.
