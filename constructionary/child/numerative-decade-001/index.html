For young readers

What do twenty, thirty and forty all end with?

Words like twenty, seventy and ninety name whole tens. They end in -ty. Seventy means 70. You can count by tens with them.

Examples

  • I counted twenty stones.
  • He is seventy years old.

Play

Count by tens out loud, from twenty up to ninety. Stop at each -ty word, and clap on each one.
