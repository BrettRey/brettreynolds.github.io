What happens to the word third when you have two pieces?

A fraction word names the size of the pieces. One third has one piece. With two pieces you say two thirds, adding an s. Half and quarter are special words.

Examples

  • I coloured two thirds of the picture.
  • She ate one quarter of the pizza.

Play

Say one third, then two thirds. Now try one fifth and two fifths. Which part of the word changes when there's more than one piece?
