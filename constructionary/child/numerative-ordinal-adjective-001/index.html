For young readers

What words tell you who is first, second and third in a race?

Some words tell you the place in a line. First, second and third are special words. Some others are made by adding th to the number. They can go before a noun, like the third chapter.

Examples

  • Mia finished first.
  • Read the third chapter.

Play

Line up toys and say each one's place, like the first one and the second one. Then make up a sentence that puts a place word before a noun.
