What is the difference between thirteen and thirty?

Thirteen ends in teen and is ten more than three. Thirty ends in ty and is a different amount. Both start from the same word.

Examples

  • She has thirteen marbles.
  • He has thirty marbles.

Play

Say two number words that end in teen and two that end in ty. Can your friend hear the difference? Make a sentence with each.
