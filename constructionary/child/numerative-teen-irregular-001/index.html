Do eleven and twelve end in teen like thirteen does?

Thirteen ends in teen. Eleven and twelve don't. They're special words that don't follow the teen pattern. You use them just like the other numbers.

Examples

  • I have eleven stickers.
  • There are twelve eggs.

Play

Count from eleven up to nineteen out loud. Clap on the two words that don't end in teen.
