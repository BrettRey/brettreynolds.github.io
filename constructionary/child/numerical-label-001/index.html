For young readers

If you're in Room 101, are you in a hundred and one rooms?

Sometimes a number is a name. Room 101 is just one room, and the number tells you which one. In 101 rooms, the number tells you how many.

Examples

  • Our class is in Room 101.
  • Flight 447
  • We live at 20 Pheasant Street.

Play

Find numbers on doors, buses or signs. For each one, decide if it names one thing or counts several.
