For young readers

In Jo persuaded Kai to stay, who is going to stay?

Kai is the one who gets persuaded. Kai is also the one who stays. One person does both jobs in the sentence.

Examples

  • Jo persuaded Kai to stay.
  • They persuaded Kai not to wait.

Play

Make up a sentence with persuaded, a friend's name, to, and something to do. Then ask who gets persuaded and who does the thing.
