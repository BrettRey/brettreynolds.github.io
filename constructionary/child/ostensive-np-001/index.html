What do you shout when you see a snake?

Sometimes just a name or a thing is the whole message. A snake! tells everyone to look. Mom! calls someone over. You don't need a verb.

Examples

  • A snake!
  • Mom! (calling)
  • The bus!

Play

Take turns shouting just a thing or a name. Can your friend tell whether you're calling someone or pointing something out?
