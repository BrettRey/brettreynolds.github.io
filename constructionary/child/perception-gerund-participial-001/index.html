For young readers

If you see someone crossing a street, did you see them get to the other side?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

The -ing word shows the person in the middle of doing something. You can say I saw Kai crossing the square even if you left before Kai got across.

Examples

  • I saw Kai crossing the square.
  • We saw Mia painting a fence.

Play

Look out of a window for one minute. Write what you saw people doing, using saw, a name and an -ing word.
