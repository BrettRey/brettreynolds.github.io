For young readers

When someone asks Did you lock up? what short answer can you give?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

You can answer a yes/no question with a short reply. Did Jo lock up? Yes, she did. The reply uses did from the question and leaves out the rest.

Examples

  • Did Jo lock up? Yes, she did.
  • Can Sam swim? Yes, he can.

Play

Ask each other yes/no questions. Give short answers like Yes, she did.
