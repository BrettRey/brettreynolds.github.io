What is the difference between more cookies and more of these cookies?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

More of these cookies means more from a set you can point to. The words after of tell which set you mean.

Examples

  • We should keep more of these samples.
  • Could I have more of this soup?

Play

Point at a pile of things and say what you want more of, starting with more of these or more of this.
