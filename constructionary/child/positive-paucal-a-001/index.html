For young readers

If you have a few minutes, is there time to play?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

A few and a little name a small amount. We still have a little time. We still have a few minutes. A few goes with things you can count.

Examples

  • We still have a little time.
  • We still have a few minutes.

Play

Look in your pencil case. Say what you have a few of and what you have a little of.
