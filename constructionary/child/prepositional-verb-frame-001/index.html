Which little word goes with the verb rely?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

Some verbs go with a certain small word. We relied on Jo. The word on belongs with rely here. In this sentence it sits right next to Jo.

Examples

  • We relied on Jo.
  • Who did you rely on?
