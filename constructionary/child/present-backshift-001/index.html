For young readers

If Jo says I'm hungry, how do you tell it tomorrow?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

When you tell what someone said before, the verb can take a past form. Yesterday Jo said she was hungry. She could have been hungry right when she said it.

Examples

  • Yesterday Jo said she was hungry.
  • Yesterday Sam said he was tired.

Play

One person says a sentence with I am. Another tells it later starting with said, using a past form.
