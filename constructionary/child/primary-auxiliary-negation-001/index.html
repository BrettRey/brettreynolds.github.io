Where does the word not go when you say you haven't done something?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

Look at She has not received it. The little word not comes right after has. A helper word like has comes first, and not follows it.

Examples

  • She has not received it.
  • He has not finished his lunch.

Play

Pick a sentence with a helper word, such as She has finished. Make it say the opposite by adding not. Say where you put it.

Interacts with
