What do you call a room with no windows?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

Some words end in -less. The ending tells you something is missing. A windowless room has no windows.

Examples

  • The room was windowless.
  • The old house was doorless.

Play

Put -less on the end of a noun, like sun or roof. Say what the new word would describe. Make up a sentence with it.
