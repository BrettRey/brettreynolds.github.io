For young readers

Is the house finished if she was building it?

Words like is, was and were plus an -ing word can show something going on. She was building a house means she was in the middle of it. She built a house means it got done.

Examples

  • She is reading.
  • They were walking home.
  • She was building a house.

Play

Say what you are doing right now with is or am and an -ing word. Then say what you did yesterday without -ing. Does it sound finished or still going?
