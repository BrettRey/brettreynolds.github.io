What's the difference between a girl and Jo?

A name points to one particular person or thing. Jo is one person. Sometimes two people share a name, and then we can say two Jos.

Examples

  • Jo waved at us.
  • There are two Jos in our class.

Play

Think of a name that two people you know share. Say a sentence about each one using the same name.
