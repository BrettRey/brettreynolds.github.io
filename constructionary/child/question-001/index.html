How do you know something is a question?

A question comes with a set of possible answers. Have you seen it? can be answered with yes or no. Who broke it? can be answered by saying who.

Examples

  • Have you seen it?
  • Who broke it?
  • Which one?

Play

Ask a friend a question that needs a yes or no. Then ask one that starts with who. Which kind was easier to answer?

Related constructions

Kinds of this

  • Repeating what someone said as a question
  • Questions tacked onto the end of a statement
