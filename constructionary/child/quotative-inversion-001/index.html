Why does the speaker come second in a story?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

In stories, a person's words can be followed by a speaking word and then the speaker. In “I can explain,” said the clerk, the word said comes before the clerk.

Examples

  • “I can explain,” said the clerk.
  • “Come in,” said the teacher.

Play

Open a story and find some speech. Does the speaker come before or after the speaking word? Rewrite one to flip the order.
