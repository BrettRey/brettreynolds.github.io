Why do we say friends when it's just one of you?

A friendship has two sides. One side is the person the sentence is about. The other side comes after with.

Examples

  • I’m no longer friends with him.
  • Mia is friends with Tom.

Play

Say who you're friends with using friends with and a name. Then try it with someone else.
