What happened if someone cried herself to sleep?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

She cried, and then she fell asleep. The crying caused the sleep. The words to sleep tell you the state she ended up in.

Examples

  • The child cried herself to sleep.
  • Mum sang herself to sleep.
