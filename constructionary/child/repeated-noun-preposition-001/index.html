What do we call it when one day follows another again and again?

Say a word twice with after in the middle. Day after day means day, then another day, and another. With by it means doing something one piece at a time.

Examples

  • day after day
  • She ate her dinner mouthful by mouthful.
  • We read page after page.

Play

Pick a noun, like step or page. Make up a sentence with it twice around after. Then try it around by and see how the meaning changes.
