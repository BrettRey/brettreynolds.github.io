For young readers

If you owe a friend a game, has the friend got it yet?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

Owe is followed by the person and then the thing. I owe Lee an apology means Lee should get an apology. It doesn't mean Lee has it yet.

Examples

  • I owe Lee an apology.
  • We owe Grandma a phone call.

Play

Say what you owe someone, with the name first. Then ask a friend, "Has it happened yet?"
