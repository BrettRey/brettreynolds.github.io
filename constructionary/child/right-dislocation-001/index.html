For young readers

When you say "him", how does a friend know who you mean?

You can say it first, then say the name at the end. I really like him, your dad. The end part tells who "him" is.

Examples

  • I really like him, your dad.
  • It's fascinating, this book.

Play

Say a sentence with him, her or it. Then add the person or thing at the end, after a little pause.
