For young readers

What do you say when someone calls something by a grand name it doesn't deserve?

You call that a sandwich? can say the thing is not good enough to have that name, instead of asking about the word. The tone of voice helps.

Examples

  • You call that a sandwich? It's just bread with lettuce.
  • You call that music?

Play

Pick something that's not a good example of its name. Say You call that ...? in a surprised voice and add one reason.
