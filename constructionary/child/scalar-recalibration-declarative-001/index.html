For young readers

What do you say when you've got it even worse than your friend?

You think you're tired? can sound like a question about what you think. Sometimes it means "I'm even more tired." What the speaker says next shows which.

Examples

  • You think you're tired? I've been up for 36 hours.
  • You think that's expensive. Wait till you see the repair bill.

Play

Take turns. One person says "I'm tired" or "I'm cold". The other answers You think ...? and tops it with a reason.
