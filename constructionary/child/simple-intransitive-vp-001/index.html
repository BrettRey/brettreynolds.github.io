Which words can finish a sentence all by themselves?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

Some verbs need nothing after them. The guests laughed is a whole sentence. You can add more, like when or where, but you don't have to.

Examples

  • The guests laughed.
  • The baby slept in the car.

Play

Make up short sentences with just a doer and a verb. Then add where or when and see that the verb didn't change.
