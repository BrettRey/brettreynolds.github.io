What word do we use for just one thing, like it or this?

A singular word can stand for one thing, like it or this. Words like rice and water are singular too, even though you don't count them one by one.

Examples

  • The rice is ready.
  • The water is cold.

Play

Look at things around you. Say the cat is or the rice is, and see which words go with is.
