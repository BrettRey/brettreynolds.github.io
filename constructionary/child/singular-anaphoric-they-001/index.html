Can they ever mean just one person?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

Sometimes they points back to one person at a time. Every applicant said they were ready. The verb is were, just like when they means a group.

Examples

  • Every applicant said they were ready.
  • Each guest said they were hungry.

Play

Start a sentence with Every kid said or Each player said. Finish it using they and a verb.
