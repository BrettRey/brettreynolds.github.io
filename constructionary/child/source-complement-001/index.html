How do you say where a trip started?

Words like from, off and out can tell where something started. She came from Toronto. Kim jumped off the pier. They flew from Toronto to Ottawa.

Examples

  • She came from Toronto.
  • Kim jumped off the pier.
  • They flew from Toronto to Ottawa.

Play

Act out a short trip. Say where you started with from, off or out, and where you ended with to.
