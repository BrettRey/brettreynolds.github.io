What do you mean when you say the kettle hasn't boiled yet?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

Not yet says something hasn't happened up to the time we're talking about. It can hint that it is still expected. Still says something is carrying on.

Examples

  • The kettle hasn't boiled yet.
  • The dog is still barking.

Play

Look at a clock. Say one thing that hasn't happened yet today and one thing that is still going on. Take turns with a friend.
