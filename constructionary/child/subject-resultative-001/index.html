What word tells you how the water ended up after it froze?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

Some words after a verb tell how something ended up. The pond froze solid. Solid is how the pond ended up after it froze.

Examples

  • The basin froze solid.
  • The pond froze solid.

Play

Pick a verb such as froze or melted. Add a word that says how the thing ended up. Read your sentence to a friend and see if they can say how it ended.
