For young readers

Which pet is the smallest of your three?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

Words like smallest and narrowest pick one thing from a group. The group is the set you're comparing. Change the group and the answer can change.

Examples

  • Jo chose the narrowest of the three aisles.
  • Sam picked the smallest of the four puppies.

Play

Line up three toys. Say which is the biggest of the three. Swap one toy and say it again.
