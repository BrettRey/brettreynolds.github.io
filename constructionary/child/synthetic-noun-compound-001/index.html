What does a window-cleaner do?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

Some words put what you work on first, then the person who does the job. A window-cleaner cleans windows. A dog-walker walks dogs.

Examples

  • They hired a window-cleaner.
  • We met a dog-walker.

Play

Make a job word like this from a thing and a doer. What does the person do? Say it with a verb.
