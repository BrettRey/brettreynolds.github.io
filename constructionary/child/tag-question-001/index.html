For young readers

How do you ask a friend to agree with something you just said?

A tag question is a tiny question stuck on the end of a sentence. It uses a short helper word like is, has or does, and a pronoun like he or she. Your voice rising or falling changes what you mean.

Examples

  • He is ill, isn't he?
  • Everybody has read it, haven't they?
  • She likes tea, doesn't she?

Play

Say a sentence about a friend, then add a tiny question to check it. Say it twice, with your voice going up and then down. Does it sound different?
