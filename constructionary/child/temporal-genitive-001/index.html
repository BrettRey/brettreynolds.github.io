Why do we say tomorrow's trip?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

You can put 's on a time word to say when something is. Tomorrow's trip is the trip that's tomorrow. The word tomorrow tells you when.

Examples

  • They cancelled tomorrow's inspection.
  • We're packing for tomorrow's trip.

Play

Pick a time word like today or yesterday. Add 's and a noun, like yesterday's game. Make up three of your own.
