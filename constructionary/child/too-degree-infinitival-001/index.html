Why can't a big dog fit through a small cat flap?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

Sometimes too means more than the limit for a job. The cat flap is too small for the dog to fit through. The job is fitting through.

Examples

  • The box was too heavy to lift.
  • The soup was too hot to eat.

Finish sentences that start "This is too big to ..." and "That is too far to ...". Say which job sets the limit each time.
