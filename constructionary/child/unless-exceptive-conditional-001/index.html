What could change your plan?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

The word unless names an exception. We'll eat outside unless it rains. Rain is the exception.

Examples

  • We'll eat outside unless it rains.
  • The door will stay locked unless Lee comes back.

Play

Say a plan, then add unless and an exception. Take turns, and make the exceptions silly but possible.
