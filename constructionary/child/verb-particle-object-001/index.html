Can you say "took the label off" and "took off the label"?

Some small words like off and down can go before or after the object. She took the label off. She took off the label. With it, the little word goes after it.

Examples

  • She took the label off.
  • She brought the bed down.

Play

Take a sticker off a page and say what you did two ways. Then say what you did with it, like "I took it off."
