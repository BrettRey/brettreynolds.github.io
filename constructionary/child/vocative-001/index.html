For young readers

When you call someone's name, what happens to your voice?

When we speak straight to someone, we can say their name. Mom, can you help me? The name stands apart from the rest of the sentence.

Examples

  • Mom, can you help me?
  • I think, Brett, you're wrong.

Play

Say the same sentence three times, with a friend's name at the start, middle and end. Does the name sound set apart each time?
