For young readers

When you squeeze through a crowd, how would you say it with the word "way"?

Some sentences use someone's own way to show them making progress toward a place. He pushed his way to the front. Here pushed tells how he moved, and the last part tells where he was going.

Examples

  • We made our way to the station.
  • He elbowed his way to the door.
  • They pushed their way to the front.

Play

Pick a place, like the door or the front. Then say how you'd get there with your way, using words like pushed, squeezed or made.
