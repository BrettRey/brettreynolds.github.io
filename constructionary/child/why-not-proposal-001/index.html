How do you suggest an idea without saying "You should"?

This page is an early sketch, so there’s more still to find out about this pattern.

Why not can be a way to suggest something. Why not call again tomorrow? It asks for a reason not to, and a friend can just say Good idea.

Examples

  • Why not call again tomorrow?
  • Why not ask your teacher?

Play

Take turns making a Why not suggestion. The next player says yes or gives a different idea.

Compare
