How can you tell someone a letter is not a capital?

We can say a letter is small, like with a small c. It tells people how to write the word. The word can then mean an ordinary thing.

Examples

  • Catholic with a small c
  • romantic with a small r

Play

Say a word to a friend and add with a small and the first letter. Your friend writes it down.
