Does the number in "born in 1994" count something?

A year's number is its name. Born in 1994 tells us which year. Two years is different. It counts how long.

Examples

  • born in 1994
  • They waited two years.

Play

Say a year, like the year you were born. Then say how many years something took. Which one is a name and which one counts?
