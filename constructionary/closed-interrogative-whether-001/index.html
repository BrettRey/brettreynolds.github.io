Questions inside sentences with whether

Whether can introduce a question or a choice between alternatives inside a larger sentence.

Early sketch. The full entry for this pattern has one example so far and its evidence isn’t written up yet, so treat this page as a starting point.

In Whether to reschedule still needs discussion, whether introduces a choice between alternatives, and the whole phrase acts as the subject of the sentence. Whether can be followed by a full clause or by to and a verb.

It also works in places where the question word if isn't available, such as before to and a verb. Some other positions, including some where the phrase is the subject, favour whether or require it.

Examples

  • Whether to reschedule still needs discussion.
  • I wonder whether Kim finished the report.

Try it

Compare I wonder whether to go and I wonder whether we should go. Is the choice between alternatives the same in both?
