Saying how many times as much

A multiplier such as twice, followed by as and a measure word, compares two amounts as a ratio.

Early sketch. The full entry for this pattern has one example so far and its evidence isn’t written up yet, so treat this page as a starting point.

This cabinet is twice as deep as that one says that one depth is two times the other. Twice here gives a ratio between two depths. It doesn't mean the cabinet is repeated.

This is a different kind of comparison from two centimetres deeper. That version gives a difference between the depths, while twice as deep fixes how many times one is the other.

Examples

  • This cabinet is twice as deep as that one.

Try it

Compare This cabinet is twice as deep as that one with This cabinet is two centimetres deeper than that one. Which one tells you a ratio?
