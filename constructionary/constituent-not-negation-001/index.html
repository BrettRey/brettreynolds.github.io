Saying not about one part of a sentence

Not can deny one part of a sentence, such as a description or a time, instead of the whole action.

In That is not a solution, not denies what is said about that: it isn't a solution. In She is not in the box, it denies a place. The description or relation is what gets negated.

Not can also target one part while the rest stays asserted. In Lee came not on Tuesday but on Wednesday, the coming still happened. Only the time is corrected. Jo ordered tea, not coffee targets just the drink, unlike Jo didn't order coffee.

Examples

  • That is not a solution.
  • She is not in the box.
  • Lee came not on Tuesday but on Wednesday.

Try it

Say Jo ordered tea, not coffee. What does not apply to? Compare Jo didn't order coffee.
