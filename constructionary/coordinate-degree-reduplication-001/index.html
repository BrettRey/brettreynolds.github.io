Fainter and fainter

Repeating a comparative word with and says that something keeps changing by degrees.

Early sketch. The full entry for this pattern has one example so far and its evidence isn’t written up yet, so treat this page as a starting point.

In The sound grew fainter and fainter, the same word is said twice, joined by and. The sentence doesn't mention two different qualities. It says the faintness kept increasing.

What's repeated has to be a comparative, the kind of form that compares degrees. Repeating the plain form has other uses, and this dictionary treats the pattern as a separate case. The analysis here is provisional.

Examples

  • The sound grew fainter and fainter.
  • The days grew warmer and warmer.
