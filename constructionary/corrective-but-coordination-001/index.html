Not this but that

The not-but pattern turns down one description and puts another in its place.

Early sketch. The full entry for this pattern has one example so far and its evidence isn’t written up yet, so treat this page as a starting point.

In I ordered not tea but coffee, not marks tea as the wrong candidate, and but brings in the correction. The speaker is saying what was ordered by ruling out one choice and naming the right one.

This differs from the but in a sentence like It was cheap but good. There, both parts can be asserted. In the not-but pattern the first part is rejected and the second is offered in its place. This dictionary treats the analysis as provisional.

Examples

  • I ordered not tea but coffee.
  • She wanted not a quick answer but a good one.
