Nouns ending in -ity

Adding -ity to an adjective can name the quality it describes, sometimes changing how the word sounds.

Curious is an adjective, and curiosity names the quality of being curious. Eccentric becomes eccentricity in the same way. The ending -ity typically puts the stress on the syllable just before it.

The base word can change too, as in curious and curiosity, where the end of the word is altered. The -ness ending does something similar, but it typically leaves the base word's shape and stress as they were. Which adjectives take -ity depends on the adjective type.

Examples

  • eccentricity
  • curiosity
