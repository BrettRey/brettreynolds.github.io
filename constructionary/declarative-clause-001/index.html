The plain statement-shaped sentence

A declarative sentence is the default sentence shape, and it is built for making statements but can do other jobs too.

Kim finished the report is the plainest kind of sentence. It has no question word, no exclamation form and no command form. This dictionary calls that shape declarative. It is the default type, and it turns up both as a sentence on its own and inside another sentence, as in She says that Kim finished the report.

Making a statement is the characteristic use of this shape, but the shape doesn't fix the job. Passengers are requested to remain seated has the same shape and works as a directive. Declarative names a form, not a purpose. This dictionary treats the analysis as provisional.

Examples

  • Kim finished the report.
  • Passengers are requested to remain seated.
  • She says that Kim finished the report.

Try it

Look at a sign or announcement that sounds like a statement. Is it telling you something, or asking you to do something?
