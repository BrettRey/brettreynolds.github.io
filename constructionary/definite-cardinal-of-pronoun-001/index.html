The two of us, the four of us

The, a number and of plus a pronoun together name a group and say how many are in it.

I was there with Jo, Lee and Pat. The four of us shared a tent. The phrase the four of us can point to a group of four that includes the speaker, and it gives the group's size at the same time. When a larger group is also in the picture, the phrase needs a set of four the listener can pick out.

Without the, it works differently. In There were six of us, and four of us had bikes, four of us picks out some of a larger group rather than naming the whole group. This dictionary treats the analysis as provisional.

Examples

  • I was there with Jo, Lee and Pat. The four of us shared a tent.
  • There were six of us, and four of us had bikes.

Try it

Think of a group you were part of. How would you name it with the, a number and of us? What if only some of the group did something?
