This one or that one

This, that, these and those direct attention to something and can present it as near or far.

Early sketch. The full entry for this pattern has one example so far and its evidence isn’t written up yet, so treat this page as a starting point.

In Could you pass me that blue folder over there?, that points to one folder among others, and over there adds where it is. The demonstrative words are this and that, with these and those for more than one. They present the thing as near or far in some relevant dimension.

Distance in space is one such dimension, but it isn't required. This and that can also pick out different candidates without any earlier noun phrase to point back to, and they can refer to things said earlier. This dictionary treats the analysis as provisional.

Examples

  • Could you pass me that blue folder over there?
  • I'll take these shoes.

Try it

Point at two similar objects, one near and one farther away. Which would you call this one and which that one?
