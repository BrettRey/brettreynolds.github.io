That husband of Mary's

A demonstrative plus a phrase like of Mary's can pick out a person or thing through a relation, even when only one fits.

In that husband of Mary's, the word that points to the husband, and the phrase of Mary's says whose husband he is. The same pattern appears in those green eyes of Mary's and that nose of his.

The pattern works even when there is only one husband, or one nose, in the picture. This dictionary links this use to focus on the head noun and to speaker feeling, but how far feeling is needed across all the examples remains open. This dictionary treats the analysis as provisional.

Examples

  • that husband of Mary’s
  • those green eyes of Mary’s
  • that nose of his

Compare that nose of his with his nose. Does the first one feel different?
