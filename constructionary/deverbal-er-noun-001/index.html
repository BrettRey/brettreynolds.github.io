Nouns made by adding -er to a verb

Adding -er to a verb gives a noun for someone or something tied to that action.

A singer is a person who sings. The central reading names the person who does the activity. Some -er words name a tool instead, as in mixer.

Agent nouns like these can carry a complement that matches the object of the verb. In the reviewer of the manuscript, the manuscript is what gets reviewed. The same ending also shows up in islander, but that follows a different pattern, because it starts from a noun rather than a verb.

Examples

  • the reviewer of the manuscript
  • a mixer

Try it

Think of a verb for a job. Add -er. Is the new word a person or a tool?
