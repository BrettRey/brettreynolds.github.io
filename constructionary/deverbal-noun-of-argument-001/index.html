Event nouns followed by of

A noun made from a verb can be followed by of and a phrase naming someone or something involved in the event.

In the arrival of the guests, arrival names an event, and of the guests says who arrived. In the destruction of the city, the city is the thing destroyed. The of-phrase names a participant in the event, and the noun decides which role that participant has.

When the event has someone who does it, that person can come first with 's, as in the enemy's destruction of the city: the enemy did the destroying, and the city was destroyed. The of-phrase can also help pin down which event is meant, as in the failure of Washington Mutual.

Examples

  • the failure of Washington Mutual
  • the arrival of the guests
  • the destruction of the city
  • the death of the king
  • the enemy's destruction of the city
