Adding force to yes and no

Informal speakers put an emphatic word in front of yeah, yes or no to give the answer extra feeling.

You can answer a question with a plain yeah or no. You can also put an emphatic word in front, as in hell yeah, to show you feel strongly about the answer. The word added doesn't change the answer: hell yeah still means yes, and hell fucking no still means no. It adds emotion.

Some of these combinations use profane words. They're also a small, specific set, not a rule that lets any emphatic word go with any answer. Some are also used to start a longer reply, with the rest of the turn following.

Examples

  • hell yeah.
  • Hell yeah, count me in!
