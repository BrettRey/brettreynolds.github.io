A noun of a noun, used to judge

In a phrase like a nitwit of a husband, the first noun is a judgment about the person or thing named by the second.

In her stupid nitwit of a husband, the husband is the target of the evaluation. The first noun, nitwit, is said of the husband. This is different from a statue of bronze or a cup of water, where of expresses a material or a measure.

The first part can be bigger than one word. In that destroyer of education of a minister, the education is what the minister destroys, and destroyer of education is the judgment passed on the minister.

Examples

  • her stupid nitwit of a husband
  • wobbly jellies of men
  • that destroyer of education of a minister
