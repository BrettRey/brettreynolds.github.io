Showing how you feel about what happened

An adverb such as fortunately can open a sentence and express the speaker's evaluation of what is reported.

Early sketch. The full entry for this pattern has one example so far and its evidence isn’t written up yet, so treat this page as a starting point.

In Fortunately, the backup worked, the word fortunately doesn't describe how the backup worked. It shows how the speaker feels about the fact that it worked. The evaluation is attached to the whole reported situation.

These adverbs are often separated from the rest of the sentence by a pause in speech.

Examples

  • Fortunately, the backup worked.
  • Sadly, the party ended early.
