Short bursts of strong feeling

An exclamation is an utterance that expresses strong feeling, and the way it's said is part of what makes it one.

Ouch! is the clearest exclamation. It's a complete utterance on its own and it just expresses feeling. But the category is wider than that. What a lovely day! and A snake! can be exclamations too, and so can an ordinary-looking statement like That's amazing!

What these share is an exclamatory tone of voice and a heightened feeling. With Ouch! that is pure feeling; with the others it is directed at something. Take away the tone and the exclamation goes with it. The feeling also can't be argued with in the usual way: replying That's false doesn't target the feeling expressed. This dictionary treats exclamation as a pattern of use, and notes that the grammar book it draws on doesn't recognize it as one tidy class.

Examples

  • Ouch!
  • What a lovely day!
  • A snake!
  • That's amazing!

Try it

Say That's amazing in a flat voice, then say it as if you've just seen something astonishing. Which one is an exclamation?
