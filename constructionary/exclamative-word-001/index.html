What and how as words of amazement

Of the question words, only what and how are used in exclamations to present something as remarkable.

In What a day it's been! and How fast she ran!, the words what and how aren't asking anything. They present the day or the speed as standing out against what we'd expect. Only these two words from the family of question words are used this way.

How goes with a describing word or an action, as in how fast. What comes before a noun, as in what children or what a day. In a question, the same words do a different job in a different kind of sentence.

Examples

  • what children
  • how fast
  • What a day it's been!
  • What awful weather we've had!
