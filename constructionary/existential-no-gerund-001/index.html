There was no stopping her

There plus be plus no plus an -ing verb presents an action as unavailable in the circumstances.

There was no stopping her says that stopping her couldn't be done. The pattern is there, a form of be, the word no, and a verb ending in -ing. It presents an action as unavailable in the circumstances.

There was no moving it normally suggests that moving it was impossible or impracticable. If someone could easily have moved a crate and just chose not to, Nobody moved it fits directly, and There was no moving it would call for more explanation. A rule can be the obstacle too. Where this sense of "can't" comes from is left open in this dictionary.

Examples

  • There was no stopping her.
  • There was no telling what he might do next.
  • There'll be no stopping her.
  • There was no moving it.

Try it

Picture two crates, one bolted to the floor and one that people simply left where it was. For which does There was no moving it fit better?
