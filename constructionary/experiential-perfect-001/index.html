Asking whether something has ever happened

A have plus past-participle sentence can place earlier happenings within a stretch of time that matters now.

Have you ever driven a bus? asks whether at least one earlier occasion fits. The speaker isn't asking about a particular day. The word ever can ask about any occurrence before the time of speaking.

Compare Did you drive the bus yesterday? Once the occasion is finished and has a definite date, the simple past is the natural choice. So have plus a verb like driven fits the experience question, and did drive fits the dated one.

Examples

  • Have you ever driven a bus?
  • Did you drive the bus yesterday?

Try it

Ask a friend Have you ever ... about something they might have done. Then ask about one particular day. Which verb form fits each question?
