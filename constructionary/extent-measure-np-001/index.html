Saying how far

After a verb of movement, a measured distance can answer the question "how far".

Early sketch. The full entry for this pattern has one example so far and its evidence isn’t written up yet, so treat this page as a starting point.

In We walked three kilometres before lunch, the phrase three kilometres says how far the walking went. It measures the distance covered, or the spatial extent of something.

That differs from a measure of time. Walked three kilometres measures distance, while walked for an hour measures how long the walking lasted and uses a for phrase. A separate phrase can also say where the walking led.

Examples

  • We walked three kilometres before lunch.
  • They ran two kilometres along the river.
