Their arriving so late

A word like their can sit in front of a verb ending in -ing to say who is doing the action.

Early sketch. The full entry for this pattern has one example so far and its evidence isn’t written up yet, so treat this page as a starting point.

In I disliked their arriving so late, the word their says who arrived. What follows is still verb-like: arriving keeps its own add-on, so late. The word their marks the one who does the action, rather than turning arriving into an ordinary noun.

The same job can be done by a different form. We objected to their blocking the exit and We objected to them blocking the exit both fit. This dictionary notes that which form is available depends on the larger pattern and on the variety of English.

Examples

  • I disliked their arriving so late.
  • We objected to their blocking the exit.
  • We objected to them blocking the exit.

Try it

Say We objected to their blocking the exit, then We objected to them blocking the exit. Which one would you say, and does the other sound natural to you?
