How odd!

How followed by an adjective can stand alone as an exclamation, showing a strong reaction to how far something goes.

Early sketch. The full entry for this pattern has one example so far and its evidence isn’t written up yet, so treat this page as a starting point.

How odd! has no subject and no verb, yet it's a complete exclamation. It expresses a reaction to a degree the speaker finds remarkable.

A longer version can spell out the situation being judged, such as How odd it is. The short form works by itself, without a subject or predicate.

Examples

  • How odd!
  • How odd it is!

Try it

Think of something that surprised you. Say it with How and one adjective.
