How come

How come asks for an explanation of something that seems to be the case.

Early sketch. The full entry for this pattern has one example so far and its evidence isn’t written up yet, so treat this page as a starting point.

How come the gate is open? asks why the gate is open. It treats the open gate as a fact that needs explaining.

The pattern differs from an ordinary why question. Why is the gate open? puts the verb before the subject. How come keeps the subject first: how come the gate is open.

Examples

  • How come the gate is open?
  • Why is the gate open?
