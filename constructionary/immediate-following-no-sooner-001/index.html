No sooner ... than

No sooner ... than presents one event as following almost at once after another.

Early sketch. The full entry for this pattern has one example so far and its evidence isn’t written up yet, so treat this page as a starting point.

In No sooner had we parked than the rain started, the rain starts almost as soon as the parking is done. The sentence starts with no sooner, and the helper word had comes before we.

The second part begins with than, which brings in the later event. Placing no sooner first is what brings in the changed word order in the first part.

Examples

  • No sooner had we parked than the rain started.
