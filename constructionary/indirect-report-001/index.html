Reporting what someone said

An indirect report gives the content of what someone said from the reporter's point of view, without repeating their exact words.

Early sketch. The full entry for this pattern has one example so far and its evidence isn’t written up yet, so treat this page as a starting point.

In Jo said that she would call the next day, the reporter tells us what Jo said. The reporter doesn't quote Jo. The person, the tense and the way the day is named can be adjusted to fit the reporter's situation.

So the reported part need not repeat the words Jo used. It reports the content, and it can report a statement or a question.

Examples

  • Jo said that she would call the next day.

Try it

Think of something a friend said to you. Can you report it to someone else without using the friend's exact words?
