Doing something with a tool

A phrase with with and a thing can name what was used to do something.

Early sketch. The full entry for this pattern has one example so far and its evidence isn’t written up yet, so treat this page as a starting point.

In Lee opened the case with a small screwdriver, the phrase with a small screwdriver names what Lee used. The screwdriver helped the action happen.

The same words with can introduce someone who came along, as in with the watchman. That's a different meaning, so the sentence around it matters.

Examples

  • Lee opened the case with a small screwdriver.
  • She stirred the soup with a wooden spoon.

Try it

Say Lee opened the case with a screwdriver, then Lee opened the case with the watchman. How does the meaning of with change?
