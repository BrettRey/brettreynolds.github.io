I don't know who

A question can be reduced to its question word when the rest can be recovered from what came before.

Early sketch. The full entry for this pattern has one example so far and its evidence isn’t written up yet, so treat this page as a starting point.

In Someone moved the keys, but I don't know who, the word who stands for the whole question who moved the keys. The first part of the sentence supplies what's missing.

A longer question phrase can stay too, as in which person. Only the part being asked about is spoken, and the listener fills in the rest.

Examples

  • Someone moved the keys, but I don’t know who.
  • Mia left early, but I don't know why.
