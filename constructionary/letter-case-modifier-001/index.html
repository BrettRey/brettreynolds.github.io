Small-c and big-D labels

A word like small-c or big-D placed before a label tells the reader which kind of meaning the label has.

Putting small-c before a word tells you to read it in its ordinary, descriptive sense, not as the name of an institution. Putting big-D before a word does the opposite. In She is a big-D Democrat, the label points to the party, while small-c catholic in He's a small-c catholic points to the ordinary sense.

The little modifier names a letter and whether it's capital or small. It works as a quick instruction about how to read the word that follows. The longer version puts a phrase with a capital D after the label.

Examples

  • He's a small-c catholic.
  • She is a Big-D Democrat.
  • That was little-p politics.
