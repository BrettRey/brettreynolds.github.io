With Plus an Absolute Predication

absolute-with-predication-001 · mixed · provisional

PP[with + NP + participial or verbless predicate]

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Presents a background condition with its own subject and predicate.

  • With the shutters closed, the room stayed cool.Constructed illustration.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The shutters are understood as closed. The relation to the main situation can be merely concomitant or explanatory, whereas what with conventionally gives a reason.
