Activity Weak Definite

activity-weak-definite-001 · mixed · provisional

Host expressing a conventional activity + NP[the + N]

Meaning

Invokes a conventional activity with a definite NP that need not identify a unique participant or object.

In take the bus, the transport activity can be understood without identifying which vehicle is involved. The same NP can identify a particular vehicle with a different predicate. The host and the noun both matter, and the activity frame may also allow an ordinary identifying reading in context.

  • We took the bus.Conventional transport use. The NP sits inside the bracketed activity frame.Huddleston, Rodney & Geoffrey K. Pullum (2002), The Cambridge Grammar of the English Language. Cambridge: CUP, Ch. 5 §8.4(b), p. 408 [17v]
  • Lee parked the bus.Constructed identifying control. The verb and activity are different, with the noun and article held fixed.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

With a suitable host and noun, the hearer can recover the activity without identifying a unique participant of the kind denoted by the noun.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL's activity examples and Klein et al.'s Experiment 4, which combines and varies the verbs and nouns in licensing environments.

Confidence: medium.
