Raising with an Adjectival Predicative

adjectival-raising-001 · mixed · provisional

Clause[Subject:NPᵢ + be + PredComp:AdjP[Adj + to-infinitival with understood subjectᵢ]]

Meaning

Locates likelihood or a related assessment in the situation expressed by the infinitival.

Likely assesses the situation. It permits dummy there in the subject position because that position serves the infinitival predication. In determined to leave, the adjective ascribes determination to its subject and does not permit dummy there.

  • There is likely to be enough food left.Dummy there is licensed within the existential situation assessed.Huddleston, Rodney & Geoffrey K. Pullum (2002), The Cambridge Grammar of the English Language. Cambridge: CUP, Ch. 14 §8.1 [4ivb]
  • Liz was likely to convince them.The situation remains the same in They were likely to be convinced by Liz.Huddleston, Rodney & Geoffrey K. Pullum (2002), The Cambridge Grammar of the English Language. Cambridge: CUP, Ch. 14 §8.1 [3b], [4iib]

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Dummy subjects are compatible with the raising pattern. Substituting a passive description of the same situation need not shift the target of likely.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL tests matched raising and non-raising adjectives with dummy subjects, passive alternation and selection. CASA records the likely construction.

Confidence: high.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
