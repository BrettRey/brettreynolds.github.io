Adjectival tough Construction

adjective-hollow-infinitival-001 · mixed · provisional

Clause[Subject:NPᵢ + be + PredComp:AdjP[Adj + to-infinitival Clause with non-subject gapᵢ]]

Meaning

Characterizes the matrix subject by evaluating an action involving it.

In their argument was impossible to follow, the argument is understood as the object of follow. A preposition can supply the gap instead. The infinitival may have its own subject with for. Compare Kim was anxious to follow the argument, where Kim is the understood subject of follow. The construction makes an evaluation about the matrix subject, which excludes dummy there on this reading.

  • Their argument was impossible to follow.The argument is the understood object of follow.Huddleston, Rodney & Geoffrey K. Pullum (2002), The Cambridge Grammar of the English Language. Cambridge: CUP, Ch. 14 §8.1, p. 1257 [2i]
  • His performance was easy to find fault with.The gap is the object of with.Huddleston, Rodney & Geoffrey K. Pullum (2002), The Cambridge Grammar of the English Language. Cambridge: CUP, Ch. 7 §4.1, [3vii]
  • The instructions were hard for us to follow.Constructed with an overt subject in the infinitival.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The matrix subject supplies a non-subject argument of the infinitival. The infinitival can separately identify who undertakes the action.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL compares hollow and ordinary infinitivals and contrasts their subject restrictions. CASA records the difficult-to-answer pattern.

Confidence: high.
