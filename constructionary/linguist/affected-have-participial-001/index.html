Have with a Past-Participial Predication

affected-have-participial-001 · mixed · provisional

VP[have + NP + past-participial predicate]

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Presents an outcome as arranged by or affecting the subject.

  • We had the roof replaced in June.Constructed illustration.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The subject can be an arranger, but had her bag stolen can describe an unwanted event she experienced. The post-have NP is understood as the affected participant of the participial.
