All Modifier Restricting a Role

all-class-role-modifier-001 · mixed · provisional

Nom[all + class-denoting nominal or adjective + N]

Meaning

Restricts the occupants of a salient role associated with the head noun to one class.

With an all girls’ school the relevant role is eligibility to attend. The school can educate only some of the town’s girls and have men on staff. With an all-women team the team supplies the members. Genitive and plain-case nominal forms and adjectives support the interpretation. CGEL treats all girls’ as a genitive NP with peripheral all. The related patterns leave that token’s internal bracketing open.

  • an all girls’ schoolThe restriction concerns who can attend the school.Huddleston, Rodney & Geoffrey K. Pullum (2002), The Cambridge Grammar of the English Language. Cambridge: CUP, Ch. 5 §16.3, p. 470 [45]
  • all-female police unitAdjectival class specification.Klossek, Lara & Elisabeth Johansson-Nogués (2021), The Female ‘Boot on the Ground’: Indian Ambivalence Over Gender Mainstreaming in UN Peacekeeping Operations. International Peacekeeping, opening discussion, p. 1 of author manuscript

The same contingent is described by both published expressions.Klossek, Lara & Elisabeth Johansson-Nogués (2021), The Female ‘Boot on the Ground’: Indian Ambivalence Over Gender Mainstreaming in UN Peacekeeping Operations. What recognizing it lets you predict

The head helps select the set to which all applies. The restriction applies to students in the school case and members in the team case.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL's exclusivity gloss and the two expressions for the same Indian police contingent support the cross-form comparison.
