All of with a Stated Magnitude

all-of-magnitude-001 · mixed · provisional

all of + (approximator) + numeral expression, with no definite article after of

Meaning

Calls attention to a stated magnitude relative to an expectation.

The numeral gives the measured or counted magnitude. A distinct approximator can still make it inexact. All of the fifty volunteers instead quantifies over a separately identified group. The direction of the evaluation in the magnitude use remains open and can be tested by varying the expectation while holding the magnitude fixed.

  • They had recruited all of fifty volunteers.The magnitude supplies a count of the recruits.Huddleston, Rodney & Geoffrey K. Pullum (2002), The Cambridge Grammar of the English Language. Cambridge: CUP, Ch. 5 §11(b), p. 431
  • all of about five minutesOvert approximation in an oral history.Rosenthal oral history, 16 May 1973. National Museum of American History, Smithsonian Institution, 16 May 1973 oral history
  • all of about ten minutesOvert approximation in an oral history.A. Mark Rabin oral history. Illinois Supreme Court Historic Preservation Commission, p. 61

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The pattern can retain about and its approximation while inviting an assessment of whether the magnitude is noteworthy in context.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL's example and the two oral histories establish the form. The role of expectations is a proposal to test.

Confidence: low.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
