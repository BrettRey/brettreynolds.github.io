Concessive Although Adjunct

although-concessive-001 · mixed · provisional

PP[although + finite or licensed reduced predication]

Meaning

Acknowledges a circumstance that might have counted against the main claim.

  • Although the steps were icy, we reached the door.Constructed illustration.
  • Even if the steps are icy, we’ll reach the door.Constructed comparison. Conditional concession leaves the ice uncertain.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Both the ice and the arrival are presented as actual. The contrast comes from an expectation about what the ice might impede.
