Singular Agreement with an Amount Expression

amount-singular-agreement-001 · syntactic · provisional

Clause[plural-shaped measure NP as subject + singular agreement]

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Treats a multi-unit expression as a single amount for agreement.

  • Three hours is enough.Constructed illustration.

Related constructions

A kind of

  • Verb Number Agreement

Interacts with

  • Measure Pseudopartitive with OfA measure pseudopartitive can be the subject of singular amount agreement, as in Three litres of milk is enough.

Compare

  • Notional Agreement with a Collective NPThree litres is enough treats a measurement as one amount. A collective NP such as the committee can allow plural agreement on a member-oriented reading.
  • Grammatical Number

Sources

  • Huddleston, Rodney & Geoffrey K. Pullum (2002), The Cambridge Grammar of the English Language. Cambridge: CUP, Ch. 5 §18.3, measure phrases. discussed · bears on projection

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The singular is natural when the predicate evaluates a duration as a whole. An individually predicated series of hours can instead take plural agreement.
