Anaphoric So with an Attitude Predicate

anaphoric-propositional-so-001 · mixed · provisional

licensed predicate + so

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Picks up a prior proposition as the content of an attitude or utterance.

  • “Are the keys inside?” “I think so.”Constructed illustration.

Related constructions

Alternates with

  • Anaphoric Not with an Attitude PredicateI hope so and I hope not share an attitude predicate. So picks up the proposition in play. Not picks up its negative counterpart.

Compare

  • Anaphoric Do So
  • Proposition Affirmation with Fronted So

Sources

  • Huddleston, Rodney & Geoffrey K. Pullum (2002), The Cambridge Grammar of the English Language. Cambridge: CUP, Ch. 9 §7.2; Ch. 17 §7.7.2. discussed · bears on projection

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

So is interpreted as the proposition that the keys are inside. Substitution of do so gives an action anaphor and fails to express that content in this frame.
