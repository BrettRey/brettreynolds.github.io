Ordered Auxiliary Chain

auxiliary-chain-001 · syntactic · provisional

VP[(modal) + (perfect have + past participle) + (progressive be + gerund-participle) + (passive be + past participle)]

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Composes several auxiliaries with locally selected verb forms and restricted relative order.

  • It might have been being repaired.Constructed illustration.

Interacts with

  • Perfect HAVE + V-enIn may have been waiting, perfect have follows the modal and selects the past-participial form been.
  • Progressive -ingIn has been waiting, progressive be follows perfect have and takes a gerund-participle.
  • Verbal Be-PassiveIn is being repaired, passive be follows progressive be and selects a past participle. The form being is selected by the progressive.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Each auxiliary determines the immediately following form. The components normally retain the modal–perfect–progressive–passive order even when intermediate components are omitted.
