Past Perfect Anteriority

backshift-past-perfect-001 · mixed · provisional

Clause[preterite had + past-participial VP] with a past temporal anchor

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Places the event or state before a distinct past reference point.

  • When I arrived, Jo had already left.Constructed illustration.

Related constructions

A kind of

  • Perfect HAVE + V-en

Compare

Sources

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The departure precedes the arrival on the ordinary reading. The reference point can be supplied by the surrounding narrative without a separate time adjunct.
