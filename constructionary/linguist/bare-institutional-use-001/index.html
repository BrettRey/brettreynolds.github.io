Institutional Bare Singular

bare-institutional-use-001 · mixed · provisional

bare singular institutional noun in a licensed frame

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Refers to participation in an institutional activity or status.

  • The children are at school.Constructed illustration.

Related constructions

A kind of

  • Bare NP Construction

Overlaps with

  • Bare Role Construction

Compare

  • Activity Weak DefiniteWent to school and took the bus both evoke an institutional activity. School is bare in the first frame, and bus keeps the in the second.

Sources

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

At school can describe the relevant institutional setting without selecting a particular building in the same way as at the school. Licensing depends on noun, context and variety.
