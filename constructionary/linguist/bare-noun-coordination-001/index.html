Bare Singular Noun Coordination

bare-noun-coordination-001 · mixed · provisional

Coordination[bare singular count form + and + bare singular count form]

Meaning

Presents two linked individuals or roles through one bare coordinate phrase.

The context may make the pair identifiable or introduce its members as new. In the invitation example, the preceding text introduces the bride and her dog, which lets the coordination refer to their depictions. The pairing can support an argument use whose individual bare nouns would require separate licensing.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Context can license the whole coordination as an argument, with a recoverable relationship between the individuals and either a familiar or a new pair.

Evidence for the projection

Le Bruyn and de Swart document both interpretations and the role of contextual links, extending the pattern beyond familiar collocations.

Confidence: medium.
