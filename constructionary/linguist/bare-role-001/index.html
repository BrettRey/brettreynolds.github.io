Bare Role Construction

bare-role-001 · mixed · provisional

NP[Det:absent + Nom[Head:singular count role noun]], in a licensed role slot

Meaning

Meaning 1

Ascribes an office or institutional role, or gives the content of an office expression.

The same nominal can follow be, become, remain, appoint, or elect when the verb licenses a predicative complement. It can also occur with as and in role of or position of, in a matrix-licensed except phrase, or as an ascriptive supplement. In an appointment construction the direct object identifies the appointee and the bare NP gives the office.

  • They appointed Lee treasurer of the club.Constructed object-oriented predicative. In They questioned the treasurer of the club, the article-marked NP instead identifies the person questioned.
  • Lee served as treasurer.Constructed prepositional frame. Compare Lee applied for the position [of treasurer]. Both retain a bare role nominal.
  • Faatulu was named co-captain along with fellow junior Jamie Peck.The sports report explicitly names two holders of the role.Arizona State University Athletics, Jolynn Faatulu Overcomes Injuries to Shine, Paragraph beginning As one of the few upperclassmen
  • Lee is secretary of the club.Constructed after CGEL’s comparison. Lee is the secretary of the club can also specify the incumbent in answer to Who is the secretary?
  • I’ve been everything except president.The except phrase picks out a role from the alternatives made available by the matrix clause.Huddleston, Rodney & Geoffrey K. Pullum (2002), The Cambridge Grammar of the English Language. Cambridge: CUP, Ch. 7 §5, matrix-licensed complements [21ii]
  • Wilson, Secretary to the Cabinet, had informed the Prime Minister immediately.Ascriptive supplement. The bare NP cannot replace Wilson as the referential subject.Huddleston, Rodney & Geoffrey K. Pullum (2002), The Cambridge Grammar of the English Language. Cambridge: CUP, Ch. 15 §5 [22ii]

Meaning 2

Ascribes a professional assignment construed as a post.

A workplace can make a particular assignment available for the construction. The availability of a bare form is sensitive to lexical choice and how a job title is used in the institution.

  • They appointed Lee lead counsel for the appeal.Constructed. Lead counsel specifies the assignment.
  • Lee became a lawyer.Constructed comparison. In ordinary standard usage, became lawyer is degraded on the professional-class reading.

Meaning 3

Ascribes a relational or ceremonial role in a licensed frame.

A following relational dependent can specify whom the role is held in relation to. This use is limited to independently available role expressions. It is distinct from using a kin term as a form of address or as a standing name. Holding a kin role doesn't confer the kin term as a designation: Schneider and Homans report that when a child is reared from infancy by an aunt playing a maternal role, she “is still not called ‘mother’” (1955: 1203).

  • They asked Lee to be godfather to their son.Constructed relational example. The to-phrase supplies the other participant.
  • There can likewise be only one person who is father of the student I have in mind.CGEL’s own prose, discussing the identifiability of the father of the student; the sentence's opening In [2ii] is omitted. A kin noun heads the bare predicative, and of the student gives the relatum.Huddleston, Rodney & Geoffrey K. Pullum (2002), The Cambridge Grammar of the English Language. Cambridge: CUP, Ch. 5 §6.1, p. 369
  • She was mother to three boys.Constructed. A single kin noun, with the to-phrase giving the relatum. Kin nouns under this meaning are an adjudication (Brett, 2026-11-19): CGEL lists kin or similar relations separately from offices or roles (Ch. 4 §5.5.1(e)).

Constraints

  • syntactic A bare role NP can describe the subject or object. It does not ordinarily occupy the subject or object position as a referential expression for the roleholder.
  • semantic The choice of noun and context must support a role reading. Predicative function alone does not license removal of the article from become a miser or become a teacher.
  • semantic The role can have multiple holders. A counterpart with an overt determiner can also ascribe a role, as in became a co-captain or remained our captain. With be, a counterpart with the can additionally identify the contextually relevant incumbent.

Dimensions

Form
A singular count nominal, optionally modified, with no determiner
Meaning
Presents an office, assignment, or role that can be held or taken on
Function
Subject- or object-oriented predicative, complement of a preposition in a licensed frame, or ascriptive supplement
Distribution
Available in particular role-denoting senses and constructions. Ordinary referential subject and object positions generally require a determiner with the same singular count noun.

Related constructions

A kind of

  • Bare NP ConstructionThis is one use of a determinerless NP headed by a nominal.

Overlaps with

  • Institutional Bare SingularBoth license bare singular count NPs. Compare remained treasurer with went to school. The licensed slots and interpretations differ.
  • Matched Bare EndpointsRole nouns can also occur bare as paired endpoints, as in from teacher to pupil. The pairing licenses a source–goal interpretation.
  • Strong Proper Name ConstructionBoth are determinerless singular NPs whose definite interpretation is relative to a domain, an organisation for treasurer and a household for Mom. Function distinguishes them: a bare role NP is predicative or in a licensed frame (Lee remained treasurer), while a strong proper name is a referential argument (Mom called; compare CGEL’s ∗I’m told treasurer strongly supports this proposal, p. 409). Inserting the keeps the ascription in Henry became the treasurer (p. 409) but loses the name in The mom called (constructed). A role form used as a standing name independently licenses referential argument uses.

Interacts with

  • Subject-Oriented Ascriptive PredicativeA bare role NP can ascribe a post to the subject.
  • Object-Oriented Ascriptive PredicativeIn the appointment frame the predicand is realized as object.
  • Preposition PhraseA bare role NP can be the predicative complement of as or a complement of selected of or except.

Alternates with

  • Indefinite NP ConstructionCompare became co-captain and became a co-captain. Both can ascribe the same post.
  • Definite NP ConstructionCompare remained captain and remained our captain. The determiner adds a relation to the speaker’s group. With be, the captain can also give a specifying reading.

Compare

  • Specificational Copular ClauseCompare Lee is secretary of the club and Lee is the secretary of the club. The latter can answer Who is the secretary?
  • Supplementary Appositive NPWilson, Secretary to the Cabinet shares the supplement packaging. Its bare supplement cannot replace Wilson as subject, which is CGEL’s diagnostic against apposition here.
  • Vocative ConstructionCompare Chair, may I respond? with Lee remained chair. The role noun serves as a form of address in the first example and a predicative in the second.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

In ordinary standard English, with a reading that ascribes an assignment or gives its content, a nominal such as treasurer can remain bare in She stayed treasurer, They appointed her treasurer, and As treasurer, she signed. Reuse as a stand-alone referential object, as in They questioned the treasurer, normally calls for a determiner. The same bare office ascription can apply to more than one holder.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL compares the same noun in predicative and object positions and documents the prepositional and supplementary uses. The Arizona State report documents shared tenure. Whether a counterpart with the preserves the bare reading depends on the frame and context.

Confidence: high.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.

Boundary cases

  • Dad called, but Sam answered (constructed). Dad identifies a person as subject when the family uses it as a name: an instance of strong-proper-name-001, not of this construction.
  • Captain called, said of Lee, a former captain the team still calls Captain, while Sam holds the post (constructed). Captain identifies Lee, the captain identifies Sam, and Lee is captain is false: the designation outlasts the tenure, so the referential use belongs to strong-proper-name-001, not to this construction.
