Bare Deictic Calendar NP

calendar-deictic-np-001 · mixed · provisional

NP[this/last/next + calendar noun or weekday] in temporal adjunct use

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Uses the contextual present to select an adjacent or current calendar period.

  • I’ll call next Tuesday.Constructed illustration.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The NP can be a time adjunct without on. The relevant Tuesday is selected by the deictic expression, with some variation in how speakers resolve next.
