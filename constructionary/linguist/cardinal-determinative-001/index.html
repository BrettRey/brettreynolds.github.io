Cardinal Determinative

cardinal-determinative-001 · syntactic · provisional

Det[cardinal]

Meaning

Cardinal numerative that can determine a nominal by giving a numerical value

  • I saw three cats.Low cardinal determiner
  • She has twenty-six students.Basic compound determiner
  • The building has one thousand twenty-four rooms.Complex cardinal determiner

Constraints

  • syntactic Functions as determiner in NP structure; can appear as fused head (e.g., "I bought three").

Dimensions

Form
Basic (0-99) or magnitude determinative; no number inflection
Meaning
Supplies a cardinal value. Interpretation as an exact total or as a lower boundary depends on context.
Function
Heads DPs functioning as determiners, factors, or fused heads
Distribution
Determiner in NP; factor in magnitude phrases; fused determiner-head

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

A cardinal determiner in thirty copies can alternate with some copies. In two hundred, two is internal to the numerative and can coexist with an external determiner in a two hundred, on the token reading.

Evidence for the projection

Reynolds uses coexistence with an external determiner to distinguish determiner function from multiplicative modification.

Confidence: medium.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
