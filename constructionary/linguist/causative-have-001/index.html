Causative Have with a Bare Infinitival

causative-have-001 · mixed · provisional

VP[have + NP + bare infinitival]

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Presents an event as arranged, instructed or brought about by the subject.

  • I’ll have Jo check the figures.Constructed illustration.

Related constructions

  • Verb Phrase

Compare

  • Perfect HAVE + V-en
  • Have with a Past-Participial PredicationHad Kai replace the roof makes Kai the understood replacer. Had the roof replaced presents the roof as affected and can describe an arrangement. Had his phone stolen can describe an unwanted experience.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Jo undertakes the checking. Have Jo check contrasts with perfect have checked and with affected have the figures checked.
