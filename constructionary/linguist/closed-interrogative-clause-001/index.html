Closed Interrogative Clause

closed-interrogative-clause-001 · syntactic · predication · provisional

Clause: (Subject:XP +) Head:VP[closed interrogative; main-clause subject–auxiliary inversion]

Meaning

A headed clause type characteristically used to present a closed question

Closed and open interrogatives are distinct primary clause types in CGEL, not syntactic subtypes defined by a shared interrogative form. In main closed interrogatives, clause type itself triggers subject–auxiliary inversion and do-support where no other auxiliary is available. Subordinate closed interrogatives normally use whether or if without inversion. Closed-question force is characteristic rather than necessary: Would you mind opening the door? is syntactically closed interrogative but conventionally functions as a directive (CGEL pp. 853–57, 865–72, 951–55).

  • Are you generous?Main closed interrogative with subject–auxiliary inversion
  • Did Kim finish the report?Do-support supplies the auxiliary required by main-clause inversion
  • I wonder whether Kim finished the report.Subordinate closed interrogative with whether and basic Subject–Predicator order
  • Would you mind opening the door?Closed interrogative conventionally used as an indirect directive

Constraints

  • syntactic The construction remains a Clause headed by VP. In a main clause the finite auxiliary precedes the Subject through subject–auxiliary inversion; subordinate closed interrogatives normally have whether/if marking and no inversion.
  • semantic The characteristic question defines a closed answer set; alternative questions are a use of coordination with closed-interrogative syntax, not a sixth clause type.
  • pragmatic Inquiry force is not necessary: indirect directives and strongly biased or rhetorical uses retain closed-interrogative clause type.

Dimensions

Form
Clause headed by a VP; main clauses show clause-type-triggered subject–auxiliary inversion, while subordinate content clauses are normally marked by whether or if and retain basic Subject–Predicator order
Meaning
Characteristically presents a closed set of answers, prototypically the positive and negative values of a proposition
Function
Characteristically asks a closed question, but can perform directives, acknowledgements, statements, and other indirect speech acts
Distribution
Tensed main clauses and subordinate closed-interrogative content clauses

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Clause-type-triggered inversion in a main clause, or subordinate whether/if marking, predicts a closed answer space and the corresponding main/subordinate distribution, while leaving room for indirect forces such as directives

Evidence for the projection

The inversion and subordinator diagnostics distinguish closed interrogatives from declaratives and open interrogatives independently of whether a particular utterance is used to inquire

Confidence: high.

Core properties

  • Main-clause subject–auxiliary inversion (finite auxiliary before Subject; do-support without another auxiliary)
  • Subordinate whether/if marking (whether or interrogative if; basic Subject–Predicator order in the standard variety)
  • Closed answer space (polar answers; polar-alternative answers)
