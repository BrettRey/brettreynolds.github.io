Subordinate Closed Interrogative with whether

closed-interrogative-whether-001 · syntactic · provisional

whether + subordinate declarative-order nucleus, or whether + to-infinitival

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Introduces an embedded closed question or choice between alternatives.

  • Whether to reschedule still needs discussion.Constructed illustration.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Whether can introduce an infinitival, where interrogative if is unavailable. Certain finite prepositional and subject environments likewise favour or require whether.
