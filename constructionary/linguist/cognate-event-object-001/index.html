Cognate Event Object

cognate-event-object-001 · mixed · provisional

VP[Head:V[event] + Od:NP[event nominal cognate with V]]

Meaning

Elaborates the event already described by the verb.

An otherwise intransitive verb such as die can occur with a cognate event noun and its modifiers. The object in died a long and agonising death adds duration and manner without introducing a second dying event. Modification is commonly needed to make the object informative. This entry targets the event-reading construction, including otherwise intransitive verbs.

  • He died a long and agonising death.The NP supplies information about how he died.Huddleston, Rodney & Geoffrey K. Pullum (2002), The Cambridge Grammar of the English Language. Cambridge: CUP, Ch. 8 §2.1, p. 673 [11b]
  • She fought a heroic fight.The event nominal elaborates the fight.Huddleston, Rodney & Geoffrey K. Pullum (2002), The Cambridge Grammar of the English Language. Cambridge: CUP, Ch. 8 §2.1, p. 673 [11a]

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

An intransitive verb can take an event NP when its head and the verb describe the same event type. The NP commonly adds information through modification.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL documents the manner reading, and Bouso, Hundt and Van Driessche distinguish the event-denoting cognate from other object-like expressions.

Confidence: high.
