Collective Numeral Construction

collective-numeral-001 · syntactic · provisional

in + N[cardinal, plural] + (and + N[cardinal, plural])

Meaning

Cardinal nouns denote group sizes for collective arrangement

  • in twos and threesArriving/arranged in small groups
  • by the hundredsMagnitude reading, often large numbers rather than batches of exactly one hundred
  • count by fivesGrouping for counting

Constraints

  • syntactic Cardinal noun is a common noun; takes plural morphology.
  • structural Typically in PP with 'in' or 'by'.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

People entered in threes can describe an arbitrarily large crowd. Three counts the members in each group even though threes is itself grammatically plural. A phrase such as by the hundreds can instead indicate large numbers without fixed groups.

Evidence for the projection

Reynolds assigns the grouping use its own semantic role among cardinal nouns. The constructed contrast checks whether the number applies to the total or to the groups.

Confidence: medium.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
