Comparative -er

comparative-er-001 · morphological · provisional

XP[X + -er] where X < {tall, deep, soon, near, few, ...} >

Meaning

Meaning 1

Denotes scalar superiority

Semantic operation comparing two points on scale denoted by base

  • taller/deeperProperty comparison
  • sooner/nearerRelation comparison
  • fewerQuantity comparison

Meaning 2

Denotes scalar difference

Semantic operation measuring interval between compared points

  • much tallerRelative interval
  • two inches tallerMeasured interval

Meaning 3

Denotes comparison basis

Semantic operation establishing reference point

  • taller than BillExplicit reference
  • the taller buildingContextual reference

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The same comparative relation can be expressed by a suffix with tall or by more with expensive. The suffix does not freely spread from one gradable adjective to another. Either realization can introduce a than-standard and accept a differential where the adjective allows it.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL treats comparative realization and the form of the standard as distinct choices and documents lexical variation in grade inflection.

Confidence: medium.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
