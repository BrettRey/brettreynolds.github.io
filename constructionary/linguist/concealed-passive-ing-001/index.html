Concealed Passive in an -ing Complement

concealed-passive-ing-001 · mixed · provisional

VP[need/want/require or other licensing V + gerund-participial]

Meaning

Uses an active-shaped complement with a passive interpretation.

  • The hinge needs oiling.Constructed illustration.
  • The hinge needs to be oiled.Constructed comparison. The infinitival counterpart displays the passive overtly.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The hinge is understood as what someone oils. A to-infinitival with analogous meaning normally requires passive morphology, as in needs to be oiled.
