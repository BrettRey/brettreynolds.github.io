Noun with a Declarative Content Complement

content-clause-in-np-001 · mixed · provisional

Nom[N + finite content clause]

Meaning

Supplies the propositional content associated with a noun such as claim or fact.

  • The claim that Jo knew the code surprised us.Constructed illustration.
  • The claim that Jo rejected surprised us.Constructed comparison. Integrated relative with a missing object corresponding to claim.
