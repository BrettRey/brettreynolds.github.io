Continuative away at

continuative-away-at-001 · mixed · provisional

VP[Head:V + away + PP[at + NP[target]]]

Meaning

Describes sustained or repeated activity directed at a target.

The activity can consist of many small effects on the target without presenting a finished result. The combination sometimes allows a verb that is markedly less acceptable with at alone. Away here has an aspectual contribution. A directional away from construction has a separate interpretation.

  • Simmy was carving away at the roast.Published comparison. The version without away was judged degraded.Jackendoff, Ray (1997), Twistin' the Night Away. Language 73(3): 534-559, p. 540
  • They worked away at the problem all afternoon.Constructed example. The duration concerns the work and does not imply a solution.

Related constructions

Compare

  • Conative atThe two patterns share a target at-phrase and differ in aspect and available verbs.
  • Time-away ConstructionBoth have nonlocative away, but the time-away pattern licenses a time object.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Away favours an extended or repeated reading. In the published carve pair it also improves the target-at frame without adding a spatial direction.

Evidence for the projection

The Birmingham inventory separates duration, effort, bit-by-bit damage and ingestion. Jackendoff gives a comparison holding the verb and target fixed.

Confidence: medium.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
