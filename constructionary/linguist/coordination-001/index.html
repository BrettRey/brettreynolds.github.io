Linguist

Coordination

coordination-001 · syntactic · provisional

Coordinate (+ Marker:coordinator) + Coordinate ...

Meaning

A non-headed constituent made up of coordinates; not a phrase

Coordinations contrast with phrases, which are headed (see phrase-001). No coordinate is the head; the coordination's distribution follows from the coordinates jointly, and a difference of category is generally tolerated where there is likeness of function (e.g., the DP + NP numerative coordinations in two thousand and twenty-seven).

Constraints

  • syntactic No head; each coordinate could typically stand in the coordination's position alone.

Dimensions

Form
Two or more coordinates, optionally linked by coordinators (and, or, but, nor); no head
Meaning
Joint contribution of the coordinates (conjunction, disjunction, contrast, addition)
Function
Whatever function the coordinates jointly serve
Distribution
Distributes as its coordinates allow; category difference between coordinates is tolerated where there is likeness of function

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Identifying a constituent as a coordination predicts headlessness (no single element determines distribution), coordinate-wise distribution, and tolerance of category mixture under likeness of function

Evidence for the projection

Coordinate-category mixture under functional likeness is attested across the numerative coordinations and ordinary NP/AdjP coordination

Confidence: high.
