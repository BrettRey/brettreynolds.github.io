Deadjectival Noun in -ity

deadjectival-ity-noun-001 · mixed · provisional

N[adjectival base + -ity], sometimes with a stem alternation

Meaning

Names a quality or state associated with the adjective.

-ity typically stresses the syllable immediately before the ending. It may also alter the end of the base, as in curious and curiosity. Formations cluster with particular adjective types. The corresponding -ness formation typically preserves the base’s shape and stress.

  • eccentricityExisting derivative illustrating stress shift from eccentric and a change in the final consonant.
  • curiosityExisting derivative illustrating shift of stress and the form of the base.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Recognizable -ity derivatives generally have stress just before the suffix. The pattern allows changes to the base that the productive -ness pattern does not.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL gives matched stress and stem contrasts. CASA records -ity separately from -ness.

Confidence: high.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
