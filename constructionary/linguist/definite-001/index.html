Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The plumber can identify an unmentioned person via an appointment already known to the hearer. On the usual weak reading of take the bus, the same article need not identify a particular vehicle. Familiarity and uniqueness are therefore subject to independent checks.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL describes recognitional and restricted activity readings. Reynolds treats deital syntax and definite interpretation as distinguishable.

Confidence: medium.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
