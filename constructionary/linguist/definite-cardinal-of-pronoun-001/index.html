The Two of Us

definite-cardinal-of-pronoun-001 · mixed · provisional

NP[the + cardinal + PP[of + personal pronoun]]

Meaning

Identifies a group while explicitly giving its size.

The four of us can describe the group of four that includes the speaker. If a larger group is salient, the phrase needs a recoverable set of four. Without the, four of us can simply pick an unspecified subset of a larger us-group. The scope of the definite is the group picked out by the whole NP.

  • There were six of us, and four of us had bikes.Constructed subset with the article absent.
  • I was there with Jo, Lee and Pat. The four of us shared a tent.Constructed definite whose numeral matches the contextually salient group.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

A cardinal with the can count members of an already identified group. Without the, the same numeral and pronoun also admit an unspecified subset of a larger salient group.

Evidence for the projection

CASA records the cardinal and pronoun slots and the group-size reading. CGEL explicitly contrasts the two of us with both of us and dialectal the both of us.

Confidence: high.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
