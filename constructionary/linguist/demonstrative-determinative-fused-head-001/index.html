Demonstrative Determinative in Fused Determiner-Head Function

demonstrative-determinative-fused-head-001 · syntactic · reference · provisional

NP[demonstrative determinative as fused determiner-head]

Meaning

A demonstrative determinative realizes fused determiner-head function in an NP and refers deictically to a salient entity or situation

CGEL treats this, that, these, and those as determinatives; independent NP use does not reclassify them as pronouns.

  • That is incredible!That is a demonstrative determinative in fused determiner-head function, referring to the current situation.

Constraints

  • syntactic The fused determiner-head must be realized by this, that, these, or those.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

That can be an entire NP subject in That is strange. In That plan is strange, it instead determines a nominal. A prior proposition can supply the deictic target even with no physical object indicated.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL treats the two syntactic realizations separately and documents discourse-deictic demonstratives.

Confidence: medium.
