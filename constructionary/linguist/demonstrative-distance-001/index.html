Deictic Demonstrative Determination

demonstrative-distance-001 · mixed · provisional

NP[Det:this/that or these/those + Nom]

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Directs attention to a referent and presents it as proximal or distal in a relevant deictic dimension.

  • Could you pass me that blue folder over there?Constructed illustration.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

This and that can select different candidates without a prior noun-phrase antecedent. Spatial distance is one dimension and is not obligatory in discourse-deictic uses.
