Demonstrative with an Oblique Genitive

demonstrative-oblique-genitive-001 · mixed · provisional

NP[Det:demonstrative + Nom[N ... of NP[gen]]]

Meaning

Picks out a referent through a demonstrative and a genitive relation, including a uniquely possessed referent.

Barker analyzes a demonstrative construction in which that nose of his is usable for an ordinary person with one nose, though the nose of his is degraded. He links the use to focus on the head and speaker affect. The extent to which affect is required across CGEL’s examples remains open.

  • that husband of Mary’sThe phrase can be tested in a setting with a single husband.Huddleston, Rodney & Geoffrey K. Pullum (2002), The Cambridge Grammar of the English Language. Cambridge: CUP, Ch. 5 §16.5.3 [62i]

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The demonstrative form can license an oblique genitive even when the relation supplies only one relevant referent.

Evidence for the projection

Barker's controlled contrast and the range of relations in CGEL's demonstrative series.

Confidence: medium.
