Denominator Numeral Construction

denominator-numeral-001 · syntactic · provisional

Det + N[cardinal]

Meaning

Cardinal noun denotes a token numeral (score, written symbol, etc.)

  • I rolled a ten.Numeral as game score
  • She drew three tens.Plural cardinal noun for multiple tokens
  • That's a perfect ten.Rating/score use

Constraints

  • syntactic Behaves as count noun; takes determiners and plural morphology.
  • semantic Denotes a token or instance of a numeral, not an abstract number.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

I rolled three tens describes three tokens of the value ten, rather than a count of ten objects. The lexical noun takes its own plural marking and appears under a separate cardinal determiner.

Evidence for the projection

The numeral paper distinguishes the count noun for a token or score from arithmetic name and quantificational uses.

Confidence: medium.
