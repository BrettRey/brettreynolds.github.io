Depictive Secondary Predication

depictive-predication-001 · mixed · provisional

Clause[primary VP + secondary AdjP/NP predicated of a participant]

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Ascribes a condition to a participant during the primary event.

  • They carried the patient unconscious.Constructed illustration.

Related constructions

Compare

  • Subject-Oriented Resultative
  • Object-Oriented Resultative ConstructionAte the fish raw gives the state of the fish at the time of eating. Cooked the fish dry presents dryness as a result.

Sources

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The patient can already have been unconscious before being carried. That temporal compatibility distinguishes the depictive reading from a caused-result reading.
