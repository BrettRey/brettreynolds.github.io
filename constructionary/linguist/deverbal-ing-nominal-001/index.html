Deverbal Noun in -ing

deverbal-ing-nominal-001 · morphological · provisional

N[verb base + derivational -ing] with nominal dependents

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Names an activity, result, or product using nominal syntax.

  • The careful sorting of the files took hours.Constructed illustration.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Careful modifies the noun and of introduces the former object argument. A gerund-participial can instead have carefully and take the files as a direct object.
