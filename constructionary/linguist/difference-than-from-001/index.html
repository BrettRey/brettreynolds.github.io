Difference Complement after Different

difference-than-from-001 · mixed · provisional

AdjP[different + from/to/than-standard]

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Specifies the referent or situation from which something differs.

  • The result was different from what we expected.Constructed illustration.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The choice of marker depends on variety and complement form. Than can introduce a finite comparison in uses where from requires a nominal or other licensed complement.
