Dispositional Middle

dispositional-middle-001 · mixed · provisional

Clause[Theme Subject + active transitive-capable V + optional ease/manner modifier]

Meaning

Presents a referent as having a disposition affecting how an action can be carried out on it.

  • This fabric washes well.Constructed illustration.
  • The fabric was washed at noon.Constructed comparison. An eventive passive with a specific occasion, in contrast to the dispositional middle.

Compare

  • Deverbal Adjective in -able
  • Verbal Be-PassiveThis fabric washes well attributes a disposition with active verb forms. This fabric was washed at noon reports a particular event in the passive.
  • Causative and Inchoative AlternationThis fabric washes well invites an interpretation involving a washer. The door opened can simply report a change of state without invoking an implicit opener.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The subject corresponds to the ordinary object, yet the verb is active. The default generic assessment contrasts with an event report such as This fabric was washed at noon.

See the cited discussion and the constructed illustration.
