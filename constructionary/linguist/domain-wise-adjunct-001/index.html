Domain Adjunct with -Wise

domain-wise-adjunct-001 · mixed · provisional

initial domain expression in -wise + clause

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Restricts the dimension within which the following claim is assessed.

  • Cost-wise, the smaller van is better.Constructed illustration.

Related constructions

Interacts with

  • Gradation

Sources

  • Huddleston, Rodney & Geoffrey K. Pullum (2002), The Cambridge Grammar of the English Language. Cambridge: CUP, Ch. 8 §15; Ch. 19, adverbial word formation. discussed · bears on projection

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The same van can be worse in comfort without contradiction. The adjunct sets the assessment domain rather than describing how better is realized.

Evidence for the projection

See the cited discussion and the constructed illustration.
