Postverbal Durational long

durational-long-001 · mixed · provisional

VP[... + long in a non-affirmative duration frame]

Meaning

Describes a potentially long duration, typically under negation, a question, or a conditional.

Plain long in the postverbal position is natural in They didn’t stay long and Will they stay long? The ordinary affirmative They stayed long is markedly less natural for many speakers. Modified forms can behave differently, as in They stayed too long. The category analysis varies across sources. A separate use before the main verb is possible in affirmative They have long known.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Changing polarity or clause type while holding the duration predicate fixed can make plain postverbal long natural. Degree modification can change the distribution.

Evidence for the projection

FrameNet supplies documented questions, negation, conditional clauses and a separate preverbal use. CGEL independently marks the usual affirmative restriction for plain long.

Confidence: high.
