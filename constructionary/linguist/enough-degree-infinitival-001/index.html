Sufficient Degree enough with an Infinitival

enough-degree-infinitival-001 · mixed · provisional

AdjP/AdvP[gradable head + enough + to-infinitival]

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Presents a degree as meeting the threshold for the specified outcome.

  • The wall is high enough to hide the bins.Constructed illustration.

Related constructions

Interacts with

  • Gradation

Compare

  • Excess Degree too with an InfinitivalRipe enough to eat places the degree at or above a lower threshold. Too ripe to eat places it above an upper threshold.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Meeting the threshold does not assert that the bins are currently behind the wall. In the ordinary adjectival pattern enough follows the gradable adjective.

Evidence for the projection

See the cited discussion and the constructed illustration.
