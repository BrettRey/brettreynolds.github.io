Epistemic Modal over Perfect

epistemic-perfect-modal-001 · mixed · provisional

VP[epistemic modal + have + past participle]

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Assesses an earlier situation from a later point of view.

  • It must have rained last night.Constructed illustration.

Related constructions

A kind of

  • Central Modal with a Plain-Form Complement

Contains

  • Perfect HAVE + V-enPerfect have locates the assessed event before the reference time.

Compare

  • Speech-Act or Evidential Because

Sources

  • Huddleston, Rodney & Geoffrey K. Pullum (2002), The Cambridge Grammar of the English Language. Cambridge: CUP, Ch. 3 §§9.3.1, 9.9. discussed · bears on projection

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

On the inferential reading, must expresses the current conclusion and have places the rain earlier. A past requirement ordinarily calls for had to rather than this construction.
