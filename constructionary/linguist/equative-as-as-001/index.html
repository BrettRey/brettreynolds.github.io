Scalar Equative As … As

equative-as-as-001 · mixed · provisional

as + gradable expression + as-phrase/clause

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Compares a degree with a standard without asserting a greater degree in the positive form.

  • The new shelf is as wide as the old one.Constructed illustration.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The first as sets up a degree standard and the second introduces it. At least as wide explicitly allows a greater width and not as wide ordinarily implies a lower degree.
