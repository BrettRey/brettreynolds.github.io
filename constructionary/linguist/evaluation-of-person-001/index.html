Evaluative Adjective with Of and an Infinitival

evaluation-of-person-001 · mixed · provisional

Clause[dummy it + be + Adj + PP[of + NPᵢ] + to-infinitival with understood subjectᵢ]

Meaning

Evaluates a person’s conduct in doing or contemplating the action named by the infinitival.

In It was kind of you to call, of you supplies the participant whose action is evaluated. Compare It was difficult for you to call, where the assessment concerns the difficulty of your calling. With the relevant evaluative adjectives a context can supply the action instead, as in That was kind of you.

  • It is good of you to help.You is the understood helper as well as the person being evaluated.Huddleston, Rodney & Geoffrey K. Pullum (2002), The Cambridge Grammar of the English Language. Cambridge: CUP, Ch. 7 §6.2 [18]
  • It was careless of Pat to leave the gate open.Constructed example with an unfavourable evaluation.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The of-phrase can remain with a referential that when the action is supplied by the context. In the corresponding difficult for pattern, who undertakes the difficult act is part of the clause with for.

Evidence for the projection

CASA lists this adjective-of frame separately from the it-for-event frame. The contrast can be tested using the same action and participant.

Confidence: high.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
