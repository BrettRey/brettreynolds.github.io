Evaluative Binominal

evaluative-binominal-001 · mixed · provisional

NP[... Nom₁ of NP₂], with Nom₁ predicated of the referent of NP₂

Meaning

Evaluates the person or thing introduced by the second nominal.

A nitwit of a husband evaluates the husband as a nitwit. Material and measure expressions such as a statue of bronze and a cup of water supply different relations. The usual singular oblique takes a(n). Kim and Sells document bare plural obliques. The evaluation can apply to a complex first nominal, as when destroyer of education is predicated of the minister. That semantic grouping leaves the attachment of the outer PP open.

  • her stupid nitwit of a husbandThe husband is the target of the evaluation.Huddleston, Rodney & Geoffrey K. Pullum (2002), The Cambridge Grammar of the English Language. Cambridge: CUP, Ch. 5 §14.1, p. 442 [7viii]
  • wobbly jellies of menBNC example with a bare plural oblique.Kim, Jong-Bok & Peter Sells (2015), English Binominal NPs: A Construction-based Perspective. Journal of Linguistics 51(1): 41–73, p. 45 [12]

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The first nominal supplies a description of the referent contributed by the second. A bare plural second nominal can support that relation.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL's clausal paraphrase and the published singular and plural cases investigated by Kim and Sells.

Confidence: medium.
