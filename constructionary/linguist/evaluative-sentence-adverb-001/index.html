Clause-Orientated Evaluative Adjunct

evaluative-sentence-adverb-001 · mixed · provisional

evaluative AdvP + clause, often separated prosodically

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Expresses the speaker’s evaluation of the reported situation.

  • Fortunately, the backup worked.Constructed illustration.

What recognizing it lets you predict

Fortunately evaluates the situation as a whole. It does not describe a fortunate manner in which the backup operated.
