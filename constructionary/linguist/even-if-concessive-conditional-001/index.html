Concessive Conditional even if

even-if-concessive-conditional-001 · mixed · provisional

adjunct[even if + finite clause]

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Holds a consequence fixed under a condition presented as unfavourable or extreme.

  • Even if it rains, we’ll go.Constructed illustration.

Related constructions

A kind of

  • Open and Remote if-Conditionals

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The sentence can be uttered while rain is uncertain. The plan is presented as holding even in that case, unlike the ordinary expectation suggested by a bare conditional.
