Every Inch with a Nominal Predicative

every-inch-predicative-001 · mixed · provisional

PredComp:NP[every inch + a(n) + Nom]

Meaning

Emphasizes that someone exemplifies a role or type in the relevant respects.

Every inch marks completeness in the characterization. CGEL identifies this use as idiomatic and contrasts the predicative with an unacceptable use as a by-agent. The relation to every bit as good remains open.

  • She is every inch a philosopher.The relevant respects come from the description philosopher.Huddleston, Rodney & Geoffrey K. Pullum (2002), The Cambridge Grammar of the English Language. Cambridge: CUP, Ch. 5 §13(d), p. 438 [6i]

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The idiomatic frame favours predicative use and an interpretation over respects in which the description applies.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL's predicative versus by-agent comparison and its note on idiomatic every inch. A constructed test replaces inch with centimetre.
