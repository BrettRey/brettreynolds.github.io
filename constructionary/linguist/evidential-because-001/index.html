Speech-Act or Evidential Because

evidential-because-001 · mixed · provisional

clause + supplementary because-PP

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Gives grounds for making the preceding claim or directive.

  • Jo must be home, because her keys are on the table.Constructed illustration.

Related constructions

Compare

  • Content-Level Because Adjunct
  • Clause-Orientated Evaluative Adjunct
  • Epistemic Modal over Perfect

Sources

  • Huddleston, Rodney & Geoffrey K. Pullum (2002), The Cambridge Grammar of the English Language. Cambridge: CUP, Ch. 8 §12.1, act-related versus content-related cause; §18. discussed · bears on projection

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The keys motivate the speaker’s conclusion. Their being on the table need not be what caused Jo to be home.
