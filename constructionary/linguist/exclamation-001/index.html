Linguist

Exclamation

exclamation-001 · pragmatic · provisional

Utterance with exclamatory prosody and heightened-stance force

Meaning

An utterance-level force category, expressing heightened speaker stance with exclamatory prosody

Terminology: exclamative is the clause type (syntactic), exclamatory the property of uses, exclamation the utterance-level force category (pragmatic). This entry is a Constructionary pragmatic profile, not a claim that CGEL recognizes a unified grammatical class: CGEL p. 923 treats exclamation as structurally nebulous and samples several formally distinct realizations. Membership here is graded: interjections are the prototype; exclamative clauses are core; directive-ostensive NPs are prototypically members; vocatives enter only via the summons/call use; declaratives join by prosody alone (That's amazing!). The category cross-cuts ostensive-np-001 rather than containing it: tag vocatives are structural-yes/force-no, while exclamatory declaratives are force-yes/structural-no.

  • Ouch!Interjection; the prototype (pure force, no discourse referent)
  • What a lovely day!Non-headed construction with exclamative clausal semantics; cf. the headed Clause What a lovely day it is!
  • A snake!Directive-ostensive NP as exclamation
  • That's amazing!Declarative recruited by prosody alone

Constraints

  • pragmatic Not at-issue; the force resists direct denial (That's false cannot target the exclamatory stance).
  • structural Exclamatory prosody is non-reducible; deaccenting removes membership.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Locating an utterance in the exclamation profile predicts stand-alone viability, non-reducible exclamatory prosody, heightened-stance interpretation, and resistance to at-issue denial, across formally unrelated realizations

Evidence for the projection

The projections hold from interjections through exclamative clauses to recruited declaratives; the profile is realizationally promiscuous but functionally uniform

Confidence: medium.

Stable: the profile recurs. Provisional.

Core properties

  • Exclamatory prosody (fall or rise-fall contour; non-reducible under deaccenting)
  • Stand-alone utterance-hood (complete without clausal integration)
  • Heightened-stance force (resists at-issue denial; expressive rather than descriptive)

Boundary cases

  • CGEL does not posit a well-defined grammatical class of non-exclamative exclamations; this entry proposes a cross-form pragmatic profile
  • tag and address vocatives (structural profile without exclamatory force)
  • exclamatory declaratives (force without the extra-clausal structure)
  • murmured reminders (Your keys, said flatly)
