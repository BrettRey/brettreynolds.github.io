Alternative Exhaustive Conditional

exhaustive-alternative-conditional-001 · mixed · provisional

adjunct[whether + alternatives linked by or]

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

States that the outcome holds whichever of the presented alternatives obtains.

  • Whether Lee comes or not, the meeting goes ahead.Constructed illustration.

Related constructions

Compare

  • Open and Remote If-Conditionals
  • Subordinate Closed Interrogative with WhetherI asked whether Jo stayed or left seeks the answer. Whether Jo stayed or left, the lights stayed on puts both alternatives in the adjunct and asserts the same consequence across them.
  • Open Exhaustive Conditional

Sources

  • Huddleston, Rodney & Geoffrey K. Pullum (2002), The Cambridge Grammar of the English Language. Cambridge: CUP, Ch. 11 §5.3.6; Ch. 8 §14.6. discussed · bears on projection

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The adjunct does not ask or report an unresolved question. It asserts that the consequence holds on both alternatives.
