Extended Existential with a Predicate

extended-existential-001 · mixed · provisional

Clause[dummy there + be + pivot NP + integrated location or secondary predication]

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Introduces a referent and characterizes its position or current situation.

  • There were two alarms ringing.Constructed illustration.

Related constructions

  • Existential there with be

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Ringing predicates an activity of the alarms. A free event adjunct can instead modify the whole existential assertion without predicating a property of the pivot.
