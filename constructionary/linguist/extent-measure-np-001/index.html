Spatial Extent with a Measure NP

extent-measure-np-001 · mixed · provisional

VP[motion/spatial V + measure NP]

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Measures traversed distance or the spatial extent of a configuration.

  • We walked three kilometres before lunch.Constructed illustration.

Related constructions

Compare

  • Path Locative-Complement Construction
  • Measurement with a Scale Adjective
  • Duration with ForWalked three kilometres measures distance. Walked for an hour measures the duration and uses a for-PP.

Sources

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The NP answers how far and usually lacks an ordinary active-object/passive-subject alternation. A goal phrase can independently say where the walking led.
