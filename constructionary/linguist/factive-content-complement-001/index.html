Factive Predication with a Content Clause

factive-content-complement-001 · mixed · provisional

matrix factive predicate + subordinate declarative content

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Treats the content as already true or taken for granted in ordinary uses.

  • Jo didn’t regret that the shop closed.Constructed illustration.

Related constructions

Interacts with

  • Declarative Content Clause

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The closure normally remains taken for granted under matrix negation. Context and predicate choice can alter projection, so factivity must be checked per use.
