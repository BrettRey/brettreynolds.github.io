Finite do Support

finite-do-support-001 · syntactic · provisional

Clause[finite do + plain-form VP] in inversion or clause negation

Meaning

Supplies a finite auxiliary when the construction requires one and the ordinary positive clause has a lexical verb.

  • Did you check the hinge?Constructed illustration.
  • Can you check the hinge?Constructed comparison. An available finite modal supplies the operator.

Related constructions

Interacts with

  • Closed Interrogative ClauseDid Jo leave? inserts finite do when the clause needs inversion and has no other auxiliary. Has Jo left? inverts the auxiliary already present.
  • Finite Auxiliary with notJo didn’t leave uses do to host negation. Jo hasn’t left uses the existing auxiliary.
