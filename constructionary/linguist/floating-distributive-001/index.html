Linguist

Floating each

floating-distributive-001 · mixed · provisional

Clause[plural participant NP + separately positioned each]

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Distributes the predication over individual members associated with a nearby argument.

  • The three hikers each carried a radio.Constructed illustration.

Related constructions

Compare

  • Distributive Rate with each or apiece
  • each in a Partitive NP
  • Subject-determiner (Genitive NP)

Sources

  • Huddleston, Rodney & Geoffrey K. Pullum (2002), The Cambridge Grammar of the English Language. Cambridge: CUP, Ch. 5 §7.4; Ch. 8, distributive adjuncts. discussed · bears on projection

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Each can follow the subject across a finite auxiliary in The hikers had each carried a radio. The linked NP can have its own determiner and is not contained in each.
