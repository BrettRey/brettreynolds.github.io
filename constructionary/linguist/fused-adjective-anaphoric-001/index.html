Anaphoric Adjective in a Fused-Head NP

fused-adjective-anaphoric-001 · mixed · provisional

NP[Det + AdjP with a contextually supplied nominal description]

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Uses a modifier to distinguish a referent while leaving the descriptive noun recoverable.

  • I took the dry cloth and left the damp.Constructed illustration.
