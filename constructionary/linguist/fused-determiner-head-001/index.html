Fused Determiner-Head Construction

fused-determiner-head-001 · syntactic · provisional

NP[DP in fused-head function]

Meaning

A DP functions simultaneously as determiner and head of the NP, with no separate nominal head

CGEL Ch. 5 §9. Covers anaphoric uses, partitives, the compound determinatives in -body/-one/-thing/-where (traditional 'indefinite pronouns'), and demonstratives standing alone. These are not pronouns; the category stays determinative while the head function is fused. This entry is a determiner-head subtype of the broader fused-head construction. Contested: Reynolds, Determinatives as nouns in English (LingBuzz 009939), analyzes determinatives as a fourth noun subclass, with simple independent uses (Some left) as ordinary Head rather than fused Det-Head, narrowing fusion to a residue. If adopted, this entry shrinks accordingly.

  • She bought twenty books, but I bought twenty-two.Anaphoric fused head (numerative)
  • We liked none of them.Partitive; independent form of the determinative no
  • Everybody left early.Compound determinative, not an indefinite pronoun
  • Those are mine.Demonstrative determinative standing alone, not a demonstrative pronoun

Constraints

  • syntactic The fused element is a DP; NPs headed by pronouns are a different construction (np-pron-001).

Dimensions

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Several of these can serve as an argument without an overt nominal following several. Some of the fruit admits a non-count partitive domain. Several of the fruit does not admit that same reading. A determiner-like expression as an entire NP need not uniformly be anaphoric.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL tests partitive complements and non-anaphoric special readings. The head analysis is the one adopted in this record and alternatives are separately noted.

Confidence: medium.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
