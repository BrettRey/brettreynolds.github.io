Fused Relative NP

fused-relative-np-001 · mixed · provisional

NP[relative expressionᵢ + ... linked gapᵢ ...]

Meaning

Identifies a person, thing, or quantity through the relation expressed inside the NP.

In what she wrote, the initial expression supplies both the nominal reference and a dependent of wrote. The resulting NP can denote written material. The same word sequence can form an embedded interrogative about the identity of that material. In subject position a plural fused relative licenses plural agreement, whereas a clausal subject takes default singular agreement.

  • I really liked what she wrote.Fused relative contrasted with the interrogative after wonder.Huddleston, Rodney & Geoffrey K. Pullum (2002), The Cambridge Grammar of the English Language. Cambridge: CUP, Ch. 12 §6.2 [11i–ii]
  • What books she has are in the attic.The NP has plural agreement despite containing a finite clause.Huddleston, Rodney & Geoffrey K. Pullum (2002), The Cambridge Grammar of the English Language. Cambridge: CUP, Ch. 12 §6.1 [4b]

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Identical-looking subordinate strings can differ in agreement and complement distribution. A plural fused relative can control plural agreement where an open interrogative cannot.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL uses agreement, extraposition and selection as diagnostics independent of the referential contrast. The NP is also the variable expression in the CASA wh-cleft.

Confidence: high.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
