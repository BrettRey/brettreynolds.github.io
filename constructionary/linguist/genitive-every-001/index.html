Genitive with Every

genitive-every-001 · mixed · provisional

NP[Det:NP[gen] + every + Nom[sg]]

Meaning

Quantifies over instances delimited by their relation to the genitive.

Her every move ranges over her movements. CGEL contrasts each in the same position. Postma and Rooryck discuss body-part nouns alongside actions and contrast the form with every move of John’s. To test how the genitive sets the domain, keep bone fixed and compare bones of a person’s skeleton with bones that person owns as specimens.

  • They scrutinised her every move.The genitive delimits the movements under consideration.Huddleston, Rodney & Geoffrey K. Pullum (2002), The Cambridge Grammar of the English Language. Cambridge: CUP, Ch. 5 §7.4(g), p. 379 [25a]

Related constructions

Interacts with

  • Subject-determiner (Genitive NP)A genitive appears in determiner function before every.

Sources

  • Postma, Gertjan & Johan Rooryck (1996), Modality and Possession in NPs. Proceedings of NELS 26: 273–287, §§2–4. discussed · bears on projection Argues for a relational restriction and compares the oblique genitive.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The kind of relation to the genitive can affect whether the pattern is available, even when the head noun is held fixed.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL's every versus each contrast and the published discussion of actions and body parts. The owned-specimen comparison is a proposed test.

Confidence: medium.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
