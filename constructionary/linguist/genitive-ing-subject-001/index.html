Genitive Subject in a Gerund-Participial

genitive-ing-subject-001 · syntactic · provisional

gerund-participial[Subject:genitive NP + V-ing VP]

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Expresses an overt subject in the genitive inside a gerund-participial.

  • I disliked their arriving so late.Constructed illustration.

Related constructions

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The verb can retain adverbial modification and take a direct object. The genitive marks its subject rather than turning the following verb into a noun.
