Genitive 's

genitive-s-001 · provisional

Noun phrase + 's

Meaning

Meaning 1

Indicates possession/ownership

Marks the NP as possessor or controller

  • Mary's bookPersonal ownership
  • That car is John'sIndependent genitive
  • the bank's policiesInstitutional control

Meaning 2

Marks intrinsic relationship/part-whole

Indicates inherent connection rather than ownership

  • the tree's branchesPhysical part-whole
  • the novel's themeAbstract component
  • the body's cellsBiological part-whole

Meaning 3

Indicates creation/authorship

Marks the creator or source

  • Shakespeare's playsAuthored works
  • nature's beautyNatural source
  • The painting is Picasso'sCreated by

Meaning 4

Expresses time/duration relationship

Marks temporal measurement or occurrence

  • a day's workDuration measure
  • tomorrow's meetingScheduled time
  • last year's winnerTemporal association

Meaning 5

Indicates characteristic or property

Marks defining features or qualities

  • the metal's strengthPhysical property
  • the proposal's weaknessAbstract quality

Meaning 6

Marks subject-like relationship

Indicates the doer/experiencer of an action/state

  • the sun's riseSubject of intransitive
  • the child's laughterSource of action

Meaning 7

Expresses social/institutional relationship

Marks organizational or relational connections

  • the team's coachProfessional role
  • Mary's brotherFamily relationship
  • the university's presidentInstitutional position

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

With the person next door’s bicycle, the genitive marker is adjacent to door but the possessor is the whole person next door. The semantic relation also varies across uses such as Monday’s meeting and our uncle’s bicycle.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL uses phrasal placement to separate the marker’s host from the genitive’s semantic argument.

Confidence: medium.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
