Linguist

get Passive

get-event-passive-001 · mixed · provisional

Clause[affected Subject + get + verbal past participle]

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Presents the subject as undergoing a passive event, often with subject relevance or affectedness.

  • Jo got promoted last week.Constructed illustration.

Related constructions

Alternates with

  • Verbal be-PassiveJo got promoted and Jo was promoted can describe the same event. Get foregrounds the change or its relevance to Jo. Be is also available in stative uses such as is widely believed.

Compare

  • Subject-Oriented Resultative

Sources

  • Huddleston, Rodney & Geoffrey K. Pullum (2002), The Cambridge Grammar of the English Language. Cambridge: CUP, Ch. 16 §10.1.3, verbal get-passive versus adjectival get. discussed · bears on projection

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

An apparent positive outcome can occur, so adverse consequence is unnecessary. The construction can admit an agent by-phrase and contrasts with get plus an ordinary adjective.
