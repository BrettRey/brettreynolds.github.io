God Knows with an Indeterminate Expression

god-knows-indeterminate-001 · mixed · provisional

fixed authority expression + knows + indeterminate XP, including inside an argument or modifier

Meaning

Marks a value as unknown or left unspecified, often with an emphatic stance.

In He filed God knows how many pages, the expression supplies an indefinite quantity inside the object NP. In She put God knows what in the soup, it supplies the unknown item. The source lists variants with Heaven, the Lord and goodness. An independent sentence such as God knows how many pages he filed can also make a literal claim about knowledge.

  • They packed God knows what in these boxes.Constructed NP object use with an unknown item.
  • He filed goodness knows how many pages.Constructed example where the unknown value is the number inside the object NP.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

An expression with the shape of a finite clause can occur within the matrix object NP and mark an unknown content or quantity. A fully literal knowledge clause does not generally have that distribution.

Evidence for the projection

CASA lists the formula and the range of indeterminates. The constructed insertion contrasts test the embedded use. Its internal constituency is open.

Confidence: medium.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
