Linguist

Prospective Be Going To

going-to-future-001 · mixed · provisional

VP[be + going + to-infinitival]

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Presents an event as prospective in light of an intention or present indications.

  • It’s going to freeze tonight.Constructed illustration.

Related constructions

A kind of

  • Verb Phrase

Compare

  • Present Futurate
  • Progressive -ingCompare I’m going to leave and I’m going to the station. The prospective construction takes an infinitival and allows subjects with no motion interpretation.
  • Quasi-Modal Be To

Sources

  • Huddleston, Rodney & Geoffrey K. Pullum (2002), The Cambridge Grammar of the English Language. Cambridge: CUP, Ch. 3 §10.2. discussed · bears on projection

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The weather subject requires no mover and no physical destination. The prospective use can therefore be distinguished from motion in going to the station.
