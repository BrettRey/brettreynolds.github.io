Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Positive tall relies on a contextually set standard. Six feet tall can place someone on the scale without settling whether they count as tall for a basketball player.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL distinguishes scalar comparison and standards from positive-grade property ascription.

Confidence: medium.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
