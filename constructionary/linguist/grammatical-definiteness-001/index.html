Grammatical Definiteness

grammatical-definiteness-001 · semantic · provisional

Semantic feature implemented through nominal determination

Meaning

Meaning 1

Identifiability status

Whether entity can be uniquely identified in context

  • the/a catIdentifiable vs non-identifiable
  • John/some personInherent vs non-identifiable

Meaning 2

Reference scope

Whether reference exhausts contextual set

  • the students/some studentsAn established group versus a subset, on the ordinary referential readings
  • the/a winnerUnique vs arbitrary

Meaning 3

Discourse status

Whether entity is established in discourse

  • the/a bookGiven vs new
  • it/somethingKnown vs novel

Dimensions

Form
Typically realized by deital determiners (the, this, that, dependent genitive pronouns, genitive NPs) or bare proper nouns

Establishes referent accessibility; anchors information structure

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Shared knowledge can make the only exit identifiable at first mention. Conversely, the in take the train can mark the lexicalized activity without distinguishing a specific train. Familiarity, unique reference, and structural diagnostics need separate predictions.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL and Reynolds document structural and interpretive departures from the simple definite/familiar mapping.

Confidence: medium.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
