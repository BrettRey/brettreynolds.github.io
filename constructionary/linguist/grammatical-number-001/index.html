Meaning 3

Encodes the tight/loose hierarchy of count diagnostics

Tight diagnostics (a/an, low cardinals, distributives) require higher individuation precision than loose diagnostics (many, high round numerals, plural agreement).

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The standard count singular three-hour span contrasts with three hours is enough, where a morphologically plural measure can take singular agreement. This rules out deriving all agreement directly from the number of units in the situation.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL treats amount agreement as an override alongside the ordinary singular and plural agreement construction.

Confidence: medium.

Stable: the profile recurs. Provisional.

Core properties

  • Number inflection and agreement (singular vs plural morphology; agreement on demonstrative determinatives/verbs)
  • Cardinality construal (compatibility with cardinals; singular/plural reference shift)

Peripheral properties

  • Collective/aggregate construals (plural agreement with singular-looking nouns; quasi-count behavior)
  • Ordered dissociation of count diagnostics (countability-hierarchy)
