Phrasal Genitive at the NP Edge

group-genitive-001 · syntactic · provisional

NP[Determiner:genitive extended NP whose rightmost host is not its nominal head + Nom]

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Places genitive marking at the right edge of a possessor expression.

  • It was the person next door’s ladder.Constructed illustration.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Door is the rightmost host of the marker, though the ladder is associated with the person. The possessor reading is determined by the whole genitive NP.
