Habitual Used To

habitual-used-to-001 · mixed · provisional

VP[used to + plain form]

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Presents a habitual situation or state as characteristic of a past period.

  • The shop used to close at noon.Constructed illustration.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Used to can describe a past state, as in Jo used to own it. The present counterpart use to does not express a parallel current habit in ordinary affirmative clauses.
