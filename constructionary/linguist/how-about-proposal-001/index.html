Linguist

Proposal with how about

how-about-proposal-001 · pragmatic · provisional

how about + NP, gerund-participial or licensed clause

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Offers an item or course of action for consideration.

  • How about a short break?Constructed illustration.

Related constructions

Compare

  • Question
  • Topic Proposal with what aboutHow about a short break? and What about a short break? can both propose a break. What about the budget? can also take up an outstanding topic.
  • Deliberative why not with a Bare InfinitivalHow about taking a break? proposes an option with a gerund-participial. Why not take a break? puts forward a proposal using a plain verbal predicate.

Sources

  • Huddleston, Rodney & Geoffrey K. Pullum (2002), The Cambridge Grammar of the English Language. Cambridge: CUP, Ch. 10 §7.7, how about. discussed · bears on projection

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The response can accept or reject the proposal. The phrase need not ask how a break would be carried out.
