Imperative Plus And with Conditional Force

imperative-and-conditional-001 · mixed · provisional

Clause[imperative] + and + declarative consequence

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Interprets an imperative-shaped first coordinate as a sufficient condition in context.

  • Touch that wire and the alarm will ring.Constructed illustration.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The first coordinate can warn against touching, with no instruction to do it. The consequent interpretation depends on performing the mentioned act.
