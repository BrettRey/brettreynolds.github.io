Imperative Negation with do-support

imperative-negation-do-001 · syntactic · provisional

Do not/Don't + VP (imperative)

Meaning

Issues a negative directive (prohibitive imperative)

Uses do-support for negation in imperatives, even when an auxiliary could negate in declaratives.

  • Don't look at me.Prohibitive directive
  • Don't be afraid.do-support despite auxiliary be
  • Don't you look at me!Emphatic negative imperative with subject
  • Don't everyone shout it out.Negative imperative with quantified subject

Constraints

  • syntactic Imperative clause with base-form VP.
  • structural Negation is expressed with do-support (do not/don't) in imperatives.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

A prohibitive imperative can use don’t with be, as in Don’t be late, while an ordinary finite declarative uses She isn’t late. The negative inclusive Let’s not leave instantiates a different imperative subtype.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL documents the distinctive imperative do distribution and the order alternatives in negatives with let’s.

Confidence: medium.
