Indefinite Object Omission

implicit-object-use-001 · mixed · provisional

VP[transitive-capable V used without an overt object]

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Leaves a usual participant nonspecific while asserting the activity or event.

  • Have you eaten yet?Constructed illustration.

Related constructions

Compare

  • Simple Intransitive VP
  • Monotransitive VPCompare Jo ate lunch with Jo ate. The second leaves the food unspecified. Availability depends on the verb and its use.

Sources

  • Huddleston, Rodney & Geoffrey K. Pullum (2002), The Cambridge Grammar of the English Language. Cambridge: CUP, Ch. 4 §8.1.3. discussed · bears on projection

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The implied food need not have been mentioned. Leaving the relevant object unexpressed is natural for eat, even when plausible near-synonyms such as consume strongly favour a stated object.
