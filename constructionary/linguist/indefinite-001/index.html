Indefinite Construction

indefinite-001 · semantic · provisional

Indefinite determination or contextually indefinite bare plural/non-count NP

Meaning

Meaning 1

Refers without presenting the referent as already uniquely identifiable

The speaker may know the identity. The construction does not require the hearer to resolve it from prior information.

  • a dogNovel referent
  • some studentsNon-specific set

Meaning 2

New discourse entity

Entity being introduced to discourse

  • there is a solutionFirst mention
  • some people say...Discourse-new source

Meaning 3

Arbitrary instance

Under certain directives or permissions an indefinite can leave the choice of individual open. Choice and permitted quantity are separate.

  • take a cardAny card
  • some helpAny assistance

Meaning 4

Type membership

Instance of category

  • is a teacherCategory instance
  • is some kind of expertCategory type

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

A student phoned can refer to a particular student known to the speaker. A student can enter allows a general reading. The article alone does not rule out specific reference or supply an any-member permission.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL distinguishes referential from non-referential uses and documents both specific and nonspecific interpretations. A determinerless role predication does not itself introduce a novel reference.

Confidence: medium.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
