Indefinite NP with a Modified Personal Name

indefinite-modified-name-001 · mixed · provisional

NP[a/an + attributive AdjP + personal name form]

Meaning

Presents a named person in a condition associated with a particular occasion.

In a visibly shaken Giuliani, the name identifies a person already introduced in the report. The adjective supplies his state within the NP, and the clause predicates what he did. One analysis takes the modifier to characterize the bearer directly. Another takes the NP to pick out a manifestation, understood as the bearer in one of several possible states.

Related constructions

A kind of

  • Determined Noun PhraseThe overt article takes determiner function in an NP with an adjective and a personal name form.

Overlaps with

  • Proper Name in a Descriptive NPThe analyses overlap if a visibly shaken Giuliani selects one manifestation. In the Paris of my youth, a contextually delimited period is explicitly identified, whereas the CBS token provides an occasion-specific description in a report about a single event.

Compare

  • Strong Proper Name ConstructionPoor Henry and a visibly shaken Giuliani both characterize an identifiable bearer. The former illustrates the restricted class of adjectives in bare embellishments.
  • Depictive Secondary PredicationBoth patterns can characterize a participant during an event. Compare They carried an unconscious Kim with They carried Kim unconscious. In the former, the adjective is internal to the object NP. In the latter, it is a secondary predicative outside that NP.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

A temporary-state adjective can make a/an available with an already identified name bearer, in subject position and within a PP. The determiner choice varies with the adjective and the construal of its contribution.

Evidence for the projection

CBS supplies the previously identified bearer. Berezowski’s selected COCA comparisons give more a/an tokens than the or bare tokens for angry, smiling, beaming, relaxed and tired. His young and old comparisons are predominantly bare.

Confidence: medium.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.

Boundary cases

  • Poor Henry supplies an attribution in a bare NP. CGEL also gives the distraught Empress Alexandra with the and a temporary state.
  • Another Roosevelt describes a person by resemblance. A visibly shaken Giuliani keeps Giuliani as the bearer whose state is described.
