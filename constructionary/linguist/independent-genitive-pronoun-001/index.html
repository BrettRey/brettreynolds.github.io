Independent Genitive Pronoun

independent-genitive-pronoun-001 · provisional

mine/yours/his/hers/its/ours/theirs/whose

Meaning

Meaning 1

Indicates possession/ownership

Marks the pronoun as possessor or controller

  • That car is minePersonal ownership
  • These policies are theirsInstitutional control
  • Whose is this?Questioning possession

Meaning 2

Marks intrinsic relationship/part-whole

Indicates inherent connection rather than ownership

  • These branches must be yoursParts of your tree
  • The theme is theirsComponent of their work

Meaning 3

Indicates creation/authorship

Marks the creator or source

  • Those paintings are hersShe created them
  • This theory is oursWe developed it

Meaning 4

Expresses time/duration relationship

Marks temporal measurement or occurrence

  • That meeting is yoursIn your time slot
  • These tasks are theirsAssigned to their period

Meaning 5

Indicates characteristic or property

Marks defining features or qualities

  • That strength is itsProperty of it (independent its is rare but attested)
  • Those weaknesses are theirsTheir characteristics

Meaning 6

Marks subject-like relationship

Indicates the doer/experiencer of an action/state

  • That laughter is hersShe is the one laughing
  • The decision was yoursYou decided

Meaning 7

Expresses social/institutional relationship

Marks organizational or relational connections

  • That coach is oursCoaches our team
  • These employees are yoursWork for you

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Mine contrasts with my in This is mine versus my copy. With whose and his, identical surface forms can appear in independent or dependent slots. Independent its is rare rather than ruled out.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL separates dependent and independent genitive realizations. Its warning about the exceptional distribution of its limits any paradigm-wide prediction.

Confidence: medium.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
