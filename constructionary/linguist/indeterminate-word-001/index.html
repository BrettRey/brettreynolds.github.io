Indeterminate Word

indeterminate-word-001 · semantic · provisional

who/whom/whose/what/which/where/when/why/how (+ the -ever series)

Meaning

Meaning 1

Contributes an open variable restricted to a sort, available to different clause-type packagings

Term: indeterminate (cf. Kuroda 1965 for the Japanese analogue). Not a CGEL lexical category: the set cross-cuts lexical categories, and category may differ by packaging and function—interrogative what is a determinative in determiner use (what size) but a pronoun when it heads an NP (What did you see?), whereas exclamative what is an adjective; how is adverbial. Three projections support the overarching set: (i) sortal-variable semantics across packagings; (ii) upward percolation of the interrogative, relative, or exclamative property, plus non-subject fronting where required; (iii) the -ever series (whoever, whatever, whichever, wherever, whenever, however) derives from this base set. Packaging-specific category facts live in the specialization entries and lexicon layer.

  • what she sawOpen variable over things (fused relative packaging)
  • who cameOpen variable over persons (interrogative packaging)

Meaning 2

Marks scalar degree, a point or extent on an implicit or explicit scale

Degree is one of the sorts the series ranges over

  • how tallDegree of height
  • what speedExtent of velocity

Dimensions

Form
Closed series sharing historical wh- orthography (how included); internal paradigm structure (whose, whom as case forms of who); productive -ever derivation
Meaning
Contributes an open variable restricted to a sort (person, thing, selection, possessor, place, time, reason, manner/degree)
Function
Feeds interrogative, relative, and exclamative packagings; the marked property percolates upward, with non-subject fronting where the packaging requires it
Distribution
Availability and lexical category vary by member and packaging; non-subject phrases are fronted in the relevant constructions

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The same sortal form can occur in what Jo wrote after admire or wonder. With admire, the expression can denote an entity or text. With wonder, it presents an open question. The lexical shape does not determine the packaging.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL compares matching clause-like strings across fused relatives and embedded interrogatives and gives independent agreement and distributional tests.

Confidence: medium.
