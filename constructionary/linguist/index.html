English Constructionary: linguist edition

Form, meaning, constraints, relations and evidence for each construction.

Entries follow the categories and functions of The Cambridge Grammar of the English Language (Huddleston and Pullum 2002) and a projectibility-first view of kinds: each entry says what recognizing the construction lets you predict, and how far the evidence secures that. Analyses are provisional unless marked otherwise. See also the closed-class word lists.
