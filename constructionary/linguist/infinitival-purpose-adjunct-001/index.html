Infinitival Purpose Adjunct

infinitival-purpose-adjunct-001 · mixed · provisional

Clause + to-infinitival adjunct

Meaning

Presents a desired outcome as the purpose of the main situation.

  • Jo borrowed the van to move the chairs.Constructed illustration.
  • Jo borrowed the van to move the chairs, but the plan fell through.Constructed comparison. The purpose can remain unrealized.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The chair-moving need never occur. The clause can be expanded with in order, unlike many result infinitivals that report a realized later event.

Evidence for the projection

See the cited discussion and the constructed illustration.

Confidence: medium.
