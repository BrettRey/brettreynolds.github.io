Infinitival Relative Modifier

infinitival-relative-modifier-001 · mixed · provisional

Nom + to-infinitival with an antecedent-linked gap

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Characterizes a referent by a possible or appropriate action involving it.

  • We need a bench to sit on.Constructed illustration.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The bench supplies the complement of on. The relative often has a modal reading and can differ from a purpose adjunct describing why a matrix action occurred.
