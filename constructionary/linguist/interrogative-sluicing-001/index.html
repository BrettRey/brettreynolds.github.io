Interrogative Sluicing

interrogative-sluicing-001 · syntactic · provisional

interrogative XP with the remaining clause unexpressed

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Recovers a question with only the questioned part overt.

  • Someone moved the keys, but I don’t know who.Constructed illustration.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The mover role can be recovered from the preceding clause. A more explicit interrogative XP such as which person can likewise remain overt.
