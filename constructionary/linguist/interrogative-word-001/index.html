Interrogative Use of Indeterminates

interrogative-word-001 · semantic · provisional

who/whom/whose/what/which/where/when/why/how (interrogative packaging)

Meaning

Asks for the value of the indeterminate's open variable, constraining its sort

All members of the indeterminate series participate in this packaging

  • whose bookSeeks possessor identification
  • who cameInterrogative subject in basic subject position; no subject–auxiliary inversion
  • I wonder where she went.Subordinate interrogative

Constraints

  • syntactic The interrogative property percolates from the indeterminate to its phrase and clause. Non-subject phrases are normally fronted and trigger subject–auxiliary inversion in main clauses; subjects are not fronted, subordinate clauses normally do not invert, and echoes may leave the phrase in situ.

Dimensions

Form
Full indeterminate series; non-subject interrogative phrases are normally fronted, while interrogative subjects remain in basic position and echoes may remain in situ
Meaning
The open variable is packaged as a request for its value
Function
Marks the focus of the question; question-answer congruence
Distribution
Main and subordinate open interrogatives; main-clause inversion follows a fronted non-subject, while subordinate interrogatives and interrogative subjects normally retain subject–verb order

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

In Whose coat did Jo borrow?, the whole phrase whose coat fronts while whose supplies the open variable for the possessor. With an interrogative subject, Who left? has no corresponding auxiliary inversion.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL separates the interrogative feature of a word from that of its containing phrase and tests subject versus non-subject distributions.

Confidence: medium.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
