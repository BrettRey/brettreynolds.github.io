Irrealis Were

irrealis-were-001 · syntactic · provisional

Clause[1sg/3sg Subject + were] in selected remote environments

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Realizes a distinct irrealis form of be in the first or third person singular.

  • I wish I were taller.Constructed illustration.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Remote if may also have was for many speakers. The matrix-like were in Were I taller is licensed under inversion, where replacing it by was fails in standard usage.
