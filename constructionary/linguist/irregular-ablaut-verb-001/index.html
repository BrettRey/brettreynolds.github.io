Vowel Alternation in Irregular Verb Inflection

irregular-ablaut-verb-001 · morphological · provisional

selected verb stems with present, preterite and participial vowel alternants

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Distinguishes verbal inflectional forms through lexicalized changes in the stem vowel.

  • Jo sang yesterday and has sung twice today.Constructed illustration.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Sang and sung correspond to different slots for this verb. Other verbs can share one form across those slots, so the exact mapping is lexical.
