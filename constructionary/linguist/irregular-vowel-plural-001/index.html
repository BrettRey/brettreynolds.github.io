Stem-Vowel Alternation in Noun Plurals

irregular-vowel-plural-001 · morphological · provisional

selected noun stem alternation, e.g. tooth ↔ teeth

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Realizes a lexical noun plural by internal vowel change.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Plural agreement is expressed without ordinary -s. The pattern is confined to established pairs and does not productively pluralize arbitrarily similar nouns.
