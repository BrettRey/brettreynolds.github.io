Clitic it’s in Auxiliary Uses

it-auxiliary-clitic-001 · morphological · provisional

it + contracted is or has

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Realizes a reduced auxiliary on the pronoun it.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The host and following participial environment distinguish has in It’s been repaired from is in It’s being repaired. Genitive its instead combines with a nominal in its ordinary dependent use.
