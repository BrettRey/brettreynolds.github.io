Linguist

It-Cleft

it-cleft-001 · mixed · provisional

Clause[Subject:it + be + PredComp:XP + cleft relative Clause]

Meaning

Specifies the value of a variable whose descriptive content is supplied by the cleft relative.

In it was Kim who was sacked, Kim supplies the value and the relative sets out the background proposition that somebody was sacked. This packaging can present new information as background. The position after be marks grammatical foregrounding. Main stress can fall inside the relative instead. CGEL distinguishes this structure from a referential it with an integrated relative inside an NP, using contexts where the surface wording is the same.

  • It was peace and quiet that he wanted most.The value is peace and quiet.Huddleston, Rodney & Geoffrey K. Pullum (2002), The Cambridge Grammar of the English Language. Cambridge: CUP, Ch. 4 §5.5.1, p. 267 [47i]
  • It was to Boston that I flew.A PP can occupy the foregrounded slot.Huddleston, Rodney & Geoffrey K. Pullum (2002), The Cambridge Grammar of the English Language. Cambridge: CUP, Ch. 4 §6.1.1, p. 275 [5iii]
  • It wasn’t Kim who took the keys.Constructed. The denial normally targets Kim’s identification, with somebody’s taking the keys treated as background.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The relative supplies a background proposition and the post-be phrase provides a value. In an ordinary identifying context, negating the identification can leave that background proposition in place.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL’s integrated-relative contrast and two kinds of presupposition. CASA documents different foregrounded phrase types.

Confidence: high.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
