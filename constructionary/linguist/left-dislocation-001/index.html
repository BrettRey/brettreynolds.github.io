Left Dislocation with a Pronoun

left-dislocation-001 · mixed · provisional

NPᵢ + Clause[... overt personal pronounᵢ ...]

Meaning

Presents a referent before making a predication about it within the clause.

A pronoun in the clause takes the referent of the initial NP. The pronoun may be a subject, an object, or a genitive determiner. In complement preposing the relevant position has a gap. The left-dislocated NP can introduce a new referent.

  • Her parents, I don’t like them at all.The object them is overt.Huddleston, Rodney & Geoffrey K. Pullum (2002), The Cambridge Grammar of the English Language. Cambridge: CUP, Ch. 16 §8.1 [4i]
  • Some guys, they just show up without even calling first.The initial NP can introduce a class of people to the discourse.Huddleston, Rodney & Geoffrey K. Pullum (2002), The Cambridge Grammar of the English Language. Cambridge: CUP, Ch. 16 §8.1 [5]

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

An overt pronoun can appear inside an NP as a genitive determiner while the initial referent is outside the clause nucleus. The referent can be new to the discourse.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL compares preposing with matched left dislocation and documents genitive and discourse-new cases. CASA records the linked pronoun.

Confidence: high.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
