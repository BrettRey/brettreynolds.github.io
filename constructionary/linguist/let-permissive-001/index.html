Let Permissive with a Bare Infinitival

let-permissive-001 · mixed · provisional

VP[let + NP + bare infinitival]

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Presents an action as permitted or allowed to proceed.

  • They let Jo use the key.Constructed illustration.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Let licenses a plain form after Jo. An ordinary verbal passive with let and to is unavailable in this use, unlike Jo was allowed to use the key.
