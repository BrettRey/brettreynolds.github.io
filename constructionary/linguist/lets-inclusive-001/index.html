Inclusive Let’s Imperative

lets-inclusive-001 · mixed · provisional

let’s + plain-form VP

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Proposes a course of action involving speaker and addressee or the relevant joint group.

  • Let’s take a break.Constructed illustration.

Related constructions

A kind of

  • Imperative Clause

Compare

  • Imperative Negation with do-support

Sources

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The preferred negative counterpart can be Let’s not take a break. The ’s form identifies the inclusive pattern and is unavailable in a literal command to release someone.
