Letter-Case Modifier Construction

letter-case-modifier-001 · semantic · provisional

small/big/little + Letter-name (often hyphenated) + N[label]

Meaning

Implements Letter-Case Specification Construction, meaning 1: Encodes letter-case as a premodifier (small-c, big-D), specifying the intended interpretive status.

  • He's a small-c catholic.Ordinary descriptive sense; not the institution.
  • She is a Big-D Democrat.Institutional or party-affiliated sense.
  • That was little-p politics.Ordinary sense of the label.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The compact modifier big-D in a big-D Democrat can disambiguate the label before its head. The full PP with a capital D can follow the label and express an equivalent literal contrast.

Evidence for the projection

The lexical meanings of capital and small supply the comparison. The position and interpretation contrast uses constructed forms rather than claims about regional frequencies.

Confidence: medium.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
