Light Verb with an Event Nominal

light-verb-event-001 · mixed · provisional

VP[light V + Od:NP[event]]

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Packages an event in a nominal while the verbal head carries tense and complementation.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The nominal can take attributive modification or be counted, as in two quick looks. Its lexical collocation with the light verb constrains substitution.
