Measure Genitive Premodifier

measure-genitive-001 · mixed · provisional

Nom[measure NP.GEN + nominal]

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Specifies a duration or monetary measure within a nominal.

  • We had another two hours’ wait.Constructed illustration.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Two hours’ gives the duration of the wait and can be embedded within the scope of a preceding determiner. A personal genitive has an independently referential or participant interpretation.
