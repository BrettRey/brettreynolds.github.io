Measure Pseudopartitive with Of

measure-pseudopartitive-001 · mixed · provisional

NP[quantified measure expression + of + substance or plural nominal expression]

Meaning

Uses a unit or container expression to specify quantity.

  • They bought three litres of milk.Constructed illustration.
  • They bought three litres of the milk from yesterday.Constructed comparison. The construction can instead pick a quantity out of an established stock.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Milk supplies the substance measured without referring to a definite stock of which the purchase was a subset. Compare three litres of the milk, which can select from such a stock.
