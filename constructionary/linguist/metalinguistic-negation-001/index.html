Metalinguistic Negation and Correction

metalinguistic-negation-001 · mixed · provisional

negated expression followed or contextualized by correction of wording or scale

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Rejects the wording, implication, or strength of a description as inadequate.

  • It isn’t warm. It’s scorching.Constructed illustration.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

An ordinary scalar reading of scorching would imply warmth. The emphatic correction licenses negation of the weaker label while preserving the stronger characterization.
