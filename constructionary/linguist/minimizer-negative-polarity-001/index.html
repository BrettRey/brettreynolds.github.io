Minimizer under Non-Affirmative Polarity

minimizer-negative-polarity-001 · mixed · provisional

minimal quantity expression in a negative or otherwise licensed context

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Uses a minimal unit to emphasize absence of any relevant amount.

  • They didn’t budge an inch.Constructed illustration.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Under negation even the minimal unit is denied. An inch can otherwise denote a literal distance without that emphatic interpretation.

Evidence for the projection

See the cited discussion and the constructed illustration.
