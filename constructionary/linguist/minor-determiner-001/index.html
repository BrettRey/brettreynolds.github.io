Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

What size hat asks for the hat’s size, whereas over thirty hats sets a lower boundary on their number. Those slots license different phrases even though neither is an ordinary bare determinative lexeme.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL gives restricted phrase realizations of determiner function, including paired word orders for property-value NPs.

Confidence: medium.
