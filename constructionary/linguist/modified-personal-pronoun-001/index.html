Adjectivally Modified Personal Pronoun NP

modified-personal-pronoun-001 · mixed · provisional

NP[(article) + attributive AdjP + personal-pronoun form]

Meaning

Meaning 1

Picks out a stage or aspect of the person or group identified by a pronoun.

In the five-year-old you, the pronoun anchors the reference to the addressee and the adjective specifies an age. In subject position the whole NP can take third-person verbal agreement. A coreferential reflexive can still carry the person of the pronoun. Some age-modified uses are bare. The category of the head and the explanation for the agreement split remain open.

  • The five-year-old you was so cute!Was contrasts with ordinary second-person you were.Halm, Tamás (2022), Modified pronouns and split agreement: the syntax of virtual performatives. NELS 52, vol. 1, 319–329. Amherst, MA: GLSA, §8 [24a]
  • Questions A Younger Me Would Ask MyselfIn the reported title, the object reflexive retains first person.Halm, Tamás (2022), Modified pronouns and split agreement: the syntax of virtual performatives. NELS 52, vol. 1, 319–329. Amherst, MA: GLSA, §4 [6b]

Meaning 2

Characterizes the person identified by the pronoun as being in a particular state.

In an exhausted me, the speaker is still identified by me. The adjective adds the state in which the speaker experienced the situation. Halm compares this use with a visibly irritated Mr Andrews and distinguishes it from a reading that explicitly selects a stage or aspect.

  • An exhausted me has felt …Excerpt from Halm’s example of the state-characterizing interpretation.Halm, Tamás (2022), Modified pronouns and split agreement: the syntax of virtual performatives. NELS 52, vol. 1, 319–329. Amherst, MA: GLSA, §10 [31a]

Related constructions

A kind of

  • Noun PhraseThe whole expression can occupy a subject or object slot. An attributive adjective combines with a form otherwise used as a personal pronoun.

Overlaps with

  • Determined Noun PhraseThe younger me has overt determination. Halm also reports bare age-modified forms, including 12 year old me as the subject of a relative clause.

Compare

  • Indefinite NP with a Modified Personal NameAn exhausted me and a visibly shaken Giuliani combine a/an with an adjective that characterizes a participant identified independently by the pronoun or name.
  • Proper Name in a Descriptive NPThe young Isaac Newton and the five-year-old you can each pick out a life stage, anchored by a name in the first case and by the addressee pronoun in the second.
  • Personal Determination with We or YouIn we linguists, we occupies the determiner slot and precedes a lexical head. In the younger us, the article occupies that slot and us follows the modifier.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The modified NP can take a singular verb form unavailable to ordinary you, while an antecedent containing me can support myself. A stage contrast can also support third-person anaphora.

Evidence for the projection

Halm’s contrasts separate finite agreement from the selection of coreferential forms. The article alone leaves the head’s lexical category open.

Confidence: medium.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.

Boundary cases

  • Poor old me is CGEL’s bare evaluative pattern. The younger me can pick out a life stage and occurs in a pattern that also permits a younger me.
  • A he or a she can denote a person of a category and lacks the ordinary speaker or addressee anchoring tested here.
