Monotransitive VP

monotransitive-vp-001 · syntactic · provisional

VP[Head:V + Od:NP]

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Realizes one participant as a direct object of the verb.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The object can bear accusative pronoun case and, with a suitable verb, correspond to the subject of a passive. A PP complement has different internal syntax.
