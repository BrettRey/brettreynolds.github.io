Common Noun

n-common-001 · syntactic · provisional

N[Type:Common]

Provides a nominal description usable with ordinary NP determination when the count and number profile permits it

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

A use like the tallest building can identify a unique referent while keeping a common lexical head. Identification alone therefore does not predict the distribution of a proper noun.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL separates proper-noun specialization from syntactic determination and reference.

Confidence: medium.
