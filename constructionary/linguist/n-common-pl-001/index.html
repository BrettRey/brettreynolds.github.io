Plural Common Noun

n-common-pl-001 · syntactic · provisional

N[Type:Common, Number:PL]

Meaning

Plural-marked common noun; plural agreement and determinative selection. Typically denotes more than one instance, though lexicalized and non-canonical plurals diverge

  • citiesPlural common noun

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Cats licenses plural verbal agreement and cardinals greater than one. A plural-only form such as scissors also takes plural agreement, while counting individual instruments ordinarily uses pairs of scissors.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL contrasts ordinary plurals and plural-only nouns, including restrictions on count determination.

Confidence: medium.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
