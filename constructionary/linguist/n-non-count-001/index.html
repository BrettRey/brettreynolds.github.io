Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

In much coffee, coffee has non-count distribution. Two coffees permits a count reading because the context supplies portions or varieties. The individual lexical form alone does not decide the syntactic use.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL compares count and non-count uses of the same substance nouns, including drink names.

Confidence: medium.

Stable: the profile recurs. Provisional.

Core properties

  • Non-count morphosyntax (rejects cardinals; takes much/less; singular agreement default)
  • Non-individuated construal (stuff/aggregate interpretation; homogeneous part structure)

Peripheral properties

  • Aggregate-denoting and flexible nouns (portion/container shifts; count conversion in specific frames)
