Plural Noun

n-pl-001 · syntactic · provisional

N[Number:PL]

Meaning

Plural number form; selects plural agreement and combines with cardinals above one. Typically construes referents as more than one unit, but the category is morphosyntactic, not semantic (cf. pluralia tantum)

  • catsMultiple countable entities
  • booksPlural count noun

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Plural noun form does not require the spelling -s or direct counting. Sheep can show plural agreement with no suffix, and scissors typically needs a unit expression to count individual instruments.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL’s irregular and plural-only forms supply independent contrasts for number marking and countability.

Confidence: medium.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
