Inversion after Preposed Negative or Restrictive Expression

negative-initial-inversion-001 · mixed · provisional

Clause[preposed negative/restrictive dependent + finite Aux + Subject + VP]

Meaning

Uses auxiliary-first order within the nucleus after certain negative or restrictive expressions.

  • Only after the meeting did I find out.Constructed illustration.
  • After the meeting I found out.Constructed comparison. The non-restrictive fronted adjunct does not trigger inversion.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Only after the meeting triggers inversion in this use. After the meeting I found out differs in auxiliary placement, and only modifying the subject does not trigger it.
