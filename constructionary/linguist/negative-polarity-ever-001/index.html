Polarity-Sensitive Temporal ever

negative-polarity-ever-001 · mixed · provisional

temporal ever in a licensing environment

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Ranges over occasions or times, especially under negation or questioning.

  • Has Lee ever sailed solo?Constructed illustration.

Related constructions

Interacts with

  • Experiential Perfect

Compare

  • Absolute Negator Determinatives
  • Minimizer under Non-Affirmative Polarity
  • Postverbal Durational long
  • Adverbial Negation with neverHave you ever seen it? licenses ever in a question and supplies no negative assertion. Jo never saw it has negation within the temporal adverb.

Sources

  • Huddleston, Rodney & Geoffrey K. Pullum (2002), The Cambridge Grammar of the English Language. Cambridge: CUP, Ch. 9 §§4.1–4.4. discussed · bears on projection

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The affirmative counterpart Lee has ever sailed solo is marked without a special licensor. Superlatives and other suitable environments also permit ever.
