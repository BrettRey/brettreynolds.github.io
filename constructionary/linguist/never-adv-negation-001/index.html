Adverbial Negation with never

never-adv-negation-001 · syntactic · provisional

never + VP

Meaning

Negates occurrence over time (no instances at any time)

  • She never called.No calling events occurred
  • I have never been there.No prior occurrences in experience

Constraints

  • syntactic Adverbial negator modifying VP.
  • semantic Negates the occurrence across the relevant time span.

What recognizing it lets you predict

They never replied can take the positive tag did they? No auxiliary do is needed to host never in the declarative. Its temporal quantification and clausal polarity are independently testable.
