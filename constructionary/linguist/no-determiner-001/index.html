Negative determinative no in determiner function

no-determiner-001 · syntactic · provisional

no + N

Meaning

Negatively quantifies a nominal by asserting zero instances

  • no booksZero instances of books
  • no evidenceAbsence of evidence

Constraints

  • syntactic A DP headed by the determinative no functions as determiner within the NP.
  • semantic Construes the NP as having zero members or instances.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

No can determine a singular count, plural count, or non-count nominal. A clause with We saw no damage normally licenses a positive confirmation tag, linking the local determiner to wider clausal polarity.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL documents the distributions of no and the distinct polarity diagnostics. Those are separable from the claim that a contextually relevant quantity is zero.

Confidence: medium.
