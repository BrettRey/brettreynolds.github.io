Nominal Construction

nom-001 · syntactic · provisional

Optional modifiers + Required head (n-common-001 + {singular-001 | plural-001}) + Optional post modifiers/complements

Meaning

Meaning 1

Specifies/restricts feature bundles

Modifies/narrows the feature bundle denoted by the head

  • wooden chairRestricts material features
  • helpful neighborsSpecifies relational features

Meaning 2

Elaborates relational features

Extends relational aspects of feature bundle through complements

  • mother of threeElaborates parental relation
  • knowledge that it is trueElaborates contents of the knowledge

Dimensions

Form
Head noun with optional pre- and post-modifiers; number marking
Meaning
Descriptive content that restricts or elaborates the head's denotation
Function
Provides the core descriptive content of NP; hosts modification
Distribution
Immediately dominated by NP; can be bare in certain contexts

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

An expansion of chair to old chair can fit inside the frame the __ without adding another determiner. The resulting old chair still normally requires a determiner as an independent singular count argument.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL distinguishes the noun-headed phrase that determination combines with from the resulting NP.

Confidence: medium.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
