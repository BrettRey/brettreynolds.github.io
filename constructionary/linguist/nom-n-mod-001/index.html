Nominal Pre-head Modifier in Nominal

nom-n-mod-001 · syntactic · modification · provisional

Nom[Mod:Nom Head:N]

Meaning

Meaning 1

The first nominal specifies the type or category of the second nominal.

Constraints: semantic: The first nominal denotes a type or category that the second nominal belongs to. Related constructions: n-common-001 (can-fill-left); n-common-001 (can-fill-right)

  • [dog breeder]Specifies the type of breeder: one who breeds dogs.
  • [toy factory]Specifies the type of factory: one that makes toys.

Meaning 2

The first nominal indicates the purpose or function of the second nominal.

Constraints: semantic: The first nominal denotes a purpose or function associated with the second nominal. Related constructions: n-common-001 (can-fill-left); n-common-001 (can-fill-right)

  • [coffee cup]A cup intended for holding coffee.
  • [work clothes]Clothes worn for work.

Meaning 3

The first nominal specifies the origin or location of the second nominal.

Constraints: semantic: The first nominal denotes a location or origin associated with the second nominal. Related constructions: n-common-001 (can-fill-left); n-common-001 (can-fill-right)

  • [city council]Indicates the council is associated with a particular city.
  • [country music]Music originating from or associated with a particular country.

Meaning 4

The first nominal describes a defining characteristic of the second nominal

Constraints: semantic: The first nominal denotes a defining characteristic of the second nominal Related constructions: np-commonized-pron-001 (can-fill); n-common-001 (can-fill-left); n-common-001 (can-fill-right)

  • the [me generation]The first nominal describes a defining characteristic of the second nominal
  • [man hours]The first nominal describes a defining characteristic of the second nominal

Meaning 5

The first nominal has a relationship with the second nominal that is similar to that expressed by a genitive construction, indicating a relationship such as possession, association, or inalienable part.

Constraints: semantic: The relationship between the two nominals can often be paraphrased using a genitive construction (e.g., using 'of' or ''s'). Related constructions: np-gen-dep-001 (semantically-related-to); np-gen-indep-001 (semantically-related-to); n-common-001 (can-fill-left); n-common-001 (can-fill-right)

  • [student representative]Equivalent to 'representative of the students'.
  • [government land]Could be paraphrased as 'the government's land'.
  • [baby food]Meaning 'food for babies'.

Constraints

  • structural A nominal functions as a pre-head modifier within a larger noun phrase.
  • syntactic The first nominal modifies the second nominal (which is the head of the larger NP). The head of the modifying nominal is a Noun.
  • semantic The modifying nominal typically restricts or specifies the meaning of the head nominal.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Dog breeders normally pluralizes the head breeders. In book review the first nominal can supply the topic or object of a reviewing event. In coffee cup it supplies a conventional purpose. The word order alone does not select the semantic relation.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL distinguishes noun-headed phrases and compounds and analyzes dependents independently of the inferred relation.

Confidence: medium.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
