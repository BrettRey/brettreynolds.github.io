Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The head remains the source of the nominal’s ordinary number possibilities. Compare dog breeders, where breeders pluralizes, with old books, where books pluralizes. Premodifier category can be tested independently.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL’s account of nominal heads and internal modifiers separates the function from how an adjunct realizes it.

Confidence: medium.
