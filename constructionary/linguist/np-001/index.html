Noun Phrase

np-001 · syntactic · reference · provisional

(Det:licensed XP +) Head:Nom, with independently licensed fused-head and pronominal realizations

Meaning

Can refer to entities, concepts, or be purely grammatical

This is the most general NP construction, serving as a parent for all other NP types.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

A complete nominal expression such as the small box can appear as subject and object. In those positions replacing it with the small alone ordinarily requires a recoverable noun or another independently licensed fused-head use.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL compares the distribution of NPs and nominals in larger constructions and describes the fused-head exceptions separately.

Confidence: medium.
