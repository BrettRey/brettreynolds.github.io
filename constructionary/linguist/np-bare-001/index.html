Bare NP Construction

np-bare-001 · provisional

NP[Head:Nom]

Meaning

Meaning 1

Refers to/predicates non-count substance

Refers to or predicates non-count substance/material

  • Water is essentialNon-count substance reference
  • That is goldNon-count substance predication

Meaning 2

Uses a bare plural for kind reference, classification, or an indefinite set

Bare plurals can receive a kind, generic, predicative, or episodic interpretation from the construction around them.

  • Dogs are loyalKind reference
  • Those are bearsClassification
  • Dogs barked all nightEpisodic use with a contextually supplied set of dogs

Meaning 3

Ascribes a role with a bare singular count NP

A licensed role NP can be bare as a predicative, in certain prepositional frames, or as an ascriptive supplement. The same role noun generally needs a determiner when used as an ordinary argument referring to the incumbent.

  • Lee stayed presidentRole predication
  • Jo became chairRole assumption

Meaning 4

Refers to individual

Refers to uniquely identifiable individual through proper name

  • John arrivedIndividual reference
  • That is MaryIndividual identification

Meaning 5

Refers to/predicates activity

Refers to or predicates generic activity type

  • Competitive swimming takes practiceActivity reference (the adjectival modifier supports the noun analysis; bare "playing piano" is a gerund-participial clause, not an NP)
  • That is competitive swimmingActivity identification

Meaning 6

Refers to/predicates time

Refers to or predicates temporal point/period

  • Monday worksTime reference
  • That is noonTime identification

Meaning 7

Refers to/predicates place

Refers to or predicates location/space

  • Toronto was the next stop.Articleless place name with a nominal head
  • The venue is Kingston.Specifies a place by its name

Meaning 8

Refers to/predicates abstract concept

Refers to or predicates abstract notion/quality

  • Freedom isn't freeAbstract reference
  • That is justiceAbstract identification

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

A bare NP can be singular count in a restricted role or naming use, while bare plurals and non-count nouns can normally be arguments. Compare Lee became chair, Chair called on the name reading, and We bought chairs.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL provides independent distributional grounds for these kinds of bare uses. Absence of a determiner alone does not fix their interpretation.

Confidence: medium.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
