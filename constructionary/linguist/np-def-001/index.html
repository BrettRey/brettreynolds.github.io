Definite NP Construction

np-def-001 · syntactic · provisional

Definite determiner + Nominal | referential personal pronoun | NP used as a proper name

Meaning

Meaning 1

Refers to/predicates identifiable instance

Refers to or predicates property of instance of nominal's type as uniquely identifiable in context

  • The dog barkedRefers to specific dog
  • She is the winnerPredicates unique role

Meaning 2

Refers to/predicates maximal set

Refers to or predicates property of all contextually relevant instances of nominal's type

  • The students passedThe group is delimited by the context
  • They are the top performersPredicates maximal group property

Meaning 3

Refers to/predicates unique role

Refers to or predicates property of unique occupant/instance of role described by nominal

  • The CEO resignedRefers to role-holder
  • That became the main issuePredicates unique status

Meaning 4

Refers to/predicates kind

Refers to or predicates property of entire species/type

  • The dodo is extinctRefers to species
  • The wolf has returned to the regionKind-level statement in a context about species distribution

Meaning 5

Refers to/predicates generic property

Refers to or predicates property taken as generally characteristic

  • The lion is dangerousRefers to typical properties
  • He became the typical bureaucratPredicates typical properties

Dimensions

Form
Deital determiner (the, this, that, dependent genitive pronoun, or genitive NP) + Nom, or personal pronoun, or bare proper-noun NP
Meaning
Identifiable, unique, or familiar referent; anchors discourse-established entity
Function
Topic-worthy argument; hosts identificational predication
Distribution
Argument positions; existential there-resistance; partitive licensing

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The keys normally identifies an accessible set and can supply a domain in two of the keys. The radio in Jo listened to the radio can still have a conventional activity reading without identifying one physical radio.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL documents both ordinary definites and restricted non-referential readings. Reynolds uses partitive domain establishment as a structural test rather than relying on identifiability alone.

Confidence: medium.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
