Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Jo’s can occur without an overt following noun in Jo’s is blue when the relevant kind is established. In This one is Jo’s, the genitive can instead express a possessive relation predicatively.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL distinguishes independent genitives with nominal recovery from a distinct predicative-genitive construction.

Confidence: medium.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
