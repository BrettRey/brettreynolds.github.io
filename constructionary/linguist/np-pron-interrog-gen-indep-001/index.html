Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Whose can occur as a clause-level complement in Whose is this? and as an interrogative fused head in Whose did you borrow? The dependent use in Whose key is this? includes a separate nominal.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL distinguishes three slots for whose and gives an independent NP use with nominal interpretation recovered from context.

Confidence: medium.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
