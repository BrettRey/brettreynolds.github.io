What recognizing it lets you predict

A form such as mine can occupy an NP slot without an overt noun, as in mine fell. The dependent counterpart my requires a nominal. His is formally shared across the two slots.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL lists independent and dependent forms and describes their different distributions.

Confidence: medium.
