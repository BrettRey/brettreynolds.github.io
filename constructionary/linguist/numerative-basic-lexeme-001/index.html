Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Twenty-four can supply the single lexical unit that ordinal morphology targets in twenty-fourth. A construction such as three hundred twenty-four has additional phrasal structure, which is recoverable in reduced alternatives in an appropriate context.

Evidence for the projection

Reynolds combines prosody, ordinal morphology and ellipsis evidence for the lexical versus syntactic analysis.

Confidence: medium.
