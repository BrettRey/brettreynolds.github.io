Complex Numerative (Phrasal >99)

numerative-complex-001 · syntactic · provisional

[factor + magnitude] (+ addition)

Meaning

Phrasal numeratives above the lexical 0–99 range, built from magnitude phrases with optional additions

  • one thousand twenty-fourfactor + magnitude + asyndetic addition
  • two hundred and fivefactor + magnitude + and-addition

Constraints

  • structural Requires a magnitude head with a factor; additions are coordinated constituents.
  • structural Applies above the basic numerative lexicon (0–99).

Dimensions

Form
Syntactic phrase or coordination; magnitude head with factor modifier
Meaning
Multiplicative and additive composition for values above 99
Function
Same functions as basic numeratives but with internal syntactic structure
Distribution
Prosodic breaks at magnitude boundaries; allows internal modification

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Two hundred and four has an internal phrase two hundred which can also stand independently. Where the context supports the intended scale, the second coordinate of between two hundred and four and six can recover material and mean two hundred and six.

Evidence for the projection

Reynolds uses reduction and coordination to diagnose structure above the basic lexical inventory. A shorter coordinate can also get a literal small-number reading in other contexts.

Confidence: medium.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
