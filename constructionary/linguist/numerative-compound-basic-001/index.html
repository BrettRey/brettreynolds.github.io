Compound Basic Numerative (Decade + Single-digit)

numerative-compound-basic-001 · morphological · provisional

Det[decade] + Det[single-digit] (typically hyphenated)

Meaning

Specifies a cardinality formed by additive composition of tens and ones

  • twenty-one20 + 1
  • forty-two40 + 2
  • ninety-nine90 + 9

Constraints

  • structural Requires a decade base with a single-digit addition; excludes *twenty-ten.

Dimensions

Form
Decade + single-digit compound; hyphenated; lexically atomic
Meaning
Specifies cardinality from twenty-one to ninety-nine
Function
Same distribution as simple basic numeratives
Distribution
Determiner, factor, addition; resists internal coordination (*twenty and one)

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Forty-three expresses one lexicalized sum and supplies a base for forty-third. A coordinator inside forty and three produces a separate structure and is not the ordinary lexical compound.

Evidence for the projection

Reynolds argues from word formation and prosody for lexical compounding below the magnitude boundary.

Confidence: medium.
