Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Sixty can stand by itself and can supply the first component of sixty-four. The relation to six is transparent, but a new decade form cannot automatically be generated from any numerical expression by adding -ty.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL treats the decade endings as lexical word formation. Reynolds uses decade-based compounds as evidence for the basic lexicon.

Confidence: medium.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
