Factor + Magnitude Numerative

numerative-factor-modifier-001 · syntactic · provisional

DP[factor] + D[magnitude]

Meaning

Multiplicative numerative built by combining a factor with a magnitude head

  • two hundred2 × 100
  • forty-two million42 × 1,000,000

Constraints

  • structural Factor is a modifier of the magnitude head, not a determiner of the NP.
  • structural Factor is drawn from the basic numeratives (0–99).

Dimensions

Form
Basic numerative (0-99) + magnitude; factor is modifier, not determiner
Meaning
Multiplicative composition (factor × magnitude)
Function
Factor scales magnitude head; near-obligatory in most contexts
Distribution
Factor precedes magnitude head; prosodic break after magnitude

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Two multiplies hundred in two hundred. It can be embedded under a separate determiner in a count noun use such as two two hundreds. A second determiner analysis would obscure why both numerals occur and contribute differently.

Evidence for the projection

Reynolds tests factor modification against the co-occurrence of actual external determiners and against argument-selection tests.

Confidence: medium.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
