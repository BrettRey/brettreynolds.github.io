Fractional Noun

numerative-fractional-001 · morphological · provisional

Cardinal base → fractional noun (-th or suppletive)

Meaning

Fractional noun denoting a denominator; used in fraction NPs with a numeral determiner

  • fifthDenominator noun
  • quarterSuppletive fractional noun

Constraints

  • structural Fractional noun heads a nominal; numerator functions as determiner in fraction NPs (e.g., one fifth).
  • morphological Fractional noun inflects for number (one third vs two thirds); half/quarter are suppletive.

Dimensions

Form

Meaning

Function
Heads nominal in fraction NPs; numerator is determiner

Inflects for number (one fifth, two fifths); licenses factors

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Fifths in three fifths accepts plural marking and a cardinal determiner. Fifth in fifth place is adjectival and does not take plural marking there. The two distributions persist where the forms are partly identical.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL and Reynolds distinguish fractional nouns from ordinal adjectives despite the shared morphological material.

Confidence: medium.
