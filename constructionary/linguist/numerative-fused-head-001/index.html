Fused-head Numerative

numerative-fused-head-001 · syntactic · provisional

Det[numerative] (fused determiner-head)

Meaning

Numerative used as a standalone DP/NP without an overt head noun

  • She bought twenty books, but I bought twenty-two.Fused determiner-head use
  • How many thousand? Two.Factor substitution (fragment answering the factor constituent, not a fused head)

Constraints

  • structural Functions as a fused determiner-head in NP structure; may license partitive of-complements.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

In Lee bought thirty books and I bought forty, the second numerative fills the object NP and can recover the kind from context. In Forty is even, the bare form can instead name the number. Stand-alone shape does not fix the analysis.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL’s fused-head comparison and Reynolds’s cardinal-noun diagnostics separate the corresponding readings.

Confidence: medium.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
