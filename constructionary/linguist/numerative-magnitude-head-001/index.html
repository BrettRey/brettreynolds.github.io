Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Hundred remains singular in exact two hundred books and supplies its scale. In hundreds of books, the same base has count plural morphology and an oblique dependent. These diagnostics separate the head’s lexical uses.

Evidence for the projection

Reynolds identifies a determinative magnitude head in the exact phrases and distinguishes plural magnitude nouns in the categorial analysis.

Confidence: medium.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
