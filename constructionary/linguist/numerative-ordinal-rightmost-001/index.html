Rightmost-base Ordinal Formation in Numeratives

numerative-ordinal-rightmost-001 · morphological · provisional

[numerative] + ordinal suffix on rightmost simple base

Meaning

Ordinal morphology targets the rightmost simple base within a numerative

  • twenty-fourthOrdinal suffix on rightmost base (four)
  • two hundredthOrdinal suffix on magnitude base (hundred)
  • twenty-secondSuppletive ordinal on rightmost base (two → second)

Constraints

  • structural Ordinal/fractional morphology selects the rightmost simple base in the numerative constituent.
  • morphological Irregular bases (first/second/third) are lexically specified.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

In two hundred forty-second, the only overt ordinal form is the last part of the numerative, while the ordinal interpretation applies to the whole rank. Ordinalizing an earlier component cannot freely substitute for that realization.

Evidence for the projection

Reynolds uses the placement of ordinal morphology among the constituency tests, independently of spelling conventions.

Confidence: medium.
