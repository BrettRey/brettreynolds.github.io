Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The digit 0 participates as the lexeme zero and can determine a plural in zero defects. One differs in selecting a singular count nominal. The single-digit class therefore does not predict uniform agreement or determiner selection.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL gives the cardinal distribution and Reynolds includes zero in the basic lexical inventory. The comparison separates numerical representation from grammatical number.

Confidence: medium.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
