Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The -teen word form gives a value ten greater than the base related to it. The same base element can give a different value with -ty, as thirteen versus thirty shows. The two endings do not have the same lexical or prosodic distribution.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL separates the teen and decade formations. Reynolds counts both among the basic lexemes.

Confidence: medium.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
