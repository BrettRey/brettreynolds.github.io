Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Ten chapters uses ten directly with a plural nominal. In two tens, the separate cardinal two quantifies two realizations of the value and tens inflects. An independent use of ten does not force either analysis across contexts.

Evidence for the projection

Reynolds lists ten among the basic determinative forms and uses token nouns to establish separate noun distribution.

Confidence: medium.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
