Object-Oriented Ascriptive Predicative

object-ascriptive-001 · mixed · provisional

VP[Head:V + Od:NPᵢ + PredComp:NPᵢ or AdjPᵢ]

Meaning

Ascribes a property or status to the object referent.

In considered him a fool, the NP a fool describes him. The predicative does not add another participant or require a change of state. Passivizing can make the original object the subject while retaining its status as the referent described by the predicative.

  • She considered him a fool.The predicative NP describes the person referred to by him.Huddleston, Rodney & Geoffrey K. Pullum (2002), The Cambridge Grammar of the English Language. Cambridge: CUP, Ch. 4 §5.1 [7iia]
  • He was considered unreliable.Constructed passive with the adjectival predicative retained.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The predicative remains oriented to the original object under passivization. With consider, the sentence does not present the object as undergoing a change.

Evidence for the projection

CASA distinguishes NP and adjective realizations. CGEL treats them as predicative complements with the object as their predicand.

Confidence: high.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
