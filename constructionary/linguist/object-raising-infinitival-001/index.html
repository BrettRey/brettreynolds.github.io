Object Raising with a To-Infinitival

object-raising-infinitival-001 · syntactic · provisional

VP[V + raised NPᵢ + to-infinitival with understood subjectᵢ]

Meaning

Presents an assessment of a subordinate situation whose subject is realized in the higher complement sequence.

  • They expected there to be trouble.Constructed illustration.
  • Jo was expected to win.Constructed comparison. A passive counterpart preserves the embedded predication.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The raised slot admits dummy there, and an ordinary raised NP can correspond to the subject of a passive with verbs such as believe. It need not independently participate in the believing.

See the cited discussion and the constructed illustration.
