Linguist

Ostensive NP

ostensive-np-001 · syntactic · provisional

NP[extra-clausal, attention-directing]

Meaning

An extra-clausal NP whose utterance directs attention to its referent, with packaging-specific NP-internal syntax preserved

The structural parent of vocative-001 (addressee-directed: securing the channel) and directive-ostensive-001 (environment-directed: securing joint attention). Ordinary determination applies in the directive-ostensive packaging, while vocative function independently licenses bare common-noun NPs such as Mum, driver, officer, and buddy (CGEL pp. 522–23). The packagings shade into each other: Taxi! is ambiguous between hailing the driver and alerting companions, and reminders (Your keys!) sit between alerting and addressing. The construction cross-cuts exclamation-001: tag vocatives instantiate this profile without exclamatory force.

  • Mom! (calling)Vocative packaging (addressee-directed)
  • A snake!Directive-ostensive packaging (environment-directed)
  • Taxi!Boundary case, ambiguous between the two packagings

Constraints

  • syntactic Determination is packaging-sensitive: directive-ostensives preserve ordinary determination (a snake, the bus), while vocative function licenses restricted bare common-noun NPs (Mum, driver, buddy).
  • syntactic Not selected by any predicator; resists embedding, tags, and question formation.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Membership predicts packaging-specific NP syntax despite extra-clausal status, introduction of a discourse referent available to anaphora, an orientation-type rather than verbal response, and marked prosody

Evidence for the projection

Directive-ostensives preserve ordinary determination where block language sheds it, while vocatives license a restricted bare-NP inventory (CGEL pp. 522–23); both packagings retain referential and orientation behaviour unlike interjections

Confidence: medium.

Stable: the profile recurs. Provisional.

Core properties

  • Extra-clausal status (not selected by verb; resists embedding and tags)
  • Packaging-specific NP-internal grammar (ordinary determination in directive-ostensives; licensed bare common nouns in vocatives)
  • Attention-directing function (secures channel or joint attention; licenses orientation response)
  • Discourse-referent introduction (referent available to subsequent anaphora)

Boundary cases

  • Taxi! (vocative/directive-ostensive ambiguity)
  • block language (form without prosody or determination)
  • clausal alerts (There's a fire!: function without the form)
