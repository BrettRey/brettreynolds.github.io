Perception with a Gerund-Participial

perception-gerund-participial-001 · mixed · provisional

VP[perception V + NP + gerund-participial predicate]

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Presents a perceived participant in the course of an activity or event.

  • I saw Kai crossing the square.Constructed illustration.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Seeing Kai crossing the square is compatible with leaving before Kai reached the other side. The -ing complement can describe the phase perceived.
