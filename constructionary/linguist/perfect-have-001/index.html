Perfect HAVE + V-en

perfect-have-001 · syntactic · provisional

Aux HAVE + V-en

Meaning

Meaning 1

Places a prior situation relative to a temporal anchor

The present perfect has a present anchor and often suggests current relevance. In many varieties it resists an explicitly finished past-time setting. The preterite perfect instead uses a past anchor.

  • She has finished the reportPrior completion relevant now
  • They had already leftPrior event relative to a past reference time

Meaning 2

Marks experiential or repeatable prior occurrence

  • I have visited JapanExperiential perfect
  • We have met beforePrior occurrence with relevance

Meaning 3

Marks a continuing situation spanning up to the reference time (universal/continuative perfect)

  • I have lived there for three yearsResidence continues up to now
  • She has worked here since 2020Ongoing state with inception in the past

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Perfect have selects a past participle. With a past anchor, had left precedes that past time. A present anchor is only one possibility, even when no time expression is overt.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL separates perfect anteriority from primary tense and from the contextual interpretation of current relevance.

Confidence: medium.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
