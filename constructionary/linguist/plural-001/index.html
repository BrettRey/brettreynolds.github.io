Plural Construction

plural-001 · morphological · provisional

Applies to common and proper nouns, pronouns, and determinatives

Dimensions

Form
Plural morphology (-s, -es, irregular, zero); agreement marking
Meaning
Non-singular reference; more than one or construed as aggregate
Function
Signals non-singular construal; triggers agreement
Distribution
Nouns, pronouns, determinatives; agreement targets in clause

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The form controls ordinary plural agreement in the scissors are blunt. The lexical description can nevertheless be about one instrument.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL’s discussion of plural-only nouns separates grammatical number from the number of objects described.

Confidence: medium.
