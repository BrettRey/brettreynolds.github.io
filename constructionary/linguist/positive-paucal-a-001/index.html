Positive Paucal a few and a little

positive-paucal-a-001 · mixed · provisional

NP[Det:a few + plural count nominal, or Det:a little + non-count nominal]

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Presents a small quantity as a positive amount.

  • We still have a little time.Constructed illustration.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

A little asserts some available time. Bare little favours an inadequate-amount reading and patterns differently in environments sensitive to negative orientation.
