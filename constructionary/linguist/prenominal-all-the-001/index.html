Predeterminer All before a Determined NP

prenominal-all-the-001 · syntactic · provisional

NP[all + Det + Nom]

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Applies universal quantification outside a following determiner phrase or constituent.

  • They catalogued all those shells.Constructed illustration.

Related constructions

A kind of

  • Determined Noun Phrase

Sources

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

All can precede those and the in the same NP. Each and every do not generally occupy that same position before an existing central determiner.
