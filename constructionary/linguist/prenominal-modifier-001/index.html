Bare Plural with Prenominal Modifier

prenominal-modifier-001 · syntactic · reference · provisional

NP[Head:Nom[Modifier:AdjP + Head:N[plural]]]

Meaning

Category/kind modification with property attribution

  • old menPrototypical property-denoting usage; Direct property modification (age attribute)
  • other menRequires contextual reference set; Complementary set relation

Constraints

  • syntactic The modifier function must be realized by an AdjP; the lexical head is the plural noun
  • morphological Head must be plural

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

In a clause such as Old men were waiting, the modified bare plural can be an ordinary subject without an article. In the same episodic use an old man needs the article. The difference in bare licensing follows the head’s count and number profile and survives expansion to very old.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL gives the range of attributive AdjPs and the ordinary order within the nominal.

Confidence: medium.
