Preposition

preposition-001 · syntactic · provisional

P

Meaning

Lexical category heading preposition phrases, with optional complements for much of the class

The CGEL category is much larger than the traditional one: it includes the traditional prepositions (in, on, of), objectless members traditionally called adverbs (home, abroad, ashore, upstairs; ago, with its obligatory pre-head measure modifier), the rare complement-head members (notwithstanding), and clause-taking members traditionally called subordinating conjunctions (because, although, while). See data/lexicon/gold/prepositions.yaml for the curated inventory.

  • in the gardenTransitive: the garden bears the object function within the PP
  • She waited outside.Intransitive use (no object)
  • ten years agoIntransitive, with an obligatory pre-head measure modifier (cf. ten years before Christ); the Constructionary rejects CGEL's complement analysis on p. 632
  • because it rainedClause complement (traditional subordinating conjunction)

Constraints

  • syntactic Heads a PP; accepts right/straight modification in locative uses (right outside, straight home).

Dimensions

Form
Mostly short, closed-class lexemes; no inflection
Meaning
Relates an entity or situation to another in space, time, or abstract structure
Function
Head of PP; PPs serve as complements, modifiers, and adjuncts
Distribution
NP objects and other complements are optional for much of the class; a few exceptional heads follow their complement

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Category membership predicts PP distribution (goal and locative complement positions, from-embedding for locative members, modifier positions), right/straight modifiability for locatives, and non-inflection

Evidence for the projection

Distributional convergence across the traditional prepositions, the intransitive members, and the clause-taking members (CGEL Ch. 7)

Confidence: medium.
