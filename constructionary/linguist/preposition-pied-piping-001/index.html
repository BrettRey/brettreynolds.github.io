Preposition Fronting in a Relative

preposition-pied-piping-001 · syntactic · provisional

relative [PP[P + relative NP] + clause with linked gap]

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Places a preposition and relative expression together at the front of the relative clause.

  • The terminal from which they departed is closed.Constructed illustration.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

A fronted P can take which or whom. The comparable relative with that requires stranding rather than forming an initial PP from that.
