Prepositional Passive

prepositional-passive-001 · syntactic · provisional

Clause[NPᵢ + be + V-en + stranded P with gapᵢ]

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Allows the complement of a preposition to correspond to the subject of a passive.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

To remains part of the passive VP. Selection by the verb strongly supports the construction, and some independently licensed adjunct PPs can also occur under restricted conditions.
