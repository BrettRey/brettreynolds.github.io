Selected Prepositional Complement

prepositional-verb-frame-001 · syntactic · provisional

VP[Head:V + PP with lexically selected P]

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Links a verbal argument through a preposition selected by the lexical sense.

  • We relied on Jo.Constructed illustration.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The preposition in this sense can be stranded in Who did you rely on? It remains adjacent to its NP in the ordinary order and does not undergo particle shift.
