Linguist

Present Futurate

present-futurate-001 · mixed · provisional

Clause[present-tense predicate + future-time anchoring]

Meaning

Presents a future occurrence as scheduled or fixed by an arrangement.

  • The ferry leaves at dawn tomorrow.Constructed illustration.
  • The Sun sets at five tomorrow.Constructed comparison. A calculable future event with the simple present.

Related constructions

  • Declarative Clause

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The plain present can locate a scheduled departure in the future. Compare the heat will crack the pane tomorrow, where the ordinary simple present would suggest a more settled prediction or scenario.
