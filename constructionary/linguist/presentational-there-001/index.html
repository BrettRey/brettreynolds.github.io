Presentational There with a Lexical Verb

presentational-there-001 · mixed · provisional

Clause[dummy there + lexical V + postverbal NP]

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Introduces an entity through an event of appearance, presence or emergence.

  • There appeared a crack in the plaster.Constructed illustration.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Only certain lexical predicates license dummy there in this frame. The opening there need not locate the crack, and ordinary event-transitive verbs are excluded.
