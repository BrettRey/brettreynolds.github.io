Finite Auxiliary with Not

primary-auxiliary-negation-001 · syntactic · provisional

Meaning

Expresses clausal negation with not associated with the finite auxiliary.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Not follows the finite auxiliary in an ordinary declarative, and lexical verbs generally require do in this frame. Nonfinite negation places not differently.
