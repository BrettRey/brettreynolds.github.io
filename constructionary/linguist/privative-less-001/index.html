Privative Adjective in -Less

privative-less-001 · morphological · provisional

Adj[noun base + -less]

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Characterizes something by absence of a feature or entity named by the noun base.

  • The room was windowless.Constructed illustration.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The default interpretation is a room with no windows. Context and lexicalization can supply an evaluative reading, as with careless, rather than literal absence alone.
