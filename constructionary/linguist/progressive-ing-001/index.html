Progressive -ing

progressive-ing-001 · morphological · provisional

Aux BE + V-ing

Meaning

Meaning 1

Marks an ongoing or unfolding situation at a reference time (imperfective viewpoint; endpoints are not asserted)

  • She is readingSituation in progress
  • They were walking homeOngoing event in past time frame
  • She was building a houseOngoing process; does not entail completion
  • She built a housePerfective contrast with completion entailment

Meaning 2

Marks current-phase or contingent construal (often contrasted with a default habitual/simple-present reading)

  • He is living in ChicagoCurrent-phase framing of residence
  • The server is running slow todayContingent framing of a state
  • I'm living in MississaugaCurrent/contingent residence framing

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Be plus a gerund-participle typically presents an activity in progress or a current phase. An -ing complement in Jo avoided staying does not itself have progressive aspect. A future adjunct can instead give the progressive an arrangement reading.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL distinguishes progressive be from other gerund-participial structures and discusses futurate uses.

Confidence: medium.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
