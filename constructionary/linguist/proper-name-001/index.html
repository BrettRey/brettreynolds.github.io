Linguist

Proper Name

proper-name-001 · semantic · provisional

Any linguistic form that functions to uniquely identify

Meaning

Meaning 1

Refers to a unique individual, entity, place, event, etc.

Names create cognitive access points, allowing discourse participants to track and accumulate information about the referent over time. They serve as anchors for both encyclopedic and episodic knowledge.

  • The Boring CompanyCompany name using common nouns
  • Brett ReynoldsPersonal name

Meaning 2

Creates social/institutional facts through naming

Names don't just label but can constitute social reality. They can confer status, rights, responsibilities, and social relationships.

  • His Holiness the 14th Dalai LamaEstablishes institutional role

Meaning 3

Signals membership in name categories

Names often carry cultural, social, and categorical information through their form. Company names, book titles, personal names each tend to follow recognizable patterns that help hearers identify the type of entity being named.

  • If on a winter's night a travelerLiterary title following artistic conventions

Meaning 4

Indexes social relationships and hierarchies

The choice of which form of a name to use (full name, first name, nickname, title + name, etc.) can indicate social distance, formality, power relationships, and discourse context.

  • Professor SmithFormal title indicating professional relationship

Meaning 5

Establishes legal/organizational status

Certain name elements (Inc., Ltd., Co., etc.) have legal force, indicating corporate structure, liability status, and regulatory compliance. The presence or absence of these elements can affect legal standing.

  • Apple Inc.Corporate designation indicating incorporation
  • Johnson & Johnson Limited PartnershipMultiple status indicators

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

A standing name such as Jo can identify its bearer in subject or object position. Two Jos can instead count people sharing the name. A capital letter or name-shaped form alone does not fix which interpretation is used.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL contrasts ordinary name uses with description and counting uses. The construction account separates standing designation from its syntactic realization.

Confidence: medium.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
