Quasi-count Plural Noun

quasi-count-noun-001 · semantic · provisional

N[plural-only quasi-count]

Meaning

Denotes aggregates of discrete individuals with weakened individuation, yielding a mixed countability profile

  • many cattleLoose count property
  • ∗a cattleTight property rejected
  • three head of cattleSpecialized singulative counting frame

Constraints

  • syntactic Plural-only morphology with plural agreement; resists singular and a/an.
  • semantic Individuation is weakened; tight count properties (low cardinals, distributives) are degraded.
  • pragmatic Tight cardinality uses surface mainly in specialized registers.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Cattle admits many and plural agreement, while an individual count normally uses a classifier or counting expression such as three head of cattle. No claim about novel forms follows automatically from those facts.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL documents the restricted cardinal patterns and plural agreement of plural-only nouns.

Confidence: medium.

Stable: the profile recurs. Provisional.

Core properties

  • Plural-only morphology with loose count diagnostics (many/few N; plural agreement; high round numerals)
  • Rejection of tight count diagnostics (a/an; low cardinals; distributives)

Peripheral properties

  • Functional singulative anchoring (cow/officer for cattle/police; head of N)
