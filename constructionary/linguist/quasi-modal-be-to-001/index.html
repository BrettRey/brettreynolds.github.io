Quasi-Modal be to

quasi-modal-be-to-001 · mixed · provisional

VP[finite be + to-infinitival]

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Presents a requirement, official arrangement, or projected event.

  • Staff are to report at eight.Constructed illustration.

Related constructions

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The required or arranged reading occurs without a predicative NP or adjective. It can use preterite was/were for a past arrangement without asserting fulfilment.
