Linguist

Question

question-001 · semantic · provisional

Host:SyntacticExpression => Meaning:Question[set of logically possible answers]

Meaning

A semantic category in which an expression defines a set of logically possible answers

Question is not a clause type and is not identified with the pragmatic act of making an inquiry. A question determines answerhood conditions: a closed question presents a delimited answer set, while an open question contains a variable whose possible values differentiate the answers. An answer supplies the relevant missing information; a conversational response need not do so. Questions can serve acknowledgement, wondering, examination, exposition, and indirect speech acts as well as information-seeking inquiry, and the same semantic category can be realized by syntactically different hosts (CGEL Ch. 10 §§4.1–4.3, pp. 865–72).

  • Who broke it?Open-interrogative Clause defining answers by values of the variable whoHuddleston, Rodney & Geoffrey K. Pullum (2002), The Cambridge Grammar of the English Language. Cambridge: CUP, Ch. 10 §4.1, p. 865
  • You're leaving?Declarative Clause carrying question meaning without becoming interrogativeHuddleston, Rodney & Geoffrey K. Pullum (2002), The Cambridge Grammar of the English Language. Cambridge: CUP, Ch. 10 §4.2, pp. 867-68
  • Which one?Reduced syntactic host with question meaning; it is not thereby a Clause

Constraints

  • semantic Membership is determined by the answer set an expression defines, not by interrogative morphology, word order, punctuation, or any single clause type.
  • semantic An answer must resolve the relevant alternative or assign a value to the relevant variable; other conversational responses can be appropriate without constituting answers.
  • pragmatic Information-seeking inquiry is a prototypical use rather than the definition: acknowledgement, wondering, quiz, expository, rhetorical, and indirect-act uses remain possible.
  • structural When the host is a Clause, it independently satisfies (Subject +) Head:VP. A coordination or constructionally licensed fragment may host question meaning without being reclassified as a Clause.
  • semantic Independent dimensions distinguish polar, alternative, and variable questions; information and direction questions; neutral and biased questions; and ordinary and echo questions.

Dimensions

Form
No unique syntactic form: the semantic category applies to headed Clauses of more than one type, coordinations, and constructionally licensed non-clausal expressions
Meaning
A structured set of logically possible answers, closed or open according to the variables and alternatives presented
Function
Makes resolution of an informationally incomplete answer space relevant; inquiry is the prototype but not a condition of membership
Distribution
A semantic layer over syntactic hosts, including interrogative and declarative Clauses, multi-Clause coordinations, and reduced or non-headed question expressions

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Recognizing Question meaning predicts a structured class of congruent answers, a principled distinction between answers and merely appropriate responses, informational incompleteness resolvable by an answer, and semantic contrasts of answer-space, bias, direction, and echo status that remain independent of the host's clause type

Evidence for the projection

Closed, open, declarative, coordinated, and reduced realizations differ formally but preserve answer-set organization; the same organization persists across inquiry, acknowledgement, wondering, examination, exposition, and indirect-act uses

Confidence: high.

Cambridge: CUP, Ch. 10 §§4.1–4.3, pp. 865–72. cited · bears on projection Defines questions by answer sets, distinguishes answers from responses, and cross-classifies questions independently of clause type

Stable: the profile recurs. Provisional.

Core properties

  • Structured answer set (positive/negative alternatives; variable-value alternatives; answer congruence)
  • Informational incompleteness (completion by an answer; distinction between answer and response)
  • Independence from clause type (interrogative host; declarative host; coordination or reduced host)

Peripheral properties

  • Information-seeking inquiry (speaker seeks information from addressee)

Boundary cases

  • Interrogative is a syntactic property of particular words, phrases, and Clauses; it is neither necessary nor by itself a definition of Question meaning.
  • Question punctuation is an orthographic cue and does not determine semantic membership by itself.
  • A reduced or non-headed question expression may have Question meaning without satisfying the VP-headed definition of Clause.
