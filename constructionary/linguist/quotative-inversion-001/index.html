Quotative Inversion

quotative-inversion-001 · mixed · provisional

quoted utterance + V[speech] + non-pronominal or otherwise licensed Subject

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Places a reporting verb before its subject after or within direct speech.

  • “I can explain,” said the clerk.Constructed illustration.

Related constructions

A kind of

  • Direct Quotation with Reporting Predicate

Sources

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Full NP subjects readily follow the reporting verb. Plain personal pronouns usually precede it in contemporary neutral prose, though literary inverted variants occur.
