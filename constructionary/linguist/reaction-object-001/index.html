Reaction Object

reaction-object-001 · mixed · provisional

VP[Head:V[expression or action] + NP[reaction expressed]]

Meaning

Expresses a reaction through the action named by the verb.

In smiled her thanks, the smile is the means of expressing thanks. The NP can denote an attitude, communicative response, or emotion. The verb need not ordinarily take an object of this type. The sources distinguish the pattern from a cognate object naming the event itself and report restrictions on passive versions.

  • She nodded satisfaction.Published example. The NP denotes the attitude expressed by the nod.Bouso Rivas, Tamara (2022), The English Reaction Object Construction: A Case of Syntactic Constructional Contamination. Miscelánea 65, 13–36, abstract
  • He grunted assent.COCA example of a bare response NP.Bouso, Tamara, Marianne Hundt & Laetitia Van Driessche (2024), A sisterhood of constructions? Cognitive Linguistics 35(3): 313–344, §3.2 (11)

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

An action verb can combine with an NP describing what the action expresses, even when its ordinary intransitive use has no such NP.

Evidence for the projection

Bouso documents varied reaction nouns. Bouso, Hundt and Van Driessche compare reaction, cognate and superlative patterns and their distinct transitivity profiles.

Confidence: high.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
