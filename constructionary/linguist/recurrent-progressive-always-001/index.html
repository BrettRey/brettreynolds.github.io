Recurrent Progressive with always

recurrent-progressive-always-001 · mixed · provisional

Clause[be + always + V-ing]

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Presents a habit as a recurring series of occurrences, often with a speaker evaluation.

  • You’re always leaving the gate open.Constructed illustration.

Related constructions

A kind of

  • Progressive -ing

Sources

  • Huddleston, Rodney & Geoffrey K. Pullum (2002), The Cambridge Grammar of the English Language. Cambridge: CUP, Ch. 3 §8.1, occurrence series; Ch. 8 §§8–9. discussed · bears on projection

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The frame can describe repeated gate-leaving even if the gate is closed now. Irritation suits this example but an evaluatively positive action can support praise in the same frame.
