Reflexive Beneficiary in a Double-Object VP

reflexive-benefactive-ditransitive-001 · mixed · provisional

Clause[Subject NPᵢ + V + reflexive indirect objectᵢ + direct object NP]

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Presents the subject’s action as securing goods or a resource for the subject.

  • Jo found herself a quieter room.Constructed illustration.

Related constructions

Interacts with

  • Ditransitive VP (Double-object Construction)Its two postverbal NPs are indirect and direct object.

Compare

  • Direct-Object Reflexive AnaphorHere the reflexive is indirect rather than direct object.

Sources

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Jo is understood as the intended beneficiary and room is the acquired resource. In found herself in a quieter room, herself is instead the direct object and the PP locates Jo.
