Repeated Bare Noun with a Preposition

repeated-noun-preposition-001 · mixed · provisional

Nᵢ + P + Nᵢ, with bare noun forms and a choice of preposition tied to the reading

Meaning

Meaning 1

Presents successive instances over an interval.

After presents a succession of distinct instances of one kind. CGEL also gives upon in mile upon mile. Nominal uses such as flight after flight of stairs coexist with adjunct uses. The construction's interpretation and the question of what heads each subtype can be evaluated separately.

  • day after daySuccessive temporal units.Huddleston, Rodney & Geoffrey K. Pullum (2002), The Cambridge Grammar of the English Language. Cambridge: CUP, Ch. 5 §8.5(b), pp. 409–410 [20vi]
  • flight after flight of stairsAn NP use discussed by CGEL in the chapter on prepositions.Huddleston, Rodney & Geoffrey K. Pullum (2002), The Cambridge Grammar of the English Language. Cambridge: CUP, Ch. 7 §4.4, p. 634 n. 15

Meaning 2

Presents a process in successive units with by.

The repeated noun supplies the units by which an event proceeds. Which prepositions can express a particular reading is a property of the subtype. This entry records the productive succession patterns.

  • mouthful by mouthfulThe process advances by successive mouthfuls.Huddleston, Rodney & Geoffrey K. Pullum (2002), The Cambridge Grammar of the English Language. Cambridge: CUP, Ch. 5 §8.5(b), pp. 409–410 [20vi]
  • We checked the total invoice by invoice.Constructed test of the stepwise reading with a different noun.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The preposition and the matched noun slots together distinguish iteration over instances from a description of how an event proceeds.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL's matched forms and distributional contrasts. Jackendoff documents productive subtypes and lexical exceptions.
