Scalar Endpoint Adjective in a Resultative

resultative-scalar-state-001 · mixed · provisional

resultative VP with an adjectival result construed as a reachable state

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Presents the result as an attained point or region on a scale.

  • They polished the railing smooth.Constructed illustration.

Related constructions

A kind of

  • Object-Oriented Resultative Construction

Interacts with

  • Gradation

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

On the completed-event reading, the rail reaches contextual smoothness. Replacing smooth with a property that does not readily supply an attainable endpoint can lower acceptability even when the verb and object are unchanged.
