Secondary Verbal Negation

secondary-verbal-negation-001 · syntactic · provisional

not + VP[secondary]

Meaning

Negates a non-finite or subjunctive clause without do-support

  • It is vital that he not be told.Subjunctive clause
  • Not locking the doors is unwise.Gerund-participial clause
  • a plan not approved by the boardPast-participial clause
  • It looks bad for them not to smile.Infinitival clause

Constraints

  • syntactic Not premodifies the secondary VP; do-support is unavailable.
  • position In to-infinitivals, not may appear before or after to (e.g., "for them to not smile").

Related constructions

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The negative to-infinitival in a plan not to leave and the mandative that Lee not leave both keep the following verb in the plain form without do. The corresponding finite declarative Lee did not leave uses do.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL treats these as secondary verbal negation and distinguishes them by form from primary negation.

Confidence: medium.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
