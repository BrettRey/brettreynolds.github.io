Simple Intransitive VP

simple-intransitive-vp-001 · syntactic · provisional

VP[Head:V, without a core complement slot in the use at issue]

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Presents a one-place predication without a core object or predicative complement.

  • The guests laughed.Constructed illustration.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

An adjunct may be added without changing the verb's core frame. An NP after the verb can instead require a separately licensed construction, as with a cognate event object.
