Singular Construction

singular-001 · morphological · provisional

Applies to common and proper nouns, pronouns, and determinatives

Meaning

Meaning 1

Implements Grammatical Number, meaning 1: Specifies exactly one countable entity

  • cat-Single entity
  • itSingle entity reference
  • thisSingle entity determination

Meaning 2

Implements Grammatical Number, meaning 2: Marks unity/wholeness for non-count nouns

  • riceUndivided non-count substance

Dimensions

Singular morphology (often zero); singular agreement marking

Signals singular construal; triggers singular agreement

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The singular grammatical pattern travels across count and non-count uses. The water is cold has singular agreement although no distinct unit of water need have been selected.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL separates number agreement and countability, including singular morphology for non-count nouns.

Confidence: medium.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
