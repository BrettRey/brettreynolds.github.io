Inverted Additive So and Neither

so-neither-inversion-001 · mixed · provisional

Clause[so/neither/nor + finite Aux + Subject]

Meaning

Affirms a parallel predication under matching polarity.

  • Lee has finished, and so has Jo.Constructed illustration.
  • Lee hasn’t finished, and neither has Jo.Constructed comparison. Negative additive counterpart.

Related constructions

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

A positive antecedent licenses additive so, a negative one neither or nor. The new subject follows the auxiliary and the prior predicate is recoverable.
