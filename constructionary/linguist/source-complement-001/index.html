Source Locative-Complement Construction

source-complement-001 · syntactic · predication · provisional

Head:V + Comp:PP[locative, source]

Meaning

A VP contains a locative complement bearing the source role, marking the initial location of motion or change

Source is a semantic role, while locative complement is the CGEL syntactic function. Source complements are generally headed by from, away, off, or out. From takes an NP or locative-PP complement; the inner PP in from under the bed expresses the source location and is not another source complement. Away occurs alone or with a from-PP complement; off occurs alone or with an NP object; out occurs alone or with an of- or from-PP complement (CGEL pp. 257–59, 687).

  • She came from Toronto.From-PP in source-role locative-complement function
  • Ed emerged [from under the car].The whole from-PP is the source-role locative complement; the inner PP expresses the source location
  • She walked away.Complementless away-PP bearing the source role
  • Kim jumped off the pier.Off-PP bearing the source role
  • He came out of the room.Out-PP bearing the source role
  • They flew from Toronto to Ottawa.Source- and goal-role locative complements co-occurring

Constraints

  • syntactic The whole source-denoting PP is integrated as a locative complement of the verb, not added as a locative adjunct.
  • semantic The complement denotes the initial location or state and can co-occur with a separate goal-role locative complement.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

In their central motion uses, integrated locative PPs headed by from, away, off, or out predict an initial-location construal, head-specific complementation, and compatibility with a separate goal-role complement

Evidence for the projection

CGEL gives the four-head realization inventory and its distinct complement patterns on p. 687; spatial, state, and possession examples distinguish initial-location source from final-location goal on pp. 258–60

Confidence: medium.

Stable: the profile recurs. Provisional.

Core properties

  • Source role borne by a locative PP complement (initial-location interpretation; integration in a motion or change frame)
  • Overt source marking (from, away, off, or out as PP head; head-specific object or PP complementation)

Peripheral properties

  • Source-goal co-occurrence (from Toronto to Ottawa; from bad to worse)

Boundary cases

  • Away from can express retrospective direction, and off has restricted goal uses; the containing event distinguishes these from the central source uses
  • path or route complements such as through the tunnel and via Chicago instantiate the distinct path-complement construction
  • derivational sources such as made from grapes: extension beyond literal motion
