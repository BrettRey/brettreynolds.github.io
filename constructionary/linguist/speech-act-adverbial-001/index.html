Speech-Act Adverbial

speech-act-adverbial-001 · mixed · provisional

supplementary adverbial oriented to the utterance act

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Characterizes the speaker’s manner or purpose in making the utterance.

  • Frankly, the repair won’t last.Constructed illustration.

Related constructions

Compare

  • Clause-Orientated Evaluative Adjunct

Sources

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Frankly characterizes the speaker’s statement, not the repair's manner of failing. A paraphrase can mention speaking frankly without changing the content asserted.
