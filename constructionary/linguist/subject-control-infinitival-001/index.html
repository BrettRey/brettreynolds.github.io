Subject Control in a to-Infinitival Complement

subject-control-infinitival-001 · syntactic · provisional

Clause[Subject NPᵢ + V + to-infinitival with understood subjectᵢ]

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Interprets the matrix subject as undertaking, intending, or experiencing the subordinate situation.

  • Jo promised to return.Constructed illustration.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Jo is the promiser and the understood returner. A dummy there cannot replace Jo as the promiser.
