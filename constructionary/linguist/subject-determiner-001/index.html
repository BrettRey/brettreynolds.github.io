Subject-determiner (Genitive NP)

subject-determiner-001 · syntactic · provisional

NP[genitive] + N

Meaning

Genitive NP realizes determiner function with a subject-like semantic relation to the head nominal

  • Brett's use of the microphoneGenitive NP corresponds to the subject/agent of the nominalization
  • the committee's approval of the planGenitive NP corresponds to the notional subject of the event noun

Constraints

  • syntactic Genitive NP occupies the determiner position in NP structure.
  • semantic Genitive NP stands in a subject-like relation to the head noun (e.g., agent/experiencer of a nominalized predicate), not mere possession.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

In the committee’s approval of the plan, the committee can map to the subject of approved the plan. In the committee’s photograph, an ownership or other relation remains possible. Genitive position alone does not license an agent interpretation.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL contrasts argument-realizing genitives with the wider range of ordinary genitive relations.

Confidence: medium.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
