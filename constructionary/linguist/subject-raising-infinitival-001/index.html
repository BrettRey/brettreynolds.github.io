Subject Raising with a Catenative Verb

subject-raising-infinitival-001 · syntactic · provisional

Clause[Subject NPᵢ + V + to-infinitival with understood subjectᵢ]

Meaning

Combines a verbal assessment or aspectual predicate with a subject selected through its complement.

  • There seems to be a leak.Constructed illustration.
  • Jo seems to have checked the lock.Constructed comparison. The corresponding passive, The lock seems to have been checked by Jo, preserves the subordinate event being assessed.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Dummy there is acceptable, and active and passive descriptions of the subordinate event preserve what seems to hold. This distinguishes raising from subject control.

See the cited discussion and the constructed illustration.
