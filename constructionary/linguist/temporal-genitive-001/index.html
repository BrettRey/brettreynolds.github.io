Temporal Genitive Determiner

temporal-genitive-001 · mixed · provisional

NP[Det:time-denoting genitive NP + Nom]

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Locates the nominal referent or event at a contextually identified time.

  • They cancelled tomorrow’s inspection.Constructed illustration.

Related constructions

A kind of

  • Genitive 's

Overlaps with

  • Phrasal Genitive at the NP EdgeIn [the day after the election]’s agenda, the genitive gives a time and the marker attaches at the edge of the phrase, after election rather than day.

Compare

  • Bare Deictic Calendar NP

Sources

  • Huddleston, Rodney & Geoffrey K. Pullum (2002), The Cambridge Grammar of the English Language. Cambridge: CUP, Ch. 5 §§16.3, 16.5, relations expressed by genitive determination. discussed · bears on projection

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Tomorrow dates the event without denoting a participant in the inspecting. The inspector’s inspection can instead relate an argument to the nominal predicate.

See the cited discussion and the constructed illustration.
