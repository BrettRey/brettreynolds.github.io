Reduced Than Comparative Clause

than-reduced-clause-001 · syntactic · provisional

comparative governor + than + finite clause with constructionally unexpressed element

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Supplies a comparison standard through a subordinate clause with predictable reduction.

  • It took longer than we had expected it to take.Constructed illustration.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The duration slot corresponding to longer is understood without an overt degree expression. The gap can occur inside a nonfinite complement of the standard clause.
