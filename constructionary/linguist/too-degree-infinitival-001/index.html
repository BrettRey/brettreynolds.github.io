Excess Degree Too with an Infinitival

too-degree-infinitival-001 · mixed · provisional

AdjP/AdvP[too + gradable head + to-infinitival]

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Presents a degree as exceeding a threshold set by an action or outcome.

  • The gap was too narrow for the crate to fit through.Constructed illustration.

Related constructions

Interacts with

  • Gradation

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The crate could count as large enough for other purposes. The relevant threshold is whether it can fit through that gap, not a universal degree of narrowness.
