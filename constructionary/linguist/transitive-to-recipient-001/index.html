Monotransitive Transfer with To

transitive-to-recipient-001 · mixed · provisional

VP[Head:V + Theme NP + PP[to + recipient NP]]

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Packages a transferred item as direct object and its intended recipient as a to-phrase.

  • Kim sent the keys to Lee.Constructed illustration.

Related constructions

A kind of

  • Monotransitive VP

Alternates with

  • Ditransitive VP (Double-object Construction)Send licenses both frames. Other verbs, including explain in mainstream standard English, select only the to frame in this sense.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The Theme can be the ordinary passive subject, and certain verbs such as explain allow a recipient to-phrase while resisting double objects in mainstream standard usage.
