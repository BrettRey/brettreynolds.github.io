True Partitive with of

true-partitive-001 · mixed · provisional

NP[quantificational head or fused head + PP[of + definite/domain-establishing NP]]

Meaning

Selects a subset from an established domain.

  • They opened three of the boxes.Constructed illustration.
  • They bought three of the cartons of milk.Constructed comparison. Of the cartons supplies an established group of containers. Of milk identifies the contents of each.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The of-phrase supplies the domain counted over. The boxes can be more numerous than three. In three boxes of apples, of apples instead identifies the content or measure domain.
