Exceptive Conditional Unless

unless-exceptive-conditional-001 · mixed · provisional

PP[unless + finite clause]

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Presents the condition as an exception to the main claim.

  • The door will stay locked unless Lee comes back.Constructed illustration.

Related constructions

  • Preposition Phrase

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Outside cases in which Lee comes back, the door remains locked. The sentence need not determine what happens if Lee does return.
