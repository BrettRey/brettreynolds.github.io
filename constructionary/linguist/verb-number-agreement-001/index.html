Verb Number Agreement

verb-number-agreement-001 · syntactic · provisional

Subject NP[singular/plural] + finite verb agreeing in number

Meaning

Finite verb inflects to match the grammatical number of the subject NP

  • This dog runsSingular subject with singular verb
  • These dogs runPlural subject with plural verb

Constraints

  • syntactic Agreement is realized on finite verbs/auxiliaries that bear number morphology (e.g., present -s, is/are).
  • pragmatic Collective and notional agreement can override strict grammatical number in some varieties.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

In the key to the cabinets is missing, agreement tracks the subject head rather than the nearest plural noun. The number-transparent pattern a number of keys are missing needs a separate account.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL distinguishes ordinary agreement and independently conditioned overrides.

Confidence: medium.
