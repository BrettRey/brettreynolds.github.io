Article in a Weak Proper Name

weak-proper-name-article-001 · syntactic · provisional

NP[lexically conventional article + name expression]

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Realizes a conventional proper name that uses an article in ordinary argument position.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The article forms part of the ordinary naming pattern, while a label or compound can omit it in a suitable form. Arbitrary replacement with a does not preserve the primary name use.
