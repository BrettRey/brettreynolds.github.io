Topic Proposal with What About

what-about-topic-001 · pragmatic · provisional

what about + NP or gerund-participial expression

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Introduces something for consideration, often as a related topic or proposed activity.

  • What about asking Lee first?Constructed illustration.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

An activity can be proposed without asking for the identity of an object. With a referent such as the spare key, the intended issue is supplied by the conversation.
