Phrasal Exclamation with what

what-np-exclamative-001 · mixed · provisional

independent NP[exclamative what + optional a/an + Nom]

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

What recognizing it lets you predict
