Deliberative Why Not with a Bare Infinitival

why-not-proposal-001 · pragmatic · provisional

why not + plain verbal predicate

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Invites consideration of an action by asking for a reason against it.

  • Why not call again tomorrow?Constructed illustration.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The usual response can accept the suggestion instead of reporting a cause. The plain form can follow not without finite auxiliary do.
