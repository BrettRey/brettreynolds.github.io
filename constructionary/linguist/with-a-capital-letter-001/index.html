X with a capital Y Construction

with-a-capital-letter-001 · semantic · provisional

with + a + capital + Letter-name

Meaning

Implements Letter-Case Specification Construction, meaning 1: Specifies uppercase orthography for the relevant label, favoring an institutional/proper sense.

  • democrat with a capital D.Party-affiliated sense.
  • church with a capital C.Institutional sense.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

Democrat with a capital D specifies the writing of the label and can imply party affiliation. In chaos with a capital C, there need be neither an organization nor a name. The capital instruction can support emphatic construal.

Evidence for the projection

The dictionary directly documents both the literal terminology and the emphatic idiom. The constructed comparison holds the frame constant.

Confidence: medium.
