With a Lowercase Letter Construction

with-a-lowercase-letter-001 · semantic · provisional

with + a + lowercase + Letter-name

Meaning

Implements Letter-Case Specification Construction, meaning 1: Specifies lowercase orthography for the relevant label using lowercase.

  • republican with a lowercase r.Ordinary descriptive sense.
  • romantic with a lowercase r.Non-institutional sense.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

With a lowercase r and with a small r can direct a reader to the same written form in a literal clarification. The named letter generally matches the relevant initial or contrasted position in the label.

Evidence for the projection

The case contrast is documented for small and capital. The lowercase substitution illustrates the independently meaningful case terminology in the same slot.

Confidence: medium.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
