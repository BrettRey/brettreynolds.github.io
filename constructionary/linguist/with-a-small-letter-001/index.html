With a Small Letter Construction

with-a-small-letter-001 · semantic · provisional

with + a + small + Letter-name

Meaning

Implements Letter-Case Specification Construction, meaning 1: Specifies lowercase orthography for the relevant label, favoring an ordinary/common sense.

  • Catholic with a small c.Common descriptive sense, not the institution.
  • romantic with a small r.Ordinary sense of the label.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

In the literal reading, with a small c specifies lowercase c without committing the speaker to an account of word category. A nomenclatural convention or a descriptive contrast supplies the interpretation in context.

Evidence for the projection

The dictionary includes intentional lowercase spelling of a name, showing that lowercase cannot diagnose common-noun status by itself.

Confidence: medium.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
