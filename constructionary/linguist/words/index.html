Linguist

Words

Closed-class word lists and words with several construction-specific uses.

The lists assign CGEL categories to words that dictionaries often label differently (many traditional adverbs are prepositions here, for example). They began as a seed from Simple English Wiktionary, curated by Brett Reynolds; the lists and glosses keep that source's CC BY-SA 4.0 licence.

Words with several uses

need verb

  • Modal auxiliary: Need we leave? · Central Modal with a Plain-Form Complement
  • Lexical verb with a to-infinitival: Does there need to be a vote? · Subject Raising with a Catenative Verb · Finite Do Support
  • Lexical verb with a concealed passive: The hinge needs oiling. · Concealed Passive in an -ing Complement

tell verb

  • Communicating content to a recipient: Tell Jo the story. / Tell the story to Jo. · Ditransitive VP (Double-object Construction) · Monotransitive Transfer with To

explain verb

  • Making content clear to a recipient: Explain the rule to Jo. · Monotransitive Transfer with To · Ditransitive VP (Double-object Construction)

that subordinator

  • Declarative content-clause marker: I heard that Jo left. · Declarative Content Clause
  • Integrated relative marker: The book that Jo read was mine. · Integrated Relative Modifier

that determinative

  • Demonstrative with an overt nominal: Hand me that folder. · Deictic Demonstrative Determination
  • Independent demonstrative: That will do. · Demonstrative Determinative in Fused Determiner-Head Function
