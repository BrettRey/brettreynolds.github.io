Year Name Construction

year-name-001 · syntactic · provisional

N[cardinal proper] (as temporal reference)

Meaning

Cardinal noun names a specific year through direct reference

  • born in 1994Year as PP complement
  • The year 1066 saw the Norman Conquest.Year as appositive to embellishment noun
  • 2025 will be different.Year as bare NP subject

Constraints

  • syntactic Heads a bare NP; resists determiners and attributive modifiers.
  • morphological Typically uses basic numeratives and 'oh' (ten sixty-six, not one thousand sixty-six).
  • semantic Rigidly designates a unique temporal point.

Dimensions

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

In They met in 2002, the numerative identifies a calendar year without specifying an amount of elapsed time. In They waited two years, the cardinal instead quantifies a duration. Spelling with digits can obscure the distinction.

Evidence for the projection

CGEL distinguishes temporal location from duration and Reynolds analyzes the label use of cardinal nouns.

Confidence: medium.

Evidence level: not yet assessed (stable, networked, maintained or controlled). Provisional.
