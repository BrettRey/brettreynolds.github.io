Zero Plural of Selected Count Nouns

zero-plural-001 · morphological · provisional

N[plural] realized with the same stem form as N[singular]

Early sketch: one example so far, and the evidence for the analysis isn’t written up yet.

Meaning

Realizes count plural without a distinct suffix on a restricted set of nouns.

Kind and projectibility

What recognizing it lets you predict

The numeral and verb agree with a plural even though sheep lacks a plural suffix. A singular determiner can combine with the same stem form in a sheep.
