Amounts and containers followed by of

A unit or container word followed by of says how much of a substance or how many things were involved.

In They bought three litres of milk, three litres says how much milk. The milk isn't a particular stock of milk the shopper chose from. It's just the substance being measured.

Compare They bought three litres of the milk from yesterday. Here the speaker picks a quantity out of an established stock: the milk from yesterday. Adding the changes the meaning from measuring a substance to selecting from one already known.

Examples

  • They bought three litres of milk.
  • They bought three litres of the milk from yesterday.

Try it

Say three litres of milk, then three litres of the milk. Which one points to milk you've already been talking about?
