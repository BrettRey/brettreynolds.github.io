Plural nouns without -s

A plural noun is defined by how it behaves in a sentence, not by an -s ending or by meaning more than one.

Cats and books are plural nouns. They go with plural verbs and with numbers above one. But a plural noun doesn't need to end in -s. Sheep can go with a plural verb with no ending at all.

Plural doesn't have to mean more than one thing. Scissors is plural, yet counting individual tools needs a phrase such as pairs of scissors. This dictionary calls plural a form category, not a meaning category: it typically suggests more than one unit, but that isn't what defines it.

Examples

  • cats
  • books
  • The sheep are in the field.
