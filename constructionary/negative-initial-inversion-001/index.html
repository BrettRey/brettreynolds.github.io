Flipping the helper verb after a restricting opening

After an opening phrase such as only after the meeting, the helper verb comes before the subject.

In Only after the meeting did I find out, the helper verb did comes before the subject I. The opening phrase narrows the claim, and that is what triggers the flip.

Compare After the meeting I found out. Here the opening phrase doesn't narrow the claim, the subject stays first, and no helper verb is needed. This dictionary describes the flip as tied to certain negative or restrictive opening phrases, not to opening phrases in general.

Examples

  • Only after the meeting did I find out.
  • After the meeting I found out.
