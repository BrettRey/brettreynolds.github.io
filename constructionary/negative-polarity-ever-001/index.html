The word ever in questions and negatives

Ever meaning at any time fits in questions, negatives and some other settings, but is marked in a plain positive statement.

Early sketch. The full entry for this pattern has one example so far and its evidence isn’t written up yet, so treat this page as a starting point.

In Has Lee ever sailed solo?, the word ever asks about any occasion at all. It fits in questions and under negation. It also fits in other suitable settings, such as superlatives.

A plain positive statement is different: the affirmative counterpart of the question is marked, because it has no special licensor. This dictionary links the use to the setting it appears in, not to the word on its own. Have you ever seen it? asks a question and doesn't state anything negative.

Examples

  • Has Lee ever sailed solo?
  • Have you ever seen it?
