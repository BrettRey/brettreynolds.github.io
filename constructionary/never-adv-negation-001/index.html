A word that means at no time

The adverb never says that something did not happen at any time.

In She never called, the word never denies calling across the whole relevant stretch of time. It means that no calling events occurred. In I have never been there, it means that there are no prior occasions in the speaker's experience.

Never doesn't need a helper verb: She never called has no did before it. It also changes the sentence's polarity. After They never replied, a confirmation tag such as did they? fits. This dictionary treats the time meaning and the negative sentence as two things that can be tested separately.

Examples

  • She never called.
  • I have never been there.
  • They never replied, did they?
