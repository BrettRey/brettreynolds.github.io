Phrases built on everyday nouns

A common noun phrase is built around an ordinary noun like river or book, not a name.

Ordinary nouns such as dog, river and book head phrases like the books or a river. A phrase like this can point to something definite or something indefinite, and to one particular thing or to something less specific.

So a particular, identifiable thing doesn't have to be named with a name. The river can point to one particular river, and the books to a set of books.

Examples

  • dog
  • the books
  • We walked along the river.
