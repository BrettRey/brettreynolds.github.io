Noun phrases that start with a pointing or counting word

Some noun phrases begin with a small word or phrase that tells us which one or how much.

In the new car, the word the points to a car we can pick out. In one new car, the word one counts it. In Ally's new car, Ally's does the same job, and in what size hat, the words what size ask about the hat.

The words that do this are all in the same slot in front of the rest of the phrase. Singular nouns like car generally need something there. Plural nouns and non-count nouns can sometimes go without it.

Examples

  • the new car
  • one new car
  • Ally's new car
  • what size hat
