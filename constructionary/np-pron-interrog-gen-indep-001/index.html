Asking whose it is

The word whose can stand alone as a question about the owner of something.

In Whose is this?, the word whose asks who the owner is, and it stands alone with no noun after it. We can leave the noun out because the situation shows what we're talking about.

Whose can also show up in other places. In Whose did you borrow?, it again stands for the thing and its owner together. In Whose key is this?, a noun follows it, so that's a different pattern.

Examples

  • Whose is this?
  • Whose did you borrow?
  • Whose key is this?

Try it

Hold up something that belongs to someone else and ask whose it is. Do you need to say what it is?
