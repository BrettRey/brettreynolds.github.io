Mine, yours, hers and the rest

The words mine, yours, his, hers, ours and theirs can stand alone as a full phrase, with no noun after them.

In The red one is mine, the word mine says whose the red one is, and nothing follows it. Those seats are theirs works the same way. Each of these words fills a whole phrase's spot on its own.

Compare my. It goes before a noun, as in my book. The word his is the same in both uses: his book and The book is his.

Examples

  • The red one is mine.
  • Those seats are theirs.
  • Mine fell.

Try it

Say my book. Now say whose book it is using just one word and no noun.
