Big numbers said without and

Some numbers are built by putting a small number straight after a large one, with no and between them.

In two hundred five, the five is added to two hundred, even though nothing joins them. It means the same as two hundred and five. Two thousand seven works the same way.

This dictionary says the version without and is strongly preferred in American English and in faster or less formal speech. The five here is an addition to a complete amount. That differs from five hundred, where five says how many hundreds.

Examples

  • two hundred five
  • two thousand seven

Try it

Say two hundred five and two hundred and five. Do they mean the same number?
