Big numbers said with and

Some numbers are built by joining a large amount and a small addition with the word and.

In two hundred and five, the word and joins two hundred and five. Together they make the sum. Two thousand and seven is built the same way: 2000 plus 7.

Each part can stand as a number on its own, and the whole means their sum. The seven in three hundred and seven isn't a second multiplier of hundred. This dictionary notes that this version is preferred in British English and in more formal speech.

Examples

  • two hundred and five
  • two thousand and seven
