Number words for whole tens

The words twenty to ninety each name a number of tens.

Twenty means 20, and seventy means 70. These words name whole tens, from twenty up to ninety, and each is built from a single-digit base with the ending -ty.

Sixty stands by itself as a word, and it also supplies the first part of sixty-four. Its link to six is easy to see. But you can't make a new tens word by adding -ty to just any number expression.

Examples

  • twenty
  • seventy
  • sixty-four
