Fraction words as nouns

In a fraction like one fifth, the word fifth names the size of the pieces and behaves like a noun.

In one fifth, the word fifth names the size of the pieces, and the number in front counts them. Because it's a noun, it takes a plural ending when there's more than one: one third, but two thirds.

Some fraction words are made by adding -th to a number word, as in fifth. Others, such as half and quarter, are separate words that aren't built that way. This dictionary treats fifth in a fraction as different from fifth in fifth place, where it describes a position and takes no plural, even though the two look the same.

Examples

  • fifth
  • quarter
  • one third and two thirds
