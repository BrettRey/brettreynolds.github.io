Where the ordinal ending goes in a long number

In a long ordinal such as twenty-fourth, only the last part of the number takes the ordinal form.

To turn a number into a position word, you change the last part only. Twenty-four becomes twenty-fourth, and two hundred becomes two hundredth. The special forms work the same way: in twenty-second, the last word two becomes second.

The whole phrase gives one rank, but only the final piece shows it. In two hundred forty-second, the only marked word is the last one. This dictionary says that ending on an earlier part can't freely take its place.

Examples

  • twenty-fourth
  • two hundredth
  • twenty-second
