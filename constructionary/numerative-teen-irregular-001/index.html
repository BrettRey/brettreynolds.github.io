Eleven and twelve

Eleven and twelve sit right after ten, but they aren't built with -teen.

Eleven and twelve come right after ten in the counting order, but they don't use the -teen ending that thirteen and the rest use. They're their own forms, not made by adding anything to a single-digit word.

This dictionary treats them as separate words with the same jobs as the -teen numbers: in front of a noun, as a multiplier, or added to another number. Their position-words, eleventh and twelfth, can be compared with thirteenth without treating eleven and twelve as -teen words.

Examples

  • eleven
  • twelve
  • We need twelve chairs.
