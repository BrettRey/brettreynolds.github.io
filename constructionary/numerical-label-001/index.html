Names made of numbers

A word like Room or Highway followed by a number can work as the name of one particular thing.

Some places and things are named with a number. Room 101 is one specific room, Highway 401 is one specific highway, and Flight 447 is one specific flight. In these names the number doesn't count anything. It says which room, which highway or which flight you mean.

That's why the number behaves like part of a name rather than like a quantity. Compare Room 101 with 101 rooms: in the second, the number tells you how many rooms there are. In the first, it picks out a single room in a system of labels.

Examples

  • Room 101 contains our archives.
  • Highway 401
  • Flight 447
  • 20 Pheasant Street

Try it

Try swapping the number in a name you know, such as a room or a flight. Do you now mean a different thing, or a different amount of the same thing?
