Expecting something about someone

In They expected there to be trouble, what is expected is a whole situation, not the word after expected.

In They expected there to be trouble, the thing expected is that there would be trouble. The little word there isn't a person or a place, so it can't be someone the verb is about. It simply fills the position after the verb.

This dictionary calls the pattern object raising. An ordinary noun phrase after the verb can correspond to the subject of a passive, as Jo does in Jo was expected to win. It need not take part in the expecting or believing itself. The pattern presents an assessment of a situation, here Jo winning.

Examples

  • They expected there to be trouble.
  • Jo was expected to win.

Try it

Say They expected there to be trouble. Is there a person or thing that does the expecting besides they?
