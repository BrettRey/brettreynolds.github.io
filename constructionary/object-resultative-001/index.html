Doing something until the object ends up a certain way

In He painted the fence blue, the last word says what state the fence ends up in.

In He painted the fence blue, the fence comes to be blue as a result of the painting. The word blue describes the fence, and it describes the outcome. He washed it clean works the same way: it ends up clean.

There's a related pattern with a reflexive word. In He talked himself hoarse, the talking leaves him hoarse. This dictionary says it works only with a reflexive, not with any affected person. It's different from describing how something is while the action goes on, as in He ate the steak almost raw.

Examples

  • He washed it clean.
  • He painted the fence blue.
  • He talked himself hoarse.

Try it

After someone paints a fence blue, what colour is the fence? Does the word blue describe the fence before or after the painting?
