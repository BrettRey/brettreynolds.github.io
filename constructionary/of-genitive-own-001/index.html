A car of my own

A phrase like a car of my own presents something as set aside for your ownership or use.

A car of my own presents the car as set aside for someone's ownership or independent use. The pattern is a noun, then of, then a genitive pronoun such as my, then own. The same idea without own uses a different pronoun form: a car of mine.

It needn't mean legal ownership. Someone imagining renting a room to sleep and paint in can still call it a room of my own. The word all can add emphasis, as in a room all of my own.

Examples

  • I've got a car of my own.
  • a room all of my own
  • rent a room of my own
