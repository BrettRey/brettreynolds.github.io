Talked out of doing something

In They talked Lee out of leaving, the person after the verb is steered away from an action.

In They talked Lee out of leaving, Lee is the person who would have left, and the talking is aimed at stopping that. The person after the verb is understood as the doer of the action in the -ing phrase.

This dictionary describes the meaning as causing someone to abandon an action or preventing them from carrying it out. Other uses involve stopping an ongoing course of action, or losing an opportunity through deception, as in I cheated you all out of being in my life. A report of trying to talk someone out of something doesn't say that it worked.

Examples

  • They talked Lee out of leaving.
  • I cheated you all out of being in my life.
  • They tried to talk Lee out of leaving.

Try it

Say They talked Lee out of leaving. Did Lee leave? Now say They tried to talk Lee out of leaving. What do you know now?
