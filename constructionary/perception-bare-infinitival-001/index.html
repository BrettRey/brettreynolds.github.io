Hearing or seeing something happen

A verb of perceiving, a person or thing, and a plain verb form together present what was perceived as an event.

In We heard Kai close the latch, the closing is something we perceived directly, and the plain verb form close names it. Here the closing can be presented as a whole event.

Compare We heard Kai closing the latch. The -ing form presents the closing as it was going on, a stretch of the event rather than the whole of it. Both sentences report hearing, and the choice between the plain form and the -ing form changes how the event is framed.

Examples

  • We heard Kai close the latch.
  • We heard Kai closing the latch.

Try it

Say We heard Kai close the latch and We heard Kai closing the latch. Which one sounds like you caught only part of it?
