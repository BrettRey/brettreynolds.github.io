Seeing something while it happens

A verb of perceiving, a person, and an -ing verb together present the person as in the middle of an activity.

Early sketch. The full entry for this pattern has one example so far and its evidence isn’t written up yet, so treat this page as a starting point.

In I saw Kai crossing the square, Kai is caught in the course of crossing. The -ing form describes the stretch of the event that was seen.

That's why the sentence is compatible with leaving before Kai reached the other side. Seeing Kai crossing the square doesn't require seeing the crossing finished.

Examples

  • I saw Kai crossing the square.
