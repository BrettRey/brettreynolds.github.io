Making someone do something

Make, a person or thing, and a plain verb together present one participant as causing another to do or go through something.

Early sketch. The full entry for this pattern has one example so far and its evidence isn’t written up yet, so treat this page as a starting point.

In The joke made Lee laugh, the joke causes Lee to laugh, and Lee is still the one who laughs. The verb after Lee is in its plain form, with no to.

The passive version does use to: Lee was made to laugh. The to is required there. Compare They made Kai wait and Kai was made to wait.

Examples

  • The joke made Lee laugh.
  • They made Kai wait.
  • Lee was made to laugh.
