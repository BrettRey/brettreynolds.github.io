A few and a little

A few and a little present a small quantity as a positive amount.

Early sketch. The full entry for this pattern has one example so far and its evidence isn’t written up yet, so treat this page as a starting point.

In We still have a little time, the speaker presents the time as available. A little goes with a non-count word like time, and a few goes with a plural count word, as in a few minutes.

This dictionary contrasts a little with plain little. Bare little favours a reading where the amount is inadequate, and it behaves differently in some settings that are sensitive to negative orientation.

Examples

  • We still have a little time.
  • We still have a few minutes.

Try it

Compare We have a little time with We have little time. Which one presents the time as available?
