All before this, that or the

All can stand before a determiner such as those or the, applying to the whole group named.

Early sketch. The full entry for this pattern has one example so far and its evidence isn’t written up yet, so treat this page as a starting point.

In They catalogued all those shells, all comes before those and takes in every shell in the group that those picks out. The two words sit one after the other in the same phrase.

All can also come before the, as in all the shells. This dictionary notes that each and every don't generally take that same place before a determiner that is already there.

Examples

  • They catalogued all those shells.
  • They catalogued all the shells.
