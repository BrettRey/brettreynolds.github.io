Verbs that go with a particular small word

Some verbs link an argument through one particular preposition that belongs with that sense of the verb.

Early sketch. The full entry for this pattern has one example so far and its evidence isn’t written up yet, so treat this page as a starting point.

In We relied on Jo, the preposition on isn't a free choice. The verb sense chooses it. Jo is the object of on, and the on-phrase is tied to the verb relied.

Compare picked the book up. There, the book is the object of the verb, and it can go before the little word up. Relied on Jo doesn't work that way. In the ordinary order the preposition stays next to Jo, but it can be left at the end in a question like Who did you rely on?

Examples

  • We relied on Jo.
  • Who did you rely on?
