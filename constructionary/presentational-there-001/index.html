There introducing something that appears

A sentence can begin with there, then a verb of appearing, then the thing that appears.

Early sketch. The full entry for this pattern has one example so far and its evidence isn’t written up yet, so treat this page as a starting point.

In There appeared a crack in the plaster, the word there doesn't point to a place. It opens the sentence, the verb appeared follows, and then comes the thing being introduced, a crack.

The pattern introduces something through an event of appearance, presence or emergence. Only certain verbs fit this frame, and the opening there need not say where the crack is. This dictionary marks the account as provisional.

Examples

  • There appeared a crack in the plaster.
