Words ending in -less

Adding -less to a noun makes an adjective meaning that something lacks what the noun names.

Early sketch. The full entry for this pattern has one example so far and its evidence isn’t written up yet, so treat this page as a starting point.

In The room was windowless, the adjective windowless is built from the noun window plus the ending -less. It describes a room with no windows.

The ending says that what the noun names is missing. Context and the history of a particular word can sometimes shift the meaning toward a judgment of someone, as with careless, rather than only a literal absence. This dictionary marks the account as provisional.

Examples

  • The room was windowless.
