Names and what they do

A proper name picks out one particular person, place, organization or thing, and it can do more than label.

A name like Brett Reynolds or The Boring Company points to one individual, so people can keep track of that individual and add what they learn. A name's form can hint at what kind of thing it names: company names, book titles and personal names each follow recognizable patterns.

Names can also carry social meaning. Professor Smith signals a formal relationship, His Holiness the 14th Dalai Lama establishes an institutional role, and Inc. in Apple Inc. has legal force. A capital letter alone doesn't settle things, since two Jos can count people who share a name. This dictionary marks the account as provisional.
