What makes something a question

A question is defined by the set of possible answers it opens, not by its word order or question mark.

Have you seen it? leaves two possible answers, yes or no. Who broke it? leaves open who did it. Which one? is a short phrase that does the same job, and You're leaving? shows that a statement can also act as a question.

So what makes something a question is the set of answers it defines, not one particular grammar. This dictionary says a question isn't the same as an act of inquiry either: questions can also acknowledge, wonder, examine or explain. This dictionary marks the account as provisional.

Examples

  • Have you seen it?
  • Who broke it?
  • You're leaving?
  • Which one?

Try it

Find a sentence that doesn't look like a question but would still get an answer if you said it to someone. What answer would it get?
