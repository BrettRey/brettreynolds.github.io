Being sent something

A passive sentence can make the person who receives something the subject while the thing received stays after the verb.

In Lee was sent a replacement key, Lee is the recipient and the subject of the sentence. The replacement key still sits after the verb, as an ordinary part of it.

Compare A replacement key was sent to Lee, where the key is the subject and Lee comes in a phrase with to. The two sentences describe the same sending but arrange the people and things differently. This dictionary marks the account as provisional.

Examples

  • Lee was sent a replacement key.
  • A replacement key was sent to Lee.
