Finding yourself a room

A sentence can show someone securing something for themselves by using a reflexive word, like herself, right after the verb.

Early sketch. The full entry for this pattern has one example so far and its evidence isn’t written up yet, so treat this page as a starting point.

In Jo found herself a quieter room, Jo is both the finder and the person who benefits. The word herself comes right after found, and the room follows. The sentence presents Jo's action as securing a resource for Jo.

Compare Jo found herself in a quieter room, where herself is the direct object and the phrase in a quieter room says where Jo was. This dictionary marks the account as provisional.

Examples

  • Jo found herself a quieter room.
