Which pointing back at a whole situation

A which can follow a statement and refer back to the whole situation it describes, not to a single thing in it.

Early sketch. The full entry for this pattern has one example so far and its evidence isn’t written up yet, so treat this page as a starting point.

In The train had already left, which was unfortunate, the word which doesn't point at the train. It points at the whole situation: the fact that the train had already left. That's what was unfortunate.

The pattern is a comment tacked onto the end of a statement. It's a special case of a larger family of add-on remarks that begin with which. This dictionary marks the analysis as provisional, so treat the details as a working proposal.

Examples

  • The train had already left, which was unfortunate.
  • The shop was closed, which surprised us.
