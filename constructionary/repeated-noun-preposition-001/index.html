The same noun twice with a word between

Repeating a noun around a small word, as in day after day, shows a series of separate instances or a job done in stages.

Say a noun, put after in the middle, say the noun again, and you get a long stretch made of one thing following another: day after day, flight after flight of stairs. The little word matters. With by the sense changes to a task done in steps, as in mouthful by mouthful.

The pattern lets a phrase work in more than one role. Flight after flight of stairs acts like a noun phrase, while others work as add-ons to a sentence. Which little word fits depends on which of the two meanings you want. This dictionary marks the analysis as provisional.

Examples

  • day after day
  • flight after flight of stairs
  • mouthful by mouthful
  • We checked the total invoice by invoice.

Try it

Pick a noun, say it twice with after between, and put it in a sentence. Then try by in the middle. Does the picture change?
