Doubting that something deserves its name

You call that a sandwich? can say the thing doesn't deserve the label, rather than ask what to call it.

When someone says You call that a sandwich? with a dismissive tone, they're saying the thing doesn't deserve the name. It's just bread with lettuce.

The same words can also be a plain question, if someone really wants to know what label you use. A dismissive tone and a visibly poor example point to the challenge. This dictionary marks the analysis as provisional and treats the challenge reading as a proposal whose status as a settled convention remains open.

Examples

  • You call that a sandwich? It's just bread with lettuce.
  • You call that clean? There's dust everywhere.
  • You call that music?
  • You call that an apology? You didn't even say sorry.

Try it

Think of something that barely deserves its name. Challenge it with You call that ...? and say what's missing.
