Negating a verb without do

In certain verb forms, not negates the verb without the helper do.

Plain sentences like Lee did not leave need the helper do. But some verb forms get their negation another way. In It is vital that he not be told, not stands directly in front of be. In Not locking the doors is unwise, it stands before locking. In a plan not to leave, it comes before to.

This works with forms that aren't tensed sentences: a plain-form verb after that, an -ing form, a form like approved in a plan not approved by the board, and a to form. In each, no do is used. With to, not can come before or after it, as in for them not to smile and for them to not smile. This dictionary marks the analysis as provisional.

Examples

  • It is vital that he not be told.
  • Not locking the doors is unwise.
  • a plan not approved by the board
  • It looks bad for them not to smile.
