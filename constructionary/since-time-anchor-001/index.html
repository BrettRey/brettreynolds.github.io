Where a stretch of time begins

Since marks the starting point of the period being talked about.

Early sketch. The full entry for this pattern has one example so far and its evidence isn’t written up yet, so treat this page as a starting point.

In They've lived abroad since the factory closed, the closing of the factory is the starting point. The period in question runs from then up to the later time we're talking about, which here is now.

The part after since can be a date, a noun phrase or a whole clause. Swapping since for because changes things. Because can keep a cause-and-effect reading in some cases, but it drops the contribution of marking when the period began. This dictionary marks the analysis as provisional.

Examples

  • They've lived abroad since the factory closed.
  • We've been friends since 2015.
