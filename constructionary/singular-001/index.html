What singular means

Singular is a form that nouns, pronouns and similar words can have, and it makes other words in the sentence agree with it.

Singular is a form that nouns, pronouns and similar words can have. With a count noun like cat it points to one thing, and so do it and this. The singular form is often just the plain word with nothing added, as in cat.

The pattern also reaches words for substances, like rice. There it signals wholeness, not a count of one: the rice is treated as one undivided mass. The water is cold has a singular verb, even though no distinct unit of water has to be picked out. This dictionary marks the analysis as provisional.

Examples

  • it
  • this
  • rice
  • The water is cold.
