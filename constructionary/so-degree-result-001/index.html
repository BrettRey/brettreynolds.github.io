So much that something happens

So plus a describing word, followed by a clause about a resulting or linked situation, shows how high a degree is.

Early sketch. The full entry for this pattern has one example so far and its evidence isn’t written up yet, so treat this page as a starting point.

The street was so narrow that the van couldn't turn. The word so marks a high degree of narrowness, and the part after that tells what resulted. That result shows how narrow the street was.

The result clause sets the level that counts. It ordinarily presents something that really happened. Compare too narrow to turn, which describes an obstacle without reporting that anyone tried. This dictionary marks the analysis as provisional.

Examples

  • The street was so narrow that the van couldn't turn.
  • The soup was so hot that she couldn't eat it.
