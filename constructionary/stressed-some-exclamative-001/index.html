Exclaiming that something was quite a specimen

Said with heavy stress, some in an exclamation presents something as remarkable for its kind.

When some is said with strong stress in an exclamation, it marks what you're describing as remarkable of its kind. SOME hotel that was! can carry a negative judgment, as the next sentence shows (An utter disgrace!), and the same pattern can express a positive one.

This dictionary compares it with the ordinary wine we drank, which can express surprise purely at how much there was. That was SOME wine we drank! doesn't easily mean that. It points to the character or quality of the wine instead.

This dictionary marks the analysis as provisional.

Examples

  • SOME hotel that was! An utter disgrace!
  • Those are SOME elephants!
  • That was SOME wine we drank!

Try it

Say That was some meal! first with ordinary stress, then with heavy stress on some. Does the second version sound more like a judgment on the meal itself?
