Clauses that state a requirement

After words like requested, a that-clause can use the plain form of the verb to say what is required or recommended.

In They requested that the gate remain closed, the clause after that gives the content of a request. The verb remain is in its plain form: it doesn't take an -s even though the gate is singular.

The plain form also shows up with not. In that the gate not be opened, not comes before plain be, and no do is needed. The grammar books call this the subjunctive.

This dictionary marks the analysis as provisional and gives only constructed examples.

Examples

  • They requested that the gate remain closed.
  • They requested that the gate not be opened.

Try it

Say They requested that the gate remain closed. What would the verb be in an ordinary statement, The gate remains closed? How does remain differ?
