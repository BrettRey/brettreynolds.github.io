Giving examples with such as

Such as introduces some examples of the kind of thing you've just named.

Early sketch. The full entry for this pattern has one example so far and its evidence isn’t written up yet, so treat this page as a starting point.

In Bring safety gear such as gloves and goggles, such as introduces examples of safety gear. The phrase is an offer of illustration, not a full list.

That means other safety gear can still meet the instruction. Someone who brings a helmet hasn't ignored the request just because a helmet wasn't in the list.

This dictionary marks the analysis as provisional and rests on one constructed illustration.

Examples

  • Bring safety gear such as gloves and goggles.
  • We need fruit such as apples and pears.

Try it

Write a sentence naming a kind of thing, then add such as and two examples. Could something not on your list still count?
