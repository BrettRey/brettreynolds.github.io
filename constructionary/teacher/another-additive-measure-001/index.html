Additive Another before a Measure or Cardinal

Shows how another before a cardinal or measure adds an amount of the stated size, even before a plural.

Early sketch. The linguist entry has one example so far and its evidence isn’t written up yet. Use this page as a starting point, not a settled account.

How to explain it

In the pattern another plus a cardinal or measure expression plus a nominal, another adds another amount of the stated size to the relevant course or quantity. In We stayed another three days, the added quantity is plural, even though ordinary another with no intervening quantifier selects a singular count nominal. The example adds days to the earlier period and doesn't replace it. This dictionary treats the pattern as a specialization of the indefinite NP and relates it to the measure pseudopartitive. It is marked provisional.

Examples

  • We stayed another three days.

Contrasts to teach

  • We stayed another three days.Another plus a cardinal and a plural noun, adding to the earlier period.
  • We stayed another day.Ordinary another with a singular count noun.

Watch for

  • A learner might think another must be followed by a singular noun, when the cardinal allows a plural.
  • A learner might read another three days as a replacement for the earlier period, when the example adds days.

In class

Students complete sentences such as We waited another ... with a number and a unit of time. They say what another added.
