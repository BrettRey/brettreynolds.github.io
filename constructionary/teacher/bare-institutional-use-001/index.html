Institutional Bare Singular

Explains institutional nouns like school that can be bare when they name an activity, not a particular place.

Early sketch. The linguist entry has one example so far and its evidence isn’t written up yet. Use this page as a starting point, not a settled account.

How to explain it

A bare singular institutional noun can appear in a licensed frame and refer to participation in an institutional activity or status. At school can describe the relevant institutional setting without selecting a particular building in the same way as at the school. Licensing depends on the noun, the context and the variety. This dictionary relates it to the activity weak definite: went to school and took the bus both evoke an institutional activity, but school is bare in the first frame and bus keeps the in the second. This dictionary treats the pattern as a specialization of the bare NP and marks it provisional.

Terms

Zero article is another way of describing the missing article. This dictionary calls this an institutional bare singular.

Examples

  • The children are at school.

Contrasts to teach

  • The children are at school.The institutional setting.
  • The children are at the school.Not selecting the setting in the same way; this can pick out a building.

Watch for

  • A learner might add the before school in at school, when the bare form names the institutional setting.
  • A learner might assume the same bare pattern works with every noun, when licensing depends on noun, context and variety.

In class

Give students nouns such as school, bus, church and hospital in frames with and without the. They discuss which frames express an activity and which a particular place, then compare with a dictionary or the teacher.
