Bare Role Construction

Explains why role nouns such as treasurer can lack a determiner in predicative and some prepositional frames.

How to explain it

A singular count noun that names an office or post can appear with no determiner when the sentence ascribes the role to someone: after be, become, remain, appoint or elect, after as, after role of or position of, in an except phrase licensed by the main clause, or as an ascriptive supplement (Wilson, Secretary to the Cabinet, ...). The bare NP describes the subject or object. It doesn't normally stand as a referential subject or object for the role-holder, where a determiner is needed (They questioned the treasurer). The noun and context must support a role reading: the article stays in become a teacher. A version with the or a can also ascribe a role (became a co-captain), and with be, the secretary of the club can identify the current holder, answering Who is the secretary?

Terms

Predicative complement: an NP such as treasurer in She stayed treasurer or They appointed her treasurer, which describes the subject or object. This dictionary treats this as a licensed use of a determinerless NP.

Examples

  • They appointed Lee treasurer of the club.
  • Lee served as treasurer.
  • Lee is secretary of the club.
  • Wilson, Secretary to the Cabinet, had informed the Prime Minister immediately.

Contrasts to teach

  • Lee is secretary of the club.Ascribes the role to Lee.
  • Lee is the secretary of the club.Can also answer Who is the secretary?
  • ✗Not acceptable: I'm told treasurer strongly supports this proposal.A bare role noun doesn't work as a referential subject here.

Watch for

  • A learner might assume any job noun can go without an article after become, when the choice depends on the noun and how the job title is used.
  • A learner might use a bare role noun as the subject for a particular person, as in I'm told treasurer strongly supports this proposal.

In class

Give students two columns of frames (appointed Lee ___, they questioned ___, served as ___, I spoke to ___). Students fill each with treasurer or the treasurer and explain which frames ascribe a role and which identify a person.
