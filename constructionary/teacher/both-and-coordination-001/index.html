Correlative Both and And

Shows both and and as paired markers on two coordinates.

Early sketch. The linguist entry has one example so far and its evidence isn’t written up yet. Use this page as a starting point, not a settled account.

How to explain it

The pattern is coordination of two items with both before the first and and before the second. It explicitly presents the first two coordinates as jointly included. Both signals the first coordinate and and marks the second. Both on its own can be an NP quantifier, which has a different structural relation to the following material. This dictionary treats the pattern as a specialization of coordination and relates it to either ... or. It is marked provisional.

Terms

Both and and form a correlative pair, as this dictionary’s name says.

Examples

  • We need both a permit and an inspection.
  • I like both cats and dogs.

Contrasts to teach

  • We need both a permit and an inspection.Both marks the first coordinate and and marks the second.
  • We need a permit and an inspection.Without both, the joint inclusion is not marked explicitly.

Watch for

  • A learner might read both as the same word in both of them and both a permit and an inspection, when the two uses have different structural relations.
  • A learner might leave out the second marker and, when both signals the first coordinate and and marks the second.

In class

Students complete sentences starting We need both ... and ... with two items of their own. They then compare with the same sentence without both.
