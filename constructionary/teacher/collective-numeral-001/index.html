Collective Numeral Construction

Useful for explaining plural number words that give group sizes after in or by.

How to explain it

In this construction a plural cardinal noun in a PP with in or by gives the size of groups, and the PP is an adverbial modifying the manner of the action (arriving in twos and threes). The cardinal noun is a common noun and takes plural morphology. Three counts the members of each group even though threes is grammatically plural. By the hundreds works differently: it can indicate large numbers without fixed groups, rather than batches of exactly one hundred.

Terms

Cardinal noun: a number word used as a noun. Compare a cardinal determinative, as in three people.

Examples

  • in twos and threes
  • by the hundreds
  • count by fives

Contrasts to teach

  • People entered in threes.Three counts the members of each group; the crowd can be of any size.
  • Three people entered.Three counts the total.

Watch for

  • A learner might read in threes as a total of three people.
  • A learner might read by the hundreds as batches of exactly one hundred.

In class

Students line up in twos, then in threes, and describe what happened using in twos and in threes. They then count aloud by fives and say what the number tells them.
