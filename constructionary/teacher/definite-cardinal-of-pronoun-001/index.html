Teacher

the two of us

Helps explain the four of us (a recoverable group of four) versus four of us (an unspecified subset of a larger group).

How to explain it

The pattern is NP[the + cardinal + of + personal pronoun], as in the four of us. It identifies a group while explicitly giving its size, and the article determines the whole NP. The numeral must match a contextually recoverable group, so the four of us can describe the group of four that includes the speaker. Without the, four of us can simply pick an unspecified subset of a larger us-group. Cardinals with of-phrases can also occur in ordinary partitive syntax. The analysis is provisional.

Terms

The is a determinative (often called the definite article).

Examples

  • I was there with Jo, Lee and Pat. The four of us shared a tent.
  • There were six of us, and four of us had bikes.

Contrasts to teach

  • The four of us shared a tent.Definite; the numeral matches the contextually salient group.
  • Four of us had bikes.No the; can pick an unspecified subset of a larger group.

Watch for

  • A learner might read four of us, without the, as naming a whole group of four, when it can pick an unspecified subset of a larger group.
  • A learner might use the two of us for a pair that isn't the contextually identified group.

In class

Describe a picture of a group of six, with three friends sitting together. Students write sentences about the whole group and about parts of it, with and without the, and say which group each sentence names.
