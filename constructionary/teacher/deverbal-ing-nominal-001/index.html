Deverbal Noun in -Ing

Helps contrast an -ing noun with its nominal dependents against the verb-like -ing form.

Early sketch. The linguist entry has one example so far and its evidence isn’t written up yet. Use this page as a starting point, not a settled account.

How to explain it

A deverbal noun in -ing names an activity, result or product using nominal syntax. In The careful sorting of the files took hours, careful modifies the noun and of introduces what was formerly the object. A gerund-participial can say the same thing with carefully and take the files as a direct object. This dictionary’s confidence in this analysis is marked medium.

Terms

Traditional grammars may call the noun a verbal noun and the verb-like form a gerund.

Examples

  • The careful sorting of the files took hours.

Contrasts to teach

  • Carefully sorting the files took hours.The verb-like version, with an adverb and a direct object.

Watch for

  • A learner might use careful with the verb-like form, or carefully with the noun-like form, when each pattern takes its own kind of modifier.

In class

Students rewrite each noun-pattern sentence in the verb-like pattern and back, marking where of appears and where the adverb appears.
