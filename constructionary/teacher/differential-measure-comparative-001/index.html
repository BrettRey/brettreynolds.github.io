Measure Differential in a Comparative

Helps contrast a measure phrase giving a difference (two centimetres wider) with one giving a total (two centimetres wide).

How to explain it

A measure NP before a comparative AdjP or AdvP gives the numerical difference between a degree and its standard. In two centimetres wider than the old bracket, the measure is the difference between the two widths. In the plain-grade frame, as in two centimetres wide, the same kind of measure gives the total width. The contrast helps learners see that the comparative form changes what the measure means.

Examples

  • The replacement is two centimetres wider than the old bracket.
  • It was two centimetres wide.

Contrasts to teach

  • The replacement is two centimetres wider than the old bracket.The measure is the difference.
  • It was two centimetres wide.The measure is the total width.

Watch for

  • A learner might read two centimetres wider as the total width of the new bracket.

In class

Students measure two objects in the room and write two sentences about them, one with a measure plus a comparative (three centimetres longer than ...) and one with a measure plus the plain adjective (twenty centimetres long).
