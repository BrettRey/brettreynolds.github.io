Floating Each

Helps explain floating each, which distributes an action over the members of a nearby plural noun phrase.

Early sketch. The linguist entry has one example so far and its evidence isn’t written up yet. Use this page as a starting point, not a settled account.

How to explain it

Floating each is separately positioned from the plural participant NP it is associated with, and it distributes the predication over the individual members: The three hikers each carried a radio. Each can follow the subject across a finite auxiliary, as in The hikers had each carried a radio. The linked NP can have its own determiner and isn't contained in each. This dictionary relates the pattern to the each-of partitive and to each in subject determiner function, and marks it provisional, with medium confidence.

Terms

Floating quantifier is a common textbook name for each in this position. Distributive each is used for its meaning.

Examples

  • The three hikers each carried a radio.
  • The hikers had each carried a radio.

Contrasts to teach

  • The three hikers each carried a radio.Each sits away from the group it distributes over.
  • Each of the hikers carried a radio.Each heads a partitive and is next to the group.

Watch for

  • A learner might read each as modifying radio rather than distributing over the hikers.
  • A learner might think each has to sit right next to its subject.

In class

Students write sentences about a group (the three hikers, two sisters) and place each in different spots, before and after a helper verb. They check that it still distributes over the group.
