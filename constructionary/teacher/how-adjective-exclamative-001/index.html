Phrasal Exclamation with How

Covers the short exclamation How plus an adjective, and how it relates to the full exclamative.

Early sketch. The linguist entry has one example so far and its evidence isn’t written up yet. Use this page as a starting point, not a settled account.

How to explain it

The pattern is an independent AdjP made of exclamative how plus a gradable adjective: How odd! It expresses an affective reaction to a degree judged remarkable. The phrase already supports an exclamation without an overt subject or predicate. The corresponding full exclamative can supply the evaluated situation explicitly. This dictionary relates the pattern to other verbless clause-like utterances and marks its analysis as provisional.

Terms

Exclamative how is this dictionary’s term for how in this use. Gradable adjectives are those that can be placed on a scale.

Examples

  • How odd!
  • How odd it is!

Watch for

  • A learner might treat How odd as a question about manner, when the pattern expresses a reaction.
  • A learner might add a subject and verb to the short exclamation, when this dictionary says the phrase already supports an exclamation without them.

In class

Show students surprising pictures. Each writes a short exclamation with How and an adjective, then expands it into a full sentence naming the situation.
