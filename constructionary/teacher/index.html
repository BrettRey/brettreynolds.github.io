English Constructionary for teachers

What each pattern is, how to explain it, and what learners might find confusing.

Each entry gives a short explanation for the classroom, examples, contrasts that make the pattern visible, possible confusions to watch for and an activity. Terms follow the glossary of Language Landscapes; where textbooks use other names, the entry says so.
