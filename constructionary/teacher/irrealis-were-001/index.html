Teacher

Irrealis were

Explains the irrealis were that remains in first and third person singular clauses.

Early sketch. The linguist entry has one example so far and its evidence isn’t written up yet. Use this page as a starting point, not a settled account.

How to explain it

This dictionary treats were as a distinct irrealis form of be, realized in the first or third person singular in selected remote environments, as in I wish I were taller. Remote if may also have was, for many speakers. The matrix-like were in Were I taller is licensed under inversion, where replacing it by was fails in standard usage. It relates this to if-conditionals and to the inverted conditional, and marks the analysis as provisional.

Terms

Sometimes called the subjunctive were. This dictionary calls it a distinct irrealis form of be.

Examples

  • I wish I were taller.
  • Were I taller, I'd reach the shelf.

Contrasts to teach

  • I wish I were taller.Irrealis were with I.
  • If I were taller, I'd reach the shelf.Remote if; was may also occur.

Watch for

  • A learner might think were is plural-only and cannot be used with I.
  • A learner might be unsure which of were and was to use after remote if.

In class

Students complete I wish... and If I... sentences about things that aren't so. They underline the form of be and compare it with the plain past in real past-time sentences.
