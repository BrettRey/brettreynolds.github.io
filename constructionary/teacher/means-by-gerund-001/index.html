Means By with a Gerund-Participial

Helps explain by plus an ing phrase as the means by which something was done, and how it differs from after.

How to explain it

A PP with by and a gerund-participial clause presents an activity as the means of obtaining another outcome: Jo reopened the file by restoring a backup. The restoring is interpreted as part of the method, not merely a coincidental event in the same time period. Compare after restoring a backup, which supplies temporal order and leaves the means relation to context. This dictionary treats the by-phrase as a specialization of the prepositional phrase and relates it to infinitival purpose adjuncts.

Terms

The ing form after a preposition is traditionally called a gerund. In this dictionary it is a gerund-participial clause inside a PP.

Examples

  • Jo reopened the file by restoring a backup.

Contrasts to teach

  • Jo reopened the file by restoring a backup.The restoring is the means.
  • Jo reopened the file after restoring a backup.After gives temporal order; the means relation is left to context.

Watch for

  • A learner might read by restoring a backup as showing only when something happened, when it gives the method.
  • A learner might treat after restoring a backup as saying the restoring was the method, when it supplies temporal order and leaves the means to context.

In class

Students write what they did and how, using by and an ing phrase, then change by to after. Partners say whether the meaning changed and how.
