Correlative Coordination with neither...nor

Helps teach correlative neither ... nor, which negates both of two coordinated items.

How to explain it

Neither ... nor is a correlative coordination: the markers neither and nor introduce paired coordinates, and both coordinated alternatives fall under negation. It can coordinate noun phrases (Neither Kim nor Pat replied) or verb phrases (He neither knew nor cared where his children were). It differs from additive neither, as in neither did Jo, which depends on a preceding negative assertion. This dictionary treats neither ... nor as a specialization of coordination and lists absolute negator determinatives and so-neither inversion as related.

Terms

Correlative conjunction is a traditional name; this dictionary prefers correlative coordination, with nor as the coordinator.

Examples

  • He neither knew nor cared where his children were.
  • Neither Kim nor Pat replied.

Contrasts to teach

  • Neither Kim nor Pat replied.Neither and nor pair two noun phrases.
  • Kim didn't call, and neither did Jo.Additive neither, which follows a negative statement.

Watch for

  • A learner might read Neither Kim nor Pat replied as saying only one of them did not reply.
  • A learner might treat the neither in neither did Jo as the paired neither ... nor.

In class

Students write pairs of true statements about two classmates (Kim doesn't sing, Pat doesn't sing). They combine each pair into a neither ... nor sentence, once with noun phrases and once with verb phrases.
