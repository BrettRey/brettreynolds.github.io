Nominal Construction

Explains the nominal as the head noun plus its modifiers and complements, apart from the determiner.

How to explain it

A nominal has a required head noun, optional pre-head modifiers and optional post-head modifiers or complements. Its descriptive content restricts or elaborates the head's denotation: wooden chair restricts material features and mother of three elaborates a relation. It is immediately dominated by the noun phrase and can be bare in certain contexts. An expansion of chair to old chair can fit inside the frame the ___ without adding another determiner, but the resulting old chair normally still requires a determiner as an independent singular count argument.

Terms

Nom is the CGEL label used here. Some generative textbooks call a comparable unit N-bar.

Examples

  • wooden chair
  • mother of three
  • knowledge that it is true

Contrasts to teach

  • the old chairThe determiner the plus the nominal old chair.
  • wooden chairA nominal with a pre-head modifier.

Watch for

  • A learner might treat the determiner as part of the nominal.
  • A learner might assume the nominal can stand on its own as a full noun phrase, when old chair normally still needs a determiner as a singular count argument.

In class

Students take a plain noun, add a modifier before and a complement after (chair, old chair, mother of three), and say where a determiner would go.
