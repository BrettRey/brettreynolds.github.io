Agreement through a Number-Transparent Nominal

Explains why a plural verb can follow a singular noun like number when an of-phrase supplies the plural.

Early sketch. The linguist entry has one example so far and its evidence isn’t written up yet. Use this page as a starting point, not a settled account.

How to explain it

In a licensed frame, a number-transparent nominal lets the number of the embedded complement determine agreement. In A number of applicants were waiting, the plural is licensed despite the singular number, so the verb follows applicants. With the number of applicants, the nominal normally denotes a numerical quantity and takes singular agreement. Compare an amount reading with singular agreement, as in Three litres of milk was enough. This is a specialization of verb-number agreement. The dictionary marks this account as provisional.

Examples

  • A number of applicants were waiting.
  • Three litres of milk was enough.

Contrasts to teach

  • A number of applicants were waiting.Plural agreement follows the of-complement.
  • The number of applicants was small.Number names a quantity, so agreement is singular.

Watch for

  • A learner might make the verb agree with the singular noun number in A number of applicants.
  • A learner might use a plural verb after the number of applicants.

In class

Give students sentence frames with a number of and the number of. They choose the verb form, then explain which word the verb agrees with.
