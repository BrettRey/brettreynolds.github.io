Numerative Addition Coordination (without and)

Shows that two hundred five is addition with no spoken and, parallel to the version with and.

How to explain it

This construction adds a smaller number to a complete magnitude phrase by juxtaposition, with the coordinator and omitted: two hundred five means 200 + 5. The dictionary analyzes it as coordination without a spoken coordinator. The addition coordinate can itself be a basic numerative or a magnitude phrase, so the pattern is recursive. In two hundred five, five contributes the same remainder as in two hundred and five. This differs from five hundred, where a factor selects a magnitude. The dictionary notes that this form is strongly preferred in American English and in faster or less formal registers. It marks this account as provisional.

Terms

Numerative is this grammar's term for number words, where a textbook may say numeral. Asyndetic means joined without a coordinator.

Examples

  • two hundred five
  • two thousand seven

Contrasts to teach

  • two hundred fiveAddition with no and (200 + 5).
  • two hundred and fiveAddition with and (200 + 5).
  • five hundredMultiplication, not addition (a factor selects a magnitude).

Watch for

  • A learner might think a number without and is missing something.
  • A learner might read two hundred five as two hundred multiplied by five.

In class

Dictate numbers such as 205, 2007 and 3006. Students write them in digits, then say each aloud in both styles, with and and without it.
