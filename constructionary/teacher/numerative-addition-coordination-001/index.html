Numerative Addition Coordination (with and)

Shows that numbers like two hundred and five are additions, coordinating a magnitude and a smaller number.

How to explain it

This construction forms an additive numerative by coordinating magnitude units and additions with and: two hundred and five means 200 + 5. The addition is a coordinate, and it can itself be a basic numerative or a magnitude phrase, so the pattern is recursive. In three hundred and seven, each of three hundred and seven can denote a number independently. The whole normally denotes their sum and doesn't treat seven as another multiplier of hundred. The dictionary notes that this form is preferred in British English and in more formal registers. It marks this account as provisional.

Terms

Numerative is this grammar's term for number words, where a textbook may say numeral. Syndetic means joined with a coordinator.

Examples

  • two hundred and five
  • two thousand and seven

Contrasts to teach

  • two hundred and fiveAddition with and (200 + 5).
  • two hundred fiveThe same sum with no spoken and.

Watch for

  • A learner might read and in two hundred and five as joining two separate items rather than adding.
  • A learner might treat the seven in three hundred and seven as multiplying hundred.

In class

Students write number words for 105, 207 and 3004. They mark the large part and the addition, then compare with a partner.
