Perfect have + V-en

Core pattern for the perfect, with its present, past, experiential and continuing uses.

How to explain it

Perfect have selects a past participle and relates a situation to a temporal anchor. With a present anchor (has finished) the situation is prior to now or, in the continuing use, runs up to now; with a past anchor (had left) it precedes that past time. A present anchor is only one possibility, even when no time expression is overt. This dictionary distinguishes three uses: a prior situation relative to the anchor (has finished the report, had already left), experiential or repeatable prior occurrence (have visited Japan), and a continuing situation spanning up to the reference time (have lived there for three years, has worked here since 2020). It also separates perfect anteriority from primary tense and from the contextual interpretation of current relevance. This dictionary marks the analysis as provisional, with medium confidence.

Terms

Textbooks sometimes call these the present perfect and the past perfect. This dictionary's preterite perfect is the version with a past anchor. The continuing use is also called the universal or continuative perfect.

Examples

  • She has finished the report.
  • They had already left.
  • I have visited Japan.
  • We have met before.
  • I have lived there for three years.
  • She has worked here since 2020.

Contrasts to teach

  • She has finished the report.Present anchor; prior completion relevant now.
  • They had already left.Past anchor; leaving precedes a past reference time.

Watch for

  • A learner might read has finished as a plain past event, missing the link to the present anchor.
  • A learner might read I have lived there for three years as ending in the past, when the residence continues up to now.

In class

Give students three cards (finished the report, visited Japan, lived there for three years). They write a have-sentence for each and a one-line note saying whether it is a completed event, an experience, or a situation continuing up to now.
