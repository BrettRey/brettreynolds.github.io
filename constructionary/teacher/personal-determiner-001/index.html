Personal Determination with We or You

Explains we or you followed by a plural noun, as in We volunteers need a break.

Early sketch. The linguist entry has one example so far and its evidence isn’t written up yet. Use this page as a starting point, not a settled account.

How to explain it

In this pattern a personal expression (we, us or you) is followed by a plural nominal. The personal expression locates the named group in relation to the speaker or addressee. The nominal survives after it. In many varieties a non-subject parallel can use us volunteers, which shows that case and reference matter independently. This dictionary files this under noun phrases with a determiner and marks it provisional, with medium confidence.

Terms

Traditional grammars call we, us and you personal pronouns. Here the personal word functions as a determiner before the nominal.

Examples

  • We volunteers need a break.

Watch for

  • A learner might think the noun volunteers is optional decoration, when it names the group the personal word is tied to.

In class

Students write sentences about their own group (We students, We neighbours), then say who is included and who is addressed in a version with you.
