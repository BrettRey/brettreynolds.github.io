Preference Would Rather

Explains would rather with a plain infinitival or a finite clause.

Early sketch. The linguist entry has one example so far and its evidence isn’t written up yet. Use this page as a starting point, not a settled account.

How to explain it

Would rather expresses preference between actual or imagined alternatives. It can be followed by a plain infinitival or a finite subordinate clause. A finite clause can have its own subject and a modally remote preterite, as in I would rather you stayed. In I would rather stay, the subject of stay is understood as the matrix subject. This dictionary treats the pattern as a specialization of the VP and relates it to the irrealis were. It is provisional, with medium confidence.

Terms

This dictionary describes would rather as a pattern followed by a plain infinitival or a finite subordinate clause.

Examples

  • I would rather you stayed.
  • I would rather stay.

Contrasts to teach

  • I would rather stay.The subject of stay is understood as the speaker.
  • I would rather you stayed.A finite clause with its own subject and a remote preterite.

Watch for

  • A learner might read stayed in I would rather you stayed as past time, though this dictionary describes it as modally remote.
  • A learner might take the subject of stay in I would rather stay to be someone other than the speaker.

In class

Pairs write five would-rather sentences about weekend plans, two with a plain verb and three with a clause having its own subject, then underline each verb form.
