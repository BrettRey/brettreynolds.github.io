Right Argument-Cluster Coordination

Helps explain coordination of postverbal bundles under a single verb.

Early sketch. The linguist entry has one example so far and its evidence isn’t written up yet. Use this page as a starting point, not a settled account.

How to explain it

In this coordination, corresponding sequences of postverbal arguments are coordinated, and the sequences need not be ordinary independent phrases. In I took Jo to the station and Lee home, each person pairs with a separate goal under one overt verb. Lee home is the object and goal sequence, and it isn't an ordinary nominal constituent. This dictionary treats the pattern as a specialization of coordination and marks the analysis as provisional. Present it as the same verb serving two parallel bundles.

Examples

  • I took Jo to the station and Lee home.
  • She gave Ann a book and Bo a pen.

Watch for

  • A learner might read Lee home as an ordinary phrase, when it is a person plus a goal.
  • A learner might look for a second verb in the coordinated part, though one overt verb serves both bundles.
