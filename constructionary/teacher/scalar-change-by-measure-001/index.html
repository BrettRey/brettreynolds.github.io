Differential By in Scalar Change

Helps explain by plus a measure phrase for the amount of change, versus to for the endpoint.

How to explain it

With a verb of scalar change (fall, rise and the like), a PP headed by by plus a measure NP quantifies the amount of change on an ordered scale. By measures the difference between starting and ending values. To with an endpoint instead names the final value, and it can co-occur with a differential. This dictionary treats the by-phrase as a specialization of the PP and relates it to differential measure phrases in comparatives. The analysis is marked provisional.

Terms

The by-phrase here is sometimes called a differential or amount phrase.

Examples

  • The water level fell by twelve centimetres.
  • The water level fell to twelve centimetres.

Contrasts to teach

  • The water level fell by twelve centimetres.The size of the drop.
  • The water level fell to twelve centimetres.The value reached.

Watch for

  • A learner might read fell to twelve centimetres as a drop of twelve centimetres.
  • A learner might choose by or to without checking whether the number is a difference or an endpoint.

In class

Show a simple graph with start and end values. Students write one by sentence and one to sentence about it, then swap and check which number each sentence reports.
