Temporal until with an Endpoint

Helps explain until as marking the endpoint of a situation or the delay of an event.

Early sketch. The linguist entry has one example so far and its evidence isn’t written up yet. Use this page as a starting point, not a settled account.

How to explain it

The pattern is a prepositional phrase headed by until, followed by a date, a noun phrase or a finite clause. It supplies the boundary up to which a situation holds, or up to which an occurrence is delayed. In We waited until the shutters opened, the opening ends the wait on the ordinary reading. Under negation, as in didn't leave until noon, the earlier interval is associated with absence of the event. This dictionary treats until phrases as prepositional phrases and relates the pattern to since. The analysis is marked provisional.

Terms

Until is a preposition here.

Examples

  • We waited until the shutters opened.
  • She didn't leave until noon.

Contrasts to teach

  • We waited until the shutters opened.The wait holds up to the boundary.
  • She didn't leave until noon.Under negation, the earlier interval is a time without leaving.

Watch for

  • A learner might read didn't leave until noon as saying she left before noon, when the time before noon is the period without leaving.
  • A learner might read until as marking the start of something, when it supplies the boundary up to which a situation holds or an occurrence is delayed.

In class

Students make a timeline of a school day. They write sentences with until for what holds up to each time, and sentences with didn't ... until for delayed events.
