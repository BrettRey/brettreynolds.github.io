With a Lowercase Letter Construction

Helps explain with a lowercase letter as a spelling clarification pointing to an ordinary sense.

How to explain it

The pattern is a prepositional phrase headed by with, followed by a, lowercase and a letter name. It is postposed after a label or used parenthetically. It specifies lowercase orthography and signals an ordinary or common sense, disambiguating descriptive use from institutional or proper use. With a lowercase r and with a small r can direct a reader to the same written form in a literal clarification. The named letter generally matches the relevant initial or contrasted position in the label. This dictionary marks its analysis provisional. A sibling entry covers with a small letter.

Terms

Lowercase letter is the everyday term. This dictionary lists with a small letter as a variant.

Examples

  • republican with a lowercase r
  • romantic with a lowercase r

Contrasts to teach

  • romantic with a lowercase rNon-institutional sense.

Watch for

  • A learner might name a letter that doesn't match the initial being discussed, though this dictionary says the letter generally matches it.
  • A learner might treat with a lowercase r and with a small r as different instructions, when they can point to the same written form.

In class

Students pair words such as republican and romantic with the phrase with a lowercase and the initial. They then restate each using with a small and the initial, and confirm the written form is the same.
