With a Small Letter Construction

Helps explain with a small letter as a spelling clarification that can point to an ordinary sense.

How to explain it

The pattern is a prepositional phrase headed by with, followed by a, small and a letter name. It is postposed after a label or used parenthetically. It specifies lowercase orthography and favours an ordinary or common sense, disambiguating descriptive use from institutional or proper use. In the literal reading, with a small c specifies lowercase c without committing the speaker to an account of word category. This dictionary notes that a name can be written in lowercase intentionally, so lowercase cannot by itself diagnose common-noun status. This dictionary marks its analysis provisional.

Terms

Small letter is an everyday term for lowercase. This dictionary treats with a lowercase letter as a variant.

Examples

  • Catholic with a small c
  • romantic with a small r

Contrasts to teach

  • Catholic with a small cCommon descriptive sense, not the institution.

Watch for

  • A learner might assume a lowercase letter must mean a common noun, when this dictionary says lowercase cannot diagnose that by itself.
  • A learner might treat small as describing the size of the letter, when it specifies lowercase.

In class

Write words with different meanings on the board, such as Catholic and romantic. Students say each with with a small and the initial, and discuss which sense it points to.
