With an Uppercase Letter Construction

Helps explain with an uppercase letter as a spelling clarification that can point to a proper sense.

How to explain it

The pattern is a prepositional phrase headed by with, followed by an, uppercase and a letter name. It is postposed after a label or used parenthetically. It specifies uppercase orthography and favours a proper or institutional sense, disambiguating official or proper-name use from ordinary descriptive use. When the contrast is literal, uppercase I specifies the same letter shape as capital I. In a spelling instruction, this does not tell the listener whether the word is a name. This dictionary treats with a capital letter as the variant. This dictionary marks its analysis provisional.

Terms

Uppercase letter is another name for capital letter. This dictionary lists with a capital letter as a variant.

Examples

  • internet with an uppercase I
  • reformation with an uppercase R

Contrasts to teach

  • internet with an uppercase IInstitutional or official sense.
  • reformation with an uppercase RProper or historical event sense.

Watch for

  • A learner might think an uppercase letter must show a name, when in a spelling instruction the phrase only gives the case.
  • A learner might read the phrase as saying the word is a name, though it can be only a literal case instruction.

In class

Students write a list of words with the phrase with an uppercase and the initial. They swap capital in for uppercase and check the written form is the same.
