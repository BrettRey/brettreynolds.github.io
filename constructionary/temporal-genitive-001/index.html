Tomorrow's inspection

A time word with 's before a noun says when the thing happens.

Early sketch. The full entry for this pattern has one example so far and its evidence isn’t written up yet, so treat this page as a starting point.

In They cancelled tomorrow's inspection, tomorrow's tells you when the inspection is due. It dates the event. It doesn't name anyone who takes part in the inspecting.

Compare the inspector's inspection, where the inspector is a participant in the event. So the 's form can do different jobs, depending on the word before it.

A time phrase can also take the 's at its end, as in the day after the election's agenda, where the 's attaches after election rather than day.

This dictionary marks the analysis as provisional.

Examples

  • They cancelled tomorrow's inspection.
  • the day after the election's agenda
