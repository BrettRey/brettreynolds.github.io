Until, the endpoint

Until names the point up to which something goes on, or before which something doesn't happen.

Early sketch. The full entry for this pattern has one example so far and its evidence isn’t written up yet, so treat this page as a starting point.

In We waited until the shutters opened, the opening of the shutters ordinarily ends the wait. Until supplies the boundary: the waiting held up to that point.

Until can also work with a negative. In She didn't leave until noon, the time before noon is the period when leaving didn't happen. Until can be followed by a date, a noun phrase, or a whole clause.

This dictionary marks this account as provisional.

Examples

  • We waited until the shutters opened.
  • She didn't leave until noon.

Try it

Say what you did until a certain time today. Then say something you didn't do until that time.
