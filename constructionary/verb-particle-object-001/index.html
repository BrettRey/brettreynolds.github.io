Little words before or after an object

A short word like down or off can come before or after the object of a verb, as in brought down the bed and brought the bed down.

In She brought down the bed, the small word down goes with the verb brought, and the bed is what she brought. The same thing can be said the other way: She brought the bed down.

In the dictionary's account, down isn't the start of a phrase down the bed. It stands on its own beside the verb, and the bed is a separate object.

The dictionary marks this account as provisional, and says no single test works in every case.

Examples

  • She brought down the bed.
  • She brought the bed down.
  • She took the label off.
