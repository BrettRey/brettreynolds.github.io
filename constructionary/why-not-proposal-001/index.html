Why not do it?

Why not plus an action suggests doing it, by asking whether there is any reason against.

Early sketch. The full entry for this pattern has one example so far and its evidence isn’t written up yet, so treat this page as a starting point.

Why not call again tomorrow? looks like a question about a reason, but it works as a suggestion. It invites the listener to consider calling again, by asking what stands in the way.

A reply can simply accept the idea, such as "Good idea", instead of giving a cause. The verb after not is in its plain form, call, with no extra helper like do.

The dictionary marks this account as provisional.

Examples

  • Why not call again tomorrow?
  • Why not ask your teacher?

Try it

Suggest something to a friend with Why not. Do they answer with a reason or with a yes?
