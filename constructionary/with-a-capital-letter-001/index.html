With a capital letter

Adding with a capital D (or another letter) to a word says how it is written, and can point to an official or named meaning.

Democrat with a capital D tells us how the word is written, and it can point to the party-affiliated sense. Church with a capital C does the same for the institutional sense.

So the phrase is a spelling instruction that can also point to the meaning. This dictionary notes that in chaos with a capital C, there need be neither an organization nor a name. The capital instruction can support an emphatic reading.

This dictionary marks this account as provisional.

Examples

  • democrat with a capital D
  • church with a capital C
