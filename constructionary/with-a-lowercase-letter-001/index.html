With a lowercase letter

Adding with a lowercase r (or another letter) says how a word is written, and can point to an ordinary meaning.

Republican with a lowercase r points to the ordinary descriptive sense of the word, not an institutional one. Romantic with a lowercase r works the same way.

The phrase is a spelling instruction. It can say which written form is meant, as small does in with a small r. The letter named generally matches the initial of the label.

The dictionary marks this account as provisional.

Examples

  • republican with a lowercase r
  • romantic with a lowercase r
