With a small letter

Adding with a small c (or another letter) says a word is written in lowercase, which can point to an everyday meaning.

Catholic with a small c points to the common descriptive sense, not the institution. Romantic with a small r points to the ordinary sense of the word.

The phrase tells us how the word is written. The dictionary notes that in its literal reading, with a small c doesn't commit the speaker to an account of the word's category. The context supplies the interpretation, whether a naming convention or a descriptive contrast.

The dictionary marks this account as provisional.

Examples

  • Catholic with a small c
  • romantic with a small r
