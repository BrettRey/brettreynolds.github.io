With an uppercase letter

Adding with an uppercase I (or another letter) says a word is written with a capital, which can point to an official or named meaning.

Internet with an uppercase I points to the institutional or official sense. Reformation with an uppercase R points to the proper, historical-event sense.

When the contrast is literal, uppercase I names the same letter shape as capital I. In a spelling instruction, it doesn't tell the listener whether the word is a name. It only gives the case.

The dictionary marks this account as provisional.

Examples

  • internet with an uppercase I
  • reformation with an uppercase R
