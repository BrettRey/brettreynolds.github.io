Years as names

A number like 1994 can name a particular year, working like a name rather than a count.

In born in 1994, the number doesn't count anything. It names one particular year. We can also say the year 1066 saw the Norman Conquest, or 2025 will be different, with the number standing on its own as the subject.

Compare They met in 2002 with They waited two years. The first uses a number to identify a year, with no length of time. The second counts a stretch of time. Writing with digits can hide the difference.

A year name resists words like the or an adjective in front of it. The dictionary marks this account as provisional.

Examples

  • born in 1994
  • The year 1066 saw the Norman Conquest.
  • 2025 will be different.

Try it

Say They met in 2002 and They waited two years. Which one tells you how long, and which one tells you when?
