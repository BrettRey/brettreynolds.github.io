    About this developing edition

    The base text is Albert Leitzmann’s 1906 edition, Gesammelte Schriften, volume V, pages 364–473. This is a provisional research translation, not a critical edition. The 2017 critical edition has not been acquired, and the manuscript has not been collated.

    The translation uses independent model candidates, source-based synthesis, fresh model audits and an explicit record of the resulting decisions. English-reading review concerns clarity and editorial usefulness; it is not specialist certification of German fidelity. Each section shows its own review status.

    The first seven passages carry a reported cancellation that has not been independently manuscript-verified; the Introduction explains it. Historical assertions and evaluations remain in the translation, with focused qualifications in the notes. Unresolved readings remain visible.

    Note labels identify the author: (Humboldt), (1906 Ed.) and (Trans.). The German preserves historical spelling. The English follows Oxford spelling and uses local terminology guides to explain consequential distinctions.

    This website is generated from the edition records. Permanent paragraph links and snapshot-specific citations make the current draft identifiable as it develops. Source versions, alternative translations, audit findings and correction history remain in the public repository.
