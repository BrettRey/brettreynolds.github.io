Judgment marks and how they are spoken

Printed mark — Spoken as

no mark — “with no judgment mark”, only where the absence is doing work

* — “starred”

raised ? — “questionable”

raised ! — “differing between standard varieties”

raised % — “grammatical in other varieties”

X08 is the case where the absence of a star is itself the point, and the narration says so before reading the sentence.
