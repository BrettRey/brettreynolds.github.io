Chapter 3: written reference

The epigraph, complete pronoun and determinative tables, and references accompanying the narration.

Epigraph

the domestic labour
that purchased
the silence
that wrote every poem
that you've ever read

Abby Paige, “Selected Hoems”, in Best Canadian Poetry 2020, pp. 43–44.
