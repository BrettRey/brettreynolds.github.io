Figure 1: places of articulation

The figure is a mid-sagittal diagram (lips at the left, throat at the right) numbered 1 to 18. The chapter uses these numbers: 2 lips (bilabial); 2 and 3 lips and teeth (labiodental); 3 teeth (dental); 4 alveolar ridge; 5 post-alveolar; 7 hard palate (palatal); 8 soft palate (velum, velar); 9 uvula; 11 larynx and glottis; and, for the tongue, 14 back, 15 front, 16 blade, 17 tip. The numbers 1, 6, 10, 12, 13 and 18 appear in the figure but are not named in the text. The vowels as the chapter gives them

Tongue position: high front /i/ (beet), /ɪ/ (bit); high back /u/ (boot), /ʊ/ (book); mid front /ɛ/ (bet); mid central /ə/ (the first syllable of about); mid back /ʌ/ (but); low front /æ/ (bat); low back /ɑ/ (bot). Rounded: /u/, /ʊ/, /o/. Diphthongs: /aɪ/ (ride, before voiced consonants), /ʌɪ/ (write, before voiceless consonants), /aʊ/ (loud, before voiced consonants), /ʌʊ/ (out, before voiceless consonants), /ɔɪ/ (boy), /eɪ/ (bait), /oʊ/ (boat).
