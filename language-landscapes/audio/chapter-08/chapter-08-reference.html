Notation and footnotes

-(i)fy in the verb-forming suffix list is written with the i in brackets in the book; the narration says “ify”. The footnote to “at the moment” points to https://www.ldoceonline.com/dictionary/at-the-moment. The Biber et al. page references in the chapter are 400 (verb-changing prefixes) and 401 (derivational affixes); Nation (2022: 29) is cited for proper nouns.
