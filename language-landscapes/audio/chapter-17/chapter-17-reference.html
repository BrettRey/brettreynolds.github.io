British National Corpus excerpt (section 14)

From BNC text KC8, as printed in the chapter. Square brackets mark non-speech sounds and omitted stretches; an ellipsis marks a pause or trailing off.

Gillian: I think it’s just Friday night and he’s… yeah, basically… I don’t think Billy’s telling him enough [cough] to be honest.
Robert: Why?
Gillian: I just don’t.
Robert: [yawn] What, he knows something’s happened. But he doesn’t know what it is…
Gillian: He doesn’t seem to be involved in that many… discussions about the company does he? [yawn] Or what’s happening. I mean he was moaning about erm or those trade accounts wasn’t he?
Robert: Mm… well it’s not for me to say. I don’t know anything more about this business than he does.
Gillian: Oh no.
Robert: […] go as far as to say we probably know less about what’s going on than he does.
Gillian: Do we? Oh. Such is life… I had a word with Steph on the phone.
Robert: Oh?
Gillian: She’s getting fat… […]
Robert: […]
Gillian: Yeah. She goes to hospital on Thursday. Eighteen week appointment.

Fresh Air excerpt (section 21)

As printed in the chapter (italic throughout in the book).

Donna Summer: So I always felt like I had this sort of— people to answer to and my children, and my child at the time, was one of them. And I felt that in the future I didn’t want her to say, “Mom, well, you did it.” You know? But, you know…
Gross: Did she ever say that?
Summer: Yeah, she did. Yes, she did, much…
Gross: What was your comeback?
Summer: …to my chagrin. I just told her it was a different time, and, you know, I came from a totally different life than her.
