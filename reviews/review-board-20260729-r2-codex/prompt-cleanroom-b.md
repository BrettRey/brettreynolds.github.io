## Your task

Nobody will tell you what is wrong with this page or what the redesign was
trying to achieve. Work it out yourself, from the artifact.

1. **What is this page doing?** Describe the design in plain language a
   non-designer can use.
2. **What is it claiming?** Does the layout make an argument? State it, then say
   whether the content supports it.
3. **What is wrong with it?** Ordered by severity. Be concrete and name files.
   Include anything that will not render as its author evidently intended.
4. **Audience and purpose fit.** Take the visitor types above one at a time.
   Who is served, who is failed, and where do the visuals, the words, the
   navigation, and the stated purpose come apart?
5. **Would you ship it?** Ship as is / ship with named fixes / revise
   structurally / start over. Justify in one paragraph.
